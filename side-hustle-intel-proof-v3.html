Fractional Executive Services

Local & professional servicesLow startup cost
Source: David Heacock — youtube.com/watch?v=EnSJN9zl-yA
What Is It
Offer part-time senior leadership, CFO, COO, or CMO, to businesses that need executive-level guidance but can't afford, or don't need, a full-time hire. You work with several clients at once, each paying a monthly retainer for a set number of hours or specific deliverables.
The Pitch
Package your judgment into a recurring monthly fee, typically $5,000 to $15,000 per client, and take on 4 to 6 clients at once.
Best For
Someone with a genuine, provable track record of running or operating a business, not someone who has only studied the theory of business strategy.
The Truth
Heacock backs this up with his own real hiring experience. Landing the first client still requires real, sometimes slow, relationship-based sales.

AI Ghostwriting

Marketing & contentLow startup cost
Source: Mark Tilbury and Iman Gadzhi — youtube.com/watch?v=ytAW1_g2IfI
What Is It
You use AI tools like Claude, paired with a research tool, to write LinkedIn posts, email newsletters, and ad copy on behalf of founders and creators who know they should be publishing content but don't have time to write it themselves.
The Pitch
The AI does the heavy lifting, drafting research and a first pass at the writing, while you focus on making it sound like the actual client, not a generic AI voice.
Best For
Someone who already writes reasonably well and is comfortable using AI to speed up research and a first draft.
The Truth
Tilbury is fairly upfront that AI only gets you about 75% of the way there. The remaining 25% — taste, editing, knowing what the client would actually say — is a real editorial skill this doesn't remove.

Estate Sale Company

Local & professional servicesVery low startup cost
Source: Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast (guest: Braden) — youtube.com/watch?v=E9baszgY3J8
What Is It
You contract with homeowners, or their families, to liquidate the entire contents of a house, pricing and selling everything from furniture to collectibles over a weekend sale, then taking a percentage of what sells.
The Pitch
Braden started with almost no money, just tape, a price gun, and folding tables, and says he made about $6,000 profit in his very first week.
Best For
Someone comfortable with sales, organizing, and pricing hundreds of small items, who doesn't mind physical, on-site weekend work.
The Truth
This is one of the more detailed, credible accounts in the database. Worth noting this is a single market's experience; a saturated, larger city could look different.

AI-Built Shopify Store and Dropshipping

E-commerceNeeds ongoing ad budget
Source: Mark Tilbury and Iman Gadzhi — youtube.com/watch?v=ytAW1_g2IfI
What Is It
An AI tool auto-builds a Shopify store, picks a niche, and a sourcing tool like AutoDS finds, prices, and ships the products, so the store runs with very little manual work.
The Pitch
The pitch is speed: a real, live store can be running the same day, with AI handling design, product selection, and fulfillment.
Best For
Someone with real advertising budget to spend and the patience to iterate on ad campaigns.
The Truth
Gadzhi's independent take is blunt — he describes the core dropshipping problems as unchanged by AI and calls it a race to the bottom on margins.

Compliance and Risk-Elimination Services

Local & professional servicesLow startup cost
Source: David Heacock — youtube.com/watch?v=EnSJN9zl-yA
What Is It
You become the specialized expert businesses pay to eliminate regulatory risk — product compliance certification, HIPAA compliance, OSHA workplace safety, or R&D tax credit paperwork.
The Pitch
Heacock ranks this his top category — the work is recurring, genuinely painful for the business to do themselves, document-heavy, and the customer's reputation is on the line if it's done wrong.
Best For
Someone with real experience who is willing to specialize deeply in one regulatory area rather than staying a generalist.
The Truth
Heacock mentions paying $33,600 for this kind of service himself. When he ranks this the single best remote business opportunity, that's his own opinion, not an audited statistic.

AI Voice Agent and Receptionist Services

Local & professional servicesLow startup cost
Source: Mark Tilbury and Dan Martell — youtube.com/watch?v=ytAW1_g2IfI
What Is It
Build an AI phone receptionist that answers calls, books appointments, and captures leads for local businesses like dentists, salons, or plumbers, then charge a monthly fee to keep it running.
The Pitch
Small businesses miss a large share of incoming calls every day, and most owners don't know AI receptionist tools exist yet.
Best For
Someone comfortable with sales and cold outreach, since the real bottleneck is finding the first few clients, not building the tool.
The Truth
Tilbury shows a live demo call so you can hear exactly what the tool sounds like to a customer, a useful transparency touch.

Wall Printer Mural Service for Businesses

Physical & print products$4,000 to $16,000 for equipment
Source: Chris Koerner — youtube.com/watch?v=SUYBggpA3ZU
What Is It
Own a mobile large-format printer that prints murals and graphics directly onto walls for businesses like restaurants and offices.
The Pitch
The margins here are unusually high, roughly $8 in ink materials for an $800 job, in a still-early, undereducated market.
Best For
Someone with a print or design background who's comfortable investing in equipment and doing on-site work for local business clients.
The Truth
This does require physically operating the printer on-site at each client's location, so it isn't a fully remote business.
