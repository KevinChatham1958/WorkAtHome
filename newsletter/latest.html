GIG OF THE WEEK
YouTube Thumbnail Agency

Marketing & content · Low startup cost (design software to start; hiring designers comes later)

From Iman Gadzhi's YouTube video · 7.3M views

What It Is

You run a service designing YouTube thumbnails for creators. Gadzhi's model is to do the work yourself until about $3,000 to $5,000 a month, then hire remote designers in places like Brazil, Romania or India for around $1,000 a month each, so each designer's output brings in several thousand dollars of business. He frames it as a lean, remote service business that produces cash you can actually keep, unlike inventory businesses.

The Pitch

The creator says thumbnails are "such an important aspect" of a YouTube video, and hiring remote designers is "leverage" that frees your time while you keep a margin. He says a lean, remote service business produces cash you can actually keep, unlike inventory businesses.

Who It's Best For

People with design skills, or the ability to manage designers, who can sell to YouTube creators.

The Truth (as we see it)

This video is from 2023, more than two years old, so the tools, prices and platform rules it describes may have changed. The thumbnail agency is a passing example in a mostly mindset video, with no client-finding or pricing plan, and as he notes creators don't pay much per thumbnail, so volume is needed. Hiring abroad means managing quality, time zones and contractor paperwork. Gadzhi built his name selling agency-building courses, and the video points viewers to another of his videos about business models.
