AI Lead Generation Agency

AI lead-generation agency for local businesses (Meta ads + GoHighLevel AI voice caller, clients won through your own paid ads)

AI & automation · Needs ongoing ad budget (GoHighLevel at $297-$497/month plus roughly $1,000-$2,000 set aside for your own Meta ads)

Originally published Oct 31, 2026 — Carson Reed

What It Is

You pick one niche of local businesses (real estate agents, roofers, HVAC, med spas, dentists and so on) and sell them a done-for-you system: Facebook and Instagram ads that bring in leads, plus an AI voice caller built inside GoHighLevel that phones each new lead right away, qualifies them and books them onto the client's calendar. The client pays the ad spend (about $20-$100 a day) separately from your fee. The creator suggests beginners start with a low-risk offer such as a $300 three-week ads-only trial or pay-per-result, then move up to $3,000-$5,000 paid in full for 90 days or $1,000-$2,000 a month, with a monthly maintenance fee afterward. You find your own clients mainly by running Meta ads to a booking page (AI-generated image ads or AI-actor video ads), then close them on a Zoom sales call using a discovery, demo and close structure. The ongoing work is onboarding the client, getting access to their Facebook ad account, launching and testing ads, building the AI caller, and holding weekly check-in calls.

The Pitch

The creator says this beats day trading, dropshipping, plain ad agencies and AI automation builds on startup cost, profit margin (70-80%) and deal size, because business owners are curious about AI and every business wants more booked appointments. He argues AI now does most of the building and that paid ads can put sales calls on your calendar within days, compared with months of cold outreach. He says he went from a teenager with no clients to over $100,000 a month and, in a public challenge, collected $60,000 in 30 days with a new AI agency.

Who It's Best For

People comfortable on sales calls and with spending their own money on ads, willing to commit 3-6 months or more before the first client, and able to handle scheduled client calls and reporting.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core model, running ads for a local business and following up leads fast, is a long-established agency service; the AI caller is the new part, and GoHighLevel really does include a built-in voice agent. The creator is unusually frank that a first client often takes 60-90 days or longer, that many clients get mediocre or poor results, and that local owners may blame you when they fail to close the leads you send them. His income figures ($1M+ profit by 19, $60,000 in 30 days) are his own and unaudited. The free course and 'free' community come through his GoHighLevel affiliate signup, and he also shares affiliate links for a payment processor and the Arcads AI video tool, so he earns while you pay for the software, plus a Rolex giveaway to drive signups. Budget for software and test ads before income arrives, use written agreements, and be careful with outcome guarantees and outreach rules (he himself warns that cold texting is illegal without consent).
