UV-Printed Custom Products

Desk-sized UV printer shop for custom canvas art, phone cases, signs and gifts

Physical & print products · Moderate startup cost (a desktop UV flatbed printer about $2,000-6,000)

What It Is

You use a desktop UV flatbed printer to print full-color, textured images directly onto canvas, vinyl, phone cases, wood and acrylic, selling custom wall art, personalized gifts, promotional banners and signage from a home print shop.

The Pitch

The narrator says a custom canvas costs $2-5 in materials and sells for $30-80, and estimates 10-20 pieces a day could earn $5,000-15,000 a month, noting the machine fits on a desk and connects to any computer.

Who It's Best For

Someone with design skills who can sell personalized products online or to local businesses.

The Truth (as we see it)

UV printers are a real home-business tool, but quality machines and inks are costly, and the custom-gift market is competitive. Don't print copyrighted characters or logos without permission. The video is a fast 50-machine roundup with brief segments, so it doesn't cover selling, permits or real operating experience; the income ranges are the narrator's estimates, not results shown.
