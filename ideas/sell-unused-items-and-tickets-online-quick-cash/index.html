Selling Unused Items and Tickets

Selling unused household items and event tickets online for quick cash

E-commerce · Near-zero cost (you sell what you already own)

Originally published Sep 28, 2026 — Arlan Hamilton

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Arlan Hamilton · 3 Ways to Make $300 in the Next Hour (TODAY) ↗

One of 3 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You go through your home for things you no longer need, such as brand-new items still in the package, clothing, shoes, furniture, electronics, equipment, spare collectibles, or tickets to a concert or sports event you will skip, and list them for sale online. The money comes from turning unused possessions into cash. The creator frames it as one of three ways to raise about $300 in an hour, by selling and then delivering afterward.

The Pitch

The creator calls it the option people are most used to and says almost everyone has physical things "taking up space" that they could live without. It requires no new skills or setup.

Who It's Best For

Anyone who needs a one-time cash boost and has items worth selling.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a legitimate way to raise money, but it is a one-time decluttering income rather than an ongoing business, since it is limited to what you already own. Selling within an hour is optimistic; marketplace items often take days to sell, and buyers may expect local pickup or shipping. Ticket resale can be restricted by the venue or ticketing platform, so check the rules first. The video promotes the creator's paid 10K challenge.
