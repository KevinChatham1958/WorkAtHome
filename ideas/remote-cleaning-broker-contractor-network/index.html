Running a 100% Remote Cleaning Business by Brokering Jobs to Independent Contractor Cleaners, Never Visiting a Job Site Yourself

Local & professional services · Low (a simple website, Google Business Profile, a separate phone number; paid ads optional on top)

What It Is

Build a cleaning business entirely around brokering, never doing the cleaning yourself or hiring W-2 employees: set up a simple website, Google Business Profile, and dedicated phone number in your chosen city, source cleaners for free through local Facebook/Nextdoor group posts (filtering roughly 100 applicants down to about 20 reliable independent contractors), take bookings and route them to a vetted cleaner while keeping 50-60% of each job (roughly $150-175 of a $300-350 clean), and convert first-time customers to recurring bookings via a post-job quality-control call; use paid ads (Google LSA, Thumbtack, Meta) alongside organic recruiting once running.

The Pitch

The presenter cites two friends with no cleaning background who started this as a side project alongside full-time furniture-store jobs, reaching $10,000/month within a couple months and $60,000/month in top-line revenue, still working their day jobs throughout; he frames the $40+ billion cleaning industry as extremely fragmented, meaning local competition is mostly unsophisticated solo operators.

Best For

Someone comfortable managing an online-only operation (booking, dispatching, vetting cleaners, running ads) without ever personally cleaning a house or hiring in-house staff.

The Truth

The customer-acquisition-plus-independent-contractor-network model for local service businesses is real and sound, and the specific unit economics given are consistent, plausible numbers. The case study is self-reported, and the video funnels toward the presenters' own paid one-on-one application/mentorship program.
