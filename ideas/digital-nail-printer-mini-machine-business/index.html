What It Is

A portable printer that prints a custom nail design (a photo, logo, or pattern) directly onto a client's fingernail in about 30 seconds, connected and controlled entirely from a phone, requiring no artistic skill or manicure experience.

The Pitch

A custom nail design at a salon typically runs $30-60, and this machine lets you offer a comparable service charging $15-40 per nail with only cents of ink and adhesive as cost, using a setup light enough (under half a kilo) to carry to a fair, birthday party, or quinceañera and set up a nail station anywhere with just a portable charger.

Best For

Someone looking for a very low-skill, event-based side income who's comfortable working in-person at parties, fairs, or pop-up events rather than a fixed location.
