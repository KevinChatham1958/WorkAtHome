AI-generated Ebook Bundle Sold Through an X (Twitter) Growth Account With Automated DM Funnel

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost (about $35 for an X Premium subscription and a Tweet Hunter subscription)

Originally published Sep 8, 2026 — Alex Suzuki

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Alex Suzuki · I Tried A.I. Digital Products for 48 hours (raw results) ↗

What It Is

Use an AI model to generate a bundle of several themed ebooks on a single topic (e.g. AI automation for small businesses), then grow a topic-focused X (Twitter) account by posting a small number of high-quality posts per day, and use an automation tool (Tweet Hunter) to send everyone who likes or comments on a post an automatic DM containing a checkout link to buy the ebook bundle.

The Pitch

In a filmed 48-hour live attempt, the presenter used an AI model to generate five themed ebooks (roughly 50-80 pages each) on AI automation for small businesses, bundled them for $300, built a new X account, posted six AI-generated posts over two days following stated algorithm rules (share-optimized content, no more than 3-6 posts/day, following back followers), and set up Tweet Hunter to auto-DM a checkout link to anyone who liked or commented — generating seven sales ($2,100 gross, about $2,065 after the $35 in tool costs) against an $8,000 goal.

Best For

Someone comfortable posting consistently on X around a specific topic and willing to treat this as a genuine multi-week-to-month growth project rather than an overnight win, since the presenter is explicit that his own results rely on three years of prior audience-building and dozens of existing accounts.

The Truth

The demonstrated funnel mechanics (share-optimized posting cadence, algorithm-friendly account behavior, and an automated like/comment-to-DM tool) are shown live with real, verified results via a Stripe dashboard, which is more transparent than most "I made $X" claims. That said, the presenter repeatedly notes his results ("$15 million" in career revenue, 50+ Twitter accounts, aged/verified accounts) reflect years of unrepeatable advantage, the video ends by funneling into a paid coaching community and a much longer paid course, and the live result fell well short of the stated $8,000 goal — a realistic starting point looks more like the $2,065 actually shown than the headline target.
