Medical Courier Service

Medical courier service using your own vehicle

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (your own car; no special equipment)

What It Is

Deliver medical supplies, specimens, and blood drive collections between facilities using your own vehicle, contracting with medical courier companies rather than general delivery apps like DoorDash or Uber.

The Pitch

Presenters in the video report earning $1,200-2,500 per week doing pickups and deliveries (blood drive collections, medical supply loads) using their own car, describing the pay as noticeably better than a regular job or standard gig-delivery apps.

Who It's Best For

Someone with a reliable vehicle who wants delivery-style gig work with better pay than standard rideshare/delivery apps, and who's comfortable handling sensitive medical materials responsibly.

The Truth (as we see it)

The pay rates described are genuinely higher than typical gig-delivery apps, which makes sense given the specialized, time-sensitive nature of medical deliveries. This kind of courier work typically requires passing a background check and sometimes specific certifications (e.g., handling biohazard materials) that aren't detailed in the video, so expect some onboarding requirements beyond just signing up.
