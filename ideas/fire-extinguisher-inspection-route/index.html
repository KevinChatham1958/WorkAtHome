Fire Extinguisher Testing and Inspection Route

What It Is

Inspect and tag fire extinguishers for offices, restaurants, warehouses, and apartment buildings on the legally required annual inspection schedule, building up a recurring route of accounts over time.

The Pitch

Every commercial building is legally required to get its extinguishers inspected on a set schedule, and fire marshals actually check for the tag, which means the law itself does the sales pitch for you. Once you're inspecting a building's extinguishers, switching to a new inspector means a customer has to re-explain their whole building to someone new, which the presenters say makes accounts unusually sticky once you land them.

Best For

Someone comfortable with slower, longer commercial sales cycles who's willing to build up a large route of low-ticket accounts over time rather than looking for one big score.

The Truth

The presenters are honest that this sits mid-list specifically because it isn't high-ticket per visit, so building meaningful income requires a genuinely large route and patience with commercial sales cycles. They note it's a fragmented market with no dominant national brand, a real and checkable structural fact about the industry.
