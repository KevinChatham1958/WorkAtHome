AI Logo Packs on Etsy

What It Is

You generate logo designs with AI image tools, clean them up, and bundle them into packs that small businesses and creators buy on Etsy and customize themselves. Buyers include small shop owners, podcasters and social media creators who don't want to pay a designer.

The Pitch

The presenter says some Etsy shops have done over six figures selling logo packs, some with simple $10 sets sold on volume and others with premium packages over $20 that bundle watercolor marks, monograms and sub-marks. He frames the demand as people who want a logo without paying a designer around $500. He notes AI logos often have garbled pseudo-letters that need cleanup in Photopea, a free browser-based Photoshop alternative.

Best For

People willing to learn basic image editing and who are comfortable answering buyer questions about file formats and customization.

The Truth

The six-figure shop claims are unverified examples. He is upfront that this one requires real cleanup work and that Etsy sellers carry all customer service. Buyers of logos often need vector files and clear usage rights, and AI-generated marks raise questions about originality and trademarking that a seller should understand. The presenter's broader video promotes his own KDP coaching and book tool.
