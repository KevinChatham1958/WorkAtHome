Same-Day Mini Services

Same-day done-for-you mini service (landing page, edit, email sequence)

What It Is

Instead of teaching someone, you do a small task for them and hand it back quickly, often the same day. Examples in the video are building a landing page with the AI site builder Lovable, editing a video, writing an email sequence, setting up a Stan Store page, helping with an AI setup or reworking a pitch deck for investors. You create an offer with limited spots and a price, take payment through Stan Store, PayPal or Stripe, promote it to people you already know, and deliver afterward. The creator says $300 is the minimum she would charge for a landing page.

The Pitch

The creator says the skills you take for granted from the past week or two are things somebody else needs, and that clients are impressed by fast turnaround. She describes reworking two founders' pitch deck in a couple of hours as a favor and says, "somebody could do that and charge for that."

Who It's Best For

People who can already build a simple page, edit video or write marketing copy and want to turn that into paid one-off jobs.

The Truth (as we see it)

Small productized freelance jobs are a common and legitimate way to start earning from a skill, and promoting to a warm network does work for many beginners. The quality bar matters: a rushed same-day landing page or deck can disappoint a client, so the price should match what you can genuinely deliver. Selling still requires reaching out directly to contacts, and demand for each service varies. The video is partly a pitch for the creator's paid 10K challenge and references her own Lovable training; any income figures are hers and unverified.
