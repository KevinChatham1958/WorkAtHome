Notary Template Packs

Template packs for notary loan signing agents sold on Etsy

Digital & subscription products · Near-zero cost (Etsy listing fees and a design tool)

What It Is

You build ready-made paperwork bundles for loan signing agents, the notaries who walk homebuyers through mortgage signings. A pack can include client intake forms, signing prep sheets, invoice templates, price sheets and marketing flyers for title companies. Each pack is made once and sold repeatedly as a digital download for roughly $5 to $25. The creator suggests listing them on Etsy and marketing them specifically to notaries.

The Pitch

The creator says you are selling to people who are trying to earn money themselves, which he considers a better market than hobby buyers: a notary earning $75 to $200 per signing will happily pay $15 for a template. He adds that thousands of people become notaries every year, so the pool of new buyers keeps refilling.

Who It's Best For

People who understand small-business paperwork, or who have notary or real estate experience, and are comfortable doing a little niche marketing.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling templates to a specific profession is a sensible, low-risk digital product, and the creator is right that business buyers are less price-sensitive than hobbyists. He gives no sales figures for this particular niche, so demand on Etsy is unverified, and established notary training sites already give away or bundle similar forms. Forms touching legal and mortgage paperwork should avoid anything that looks like legal advice, and rules for notaries differ by state. The creator sells a one-on-one book-publishing mentorship ("Road to Hero") promoted in this video.
