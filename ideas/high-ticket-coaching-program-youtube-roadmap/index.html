Building a High-ticket Group Coaching Program From an Existing YouTube Channel

Local & professional services · Varies (mostly time; low direct cost to start)

Originally published Aug 17, 2026 — Ed Lawrence

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Ed Lawrence · The ONLY System You Need To Make $100k/mth From YouTube ↗

What It Is

A roadmap for creators who already have a YouTube channel and audience in a health, wealth, relationship, or serious-hobbyist niche (music, photography, and similar) to build and launch a group coaching program: survey and interview your existing viewers to find their top three problems and the false beliefs stopping them from buying, then build an offer, pricing, and sales page copy directly around what that research reveals rather than guessing.

The Pitch

Lawrence, who says he's generated eight figures from high-ticket programs and helped hundreds of creators with a claimed 90% success rate, illustrates the method with a real pivot: a client's planned generic "productivity" program was reshaped, after viewer surveys and calls revealed the audience was mostly high-pressure tech workers, into a program specifically about managing career pressure, which then sold out at over $5,000 per seat. He gives a specific readiness checklist (niche, comment engagement ratio, email list size and open rate, existing sales, and returning-viewer rate) and a concrete pricing tactic: start at a discounted "founding member" rate below your long-term target price to make the first launch feel less intimidating.

Best For

An existing content creator with a genuine, engaged audience (even a small one, Lawrence stresses total view count matters less than returning-viewer loyalty) who's willing to do direct audience research before building anything, rather than assuming they already know what their viewers want.

The Truth

Lawrence's headline outcomes (a program hitting $60K in six weeks, another reaching $100K/month in 120 days) are specific client examples he cites but doesn't independently document within the video itself, and he runs a paid tool and coaching offer that this video partly promotes. The core research method, surveys and calls to uncover real audience problems and objections before building an offer, is a general, independently applicable principle that doesn't require his specific program or tools to execute.
