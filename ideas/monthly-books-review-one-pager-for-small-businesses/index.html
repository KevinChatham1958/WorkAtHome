Monthly Books-Review One-Pager

Monthly "books review" one-pager for small businesses, drafted by an AI agent from QuickBooks and Stripe

What It Is

Each month you give small-business clients a one-page summary of their finances: profit and loss, cash flow, expenses that look off, and overdue invoices, with payment reminders drafted for their approval. An AI agent connected to the client's QuickBooks and Stripe drafts it and you review it. The creator suggests about $200 a month per client and calling it a books review rather than accounting.

The Pitch

The creator points to a shortage of bookkeepers, tens of millions of small businesses that must file every year, and a bookkeeping startup that shut down in late 2024 and left thousands of businesses stranded. Comparable services cost $250-500 a month.

Best For

Someone with bookkeeping or finance familiarity who is careful with numbers and comfortable handling clients' financial data.

The Truth

Recurring monthly reporting is a solid service model, but accuracy is everything, and the line between a summary and actual bookkeeping or tax advice matters, as the creator hints. Clients will expect you to catch errors the agent misses. The figures are his suggested pricing, not reported results. The creator offers free prompts through his community.
