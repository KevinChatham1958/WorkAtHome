AI Workflow Consultant

What It Is

Walk into a local business, a plumber, dental practice, or small agency, and ask three simple questions to find one repetitive task worth automating, then rebuild that task using free or low-cost tools like Claude and Zapier so it runs on its own, charging an upfront audit fee plus an ongoing monthly retainer to keep it running.

The Pitch

Hawksworth frames the pitch as a simple three-question framework anyone can use on a cold walk-in or call: what task do you do most and hate most, does it follow the same steps every time, and what does an hour of your time actually cost. He suggests charging roughly £1,000 for the initial audit and £300 to £500 a month to maintain the automation, arguing that businesses aren't really buying AI, they're buying relief from stress.

Best For

Someone comfortable approaching local business owners directly, in person or by phone, and translating a vague complaint about busywork into a specific, sellable automation.

The Truth

The pricing figures given (£1,000 audit, £300-500/month retainer) are the presenter's own suggested starting points, not verified case studies of what businesses have actually paid him. The three-question diagnostic framework itself is straightforward and genuinely reusable regardless of the specific price points.
