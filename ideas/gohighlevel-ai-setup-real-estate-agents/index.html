GoHighLevel AI for Realtors

GoHighLevel AI setup for real estate agents (Zillow client sourcing)

Local & professional services · Low startup cost (software subscription)

Jason Wardrop · I Built This AI Setup For Realtors → Charge $997/mo Per Client ↗

What It Is

Use Zillow's own agent directory to find real estate agents who are actively selling (filterable by top agent, buyer/seller agent, and area), build each one a free AI-generated website with an embedded AI chatbot trained on their listings and reviews, then convert the free build into a paid $299 to $997 a month retainer for ongoing lead follow-up and social content.

The Pitch

Wardrop, who says he worked with over 15,000 real estate agent clients from 2015 to 2021, walks through the exact mechanics: search Zillow's agent directory for a specific city, filter to top-performing agents since part-time agents likely can't afford the service, then use a single AI prompt to auto-generate a professional website, complete with a chatbot trained specifically on that agent's listings and reviews. The pitch to the agent is built on reciprocity, showing up with a finished, free website rather than asking for anything upfront.

Best For

Someone comfortable with a give-first outreach approach and willing to package one repeatable offer (website plus chatbot plus lead follow-up) that can be resold to agent after agent with minimal customization.

The Truth

Wardrop discloses he earns an affiliate commission from the AI software he's demonstrating and is promoting his own free workshop and course throughout the video. The underlying mechanic, Zillow as a sourcing tool, a free build as a reciprocity hook, is specific and independently testable regardless of which particular software you choose to build with.
