Trending Meme Print-on-Demand Store

A Shopify print-on-demand store where an AI agent turns trending X moments into daily limited-time shirt and mug designs

E-commerce · Low startup cost (Shopify plan, AI image tool, email marketing tool)

What It Is

An AI agent watches X for rising, harmless viral moments, generates meme designs with an AI image tool (Higgsfield), creates the products through Printful and drafts the Shopify listings for you to approve. Each design is a "daily drop" with a countdown, and an abandoned-cart email sequence runs through ActiveCampaign or Klaviyo. Printful prints and ships each order, so you hold no stock. The creator projects about $79 a day from eight shirts at $24 each, with Printful charging about $12.95 per shirt.

The Pitch

The creator says it could not be easier because the bot handles trend spotting, design, store building and listings, and print-on-demand means no inventory. He shows a live store the bot built, including upsells, cart drawer and abandoned-cart emails.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants a low-inventory e-commerce test and will review every design for taste and rights issues before it goes live.

The Truth (as we see it)

The $79-a-day figure is a projection; no store sales are shown. Viral moments often involve real people, pets, brands or footage owned by others, so turning them into merchandise risks copyright, trademark and right-of-publicity complaints and Shopify or Printful takedowns. Daily trend stores usually need traffic from ads or social posting, which the video does not cover in detail. The creator sells ready-made bot templates in his paid community.
