Commercial Pressure Washing

Commercial-focused pressure washing priced per square foot instead of per residential job

Local & professional services · Low to moderate startup cost (roughly $3,000 for a commercial-grade pressure washer, hoses, and extension wand)

What It Is

Rather than competing on $100-200 residential driveway jobs, target commercial exteriors, parking garages, and roofs, pricing by square footage (roughly $0.25-0.50/sq ft) so a single commercial job can produce $1,500 or more in half a day, using proper soft-washing chemicals and low-pressure technique (rather than blasting everything at full pressure) to avoid property damage, and marketing with before/after photos and review requests after every job.

The Pitch

The illustrative account describes a construction worker with 25 years of trade experience picking up the skill from a friend, investing about $3,000 in equipment, and immediately out-earning his prior day rate — but also describes a costly early mistake: using full water pressure without understanding proper chemical application blew out a seal and flooded a client's unit, an expensive lesson that led to properly learning soft-wash technique before taking on more commercial work.

Best For

Someone willing to learn proper chemical/pressure technique before taking commercial jobs (the margin for error is real and costly), and interested in targeting business and property clients rather than only residential driveways.

The Truth

This reads as an illustrative/composite story rather than a verified individual, so treat the specific dollar figures as representative rather than audited. The commercial-versus-residential pricing distinction and the soft-washing safety lesson are genuine, checkable facts about the pressure washing trade independent of the narrative; property damage from improper technique is a real and documented risk in this industry, not just a dramatic plot point.
