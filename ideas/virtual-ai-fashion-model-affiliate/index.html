What It Is

Create short AI-generated videos of a virtual fashion model wearing real, purchasable clothing, post them on Instagram, and monetize through affiliate links (commonly Amazon Associates) placed in the bio, earning a commission whenever a viewer buys the featured item or anything else during that shopping session.

The Pitch

Sarah points to real, viral examples of this content style already earning millions of views, with viewers directly asking in the comments where to buy the featured clothes, evidence of real purchase intent, not just passive viewing. The Amazon Associates program pays out on anything a referred visitor adds to their cart within 24 hours of clicking the link, not just the specific item promoted, which can meaningfully add to the commission total beyond the featured product alone.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with AI image and video generation tools and consistent, frequent content posting, since this model depends on a steady stream of new short-form videos rather than a one-time setup.

The Truth (as we see it)

The engagement numbers cited (millions of views, direct buy-intent comments) are drawn from real, visible public posts, a genuinely checkable claim, but no actual affiliate earnings figures are shown for any specific account, so the income potential itself is inferred from engagement and commission structure rather than demonstrated. Building the linked storefront also requires a separate free tool (Beacons) beyond the AI generation tools themselves.
