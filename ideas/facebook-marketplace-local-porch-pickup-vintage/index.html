Local-Pickup Marketplace Reselling

Selling vintage finds, decluttered items and refinished furniture on Facebook Marketplace with local porch pickup only

  • Online stores
  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Video published Nov 15, 2026 by Flips & Finds with Sarah

Startup cost: Near-zero cost (start with items already in your home) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Source video

Flips & Finds with Sarah · I made MORE money on Facebook Marketplace than my local Vintage booth this year! ↗

What It Is

You list household items, vintage finds and refinished furniture on Facebook Marketplace and sell them for cash or app payment with local pickup only, never shipping. Buyers collect from your porch: items sit in a tub on an old dresser, and buyers leave payment in the drawer, so late arrivals cost you no time. The creator lists at least one to three items every day, uses up to 10 photos and a video with full measurements and flaws in the description, prices $5 to $10 above her bottom line to leave room for haggling, and holds her price for at least two weeks. She checks buyer profiles before giving out her address and never lists anything free or under $10. She says she made over $10,000 this way by mid-November, mostly from decluttering plus some furniture, after leaving a vintage booth she had rented for three years.

The Pitch

The creator says she made almost double what her vintage booth earned in its last full year, with no booth rent and no platform fees, because local sales on Marketplace carry no commission. She says porch pickup is close to set-and-forget, and that her local sales dropped sharply in the month she turned on shipping, then recovered when she went back to local only.

Who It’s Best For

People with things to sell and room to store larger pieces, especially furniture refinishers and thrifters who would rather not pack and ship. It suits sellers who can answer messages within an hour or two.

The Truth (as we see it)

The tips are practical and come from years of selling, and the no-fee, no-shipping model is real, but the $10,000 figure is her own unaudited number and most of it came from decluttering her home, which runs out unless you keep sourcing. Her claims about how the algorithm favors daily listing and penalizes shipping sellers are her own observations, not anything Facebook has confirmed. Porch pickup means sharing your home address with strangers; she vets buyers carefully, but she also says it is your call, and meeting in public is the safer option. On taxes, she is clear she is not a tax preparer: selling for a profit as a reseller or furniture flipper is taxable income, and you should check the rules where you live. No paid product is promoted.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
