What It Is

Produce portable, individually-jarred mini cheesecakes (classic, strawberry, chocolate, salted caramel, plus seasonal flavors) for sale as singles or four-packs, with corporate and wedding customization available via lid labels, sold at farmers markets, coffee shops, and via Instagram pre-orders.

The Pitch

Focus on creamy texture, fresh toppings, and visually appealing layering, using insulated carriers for local delivery and behind-the-scenes baking content to build a following; rotating limited flavors is suggested to keep demand high and encourage repeat purchases rather than a single static menu.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with food production and refrigerated-product logistics (proper cold storage and transport), interested in both retail market sales and event/corporate catering orders.

The Truth (as we see it)

Jarred, portioned desserts are a well-established, popular format at markets and events because they're easy to sample and transport, and the wedding/corporate customization angle is a credible way to land higher-value orders. This is a perishable, refrigeration-dependent product, so cold-chain logistics and any local cottage-food/health regulations need real attention before scaling beyond a hobby volume.
