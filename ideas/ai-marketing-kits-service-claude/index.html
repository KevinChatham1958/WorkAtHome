AI Marketing Kits

What It Is

Use a single detailed Claude prompt to generate a complete marketing kit for a business, ideal-customer research, brand positioning, website copy, social content pillars and scripts, a posting calendar, and image-generation prompts, then package it as a polished deliverable (a Google Doc or PDF) rather than raw AI output.

The Pitch

The presenter positions this as one of the most flexible offers because it can be sold on its own ("I'll create a complete AI marketing kit for your brand") or bundled to make other offers, like a Shopify store build or content creation package, feel far more valuable and complete. He demonstrates the output live: a few basic inputs (business name, audience, product, brand tone) produce a full customer-research profile, headline and offer copy, objection-handling FAQ, and a social content strategy with specific hooks and scripts.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants a single, repeatable, high-perceived-value deliverable that doesn't require ongoing execution work, since the kit itself is the product rather than an ongoing service relationship.

The Truth (as we see it)

The demonstration in the video uses a fictional example business, so the specific output quality shown is a live but staged proof of concept rather than a real client result. The underlying prompt-based approach is straightforward enough to test yourself with a free-tier AI tool before committing to selling it as a paid service.
