Faceless YouTube Channel Monetized Through ClickBank Affiliate Commissions

What It Is

Build a faceless YouTube channel (no camera, AI-generated or self-recorded voiceover only) around a digital product found on the ClickBank affiliate marketplace, using Claude to write the video script and an AI idea-generation tool to pick topics with a higher predicted chance of going viral, then earning a commission every time a viewer clicks through and buys the promoted product.

The Pitch

The pitch leans on removing the traditional barriers to YouTube monetization (subscriber and watch-hour thresholds) entirely, since income here comes from affiliate commissions, not YouTube's own ad program, so it can start on day one with zero subscribers. The suggested workflow is specific: sort ClickBank's marketplace by "gravity" (a real metric indicating how many affiliates are currently making sales on a given product) to find already-proven offers, use Claude to draft a script matching a chosen high-scoring topic, record a voiceover (options ranging from a cloned AI voice to a $10 Fiverr voice artist), and outsource editing cheaply on Fiverr rather than doing it yourself.

Best For

Someone comfortable writing and iterating on AI-generated scripts and thumbnails, and willing to treat this as a genuine content-volume business (repeatable across niches and products) rather than a single-video bet.

The Truth

The ClickBank "gravity" metric is a real, transparent feature of the platform and a genuinely useful way to gauge existing demand before promoting a product, a good sign. However, the specific product highlighted in the video as the example, a "brain-frequency" audio product marketed with vague neuroscience-adjacent claims, is the kind of unverifiable wellness product worth being skeptical of promoting, separate from whether the underlying affiliate-marketing business model itself is sound. The video also heavily promotes a paid idea-generation tool as close to mandatory ("I genuinely don't recommend trying this method without it"), a clear financial incentive behind that specific recommendation, though the core ClickBank-plus-faceless-content mechanic works without it.
