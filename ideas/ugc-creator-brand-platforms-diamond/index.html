What It Is

You make short, authentic product videos that brands use in their ads and social media. You don't need followers: you build a portfolio of sample videos with products you already own, apply for free-product campaigns to practise, then pitch paid deals on platforms like Collabary, Social Cat, Instant and Popular Pays. It's all filmed at home.

The Pitch

A creator shown in the video says she made over $1,200 in paid UGC deals after starting at the beginning of October and was in talks with four more brands. She recommends picking a niche, such as skincare, so brands see you as an expert.

Who It’s Best For

People who are comfortable on camera and good at clean, simple filming and editing.

The Truth (as we see it)

Many people are now chasing UGC work, so early deals are often gifted products rather than cash. Income is irregular and depends on constant pitching. Diamond uses an affiliate link for HighLevel elsewhere in the video.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
