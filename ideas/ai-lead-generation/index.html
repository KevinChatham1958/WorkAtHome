What It Is

Use AI tools to find and qualify sales leads for businesses, then sell the qualified prospect list or contact information directly to companies who need new customers.

The Pitch

This is directly tied to revenue for the buyer, a business can immediately see whether the leads you're providing turn into sales, which makes it easier to justify paying for.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with sales or marketing instincts who's comfortable proving results with real numbers rather than promises.

The Truth (as we see it)

Gadzhi rates this his top tier, calling it a real business need, not hype. Worth knowing: this is a mature, well-established category, meaning there's real competition already in the space, and some practitioners report that certain traditional lead-generation tactics have grown less effective as more operators have entered the market in recent years. A differentiated approach matters more here than in newer AI categories.
