Diagnostic Quiz App for Coaching

Claude- and Lovable-built diagnostic quiz app that feeds a coaching offer

AI & automation · Near-zero cost (free tiers of Claude and Lovable; no ad spend)

Originally published Aug 5, 2026 — Luna Vega

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Luna Vega · I Made $13,400 Selling Mini Web Apps With Claude AI ↗

What It Is

You use Claude to scan Reddit for the problems your audience vents about, then pick one narrow problem (for example, tracking protein rather than fixing a whole weight-loss journey). Claude drafts the app spec (3 to 5 questions, background scoring, a result screen and a call to action), and Lovable builds it, including an email capture before results. The quiz is free or about $7, and its result screen and follow-up emails point users to a paid offer such as a $500 cohort, a retainer or a $2,000 coaching program. Traffic comes from Threads posts that move from awareness to problem to an invitation to try the app.

The Pitch

The creator says information is now cheap because anyone can ask AI, but people pay for tools that give them results. She says this funnel produced 287 leads, 41 app users, 14 qualified prospects and 4 clients, totaling $13,400, with zero ad spend. She also cites a client who reportedly made $10,000 in 30 days with the method.

Who It's Best For

Coaches, consultants or experts who already have, or want to build, a higher-priced service and are comfortable on sales calls or webinars.

The Truth (as we see it)

The money in this model comes from selling coaching or services, not from the app itself, so it suits people with a real offer and expertise; the creator admits it is harder if you are new. Her funnel numbers are specific but unverified. Building a working quiz with no-code AI tools is realistic, though you should test it carefully, as she advises. The video promotes her free guide of seven Claude prompts, and she sells coaching.
