Low-Cost Facebook Ad Management

Low-cost Facebook and Instagram ad management for small local businesses, run with an AI agent

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (AI agent subscription; clients pay their own ad spend)

What It Is

You run paid Facebook and Instagram ads for small businesses that don't advertise yet, or tune the ads of those that do. An AI agent connected to the client's page and ad account does the research, sets up and adjusts campaigns, and produces reports, while you manage the client relationship. The creator suggests $1,000-5,000 a month, starting at the low end to undercut agencies.

The Pitch

The creator says about half of small businesses run no paid ads, and that an agent built by the platform's own company can plug straight into their accounts, letting one person manage several clients at a fraction of agency cost.

Best For

Someone who understands advertising basics and can sell to and report back to local business owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

Ad management is an established service, but results depend on the client's offer, budget and your judgment, not just the tool, and owners will judge you on sales. Winning clients takes outreach. The pricing is the creator's suggestion, not reported results. The creator offers free prompts through his community.
