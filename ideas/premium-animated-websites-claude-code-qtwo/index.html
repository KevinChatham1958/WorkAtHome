Animated Custom Websites

Premium animated custom websites built with Claude Code for businesses

AI & automation · Low startup cost ($20/month Claude plan, about $10/month hosting and a domain)

Originally published Oct 2, 2026 — Christian Peverelli

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Christian Peverelli · How to Build $10,000 Websites Using Claude Code (Full Guide) ↗

What It Is

You use Claude Code with design add-ons (Anthropic's front-end design skill and a community UI/UX skill) to build custom, animated websites without writing code yourself. The video builds a car showcase site: a written brief turned into a first prompt, interior photo sections with motion, an interactive 3D-style hero adapted from a component library, a unified color scheme, mobile testing, and deployment to a custom domain through a Hostinger connection. The creator defines a '$10,000 website' as one with a clear goal such as booking calls, custom design, motion, mobile support, and working forms connected to a CRM or email tool. You can build such sites for your own business or sell them to clients.

The Pitch

The creator says AI-built sites usually look generic, and the skills, component libraries and step-by-step design passes shown here let a non-coder produce something that looks premium enough to command a high price.

Best For

Design-minded people who enjoy iterating on visuals and can explain a website's business purpose to clients.

The Truth

The build process is shown in detail and is realistic, but the '$10,000' figure is a label, not a sale; the video shows no client paying that price and says nothing about how to find clients. Heavy animation and 3D elements can slow sites and break on phones, so testing matters. The creator uses a Hostinger discount code and offers prompts through his free community, which likely leads to paid offers. Charging premium prices usually depends on a portfolio and sales skills more than on the tools.
