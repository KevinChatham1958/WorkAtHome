What It Is

Offer video editing services to creators and brands, using AI tools to speed up cuts, captions, and repurposing footage into multiple formats.

The Pitch

AI has significantly lowered the skill floor for competent video editing, making this an accessible service to offer even without a traditional editing background.

Who It's Best For

Someone looking for a straightforward, in-demand service with a short learning curve.

The Truth (as we see it)

Because the tools have made this easy to learn, it's also easy to copy, Gadzhi frames it plainly as a race to the bottom on pricing unless you find a way to specialize or work with higher-value clients.
