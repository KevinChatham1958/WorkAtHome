PDF Guides via Influencer Sponsorships

AI-generated PDF guides sold via influencer sponsorships

  • Low startup cost

Source video

Money Talk With Leon · I Just Used Claude AI To Make $50,000 In 30 Days Online ↗

What It Is

Use a paid trend-research tool to identify specific problems people are actively searching for solutions to within a niche, then have Claude write and design a complete PDF guide addressing that problem, sell it through a simple Shopify store, and drive traffic by paying niche YouTube influencers to mention the product.

The Pitch

The creator says this process generated roughly $211,000 over four months (about $52,000 a month on average) selling a parenting-focused PDF guide, and walks through his exact influencer outreach process, including how he estimates return before paying an influencer for a mention.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable reaching out to and negotiating directly with content creators for paid placements, since that's the actual distribution engine here, not organic search or social traffic.

The Truth (as we see it)

Worth flagging clearly: the creator promotes and sells the trend-research tool he's demonstrating, including an upsell for a fully built Shopify store, so he has a direct financial incentive to make the process look simple and repeatable. The revenue figures are self-reported without account screenshots. That said, the underlying mechanics, using AI to speed up content creation and paying smaller niche influencers rather than only chasing million-subscriber creators, are concrete and specific enough to be genuinely useful, independent of whether his particular paid tool is worth buying.
