Coloring Books With GenSpark

AI-generated coloring books published via Amazon KDP using GenSpark AI

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost (a GenSpark AI subscription; no inventory since KDP handles printing)

Originally published Sep 4, 2026 — Kimberly Mitchell

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Kimberly Mitchell · The $1.7M Coloring Book Trend (How to Start From Home in 2026)| Genspark AI ↗

What It Is

Use an AI workspace tool (GenSpark AI) to research a profitable, low-competition coloring book niche using live Amazon sales data, generate a complete 50-page coloring book interior and full wraparound cover, then publish it through Amazon KDP as a print-on-demand paperback with no inventory, warehouse, or shipping required.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a real example: a self-published coloring book brand did about $283,000 in UK Amazon sales in 2024, then was signed by Penguin Random House in a six-figure deal, with those titles reportedly becoming worth $1.7 million the following year. She walks through the full process live: an AI deep-research prompt to find a profitable niche (landing on "bold and easy" flower/botanical coloring books based on real keyword and bestseller data), generating the full interior and cover with AI, formatting for KDP's requirements, and publishing within about 72 hours — plus generating a full brand kit and a week's worth of Pinterest and Instagram marketing content from the same tool.

Best For

Someone willing to research a specific niche using data rather than guessing, and comfortable disclosing AI-generated content to Amazon (KDP requires this) as part of the publishing process.

The Truth

The mechanic itself is real and low-cost to test — the presenter builds a complete, publishable product in the video using free/low-cost tools. The headline $1.7 million example is one breakout success story, not a typical outcome, and coloring books are a genuinely crowded category on KDP, so standing out will depend more on niche selection and marketing (the Pinterest/Instagram content generation shown) than on the AI generation step itself, which many competitors now also use.
