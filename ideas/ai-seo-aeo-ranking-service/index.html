AI Search Optimization (AEO/GEO)

Selling AI-search-engine optimization (AEO/GEO) alongside traditional SEO, helping brands get recommended by ChatGPT and Claude, not just rank on Google

Marketing & content · Very low (a Claude subscription; Upwork or direct outreach to find clients)

What It Is

Offer businesses either traditional SEO (helping them rank on Google/Bing) using AI to mass-produce optimized content, or the newer discipline of AEO/GEO, optimizing a business's content and site so AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude recommend them when a user asks a related question; find prospects by targeting businesses built on platforms like Webflow (which is actively pushing AEO messaging to its users) that already do some SEO but haven't started on AI-platform visibility, and pitch the AI-ranking opportunity specifically as the next wave they're missing.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a client, previously with zero SEO/AI experience, who landed her first AI-optimization client on Upwork at $3,000/month and increased that client's SEO traffic by roughly 1,000%; he scores this 23/25 (ease 4, speed 4-5, validated 5, competition 4-5, long-term 5), calling it one of the easiest, most beginner-friendly ideas on his list given how new and low-competition the AEO side specifically still is.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to learn what content actually ranks on AI platforms (a skill the presenter says can be picked up in about two weeks) and comfortable with early-stage, still-developing service pricing.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI answer-engine optimization (AEO/GEO) as a growing, real service category, and platforms like Webflow actively promoting it to their user base, are accurate, current, and verifiable trends. The cited client case study and its specific numbers are self-reported and not independently audited, and the video promotes a free companion Claude skill and the presenter's paid training.
