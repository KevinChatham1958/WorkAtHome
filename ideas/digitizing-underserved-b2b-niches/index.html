What It Is

Building simple, modern AI-powered software for old, non-digitized B2B service industries, such as property inspectors and surveyors, that are still relying on outdated, expensive legacy software, replacing manual paperwork with an AI voice-to-report tool.

The Pitch

The host describes researching an inspection-industry vendor charging thousands of euros a year for outdated, difficult-to-use software in an industry doing tens of millions in revenue, and argues that in a day or two, a modern AI tool, a voice agent to capture notes and photos transcribed directly into the required report format, could replace that entire workflow. The broader strategy: look for niche B2B sectors that have never really adopted modern software and still generate significant revenue, since those businesses tend to be underserved both by better tools and by any real marketing.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to research a genuinely obscure, unglamorous B2B niche in depth before building anything, rather than someone looking for a well-known, already-crowded market.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a spontaneous idea shared live on a podcast, not something the presenter has personally built or sold yet. No specific revenue example or working product is shown, so treat it as a promising direction to research rather than a proven business.
