What It Is

Building small, single-purpose free web tools, a calculator, converter, or evaluator solving one specific problem, with Claude, using the free tool as a lead magnet that funnels users toward a related paid product, affiliate offer, or service.

The Pitch

The presenter lays out a repeatable four-part framework: find a mass need people already search for, not just a cool idea, build a free tool that solves that need in under 60 seconds with no login required, deliberately leave a gap the free version doesn't fully close as the upsell, then generate traffic via a newsworthy angle, a real number, a rip-off-exposed story, a contrarian result, that press outlets and directories will pick up organically. Walks through several of his own real tools, a domain-appraisal tool and a car-price dealer-fee calculator, as working examples of the pattern.

Best For

Someone comfortable identifying a real underlying business or affiliate offer to attach a free tool to, since the tool itself isn't the business, the angle and the paid offer behind it are.

The Truth

The presenter opens with an unusually direct disclaimer that most people watching videos like this make nothing, and explicitly warns that just-clone-an-app advice found elsewhere can create real legal exposure, a notably candid framing compared to many similar videos. No personal revenue figures are given for this exact framework; the presenter's own examples are shown as demonstrations of the method, not accompanied by account-level income screenshots.
