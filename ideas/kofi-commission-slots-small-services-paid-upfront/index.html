Small Services Paid Upfront

Small paid services sold through a Ko-fi page (paid upfront, limited order slots)

Local & professional services · Near-zero cost (free page; you bring your own audience)

What It Is

You set up a page on a creator-payments platform (named as "Coffee" in the video; it matches how Ko-fi works) and list a small service such as proofreading, resume help, short bios, captions or English conversation practice. You describe the offer, set a price and choose how many orders you'll take at a time. Buyers pay when they order, and the payment goes straight to your PayPal or Stripe account; then you deliver the work.

The Pitch

The creator likes that you get paid before you do the work and can cap how many orders you accept. He says that if you already use social media, it's a simple way to take orders and collect payment.

Who It's Best For

People with a small, specific skill and an existing social following they can send to their page.

The Truth (as we see it)

Taking payment upfront for small one-off services is sensible and low-risk, and the setup is quick. The creator admits the catch: the platform brings you little or no traffic, so you have to find buyers yourself, which is usually the hardest part. Expect modest amounts per order unless you have an audience that already trusts you. The creator says links to everything mentioned are in the description; some may be referral links, so weigh the platform choice on its own merits.
