3D PVC Patches and Keychains

Custom 3D PVC logo patches and keychains made with a dispensing machine

Physical & print products · Moderate startup cost (machine about $3,500-$4,600, plus a mold for each new design)

What It Is

An automatic multicolor PVC dispensing machine uses several nozzles to fill a mold with colored liquid PVC, building raised logos, patches, keychains and similar custom pieces without hand-filling each section. You sell directly to brands, schools, gyms and event companies that want merchandise with their logo, or do white-label production for print shops and promotional-product sellers who bring you orders. The creator's example is 300 pieces at $3 each, a $900 order before materials, molds, finishing, packaging and rejects.

The Pitch

The creator says it turns an ordinary product into a custom order businesses already buy, and that once a design is set one operator can let the machine repeat work that would be painfully slow by hand. He points to repeat customers as the key, since they reuse an existing mold.

Who It's Best For

People with a small home workshop who are interested in custom merchandise and willing to build relationships with schools, gyms, clubs or local print shops.

The Truth (as we see it)

Custom PVC merchandise is an established product category, and the white-label route to print shops is a sensible way to find volume. The creator is open that the hidden cost is the mold, since each new shape may need one, so one-off designs eat margin. Order values given are revenue only, the machine price is from supplier listings, and nothing in the video shows real sales. You will need ventilated workspace for PVC materials and curing, room for stock, and you will be packing and shipping finished goods.
