What It Is

You become the specialized expert businesses pay to eliminate regulatory risk, things like product compliance certification, HIPAA compliance, OSHA workplace safety, or R&D tax credit paperwork. Done well, this work can save a company millions in fines or lawsuits; done poorly, it can cost them just as much.

The Pitch

Heacock ranks this as his top category because it hits every trait that supports premium pricing at once: the work is recurring, it's genuinely painful for the business to do themselves, it's document-heavy, which plays well with AI-assisted tools, and, most importantly, the customer's name and reputation is on the line if it's done wrong. That combination means businesses will pay well above what the raw hours would suggest.

Who It's Best For

Someone with real experience who is willing to specialize deeply in one regulatory area rather than staying a generalist.

The Truth (as we see it)

In the video, Heacock uses a real anecdote to ground the pitch, he mentions paying $33,600 for this kind of service himself, which shows he's speaking from firsthand experience as a buyer. But when he ranks this category as the single best remote business opportunity, that ranking is his own opinion, not an audited industry statistic.
