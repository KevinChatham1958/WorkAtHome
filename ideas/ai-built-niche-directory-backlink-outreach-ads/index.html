Niche Directory Website

AI-built niche directory website (one keyword, display ads, $19 data product, paid link swaps)

AI & automation · Very low startup cost (an AI coding subscription and a domain; later $50-$100 per backlink)

What It Is

You build a simple website that lists one specific kind of place or thing people search for, such as a particular type of thrift store, hotels with in-room jacuzzis, or scratch-and-dent appliance outlets. The guest, Frey, finds ideas by searching Google for a keyword plus "directory" and looking for an existing site that gets traffic but has low SEO authority (domain rating under about 20-30 in Ahrefs), then has Claude Code scrape the data and build the site on free tools (Vercel, GitHub, Supabase, Astro). To rank, he emails owners of sites that already link to competitors and offers $50-$100 to add a link to his directory, using a five-sentence template. Money comes from display ads, featured listings, selling leads to businesses, affiliate links, or a small paid data product (he sells a $19 product built from calling about 100 stores).

The Pitch

Frey says his first directory, a thrift-store list, peaked around 90,000 monthly visitors, made $2,300-$3,000 a month at its best, and has earned about $150,000 over four years while needing roughly 30 minutes every few months. He credits a single $50 link swap with getting it onto Google's first page. He argues directories focused on one narrow problem still get early organic traffic, including from AI overviews and ChatGPT, and can be sold later (his best offer was about $50,000).

Who It's Best For

People comfortable learning SEO basics and using AI coding tools who want a slow-building, mostly hands-off website asset and can wait months for traffic.

The Truth (as we see it)

The method is concrete and low-cost, and the guest is candid that about 30% of his directories fail and that lead-gen directories only pay if you are willing to sell leads to local businesses. All traffic and income figures are his own and unaudited, and display-ad income swings with niche and visitor country. Google algorithm changes and AI search answers are a real risk to this kind of site, and paying for links is against Google's guidelines, which can carry a penalty risk he does not discuss. The host promotes a paid $19 directory guide and his own paid AI community in the episode.
