Selling AI-generated Clip Art PDF Packs on Etsy, Using Claude Only for Listing Titles, Tags, and Descriptions

What It Is

Use a trend-research tool to find in-demand clip art niches (e.g. seasonal themes like Halloween cats), generate matching images with an AI image generator, bundle 20+ variations into a PDF sold for $3-5 with commercial usage rights to crafters, teachers, and parents, then use Claude to write SEO-optimized Etsy titles, tags, and descriptions so listings rank organically without paid ads.

The Pitch

The presenter shows several Etsy clip-art shops making an estimated $6,400 to $271,000 in lifetime sales tracked via a third-party revenue-estimate tool, all selling simple $3-5 image packs, and frames the business as unusually low-friction because buyers just need to find the art appealing. He walks through the full stack live: trend research, image generation, and Claude-written listing copy, all without a paid course gate.

Best For

Someone with no design skill who's comfortable using AI image-generation and trend-research tools repetitively to build out a large catalog of niche-specific image packs over time.

The Truth

Selling AI clip art on Etsy is a real, low-cost-to-test niche, and Claude genuinely helps with SEO-style listing copy. The specific shop revenue figures come from a third-party estimation tool, not Etsy's own sales data, and because the barrier to entry is this low, the niche is easy for others to replicate once a trending style is spotted, so consistent output volume matters more than any single listing.
