Mining Competitor Product Reviews for Unmet Customer Complaints to Build and Sell a PDF Digital Product

Digital & subscription products · Very low (an AI chat subscription, a digital product delivery platform, no ad spend required)

Passive Biz Lucca · I Stole Iman Gadzhi's Method To Sell PDFs ($34,626/mo) ↗

What It Is

Instead of asking an AI to invent PDF ideas, research existing paid products in a niche and read their customer reviews specifically for complaints and unmet requests, use those real complaints as the product spec, build the PDF with AI section-by-section around solving exactly those gaps, package it as an outcome/'system' rather than a bare PDF, then find already-successful content in the same niche and create similar content around the same specific problem that funnels viewers to the product, adding a small upsell at checkout to increase average order value.

The Pitch

The presenter reports $34,626 in the last 30 days from this exact workflow, crediting Iman Gadzhi's 'find demand first' digital-product framework, and walks through the full math from views to clicks to conversions compounding toward the five-figure number across many pieces of content.

Best For

Someone comfortable digging through reviews/complaints for market research and consistently producing niche-specific short-form content that funnels to a product.

The Truth

Mining reviews for unmet customer needs is a genuinely sound, underused market-research technique that goes beyond generic AI brainstorming, and building content around an already-proven topic is solid organic-marketing logic. The specific $34,626 figure is self-reported, and the stated math is illustrative rather than guaranteed, requiring genuinely consistent content output to reach that scale.
