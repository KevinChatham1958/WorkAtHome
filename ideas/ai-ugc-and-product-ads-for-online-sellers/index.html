AI UGC and Product Ads

AI-generated UGC videos, product photos and 3D motion ads for online sellers

What It Is

You use AI video and image tools with Claude skills to make UGC-style testimonial videos with AI actors, polished product photos and short 3D motion ads for marketplace and online sellers. You land clients by offering three free product photos, then sell motion ads at a starter price (about $150) and packages of UGC clips, and turn repeat buyers into a monthly content retainer.

The Pitch

The hosts say brands pay creators $200 or more per 15-second UGC clip and wait a week, while AI versions can be made in hours and sold for $50-100 each, so a five-clip package from five clients a month is about $1,250. They argue content wears out quickly, so sellers need fresh batches every month.

Who It's Best For

Someone with a good eye for ads who is willing to send many cold emails to online sellers.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI UGC is a real and growing service, but quality varies and some platforms and buyers dislike undisclosed AI actors presenting as real customers, so be transparent with clients about what the videos are. The prices and the 'sometimes I can't tell it's AI' claims come from the tool's own marketing, and cold email has legal rules that differ by country, as the hosts note. The video is published by Higgsfield, the AI video tool used throughout, with Iman Gadzhi, who is an investor and advisor in the company and sells business courses, so it doubles as product marketing.
