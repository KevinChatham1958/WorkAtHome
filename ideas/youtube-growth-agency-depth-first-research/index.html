YouTube Growth Agency Using a Data-driven "depth-first Search" Topic Research System

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (an AI tool for research, plus time; scales with hired editors/VAs as client load grows)

Originally published Sep 8, 2026 — Jake Trinder

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jake Trinder · The Most Valuable YouTube Training You'll Ever Watch ↗

What It Is

Run a YouTube channel-growth agency for businesses and personal brands by systematically researching what topics already have proven demand on YouTube (using a manual "depth-first search" process across search terms, outlier videos, and channel recommendations, or delegating that research to a VA or AI), then packaging validated topics with proven title/thumbnail formats, a promise-proof-plan hook structure, and value-first calls-to-action (a useful free resource/tool tied to the topic) instead of a hard sales pitch.

The Pitch

The presenter runs over 100 YouTube channels for clients (citing examples like a doctor's channel going from ~1,000 to 100,000+ daily views in under a month after switching from guessed topics to validated-demand topics) and argues the core service is: validate that a specific market already searches for a topic before filming, reverse-engineer why a proven title/thumbnail format works for a given audience rather than copying it blindly, and design every video's call-to-action as a useful next step (a free tool, template, or resource) that naturally feeds a sales funnel rather than a generic pitch.

Best For

Someone comfortable with content strategy and data research (or willing to delegate that research to a VA/AI) who wants to offer YouTube channel growth as a service to businesses, rather than growing only their own channel.

The Truth

The core methodology — validating topic demand before producing content, and treating a channel as one cohesive audience rather than a grab-bag of videos — is sound, evidence-based content strategy that applies whether you're running this as an agency or growing your own business's channel. The presenter's cited client results (Gary Vee, Instantly AI, various six-figure claims) are his own reported case studies, not independently verified, and building this into a full agency requires real content-production capacity (editors, research staff) to service multiple clients at once.
