What It Is

A packaged $300-plus offer sold to small businesses and coaches consisting of an AI-built landing page using a no-code tool like Lovable, an audit of the client's current site or marketing, and a Loom walkthrough video explaining what was built and why.

The Pitch

The presenter's core framework is one offer, one buyer, one problem, arguing most beginners fail by juggling several disjointed offers instead of nailing one clearly defined package. The $300 bundle is taught as a starter offer that can be raised to $500 or more after a few sales. Client acquisition is warm-outreach based: posting content, direct messages to people who engaged with a post, texts to existing contacts asking for referrals, and free audits used as a foot in the door before pitching the paid package.

Best For

Someone comfortable using a no-code AI site builder like Lovable and willing to do consultative, warm-relationship outreach rather than cold email or paid ads.

The Truth

The presenter's own backstory, self-described as formerly homeless and now averaging $5,000 a day, is stated on camera but not independently verifiable. The video repeatedly funnels toward the presenter's paid programs, so the free framework should be read as a lead-in to those upsells. That said, the underlying offer-design and warm-outreach framework taught is coherent and matches standard consultative-sales practice independent of the upsells being pitched.
