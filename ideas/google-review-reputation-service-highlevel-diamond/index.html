What It Is

You find local businesses with few Google reviews, contact them by phone or email, and sell them a monthly service that automatically asks their customers for reviews. You run it with HighLevel software, which also gives clients booking, messaging and payment tools. Everything is done remotely.

The Pitch

A creator shown in the video says you can make $10,000 a month with 34 clients paying $297 a month, and that clients get so many features the price feels small.

Who It’s Best For

Confident communicators who don't mind cold calling and can support clients over time.

The Truth (as we see it)

Getting 34 paying clients takes a lot of calls and rejections, and HighLevel costs about $97 to $297 a month before you earn anything. Many agencies already sell this. Diamond uses an affiliate link for HighLevel in the video.
