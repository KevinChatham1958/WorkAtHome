Free Local Directory Icebreaker

Building a free local business directory as a warm-lead icebreaker, instead of cold-selling marketing services directly

  • Services from home
  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

From Ron Schrader

Startup cost: Low (a directory-building tool or website; postcard printing/mailing if used for follow-up promotion) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Ron Schrader · STOP Cold Selling to Local Businesses! Do This Instead... ↗

What It Is

Instead of cold-selling a marketing service (postcard ad spots, web design, local marketing) directly to local businesses, build a free, zip-code-searchable local business directory and offer businesses a free listing, framed as a goodwill resource rather than a sales pitch. As part of registering, businesses opt in to receive occasional email (capped monthly, not mass spam); once enough businesses have registered, send a short email about a paid opportunity (like open postcard-campaign ad spots) as a natural follow-up from a platform they already trust, converting what would have been many cold conversations into inbound replies from opted-in businesses.

The Pitch

The presenter is candid he personally dislikes traditional selling and built this specifically to solve his own aversion to cold outreach, framing the directory as an 'icebreaker' that opens a relationship before ever asking for money, and suggests promoting the directory itself via postcards, local Facebook ads, or word of mouth.

Who It’s Best For

Someone offering local marketing/advertising services who wants a warmer, permission-based way to open conversations with local businesses rather than cold calling or emailing.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core strategic insight, that a free, ungated resource creates a warmer, opt-in relationship before any sales ask, is sound, well-established lead-generation logic. The specific directory software demonstrated is only available inside the presenter's own paid membership, though the underlying concept is independently buildable with other directory tools. No revenue or results figures are given for this method.
