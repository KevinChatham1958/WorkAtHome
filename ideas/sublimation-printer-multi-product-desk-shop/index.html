Sublimation Print Shop

Sublimation printer and heat press producing shirts, phone cases, signs and ornaments

What It Is

Print full-color designs on transfer paper with a sublimation printer, then use heat to bond the dye into polyester and coated blanks such as shirts, mugs, phone cases, keychains, mouse pads, metal signs and ornaments. The video says material cost lands around $1-$4 per item. Sell finished products for $15-$40 and change the product line as trends shift.

The Pitch

The video says the printer's versatility is the point: with a heat press it covers dozens of product types with photo-quality prints that don't crack or peel. It calls it a print-on-demand business that fits on a desk.

Who It’s Best For

People who want to make many kinds of custom products from one home setup.

The Truth (as we see it)

The video is a narrated list with typical costs and prices but no named seller or sales records, so treat the figures as rough estimates. Sublimation only works well on polyester or specially coated items and light colors, which limits products. You also need a heat press, and a fast-changing product line still has to be listed, photographed and marketed.
