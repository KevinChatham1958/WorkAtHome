AI SEO Consulting Sourced Through Upwork

Marketing & content · Low startup cost

Originally published Jun 6, 2026 — Sandy Lee AI

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Sandy Lee AI — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lDHrmDAD9y4

What It Is

Offering AI-assisted SEO consulting to clients found through freelance platforms like Upwork, using AI tools to speed up audits, research, and content work businesses pay for.

The Pitch

Sandy Lee, a former sales and marketing manager with no technical AI background, says AI SEO consulting sourced through Upwork brought in real freelance income on top of her existing full-time job, without needing a coding or development background.

Best For

Someone with existing sales or marketing experience who's comfortable pitching on freelance platforms and using AI tools to speed up SEO audits and content work.

The Truth

Important to separate the numbers here. The video's headline claims $48,000 in a single month, but Lee's own breakdown only attributes $5,500 of that to AI SEO consulting, the real, freelance, replicable piece. Another $10,000 is simply her existing full-time job salary, not new business income at all. The remaining roughly $38,000 attributed to content and AI is muddied by her own admission moments later that she didn't really monetize her YouTube channel. The AI SEO consulting freelance work is the credible, transferable part of this story; the headline total is not.
