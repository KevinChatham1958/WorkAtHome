Speak, Shape, Ship Digital Products

The 'speak it, shape it, ship it' AI workflow for creating ebooks, workshops, memberships, or courses from your existing knowledge

What It Is

Use a repeatable three-step workflow to turn existing knowledge into a sellable digital product without writing or design skills: first 'speak it' by talking through everything you know about a problem into a voice-to-text tool, then 'shape it' by having Claude organize that raw brain-dump into a structured outline, then 'ship it' by having Claude design the actual deliverable; apply this same workflow across four product formats, an ebook, a one-time-recorded paid workshop replay, a recurring membership, or a full course, choosing the format based on how much monthly income you need and how many sales/members that requires.

The Pitch

The presenter says she's sold digital products full-time since 2014 and now earns around $3,865/week using this exact method, walking through specific 'rent math' for each product type, and stresses that traffic (she uses YouTube, with a Claude project trained on her own brand voice) is the fifth, often-skipped ingredient that makes any of these products actually sell.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with existing knowledge or experience in a specific problem area, comfortable talking through what they know out loud rather than writing from a blank page, willing to also build a consistent traffic/content channel.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core workflow (voice-dump raw knowledge, then use AI to structure and design the deliverable) is a genuinely useful, low-friction way to overcome the writing/design bottleneck, and the format-selection math is sound, practical planning. Her own $3,865/week figure is self-reported, and the video is sponsored in part by a store-building tool she promotes as an alternative, so that portion is an ad rather than neutral advice.
