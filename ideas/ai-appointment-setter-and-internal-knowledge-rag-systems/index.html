What It Is

Sell two specific AI systems to home service businesses (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical). System 1: an AI appointment-setter combined with lead reactivation — a voice AI agent calls new leads within seconds, qualifies them, and books a qualified appointment, while the same agent works through a business's existing backlog of old, cold leads to re-engage them; run paid ads on the front end if the client needs more lead flow. Price at $8,000-15,000 setup plus $2,000-4,000/month retainer. System 2: an internal knowledge/RAG system — an AI trained on a business's SOPs, pricing, warranty terms, and FAQs that staff can query like a custom ChatGPT; price at $6,000-15,000. Combine both for one client to become their entire growth and operations department. Find clients via free cold calling in one home-service niche, layering in paid ads once you have cash flow, using an 'identify, quantify, prioritize' sales framework on calls.

The Pitch

The presenter, whose own AI agency makes $40,000-50,000/month and has coached students to $0-30,000/month, cites a specific case study: installing the appointment-setter/reactivation system into a roofing company generated $159,000 in additional revenue in 3 months, cutting response time from ~6 hours to under 2 minutes, backed by a cited Harvard statistic on response-time affecting close rate.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to specialize in one home-service niche and handle sales personally while delegating technical building to a hired developer.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI voice-agent appointment setting and RAG-based knowledge systems are both real, current capabilities, and the cited response-time statistics are drawn from real, independently referenced research. The roofing case study includes concrete before/after figures though self-reported and not independently audited; the presenter's own and students' revenue figures are also self-reported.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
