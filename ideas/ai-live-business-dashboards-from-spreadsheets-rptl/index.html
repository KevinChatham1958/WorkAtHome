Live Business Dashboards

AI-built live business dashboards from a client's spreadsheets (setup fee plus monthly)

AI & automation · Near-zero cost (Claude subscription; free Netlify hosting)

Originally published Sep 7, 2026 — Micah Korus

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Micah Korus · Claude + Google Sheets = $13,000/Month ↗

What It Is

You take a small business's messy spreadsheet, such as an auto repair shop's records of money owed, expenses and inventory, and use Claude to turn it into a clean dashboard showing revenue, expenses, profit, margin and unpaid balances. Linking it to the client's Google Sheet lets it refresh as data is added, and you host it on Netlify so the owner can open it like an app on a phone. To sell it, you build a sample before-and-after demo, call businesses, send a short video walkthrough, and then pitch a price on a meeting. The creator suggests something like $1,500 setup plus $200 a month for hosting, maintenance and extra data connections.

The Pitch

The creator says agencies charge about $2,000 for this kind of dashboard and AI makes it in minutes, so margins are high. He sees it mainly as a foot in the door: once you know the owner, you can sell AI receptionists, review automation and other services, which he says lifts the business toward $11,000-$13,000 a month.

Best For

People comfortable with cold calling and explaining tech simply to small business owners, who want a starter offer for an AI services business.

The Truth

The build steps are realistic, but the income figures are a hypothetical example, and the creator (who says he is 16) shows no client results in the video. A dashboard connected to a sheet shared with 'anyone with the link' raises privacy concerns for financial data, so a login or proper access control is important. Selling requires cold outreach and sales calls. The video promotes a free Discord community and the creator's own paid training on selling to local businesses.
