What It Is

You build faceless TikTok accounts, post short videos, earn from TikTok's creator payout program, and add TikTok Shop affiliate links on products you feature.

The Pitch

London says he made over $100,000 from five side hustles before turning 19, and around $7,000 from TikTok Shop and the creator program in a couple of months, which he ranks as a stepping stone.

Who It’s Best For

Young or trend-savvy creators who can post often.

The Truth (as we see it)

He ranks this the lowest of his five hustles. TikTok's programs and rules change often and differ by country. London also runs a marketing company, Evolvant, which he mentions in his videos.
