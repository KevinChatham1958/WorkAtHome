Printable Party Game Kits

What It Is

You create complete printable game kits for a theme or event (bridal showers, kids' birthdays, game nights, office team building) and sell them on Etsy. One ChatGPT prompt drafts 20 trivia questions at three levels, an 8-player scoreboard, a 16-square bingo card, host instructions with timing tips and five icebreakers; you then lay it out in Canva (or with Creative Fabrica templates). Single kits sell for $8-15 and themed bundles such as a Halloween game collection for $25 or more. Listings show everything included and a short Canva video showing how easy it is to edit.

The Pitch

The creator shows a simple bridal shower game pack she says made over $26,000 and a scavenger hunt kit with multiple sales in 24 hours. She argues people planning events every weekend want ready-made, stress-free fun and will pay $10-20 for it.

Who It’s Best For

Creative people who enjoy games and events and are comfortable designing in Canva.

The Truth (as we see it)

Party printables are a proven, steady Etsy category, but a very crowded one, so design quality and specific themes matter. AI-written trivia must be fact-checked. The revenue figures are spy-tool style estimates for other shops. The creator offers the prompts free in the video description and does not pitch a paid product in this video.
