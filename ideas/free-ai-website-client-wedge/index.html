What It Is

Build a genuinely good website for a local business using AI tools like Claude, give it away completely for free with no strings attached, and use the relationship that builds to become that business's go-to "AI guy", then upsell paid recurring services like SEO, chatbots, or lead-capture dashboards.

The Pitch

Roberts argues websites are the easiest way to get a first paying client with AI right now, because AI tools can now produce genuinely polished, conversion-focused sites (he cites having personally sold websites for over $10,000) in well under an hour. He walks through the specific process: research what the best-converting websites in a niche have in common using AI, build a free website using that structure for a business with a weak or outdated site, then open the conversation with what else they need, more leads, a better sign-up rate, which naturally leads into paid, recurring work.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who's willing to give away real value upfront before asking for anything, and who's comfortable using their existing personal network for a first client. The video cites an internal survey of over 3,000 people in the presenter's community where 80% said their first client came from personal connections, not cold outreach.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific tactic here is giving the entire first deliverable away for free as a relationship-building wedge rather than charging for it from the start. The $10,000-website and 80%-personal-network figures are the presenter's own self-reported numbers from his own community, not independently audited, and the interview functions partly as a promotion for the Hermes agent tool and the presenter's paid community, worth factoring in given how enthusiastically both are described.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
