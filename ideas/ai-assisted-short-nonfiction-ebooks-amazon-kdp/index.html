Short Nonfiction Ebooks on KDP

AI-assisted short nonfiction ebooks published on Amazon KDP

  • Digital products
  • Near-zero cost

Source video

Sean Dollwet · The SIMPLEST Way to Make Money Online with AI in 2026 ↗

What It Is

You write short nonfiction ebooks (around 10,000 words) that solve one specific reader problem, using ChatGPT to brainstorm topics, outline and draft, then edit the text yourself. You make a cover in Canva, upload the book to Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing under your real name or a pen name, and earn royalties when Amazon customers buy it.

The Pitch

The presenter frames it as a three-step loop: find a topic people already buy on Amazon, write a simpler and shorter version that solves one problem, and list it where book buyers already shop. He validates topics by checking Amazon's Best Sellers Rank, using under about 80,000 BSR as a rough sign a book earns around $500 a month. Amazon supplies the traffic, delivery, and customer service, so there is no website, inventory, or ads needed, and approval takes 24 to 72 hours. He says he has made over $2.2 million with KDP, sold one book account for $820,000, and cites students making $40 on day one and $161,000 in a first year; he suggests scaling with more books, paperback/audiobook/hardcover formats, outsourcing, and ads.

Who It’s Best For

People who can write and edit clearly, are comfortable staying anonymous, and want a low-cost product business they can build slowly book by book.

The Truth (as we see it)

KDP is a real, accessible platform and the topic-validation method using BSR is a standard, reasonable approach, though his BSR-to-income figures are rough guesses he himself says not to quote. Income claims, the $2.2 million total, the account sale, and the student results are his own unaudited figures, and he correctly notes a first book may make few or no sales. Short AI-drafted books face heavy competition and Amazon content-quality scrutiny, so real editing and a genuinely useful angle matter more than speed. The video promotes an affiliate link to the paid tool Bookbeam and ends with a pitch for his paid one-on-one mentorship program via an application and strategy call.
