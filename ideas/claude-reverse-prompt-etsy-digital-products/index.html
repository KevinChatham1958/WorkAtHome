Recreated Etsy Best-Sellers With Claude

Reverse-engineering bestselling Etsy digital products (resumes, budget trackers, print-on-demand text art) with Claude

What It Is

Find a top-selling Etsy digital product (a resume template shop, a budget/sinking-funds tracker, or pattern-pressed text art for shirts/prints), screenshot it into Claude to generate a build prompt via 'reverse prompting,' then feed that prompt into Claude Design to build a reusable generator tool (a resume builder, an interactive budget-tracker app, or a bulk pattern-text design tool) that outputs finished, sellable files (PDF/Word, HTML/Canva site, or PNG) in minutes, which you then list for sale on Etsy or turn into print-on-demand products via Printify/TikTok Shop.

The Pitch

The presenter shows three example shops making $7K-$63K/month from these product types and argues the bottleneck was never design skill — it's finding what's already selling and letting Claude generate a polished, on-brand version fast. He demonstrates the exact workflow live for each product type and uses a paid tool (ListingView, his own product) to identify high-revenue listings and auto-generate optimized titles/tags/descriptions.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable using Claude Design/Claude Code and willing to do repeated iteration (colors, spacing, mockups) to get each generated product polished enough to sell, plus ongoing listing/tag optimization work to stay visible on Etsy.

The Truth (as we see it)

The reverse-prompting technique (screenshot a bestseller, have Claude write a build prompt, then generate the product) is a genuinely repeatable method, and Claude Design's ability to output ready-to-sell files quickly is real. The specific revenue figures for referenced shops come from a paid research tool's estimates, not verified sales data, and this is a crowded, fast-to-copy niche — long-term success depends on volume and consistency rather than any one product.
