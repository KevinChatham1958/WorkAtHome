WhatsApp Lead Follow-Up Automation

WhatsApp lead follow-up automation agency (video sequences for lead-based businesses)

AI & automation · Low startup cost (CRM software subscription)

ItsKeaton · He Makes £35K/mo Selling WhatsApp Automations With GoHighLevel ↗

What It Is

You build automated WhatsApp follow-up sequences for businesses that already generate leads, usually inside a CRM such as GoHighLevel. The guest, Chris, sends each new lead a short video within seconds of a form fill, then video reminders before sales calls, webinars and onboarding, with buttons to book, reschedule, ask a question or opt out. One example was a window cleaner whose quoting and sign-up were fully automated through WhatsApp; the guest says that client added about £90,000 in annual recurring revenue. You sell it as a way to raise lead-to-sale conversion and show-up rates rather than as more leads, and he says his packages run from small trainings up to about £30,000 a year per client.

The Pitch

The creator and guest argue that most businesses waste the leads they already pay for, so improving follow-up can double sales without extra ad spend. The guest says WhatsApp is far more interactive than SMS or email and that video builds trust before the first call; he reports a 98.57% sales-call show-up rate over 18 months and a webinar show-up rate rising from 43% to 68% for one client.

Who It's Best For

Someone with marketing or sales experience who is comfortable selling to business owners and building automations, especially in countries where WhatsApp is the main messaging app.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core idea, better automated follow-up on existing leads, is sound and easy to explain to business owners, and the guest's examples are specific. All revenue and show-up figures are his own and unaudited, and his results rest on about 20 years of sales and marketing experience plus in-person trainings and keynote talks, which a beginner won't have. The podcast promotes the guest's paid 90-day WhatsApp intensive and the host's own $5 HighLevel course, and links to a script bundle. WhatsApp's business messaging rules (approved templates, 24-hour reply windows) add technical work, and clients have to be willing to record videos or pay for actors or AI clones.
