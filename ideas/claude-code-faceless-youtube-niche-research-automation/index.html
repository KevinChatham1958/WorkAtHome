What It Is

Use an AI coding assistant (Claude Code, or a similar tool) to autonomously research and browse for the most profitable faceless YouTube niches for 2026, asking it to output a ranked list (with estimated pay per 1,000 views, competition level, and affiliate potential) plus 10 video ideas per niche, and a list of inspiration channels to study, all compiled into a downloadable file; verify a channel's monetization status with a free Chrome extension before modeling videos after it; for individual scripts, feed the AI a proven high-performing video's title and transcript and ask it to write an original script that follows the same successful structure and reasoning without copying the content, explicitly avoiding YouTube's crackdown on templated, mass-produced AI content.

The Pitch

The presenter cites several other creators' real reported numbers as proof of the opportunity ($30,000/month from YouTube Shorts, $25,000/month from ranking-style Shorts, $21,000 in 28 days from an AI sleep/history channel) and says he's personally made hundreds of thousands in AdSense, walking through the entire live research-to-script workflow using Claude Code, and specifically flagging that YouTube deleted 16 channels with 35 million combined subscribers in a 2026 policy update targeting mass-produced AI content, distinguishing that from the structured, non-templated approach he demonstrates.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable directing an AI coding tool through a multi-step research and writing process rather than expecting a single one-shot script.
