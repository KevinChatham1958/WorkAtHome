What It Is

You build one complete template pack for a specific kind of business where each customer is worth real money, such as roofers, med spas, real estate agents or fitness coaches. A pack might include follow-up and sales scripts, lead-qualification questions, email and SMS templates, referral requests, objection handling and reactivation campaigns. Claude drafts the first version from a detailed prompt, and you improve it with your own knowledge and what the market needs, then sell it as a download.

The Pitch

The creator says a focused pack solving a valuable problem sells better than a generic template bundle, and a digital product can be sold repeatedly with no inventory or shipping.

Best For

People who know an industry's sales process well enough to check and improve AI-written scripts.

The Truth

Tanner Chidester sells coaching for business owners through links in the description, and the $26,000-a-day figure is a hypothetical funnel calculation, not results from this product. He admits that more experienced business owners are unlikely to buy templates and that older or local owners are the likelier buyers. Without real-world testing of the scripts the pack has little advantage over what a buyer could prompt themselves, and the video gives no guidance on where to sell it beyond general marketing.
