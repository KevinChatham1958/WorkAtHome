What It Is

Take over every part of a founder's YouTube channel except the actual filming, using Claude for video ideas and scripts and AI tools for editing and thumbnails; target founders who already run a profitable business, have tried YouTube themselves, and failed to gain traction, pitching that you'll run the whole operation while they just show up on camera.

The Pitch

The presenter scores this 18/25 (ease 2-3, speed 3, validated 5, competition 3-4, long-term 5), citing pricing examples of $1,500-2,000/month for scripting and ideation alone (roughly $300-500 per script for four scripts monthly), with editing and a profit-percentage arrangement layered on top for a full-service package.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with real scripting, editing, and content-strategy skill willing to essentially run a media operation on a client's behalf.

The Truth (as we see it)

Founders outsourcing YouTube channel operations to specialized agencies is a real, active market, and the described AI-assisted workflow (scripting, editing, thumbnails) is technically accurate to current tools. Pricing figures are the presenter's own cited ranges, not independently verified, and the video promotes his free training and paid coaching.
