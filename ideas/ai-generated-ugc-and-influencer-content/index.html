What It Is

Instead of hiring real people, you use AI video tools to create virtual influencers and user-style product videos for brands. You produce the content and sell it to brands as a service. Brands pay per video or on a package.

The Pitch

Dang presents this as a bonus option for people who do not want to manage human creators. He says AI-generated UGC has become good enough that he sometimes cannot tell it is AI while scrolling Instagram, and that some brands already use it. He recommends Higgsfield for producing the characters and videos. He gives no specific pricing or score for this one.

Best For

Fits people comfortable with AI video tools who prefer working alone rather than coordinating creators.

The Truth

This is the thinnest idea in the video, with no pricing, demo of client results, or outreach plan. Many brands prefer real people for trust, and AI-generated testimonials can raise disclosure and platform-policy issues, especially if presented as real customers. Tool costs and fast-changing capabilities also mean low barriers for competitors. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
