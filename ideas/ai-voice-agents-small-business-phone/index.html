What It Is

You build AI voice agents that answer a small business's phone, handle bookings, answer common questions and route complex calls to a person. You design the conversation flow, test it, connect it to the client's calendar and CRM, and charge a setup fee plus a monthly retainer to plumbers, dentists, med spas and similar businesses.

The Pitch

The presenter says phone calls are where local businesses lose the most money, citing a figure that about 73% of small businesses miss a large share of incoming calls. He quotes setup fees from about $500 to a few thousand dollars and retainers of $300 to $1,500 per client per month, and says 10 to 15 clients could put an agency over $10,000 a month in recurring revenue. He suggests platforms like Vapi, Retell or Bland, focusing on one industry, closing a few clients at a discount for case studies, then charging full price. He calls it the newest of his five ideas with competition still small.

Best For

Technically inclined people who can learn call-flow design and integrations and can respond quickly when a client's system breaks.

The Truth

AI voice agents are a real and growing service and the named platforms exist, but the presenter himself says it has the highest technical bar of his list. The 73% statistic and the fee and revenue figures are not sourced or verified, and the claim of little competition is doubtful given how many people are now selling AI receptionist services. Per-minute usage costs from voice platforms cut into margins and are not discussed, and a broken agent loses a client real calls, so reliability and responsiveness matter. Nothing is sold for this idea specifically, though the video promotes his paid KDP mentorship.
