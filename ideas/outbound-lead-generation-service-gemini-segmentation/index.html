Outbound Lead Generation Service Using AI-assisted Market Segmentation

What It Is

Sell local or niche businesses on finding them new customers by mapping their total addressable market (not just the easy, already-visible slice), segmenting it by geography, demographics, or behavior, and using an AI tool to build a prospect list, generate targeted outreach scripts, and draft personalized emails.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a survey where 85% of small business owners rely on word-of-mouth referrals with no active way to find new customers, framing this as the opportunity. He describes doing this for an event venue client, mapping over 100,000 qualified prospects and segmenting them by location and hiring signals, then using a Gemini-built prospecting tool to find companies, contacts, and personalized outreach angles.

Best For

Someone comfortable with B2B sales and cold outreach who wants to package market research and prospecting into a recurring, retainer-based service for local or niche businesses.

The Truth

The market segmentation approach described (total addressable market vs. the easy-to-reach slice) is a legitimate and often-overlooked sales fundamental, and packaging it as a service is a real business model that predates AI tools. The presenter's own scoring rates this as only moderately beginner-friendly and moderately fast to first income, since it requires some sales skill and faces real competition from other lead-gen providers.
