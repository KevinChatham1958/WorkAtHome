What It Is

Write and self-publish a nonfiction book on a topic you know from experience, such as gardening, faith, retirement, parenting adult children or your career field. The creator's steps: outline 12-15 chapters, use Claude to help turn your knowledge into 25,000-40,000 words, hire a cover designer on Fiverr for around $50 or make your own, and upload to Amazon KDP. He then recommends starting a YouTube channel on the same topic to send buyers to the book, which he says also makes Amazon promote it more.

The Pitch

The creator says people over 50 have real lessons to share and that you no longer need to be a writer because AI can outline, edit and polish your thoughts. He compares a book to a fruit tree that keeps selling for years after one round of work.

Who It’s Best For

People with deep knowledge of a subject who are willing to put real effort into editing a book and promoting it.

The Truth (as we see it)

Self-publishing on KDP is accessible and cheap, and a book built on genuine experience stands out from generic AI-written titles. Most self-published books sell few copies, so the YouTube promotion he describes is doing much of the work, and that takes months of consistent posting. Amazon asks authors to disclose AI-generated content, and heavily AI-written text still needs careful human editing. The KDP success story he cites, Sean Dollwet, is a client of his coaching business. Shane Hummus uses the video to promote a free live training and his paid YouTube and personal-branding coaching program.
