AI Content Agency for Local Businesses

AI-powered done-for-you content agency for local businesses

What It Is

Put up a simple one-page website offering done-for-you marketing content to one type of local business, such as realtors, law firms, insurance agents, or home services like HVAC, pest control, and roofing. The work is mostly writing: blog posts, local SEO service pages, YouTube scripts, social media posts, lead magnets, and marketing emails. You produce it with ChatGPT or Claude, following written step-by-step procedures, then edit, humanize, and deliver it. You find clients through AI-assisted cold outreach, a pitch deck, and a short discovery call, and bill them a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Enfroy says this is the fastest way to make money from a blog-style business, because it needs zero traffic. Clients come from outreach, so you're not waiting 6 to 12 months for articles to rank. He suggests charging about $1,000 to $3,000 a month (with $1,500 to $2,000 as a target) to local businesses doing roughly $500,000 or more a year. He pitches it to them as replacing a social media manager, content manager, and SEO specialist for a fraction of the cost. He says the system matters more than experience, 'kind of like the franchise model,' and that you can hire a content manager for every five new clients and grow to $10,000 to $30,000 a month. He adds that you can change what you offer as marketing changes, and later use the same skills to build your own blog for affiliate and ad income.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with cold outreach and sales calls who wants income in weeks rather than months, and who is willing to learn one content process well enough to deliver it reliably for paying clients.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling writing and SEO retainers to local businesses is a real, established freelance model, and Enfroy says he started out this way (three SEO and link-building clients at about $3,000 each, mentioned in another of his videos). The hard part is underplayed. Local owners get a lot of cold pitches, and selling AI-written content to people who can make it themselves with ChatGPT gets harder as AI tools spread. His claim that someone with no experience can deliver because 'it's about the system' depends heavily on how good your editing is. The revenue figures and the student who 'made money within a week' are unverified, and he says the week result is not typical. The video promotes his free 'AI launch kit' and his paid program Launch 10K, which sells the procedures, outreach scripts, and 'franchise docs' described here.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
