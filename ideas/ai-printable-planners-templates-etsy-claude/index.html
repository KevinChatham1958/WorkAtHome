AI Printable Planners and Templates

AI-built printable planners, templates and charts sold on Etsy

What It Is

You ask regular Claude chat to write a detailed prompt for a product, such as a budget planner for people living paycheck to paycheck, a resume template, or a kids' reward chart, then paste it into Claude's design tool, which builds the full PDF. You refine sections by commenting on them or editing elements directly. ChatGPT then creates the product mockup images for the Etsy listing. The creator also suggests building an audience on YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or Pinterest and selling through Payhip or Gumroad.

The Pitch

The creator, who says he sells digital products himself, likes them because you make them once and sell them repeatedly with no inventory, shipping or clients. He says many Etsy sellers in these categories have thousands of reviews, rates the effort as low and the potential as high, and says passive income starts to feel real at 10 to 20 products.

Who It’s Best For

People who want a make-once product and are willing to research niches and keep adding listings.

The Truth (as we see it)

Printable planners and templates are a proven Etsy category, and the AI workflow shown really does speed up production. That same speed means the category is saturated with similar products, so ranking on Etsy takes good niche selection, listing photos and keywords rather than just volume. The creator shows no sales figures of his own for these items, and Etsy requires sellers to disclose when AI was used in creating a design.
