Laser Engraving Business

Running a CO2 laser engraving business split between consumer gifts and recurring B2B signage orders

What It Is

Buy a compact CO2 laser engraver (cuts/engraves wood, acrylic, leather, paper, rubber) and sell into two markets: consumer gifts (name signs, wedding decor, engraved boxes, cake toppers, home decor) and, more importantly, B2B signage (QR code displays, opening-hour signs, desk plates, door signs, branded menu pieces, awards) — the B2B route matters because a business may order several pieces at once and return later for replacements or new designs, rather than a single one-off gift purchase.

The Pitch

The presenter specifically frames the B2B signage route as the stronger of the two markets, and recommends starting with one buyer group and a small sample collection rather than making 50 different products up front, using those samples to sell the next order.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a dedicated ventilated workspace (a garage or workshop) willing to build repeat relationships with local businesses for signage.

The Truth (as we see it)

CO2 laser engravers and the cited price ranges are real and current, and ventilation being a genuine safety requirement (not optional) is accurate. No personal revenue figures are given.
