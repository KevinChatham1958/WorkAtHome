Value-First Newsletter Monetization

A value-first approach to monetizing a newsletter

Digital & subscription products · Low cost, email platform only

Originally published Aug 4, 2026 — Mike Swigunski

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Mike Swigunski · 3 Ways to Build a $1M Email Newsletter Business ↗

What It Is

A framework for monetizing an email newsletter: treat it primarily as a way to nurture and add real value to an audience rather than a constant sales channel, focus curation on the top few most relevant items rather than trying to cover everything, and only introduce paid offers once readers are already getting consistent value for free.

The Pitch

Swigunski says he's built email lists with hundreds of thousands of subscribers that have generated hundreds of millions of dollars in revenue, and frames the newsletter's core job as warming up an audience so that when a paid offer does appear, people already trust the sender enough to buy.

Who It's Best For

Anyone already running, or considering, an email newsletter as part of a business, a methodology to apply, not a standalone idea by itself.

The Truth (as we see it)

The "hundreds of millions in revenue" figure is a broad, self-reported career claim without account-level detail to verify it, so treat it as a credibility signal rather than an audited number. The underlying approach, curate tightly, lead with value, sell only once trust is established, is a solid, well-established newsletter strategy independent of that headline figure, and results still depend heavily on actually growing a real, engaged subscriber base.
