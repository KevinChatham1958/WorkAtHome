Selling a QR-code Loyalty Program Built Natively Inside GoHighLevel to Niche-specific Local Businesses

What It Is

Build a customer loyalty program entirely inside GoHighLevel using its native automation: customers scan a QR code, submit a short form, and earn a 'visit' point via a workflow math-calculation action; after a set number of visits they redeem a reward and get asked for a Google review, and inactive customers automatically get a follow-up text/coupon after 30 days of no visits, replacing a physical punch card with something that lives on a phone and can't be lost. Sell it to a specific, narrow niche (the presenter's own first vertical was nutrition/supplement shops) rather than pitching 'a loyalty program' generically, since owning a specific niche lets you charge more and be seen as a specialist; price starting around $97/month and moving toward $297/month or $1,500/year upfront as trust and testimonials build, bundled with a simple templated website as a foot-in-the-door offer.

The Pitch

The presenter reports currently doing $20,000/month from this exact SaaS offer, and shares the origin story: he built a free homepage for a business as an opener, and the owner told him he didn't want a website, he wanted to replace physical punch cards, which became his first $97/month client and the source of his first referrals; his interviewer overlays a reusable 'validation loop' framework (pick 3 candidate niches, find out what the customer actually wishes they could buy, build the minimum viable version, land 3 paying clients, then scale).

Best For

Someone comfortable learning GoHighLevel's workflow/automation builder and willing to specialize in one narrow local-business niche rather than a generic pitch.

The Truth

GoHighLevel-native loyalty/rewards programs replacing physical punch cards are a real, currently-sellable local-business retention product, and the specific mechanics described (QR code, form, points via workflow math actions, automated win-back texts) are accurate to how the platform actually works. The presenter's $20,000/month figure is self-reported, and the video is a promotional interview steering viewers toward his own paid community for the loyalty-program template and deeper coaching.
