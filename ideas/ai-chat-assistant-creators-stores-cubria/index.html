AI Chat Assistant Setup

Installing AI chat assistants that answer, qualify and book leads for creators and online stores

What It Is

Creators selling programs through Instagram, and online stores with lots of visitors, often still answer messages by hand, slowly. You set up an AI chat assistant that replies to every message, qualifies leads and books sales calls automatically, or greets store visitors and helps them buy. Human staff then only handle serious buyers. Cubria's pitch: for creators, set it up free and take a commission instead of the commission they'd pay human setters; for stores, run it free for a week, and if conversions rise, they pay.

The Pitch

Cubria says he uses AI systems on his own Instagram and for clients and currently pays for this exact service. He estimates a few thousand dollars a month is possible and scores it 19 out of 25, noting it pays quickly now but is not a service you'll sell forever.

Who It’s Best For

Technically comfortable people who are patient with testing and tweaking a bot until its replies sound natural.

The Truth (as we see it)

Demand is real, but so is competition, as Cubria admits. Getting a bot to sound natural and avoid wrong answers takes a lot of back-and-forth, and free trials or commission-only pay put the risk on you. A badly tuned bot can lose sales or misstate prices and policies, so build in a way to hand off to a human. Cubria says he has sold over $9 million in digital products and services, a figure the video doesn't verify, and the video steers viewers to his other videos and to an Instagram message to get his own software, so treat it partly as a funnel. The 1-to-5 scores are his personal opinion, as he says himself.
