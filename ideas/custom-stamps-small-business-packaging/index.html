Custom Packaging Stamps

Custom stamp making for small business branded packaging

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (about $350-650 for a compact exposure unit, printer, and stamp-making materials)

What It Is

Make custom rubber/photopolymer stamps (logos, return addresses, wedding details, loyalty cards) primarily for small businesses that want branded packaging without ordering thousands of pre-printed bags or boxes, using a compact exposure unit and washout process rather than mass-production equipment.

The Pitch

A realistic testing setup (exposure unit, printer for artwork negatives, photopolymer material, washout tools, backing, handles) runs $350-650. The presenter warns against competing with generic decorative stamps against cheap mass-produced alternatives — the real value is accurate custom work for businesses (a bakery stamping bags, a handmade seller marking boxes) that need small, precise branding touches.

Best For

Someone with patience for a precise, technical production process (correct artwork reversal, clean exposure/washout) who wants to serve small business customers rather than compete in the general decorative stamp market.

The Truth

The presenter's warning against generic decorative stamps is a genuinely useful positioning insight — this works best as a B2B custom service, not another crowded consumer stamp shop. The equipment cost is modest, but building a base of repeat small-business customers takes direct outreach, which isn't addressed in the video.
