Personal Brand and Mentorship

Personal-brand content channel selling a high-ticket mentorship program

  • YouTube & blogging
  • Very low startup cost

Video published Aug 21, 2026 by Maddox Goforth

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Maddox Goforth · I Challenge You to Sell Digital Products for 3 Months ↗

What It Is

Building a personal-brand YouTube and short-form content channel around a skill or hobby, then selling a high-ticket mentorship program that bundles a pre-recorded course with live coaching calls to the audience that content attracts.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the first 90 days as unglamorous audience-building, posting one short-form video daily plus one long-form video weekly, that most people quit before finishing. He walks through his own path from a teenage lawn-mowing business, to documenting it for views, to selling digital products, landing eventually on high-ticket mentorship as the highest-earning format. The core advice is to pick something you're already better than most people at, not necessarily expert-level.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a specific skill or hobby they're already better-than-average at, willing to post daily short-form and weekly long-form content for months before seeing real sales, since the presenter is explicit the first 90 days are mostly audience-building.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter uses strong guarantee language more than once, a phrasing worth reading skeptically since no legitimate business model can guarantee outcomes. He also discloses, mid-video, an earlier business selling access to reseller vendor-contact lists that he later shut down because it didn't align with his brand, without fully explaining what the problem was, worth knowing as a credibility data point. The presenter started this content journey at 13 years old, and much of the track record described is self-reported without any account verification shown on screen.
