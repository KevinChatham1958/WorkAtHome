AI Agency Bundle for One Niche

High-ticket recurring AI agency bundle for one local-service niche, instead of one-off AI websites

AI & automation · Low startup cost (fulfillment outsourced to a developer team for a revenue share)

Angelo Turri · Stop Selling "AI Websites", Sell THIS Instead (With PROOF) ↗

What It Is

Running a specialized AI agency that sells a bundle of five recurring AI systems to a small number of high-ticket local service businesses in one committed industry niche, rather than selling cheap one-off AI websites to many small clients.

The Pitch

The presenter argues the AI-website business model breaks down because it's low-ticket, non-recurring, and rapidly commoditized by free tools, and instead recommends committing to a single high-value local-service niche such as roofing, HVAC, solar, or med spas for a minimum of 3-6 months. He sells a bundle of five recurring AI systems addressing specific revenue leaks, including a missed-call voice receptionist, a speed-to-lead follow-up agent, dead-lead reactivation, an internal knowledge assistant, and an AI quoting agent, quantifying each business's dollar losses during the sales call using a framework he calls identify, quantify, prioritize, selling one system at a time starting with the costliest leak, then expanding into the rest with the same client over time.

Best For

Someone more interested in sales and client relationship management than hands-on technical building, since the presenter explicitly delegates all development work, and comfortable running a structured, numbers-driven sales conversation.

The Truth

The presenter's own numbers, including per-system pricing of $8,000 to $15,000 upfront plus $2,000 to $5,000 a month, and having helped clients scale to $10,000 to $30,000 a month, are self-reported without account or client verification shown on screen. The core critique of the low-ticket AI-website model, crowded, one-time payment, no recurring relationship, is a reasonable, internally consistent business argument independent of whether his specific numbers are exact. The video ends promoting the presenter's own paid one-on-one mentorship program.
