Digistore24 Short-Video Funnel

Digistore24 high-commission affiliate funnel fed by daily short videos

Marketing & content · Low startup cost (a capture page and an autoresponder email tool)

Originally published Sep 29, 2026 — Aaron Chen

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Aaron Chen · How I'd Make My First $10,000 With Digistore24 in 45 Days (2026 Blueprint) ↗

What It Is

You pick one niche on Digistore24, a marketplace of digital programs, and choose a digital product with a high earning per sale (his example is a $1,000 program paying 50%, roughly $426 per sale). Instead of posting the affiliate link directly, you build a capture page to collect names and emails, then an autoresponder sends daily emails that teach and solve the audience's problems before linking to the product. To get visitors you post two to five short videos a day on Facebook, TikTok and Instagram, drop what does not get views, and repeat what does.

The Pitch

The creator shows dashboards with about $163,000 and $84,500 in commissions from two programs and argues that higher-priced digital products mean you only need 20 to 40 sales a month. He says free organic content builds trust because people see and learn from you before they buy.

Who It's Best For

People willing to create content every day in one niche and learn basic email marketing. It suits those who can keep going for several months, not just the 45 days in the title.

The Truth (as we see it)

The funnel-plus-content approach is a standard affiliate method, but the commission screenshots are the creator's own and cannot be independently checked, and he admits the student who hit $10,000 in 30 days is not typical. The video ends by sending viewers to his free masterclass, which is a common route into a paid program. Many high-commission Digistore24 offers are make-money or health products with aggressive claims, so check the product's quality and refund rate before promoting it.
