What It Is

Work for blog owners as someone who produces articles with AI writing tools instead of writing every word by hand. The job is to take a keyword and brief, generate a draft with tools like ChatGPT or a dedicated AI article tool, then edit it, fix grammar, optimize it for SEO, and make it read as helpful, human content before it's published. You're paid for finished, publishable articles, not for word count.

The Pitch

Enfroy calls this a new job created by AI and says he planned to add one to his own team alongside his human writer, handling 30 to 50 articles a month. He says paying writers by the word no longer makes sense when AI produces the words in seconds, so blog owners now need someone who has mastered the tools and can finish posts in under an hour. His demo shows why the human still matters: raw ChatGPT output scored about 90% AI on a detection tool, and it took rephrasing, grammar cleanup, and SEO editing to improve it. He argues the human editor is what keeps sites from publishing low-quality AI content that Google won't reward.

Who It’s Best For

A writer or editor comfortable with AI tools who can judge quality, edit fast, and understands basic SEO, and who wants steady per-article work from blog and content-site owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

Editing AI drafts for content sites is a real freelance niche, but this video only describes it as a role Enfroy planned to hire for. It gives no pay rates and no advice on finding clients. It was made in January 2023, right after ChatGPT launched, so the specific tools, AI-detection scores, and his worry about passing AI detectors are badly dated, and pay for AI-assisted writing has been under pressure since then. The video promotes his free blogging masterclass and includes an affiliate link for the Content at Scale AI writing tool. It also recommends several paid tools (Surfer SEO, Jasper, Originality.ai).
