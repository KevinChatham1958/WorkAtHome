AI Influencer Personas

Creating AI-generated faceless 'influencer' personas with Claude and an AI image/video tool, then monetizing their content

  • YouTube & blogging
  • Very low

From Mark Tilbury

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Mark Tilbury · I Tried The LAZIEST Way to Make Money With AI ↗

What It Is

Research existing successful AI influencer accounts across profitable niches (health, wealth, relationships) to see how they monetize, and pick an underserved niche if possible. Use Claude to generate a full character profile (name, backstory, look, unique selling point) for each persona, then connect Claude to an AI image/video generation tool via a connector so both work together in one chat: generate consistent character photos across multiple angles and scenes using detailed prompts Claude writes for you, then use a pre-built Claude Skill to generate viral content ideas and scripts without manually writing each one. Post the content and monetize via a digital product or other methods like brand deals and affiliate promotion.

The Pitch

The presenter, skeptical going in, gave himself 7 days and a $99 budget with the constraint of not using his own face or voice, and reports a genuinely modest result: $276 in gross revenue from 23 workbook sales, netting $157 in profit after real costs, missing his own self-set $1,000 target and having to pay a viewer $1,000 as a result.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable directing AI conversationally to build a consistent character and content pipeline, aware that a single niche/character on a tight budget may produce modest results without further scaling across platforms.

The Truth (as we see it)

Claude's connector integration with AI image/video tools and Claude Skills for repeatable content generation are real, current, technically demonstrated capabilities, and the presenter's transparent, below-target result is far more credible than an inflated success claim would be. He explicitly raises the ethical concern around AI influencers that impersonate real people without disclosure, distinguishing that from his own disclosed approach. The video includes a promotional discount code tied to the AI tool used.
