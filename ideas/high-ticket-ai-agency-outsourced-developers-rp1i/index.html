High-Ticket AI Systems Agency

High-ticket AI systems agency for one niche, with builds outsourced to developers

AI & automation · Low to moderate startup cost (developers paid a share of each deal; optional ads for lead generation)

Originally published Oct 5, 2026 — Angelo Turri

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Angelo Turri · How To Get Into The Top 1% Of AI Agency Owners ↗

What It Is

You sell AI systems such as phone answering and automated booking to one type of business (examples: roofers, dentists, med spas) and stick with that niche for at least three months. You price on the outcome rather than the technology; the creator says he charges $8,000-20,000 per system. You spend your own time on sales, relationships and closing, and hand every build to a developer who is paid a percentage of each deal. Leads come from outreach, ad campaigns, a setting team and referrals.

The Pitch

The creator says he scaled his agency to a consistent $40,000 a month without being technical, by delegating builds and focusing on selling. He argues that business owners pay for results like 'no more missed patient calls', so selling outcomes lets you charge far more than people who compete on tool features.

Who It's Best For

Confident salespeople who enjoy talking to business owners and are prepared for regular sales calls.

The Truth (as we see it)

Most of the video is mindset advice (accountability, focus, confidence) rather than a how-to, so viewers will need to learn the actual build and sales process elsewhere. The $40,000 a month figure is unverified, and the creator closes by selling one-on-one coaching with access to his developer network. High-ticket pricing depends on being able to prove results, and relying on outside developers means your reputation rests on their work. AI agencies are a crowded field among online creators, which he himself acknowledges.
