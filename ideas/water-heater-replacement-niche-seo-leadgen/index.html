Water Heater Lead Website

Building a hyper-niche website targeting only 'water heater replacement' searches, then referring the leads to a plumber for a cut

What It Is

Build a website focused exclusively on water heater replacement/repair (not general plumbing), targeting specific high-volume, low-competition keywords ('water heater replacement,' 'water heater replacement near me,' 'water heater repair'), rank it for a specific city using dedicated content pages, then either fulfill the service yourself or route the resulting leads to a plumber/contractor for a referral cut, replicable city by city.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a real plumber who flipped his entire business to focus only on water heater replacement after noticing it was his most profitable, under-targeted service, now making $700,000/year and ranking #1 in his town; keyword research shows 'water heater replacement' gets 112,000 monthly searches nationally at a 0/100 difficulty score, which the presenter says is the easiest single home-service keyword he's ever found.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to learn basic SEO/keyword research and either perform the service themselves or build referral relationships with local plumbers.

The Truth (as we see it)

The cited search-volume and keyword-difficulty figures come from a named third-party SEO tool (Ahrefs) and are independently checkable, and the underlying niche-SEO-plus-referral-or-fulfillment model is a real, established local-service business approach. The $700,000/year case study is one specific example the presenter found, not independently verified, and he's explicit that results aren't guaranteed.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
