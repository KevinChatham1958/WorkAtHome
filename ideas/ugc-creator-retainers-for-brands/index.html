UGC Creator on Retainer

User-generated content (UGC) creator on monthly brand retainers

Content & personal brand · Near-zero cost (a phone; a simple media kit)

What It Is

Brands pay ordinary people to film casual, personal-style videos about a product or app, which the brand posts on its own accounts or runs as ads. The guest describes typical retainers of about $3,000-4,000 for 30 short videos a month, often found through UGC agencies, freelance marketplaces, brand outreach, or creator platforms where brands accept applicants. His path is to prove you can get views first (for example through TikTok Shop), then send brands and agencies a media kit of your best-performing videos.

The Pitch

The guest says each video can take as little as 10 minutes, that brands want regular-looking people rather than polished influencers, and that a UGC retainer gave him steady base income when his TikTok Shop accounts were suspended. He describes stacking retainers so they cover the bills while commissions are extra.

Best For

Someone at ease talking to a phone camera who can take direction from brands and keep up a steady output of short videos.

The Truth

Retainer amounts are the guest's own examples and vary widely; landing the first clients usually takes outreach and a portfolio, which the video covers only briefly. Some tactics described, such as planted comments and hiding that a video is paid, can conflict with platform rules and advertising-disclosure laws (in the US, the FTC requires disclosure of paid endorsements), so treat those with caution. The guest sells coaching and a script-writing app that the host promotes.
