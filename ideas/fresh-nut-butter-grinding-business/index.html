Fresh Nut Butter for Cafes

Fresh nut butter grinding business for cafes and specialty food accounts

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (very small grinders around $100; heavier stainless or colloid-mill machines $250-600; mid-range commercial units $550-1,200)

What It Is

Producing fresh-ground nut butters — almond, cashew, flavored, no-added-sugar, or a custom formulation for another food business — rather than competing with mass-market peanut butter on price, supplying cafes and food businesses that want recurring bulk tubs.

The Pitch

The grinder itself is cheap and can often process product faster than a producer can fill jars by hand, meaning filling and packaging — not grinding — is the real bottleneck. A cafe wanting a custom nut butter in 5lb tubs delivered weekly is a recurring B2B supply account, which the presenter frames as a stronger business than one-off retail jar sales. Nuts, jars, roasting, cooling, allergen control, and shelf-life testing are the real cost drivers, with almonds and cashews punishing a bad formulation faster than peanuts.

Best For

Someone targeting a food-service or cafe account that wants recurring bulk tubs, rather than trying to underprice supermarket peanut butter on retail shelves.

The Truth

Margins can be thinner than they look — a jar that costs $4.50 to produce when customers will only pay $5.50 isn't fixed by a faster grinder, so the underlying economics have to work before the machine matters.
