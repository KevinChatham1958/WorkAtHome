Hosted AI Agent Subscription

Productized subscription access to a hosted AI agent, built and marketed in public

  • AI & automation
  • Low startup cost

Video published Mar 20, 2026 by Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast (guest: Robbie)

Source video

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast (guest: Robbie) · He Asked AI To Make Money. It Did. ↗

What It Is

Give an AI agent (built on the open-source OpenClaw tool) a small budget and document its attempts to build its own business on TikTok, then when the audience explicitly asks for their own version, pivot into selling monthly access to a hosted, sandboxed instance of that same AI agent as a productized subscription.

The Pitch

Robbie's agent ("Ron") failed at its first idea, freelance SWOT analyses posted on Fiverr with no existing audience to find it, but publicly documenting the failures and pivots on TikTok generated the real business: over 200 comments asking how to get their own version. He validated demand with a $10 refundable pre-order before building anything (600 signups, converting at roughly 40-45% to a $29-a-month recurring membership), reaching about $8,374 in monthly recurring revenue after 13 days, entirely through organic TikTok with no paid ads or cold outreach.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable being visibly early and imperfect in public, documenting failed attempts alongside wins, and willing to either manage technical infrastructure themselves or work with someone who can safely host other people's AI agent instances.

The Truth (as we see it)

Koerner is candid that this is a very new business model built on infrastructure that's only about 100 days old at the time of the interview, so the durability of demand and the security implications of hosting many people's AI agents at scale are both genuinely untested long-term. The specific revenue figures ($8,374 MRR, roughly 40-45% pre-order conversion) are self-reported from just 13 days of operation, a real early number but a very short track record.
