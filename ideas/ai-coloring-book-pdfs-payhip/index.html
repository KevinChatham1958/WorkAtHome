AI Animal Coloring Books on Payhip

AI-generated animal coloring book PDFs sold via Payhip

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost

Originally published May 18, 2026 — Frank Garrett

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Frank Garrett · How I Made $4,297 in 24 Hours Selling AI Coloring Book PDFs Using Claude AI ↗

What It Is

Creating simple AI-generated animal coloring book PDFs, using Claude for the book plan and prompts, an AI image tool for the illustrations, and Canva for layout, sold as an instant digital download via Payhip.

The Pitch

The presenter shows a real earnings screenshot and walks through a four-step system: have Claude generate a full book plan, cover concept plus black-and-white and colored prompts for every page, in one prompt, generate matching images in a free AI image tool using a top-selling Etsy listing's cover as visual inspiration rather than a copy, assemble the pages in Canva with white space left for coloring, then publish as a digital product on Payhip, which handles delivery and payment automatically. Also demonstrates making a short slideshow-style preview video alternating black-and-white and colored versions of each page to boost buyer confidence.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable treating this as an ongoing catalog business, multiple books, multiple niches, rather than a one-off project, since consistency across many books is what compounds results.

The Truth (as we see it)

The single-day earnings figure is shown via a self-reported dashboard screenshot, not independently verified, and no broader monthly or annual context is given for how typical that day was. The presenter is candid that AI-generated content draws criticism from some viewers and addresses it directly rather than hiding from it. A paid mentorship is promoted at the end.
