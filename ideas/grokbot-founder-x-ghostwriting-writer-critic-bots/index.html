AI-Assisted Founder Ghostwriting

Ghostwriting X posts for founders with two AI agents (a writer and an "anti-slop" critic), approved by the founder before posting

What It Is

You run a founder's X (Twitter) account, for example someone running a software company. One bot searches X for posts in their niche that are getting attention, analyses why, and drafts several hook options and posts in the founder's style using live data. A second bot acts as a strict editor, rejecting robotic phrasing, emoji overload and generic lines and forcing rewrites. You read every draft, the founder picks the ones they like for the scheduler, and weekly analytics guide the next batch. The creator charges about $1,000 a month per account plus $300 per extra platform such as LinkedIn.

The Pitch

The creator cites account owners using these bots who report tens of millions of impressions, and says founders know they should post but lack time. He suggests proving the system on your own X account first, and says five founders bring in $7,500 a month.

Who It’s Best For

A good writer who understands social media and can match another person's voice, using AI for research and first drafts.

The Truth (as we see it)

The two-bot draft-and-critique setup with human approval is a sensible way to reduce bland AI writing, and the creator honestly warns that the voice may not land and algorithms are unpredictable. The impression numbers are other people's claims and unverified. The value you sell is your taste and editing; a founder unhappy with one embarrassing post can end the arrangement quickly. Note that X's rules on automated and AI-generated activity can change. The creator closes by promoting his own seven-day "land your first AI client" challenge.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
