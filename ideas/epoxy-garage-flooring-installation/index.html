Epoxy Garage Floors

Local & professional services · Low to moderate startup cost (epoxy materials and application tools; practice runs recommended before charging clients)

Originally published Aug 7, 2026 — David Explains Money

What It Is

Apply epoxy resin coatings to garage concrete floors (and later patios, basements, and small commercial spaces) for homeowners converting garages into functional living spaces like home gyms, workshops, or game rooms, mastering one specific installation technique well and building a portfolio of before-and-after photos, since the business sells primarily through visual references and word of mouth.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the appeal as strong margins (materials cost relatively little compared to the per-square-foot installed price) combined with fast job completion (one to two days per job), and specifically recommends practicing on your own garage or a family member's before charging real clients, since the application technique has a short learning curve but any mistakes remain visibly permanent on the floor.

Best For

Someone willing to practice the technique thoroughly before taking paying clients, and comfortable building a portfolio-driven, word-of-mouth marketing approach rather than relying on advertising.

The Truth

The advice to practice on a low-stakes floor first is genuinely important given the presenter's own warning that mistakes are permanent and immediately visible, which could otherwise damage a new business's local reputation quickly. The margin and speed advantages described are real, but building a reputation strong enough to expand beyond garages into commercial spaces will take time and a demonstrated track record.
