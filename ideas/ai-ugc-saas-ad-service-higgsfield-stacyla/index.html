What It Is

Use Higgsfield Marketing Studio to generate UGC-style product videos and SaaS app advertisements from just a few product photos or a product URL, no physical product or human model required, then sell these as a content service to physical-product brands or SaaS companies, packaging output into a monthly retainer (a set number of videos per month) rather than selling one-off videos.

The Pitch

The presenter builds two full sample videos live in about 10 minutes each (a product UGC ad and a SaaS app ad) using only a product photo or website URL, an AI avatar, and a prompt template from Higgsfield's built-in library, then lays out a 5-step marketing plan: pick one specific niche, build a portfolio of 5-10 example videos for real brands in that niche, build a daily-updated lead list (20-30 businesses/day), do consistent outreach (10-20 businesses/day via email, DM, or calls), and bundle output into a monthly retainer (e.g., $5,000/month for a set number of videos, versus $200 per one-off video).

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable using AI video generation tools and willing to commit to one specific niche and consistent daily outreach volume, since the presenter is explicit that customer acquisition, not video quality, is the actual bottleneck.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific 5-step marketing plan (niche down, build proof before pitching, build a lead list, outreach at real volume, then bundle into a retainer) is more concrete and actionable than most AI-content-service pitches, and the presenter is upfront that success requires genuine outreach volume, not just good videos. No independently verified revenue figures are given for the presenter's own version of this exact service — the emphasis is on the workflow and tool, not a specific proven income result.
