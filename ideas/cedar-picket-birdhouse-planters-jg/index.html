Cedar Picket Birdhouse Planters

Building outdoor planters from cheap cedar fence pickets, including a birdhouse-backed planter box and a heart wall planter

What It Is

You build planters from cedar fence pickets, one of the cheapest woods at the hardware store. The main design is a box planter with notched 2x2 legs and a back made of three pickets cut to 16, 17 and 20 inches with pointed tops, scored lines for texture, small roofs and drilled holes so they look like birdhouses behind the plants. The second is a 21-inch wall-hanging planter with a heart cut out of the top third and a raised slotted bottom with drain holes, which can sit on the ground or hang like a wall sconce. The creator uses waterproof Titebond III glue and suggests galvanized or stainless nails for outdoor use.

The Pitch

The creator says it is 'picket season' and these are planter designs he hasn't seen anyone else building, which matters because most simple designs are already everywhere. Fence pickets are cheap, so materials cost little, and the video title frames them as a way to make money.

Who It’s Best For

A woodworker with basic power tools who wants low-cost spring and summer products for garden lovers, local markets or online.

The Truth (as we see it)

The video gives no prices, listings or sales numbers, so the money-making claim is untested here, and 'nobody else is building this' is the creator's impression. Pickets are rough and often warped, and outdoor pieces need exterior glue and rust-proof fasteners to hold up. He couldn't make his original mountain-backed idea work and changed plans mid-build. Planters are bulky to ship, so local sales may suit them better. Demand is seasonal.
