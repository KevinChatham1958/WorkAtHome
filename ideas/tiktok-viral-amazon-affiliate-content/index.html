TikTok Amazon Affiliate Videos

Content & personal brand · Low direct cash cost but high production-time cost (buying featured products, significant filming/editing time)

Originally published Aug 24, 2026 — Adam Enfroy

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Adam Enfroy · The NEW Way to Do Affiliate Marketing with AI (Free 10 Hour Course) ↗

One of 7 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Create highly produced, entertaining short-form videos unboxing or demonstrating trending Amazon products, directing viewers to a link-in-bio page or TikTok Shop listing the products with Amazon affiliate links.

The Pitch

Presented as evidence that affiliate marketing extends beyond Google/YouTube, citing a creator with over a million TikTok followers built around viral Amazon product content, offered for people who prefer short-form video over long-form writing.

Best For

People who already enjoy high-energy short-form video production, can front the cost of repeatedly buying trending products, and can sustain a high, unpredictable output volume.

The Truth

The presenter is explicit that this format 'requires a lot of work' (a single video can take a full day to produce) and depends on repeatedly going viral, which is less predictable and durable than the search-driven models the source spends most of its time promoting. He frames this almost as a cautionary contrast rather than a core recommendation — the least-endorsed idea in the source material.
