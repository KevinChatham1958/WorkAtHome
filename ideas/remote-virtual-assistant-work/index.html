Virtual Assistant

Working as a remote virtual assistant handling email, scheduling, and admin tasks

Local & professional services · Free (an Upwork/Fiverr account, or applying directly to VA agencies)

What It Is

Handle email management, scheduling, research, and admin tasks remotely for business owners, applying through dedicated VA agencies (Belay, Unbabel, which vet clients for you) or general freelance platforms (Upwork, Fiverr).

The Pitch

Real pay runs $20-50/hour, sometimes higher for specialized calendar/inbox management, and many roles are ongoing part-time or full-time positions rather than one-off gigs, with zero startup cost.

Who It's Best For

Someone organized with basic admin/scheduling skills; no degree required, though management experience helps.

The Truth (as we see it)

Virtual assistant work through named platforms is a real, established remote job category. Pay figures are general platform data, not a single verified case study.
