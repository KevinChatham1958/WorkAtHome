Free Preview Website Pitch

Free AI-built preview websites for local businesses, offered in Facebook groups, then sold as hosting or a monthly marketing package

  • Services from home
  • $100–$500 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Video published Nov 17, 2026 by Balint Adorjan

Startup cost: Low startup cost (about $100 a month for the platform's agency partner program)

Source video

Balint Adorjan · How $2 Lead Magnet gets me $1500/month Clients ↗

What It Is

You post in Facebook groups for a trade (plumbers, contractors and so on) offering to build two businesses a free website for feedback. For everyone who replies, a white-label tool builds a site in about two minutes from the business's own Google profile, photos, reviews and social links; the creator says each costs about $2 in credits. You wait three or four days, send the link with a note that it expires in seven days, and use the reply to get the owner on a call. On the call you pitch a local marketing package at $500 to $1,500 a month, or fall back to hosting the site for $49 to $69 a month on a platform that charges you $10.

The Pitch

The creator says a free, finished website of the owner's own business is far more persuasive than a cold pitch, and that the expiring link creates urgency. He says there is almost no fulfillment work because the site updates itself from the Google profile, and that this low-maintenance setup let him travel Europe for 15 months while selling.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with sales calls and follow-up who wants a recurring-revenue service for local businesses without building websites by hand.

The Truth (as we see it)

Building a sample site before the pitch is a long-established way to win web clients, and AI tools have made it much cheaper. The upsell and close-rate claims are the creator's own and unaudited, and the video is essentially a promotion: the guest founded the software company, and the creator's link sells its partner program bundled with his own paid coaching community. Your margins and service depend on one vendor's pricing and platform staying in place. Facebook groups often ban promotional posts, and selling marketing retainers still means steady sales calls and client management.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
