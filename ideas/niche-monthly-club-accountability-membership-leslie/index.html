Niche Monthly Memberships

Niche monthly membership or club for ongoing progress, resources or community

What It Is

You run a paid monthly membership that gives members continued progress, feedback, fresh resources or each other. The creator's examples include a monthly accountability club for writers, a content co-working membership for solo creators, a seasonal garden planning club, a monthly home organizing challenge and a niche business template vault with new templates added every month. Members pay a recurring fee for as long as they stay.

The Pitch

The creator says people keep paying monthly when they want continued progress, support or community. She recommends starting with a focused workshop that can later lead into a membership.

Who It's Best For

People who enjoy running a community or producing fresh material every month and can commit to showing up consistently.

The Truth (as we see it)

Memberships can provide steady income, but they demand ongoing work and members cancel when value drops. The creator warns against starting one 'simply because recurring revenue sounds good' and says to do it only when members need continued progress, feedback, fresh resources or each other, which is honest advice. Some formats, like co-working or accountability calls, involve live video. The creator is a co-founder of MiloTree, a platform for selling digital products, and the video promotes its free AI product finder and building inside MiloTree, so this is partly a product pitch.
