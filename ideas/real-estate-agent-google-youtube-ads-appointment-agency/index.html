Real Estate Lead Generation Agency

Lead generation agency for real estate agents (Google/YouTube ads plus GoHighLevel appointment booking)

Marketing & content · Low to moderate startup cost (GoHighLevel subscription and calling tools; ad spend is paid by clients)

Originally published Oct 31, 2026 — James Clark

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

James Clark · How This 20-Year-Old Makes $100K/MO With a Simple AI Agency ↗

What It Is

You run Google and YouTube ads for real estate agents, brokers and investors, then use GoHighLevel to qualify the leads automatically and book them straight onto the agent's calendar, so you sell pre-booked appointments rather than raw leads. The guest, Noah, landed his first clients by cold calling from his phone and later a power dialer, sometimes starting with free trials or pay-per-appointment deals. His first larger deal was $2,000 paid up front for six months, and he now prices the service as a high-ticket retainer. He says the work is all ads, funnels and automations, with no AI agents or chatbots.

The Pitch

Noah says the agency grew from $1,000-$2,000 a month to $15,000-$30,000 within a few months after he quit his server job, and he says it is now near $100,000 a month. His argument is that selling only ads makes you a commodity, while adding a qualification and booking system gives agents what they actually want: appointments with serious buyers or sellers. He advises picking one high-ticket niche, getting the first client before worrying about delivery, and tracking daily call numbers.

Who It's Best For

Outgoing or disciplined people willing to make lots of cold calls and handle sales calls, who want to learn paid ads and funnel software.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is mainly an interview about mindset and hustle, but the underlying agency model is a common and legitimate one. The income figures are the guest's own and unaudited, and the video title's "AI agency" framing does not match what he describes. He admits early clients got poor results, that real estate has long sales cycles, and that leads from Meta ads often disappointed clients, so expect churn while you learn. The guest also sells a paid mentorship, and the business depends heavily on cold calling and sales calls.
