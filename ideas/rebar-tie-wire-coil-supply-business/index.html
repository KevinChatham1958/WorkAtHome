Rebar Tie Wire Coil Supply Business for Concrete Contractors

What It Is

Producing coils of rebar tie wire — the wire concrete crews use to tie steel rebar together before pouring — for sale to concrete contractors, rebar fabricators, and building material suppliers.

The Pitch

Tie wire is a true consumable: once concrete is poured it's buried inside the structure forever, so every new slab, column, foundation, driveway, or commercial floor needs more. The presenter frames the opportunity as simple — sell directly to the people who pour concrete every week (concrete contractors, rebar yards, job-site supply companies) — with success coming from availability, coil quality, fair pricing, and repeat orders rather than marketing. A small setup runs $2,000-6,000.

Best For

Someone focused on consistent availability and coil quality rather than marketing, since this product sells on reliability to repeat industrial buyers, not branding.

The Truth

Unglamorous, invisible consumables like this don't need to go viral to sell — construction crews need a steady supply regardless of how boring the product looks, which is exactly why the presenter highlights it.
