High-Ticket and Recurring Affiliate Offers

Picking affiliate offers by the commission math: high-ticket and recurring programs instead of cheap Amazon items

What It Is

This is an affiliate business planned backwards from the payout. You pick a niche you can fill with 50+ pieces of content, check keyword difficulty and the authority of the sites already ranking, and only then join 2-3 programs. You choose them on commission size and how well the brand converts, favoring offers that pay $50-$500 per sale or pay every month, rather than low-priced Amazon products.

The Pitch

Enfroy opens his own PartnerStack stats: about 97,000 clicks to Thinkific earned $1.1 million and 21,000 to LearnWorlds earned $191,000, while 1,800 clicks to Printify earned $3. That's why he says a brand's conversion rate is 'the hidden factor'. He works through three examples, assuming about 20% of visitors click a link and 2% of those buy. A $40 keyboard at 4% on 2,000 visits makes about $12.80 a month. A gold IRA post with 5,000 visits and a 0.1% conversion rate can earn $8,000 from one $100K account (he shows 230 leads to Augusta Precious Metals that paid him about $200,000). A recurring webinar-software post with 3,000 visits builds from about $360 in month one to about $4,000 by month twelve. He also gives a short email script for getting approved after a rejection.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who wants to understand the economics before choosing a niche, and is willing to chase fewer, higher-value offers, often in competitive areas like software or finance, rather than lots of cheap product links.

The Truth (as we see it)

Showing which programs failed (Printify for $3) alongside the wins is a useful bit of honesty, and the worked examples make the volume problem with cheap Amazon products very clear. His big results come from a well-established blog and are labeled not typical. The gold IRA example depends on a very rare conversion and is a 'your money or your life' category where he elsewhere advises small sites to be careful. The video promotes his 10K AI income launch plan and mentions Ahrefs and the Thirsty Affiliates plugin. It has no paid sponsor segment.
