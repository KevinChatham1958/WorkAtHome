Social Media Analytics Dashboards

Interactive social media analytics dashboard built with Claude (Etsy sales plus done-for-you builds)

What It Is

You build a single-file web app that lets a business owner or creator log posts and metrics for YouTube, Threads and Instagram, see follower growth, engagement and reach in 7, 30 and 90-day views, and import their existing Excel data. The process: check demand for social media planners on Etsy with the spy tool Profit Tree, study best-sellers' features and reviews, gather spreadsheet references, plan the app in Claude chat and have it write a full build prompt, then paste that into Claude Code. Data is stored in the browser (local storage), so there is no server or hosting cost. You can sell it on Etsy or offer done-for-you custom versions for $200-500 per build, a $50-200 monthly maintenance retainer, or a broader social media automation package.

The Pitch

The creator says static social media spreadsheets on Etsy earn up to $21,000, and an interactive version is easier to read and stands out. She argues a single $20 sale covers a month of Claude, builds take about an hour, and custom work can turn into recurring retainers as the client's needs grow.

Who It’s Best For

People who enjoy building small tools and either selling on Etsy or working one-on-one with business owners and creators.

The Truth (as we see it)

Claude Code can produce a usable single-file dashboard quickly, and the 'interactive version of a best-selling spreadsheet' angle is a real gap. Browser-only storage is simple but fragile (data can be lost if the browser is cleared), and buyers will need support. The $21,000 figure is a competitor estimate from a spy tool, and the service prices are the creator's suggestions, not proven results. The creator promotes her paid AI Profit Lab membership and an affiliate discount code for Profit Tree.
