Premium Coaching Offer

Building and validating a premium coaching/transformation offer using ChatGPT and Claude in different roles

Source video

Tanner Chidester · Claude AI + ChatGPT = $250,000 in 90 Days ↗

What It Is

Build a premium, implementation-supported coaching or transformation offer (the video's example: a $5,000 90-day fitness program for a specific customer type) by assigning ChatGPT and Claude different jobs throughout the process — ChatGPT for fast creation (curriculum drafts, outreach messages, roleplay practice, marketing content) and Claude for critical analysis (reviewing customer research for real patterns vs. assumptions, critiquing the curriculum and offer as a skeptical buyer, analyzing sales call transcripts and support conversations for recurring objections and drop-off points) — then using specific funnel math (a "law of 10" model converting ad spend to leads to booked calls to closed deals) to know what conversion metrics need improving.

The Pitch

The presenter walks through a full example build: defining one specific customer and problem, generating a minimum viable curriculum, stress-testing it with Claude for likely dropout points, packaging it as a $5,000 implementation-supported offer (not just a PDF), and reverse-engineering the funnel math needed to hit a $250,000/90-day revenue target (roughly $60K ad spend at a 4x return, with specific target conversion benchmarks: a ~$1,250 or lower customer acquisition cost, ~$100-150 per booked call, and under ~$15 per lead) — then using Claude weekly to analyze real business metrics (booking rate, show rate, close rate, CAC) and identify the single biggest constraint to fix next.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with genuine coaching/subject-matter expertise willing to build a specific, implementation-supported (not just informational) offer, and comfortable running or learning a paid-ads-driven sales funnel with real conversion-rate tracking.

The Truth (as we see it)

The dual-AI workflow (one tool for fast drafting, one for critical analysis) and the discipline of tracking funnel math to a single bottleneck are genuinely useful, concrete practices applicable beyond fitness coaching specifically. The $250,000-in-90-days figure is explicitly framed by the presenter as an illustrative target built from example conversion rates, not a guarantee, and the underlying business model (high-ticket coaching sold via paid-ads funnel) requires real ad budget, sales skill, and expertise beyond what AI tools alone can supply — the presenter's own "$141 million in career sales" claim is a self-reported figure, not independently verified.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
