UGC Video Template Packs on Etsy

Instagram Reel/UGC-style video template packs sold on Etsy, researched and built with Claude and Higgfield MCP

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost (Etsy listing fees, Claude access, and a Higgfield subscription for AI video generation)

Originally published Sep 2, 2026 — Luna Vega

Source

Luna Vega · Claude + Higgfield MCP Will Make You Crazy Rich (Just Copy Me) ↗

What It Is

Research already-selling Instagram Reel template packs on Etsy using Claude to identify why a bestseller converts and generate a ranked list of underserved adjacent niches, then build a new template pack for one of those gaps entirely inside Claude using the Higgfield MCP (an AI video generation tool) to produce UGC-style video content without switching between apps, and sell it both passively on Etsy and actively via personalized cold email outreach (a free watermarked sample per prospect) and building an audience on Threads.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates the full research-to-build pipeline live: a specific Claude prompt analyzes a bestselling Etsy template pack's reviews and conversion reasons, then generates 10 adjacent underserved niches to explore, landing on a holiday-season content pack gap. She frames the real differentiator as building higher-end, UGC-styled content per niche rather than generic "AI slop" bundles, and recommends supplementing passive Etsy sales with direct cold email outreach to small creators/businesses (offering a personalized watermarked sample, potentially upselling to a monthly content subscription) and building an audience on Threads, which she describes as still under-competitive compared to Instagram or TikTok.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable using AI tools for both research and content creation, willing to do the harder distribution work (cold outreach, building a Threads presence) rather than relying on Etsy's own traffic alone.

The Truth (as we see it)

The research method (using Claude to reverse-engineer why a listing converts and surface adjacent niches) is a genuinely useful, systematic approach to product research beyond guesswork. The presenter is candid that Etsy success requires real skill in thumbnails, keywords, and differentiation that takes practice to develop, and that passive listing alone is a slow path — the cold email and Threads distribution methods she recommends require real ongoing outreach effort, not just uploading a product.
