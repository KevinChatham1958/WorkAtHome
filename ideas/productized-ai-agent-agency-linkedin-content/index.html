AI Agent Agency via LinkedIn

Productized AI agent agency built through LinkedIn content instead of cold outreach

What It Is

Instead of running an AI agency through cold calls or paid ads, build a personal brand on LinkedIn with a consistent daily content formula (60% pure value/frameworks, 30% personal story, 10% results and proof, always focused on one problem, one solution, one ideal client, one offer), funnel engaged followers into a low-cost multi-day virtual bootcamp that builds trust and answers objections for many people at once, then sell a high-ticket productized AI agent package without individual sales calls.

The Pitch

Bedoya contrasts her early results, 200-300 booked cold-outreach-style sales calls generating only two closed $20,000 deals ($40,000 total for weeks of grinding), against the same offer sold through her bootcamp-and-content system generating $150,000 with comparable effort, and describes reaching $1.2 million total with no ad spend or sales team. Her pricing ladder moves from a low-cost bootcamp or training, to a $199-299 immediate upsell, to a high-ticket build (roughly $5,000-10,000 for a custom agent), to an ongoing monthly maintenance retainer ($500-a few thousand a month), and eventually to a limited "growth partner" tier (capped near 10 clients) charging $10,000-20,000 a month plus revenue share for a deeper, long-term partnership.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to consistently create and post content, and share genuinely specific tactical tidbits rather than vague promises, since Bedoya's approach depends on demonstrated expertise building trust before any sales conversation happens.

The Truth (as we see it)

Bedoya's headline figures ($1.2 million total, $180,000 in six weeks from one campaign) are her own self-reported business results, not independently audited, though she cites specific, checkable-in-principle facts alongside them (47,000-plus LinkedIn followers, Forbes features) that at least confirm the underlying content and audience genuinely exist. The core mechanic, using free content and a low-cost group event to do the work of many individual sales calls at once, is a reusable structural idea independent of the specific dollar figures achieved.
