Back-office AI automation for thin-margin traditional businesses

What It Is

You sell AI automation to traditional, low-tech businesses with thin profit margins, such as trucking and freight, staffing agencies, distribution, manufacturing and HVAC, targeting the repetitive spreadsheet and data work their staff do by hand. You price on the savings you deliver, typically as a high-ticket project or retainer.

The Pitch

Dang argues these businesses produce lots of revenue on thin margins, so even small efficiency gains matter a great deal to them, and they are rarely approached by tech-savvy sellers. His example: if automation can replace the spreadsheet work of four employees earning $50,000 a year, that is $200,000 in savings. He also argues that selling a $5,000-a-month service to a company with money is no harder than selling a $500 one to someone without it.

Who It's Best For

People who know how a traditional industry operates from the inside and can spot which manual processes are worth automating.

The Truth (as we see it)

The reasoning about thin-margin businesses is sensible, and these sectors are less crowded with AI sellers than tech startups. The $200,000 example is hypothetical, and pitching automation as replacing staff can make owners and employees wary. Deliverables need to be reliable, since these firms often run on fragile spreadsheets. The video is sponsored by .online domains and promotes Dang's free skill, live training and coaching calls.
