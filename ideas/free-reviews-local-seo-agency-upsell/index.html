Free-Review Local SEO Agency

Local SEO agency for home service businesses (free review trial upsold to Google Maps ranking software)

  • Services from home
  • $500–$2,000 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Video published Oct 8, 2026 by The Savvy Couple

Startup cost: Low to moderate startup cost (about $297/month for GoHighLevel plus roughly $79-$99 per client for the ranking tool)

Source video

The Savvy Couple · He Makes $40K/mo Selling ONE Simple Local SEO Offer (GoHighLevel) ↗

What It Is

You cold call home service businesses such as plumbers and roofers and offer to get them 25 reviews from past customers free, using GoHighLevel's automated text review requests (a two-week trial that then rolls into about $97 a month). Once reviews come in, you upsell a Google Maps ranking package built on a white-label tool called Merchant, which optimizes the Google Business Profile, writes keyword review replies, makes geotagged posts and posts simple YouTube videos, plus a push-button website if the client needs one. The guest, Balin, says he sells one fixed package with no tiers but sets the price on each call from $499 to about $1,500 a month based on how many crews, trucks and employees the business has. Setup takes his virtual assistant about 15 minutes per client because the software handles fulfillment.

The Pitch

The guest says his agency does about $40,000 a month from 145 clients with one assistant and 5-7 hours of work a week, much of it spent on older custom clients he regrets taking on. He argues that free reviews get you past the gatekeeper because they cost pennies to deliver, that every free trial he ran converted and upsold, and that selling one repeatable, low-fulfillment offer beats custom work. His roadmap for beginners is cold calling from 7-10 a.m. across time zones and doing nothing else until about $5,000 a month.

Who It’s Best For

People who are comfortable making many cold calls and closing over the phone, and who want a recurring-revenue service with little hands-on fulfillment.

The Truth (as we see it)

The model of reselling white-label local SEO software to small businesses is real and common among WAH creators, and the low per-client cost is plausible. The $40K/month, 100% trial conversion and beta-tester results are the guest's own unaudited claims, and his edge comes from more than 20 years of phone sales, so beginners should expect far lower close rates. The "scholarship close" and price-by-guess approach are sales tactics, and ranking results are not guaranteed (he himself says 30-60 days to see movement). The hosts earn affiliate commissions on GoHighLevel and Merchant and promote their own coaching community, and the guest sells a paid community and course; A2P texting registration and per-text fees also apply.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
