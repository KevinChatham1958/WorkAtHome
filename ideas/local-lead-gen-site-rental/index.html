Local Lead-Gen Website Rental

Local & professional services · Low startup cost

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · The Most Hands-Off $192K/M Business Anyone Can Copy ↗

What It Is

Build simple, single-niche, single-city websites (towing, HVAC, tree removal, radon mitigation, etc.) optimized to rank in local Google searches, then instead of selling the leads those sites generate, rent the entire site to a local business owner for a monthly fee or a cut of the resulting jobs, while retaining ownership of the site itself.

The Pitch

Vander walked away from a client-services SEO agency making $108,000 a year because he was tired of clients controlling the relationship, and rebuilt the same skill set into owned assets instead: ranking local lead-gen sites himself, then renting them out so that if one business owner doesn't want the arrangement, he can rent it to a different local competitor instead.

Who It's Best For

Someone with real SEO or local search skills who wants to own recurring, semi-passive assets rather than trade time for client work, and who's comfortable pitching a local business owner on a rev-share arrangement.

The Truth (as we see it)

The pivot story is specific and checkable (a named dollar figure walked away from, specific service niches and cities named), a good transparency signal. Vander is candid that this took real SEO skill built up over a prior failed agency business, so it isn't a true beginner idea; someone starting from zero would need to learn competitive local SEO first. The episode doesn't fully address what happens if Google's local algorithm changes or a business owner decides to build their own site instead of renewing.
