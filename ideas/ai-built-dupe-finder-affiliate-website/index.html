Product Dupe-Finder Website

AI-built product dupe-finder website earning affiliate commissions

AI & automation · Low startup cost (AI app-builder subscription and a domain)

What It Is

You build a website where shoppers paste a link to an expensive product, such as a $4,000 couch, and see cheaper look-alike alternatives with a "shop this dupe" button. The creator builds hers, called Find My Dupe, by describing it in plain English to the Abacus AI Agent, which writes, tests and hosts the site. You then join retailer affiliate programs through networks like Impact.com, add your affiliate links in a private admin area, and earn a commission when visitors buy. Google AdSense display ads can add a second income stream. Traffic comes from Google search and faceless TikTok and Instagram content.

The Pitch

The creator says sites like dupe.com get about 2.3 million visitors a month and that this type of site makes around $1 million a year, because visitors are already ready to buy and the owner never handles products. She also says a grown site can later be sold.

Best For

People interested in affiliate marketing who are willing to keep improving an app and spend months building traffic with search or faceless social content.

The Truth

Affiliate dupe-finder sites do exist and earn commissions, but the $1 million figure and the $345,000-a-month example are hypothetical math, not known numbers for any site. The demo glosses over the hard part: finding genuinely similar, in-stock cheaper products needs reliable product data, which a simple AI-built app may not have, and stocking affiliate links product by product takes work. Traffic is the main challenge, and affiliate programs may reject new sites with little traffic. The Abacus AI tool is promoted through a description link, which is likely sponsored or an affiliate deal.
