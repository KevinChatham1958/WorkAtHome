Monthly Social Posts for Small Businesses

Done-for-you monthly social media posts for small businesses (AI-drafted 30-post calendar)

Marketing & content · Near-zero cost (an AI tool, optional image tool)

What It Is

You sell a small business a finished month of social media posts with images ready to go. Using your written rules document and the customer's information, you have AI draft 30 posts (10 that teach, 10 that show proof, 10 that sell) delivered as a table with the posting day, caption and a description of the picture. You review and polish the output with your own judgment before handing it over. The creator frames it as a flat monthly price per finished calendar.

The Pitch

The creator says small businesses "are in desperate need of great content" and many haven't posted in weeks. She points out that businesses still budget as if a person writes every post (she cites $2,000 a month), while AI lets you deliver it for almost nothing, and that a set routine cuts the work from about 8 hours for the first client to around 40 minutes by the tenth.

Best For

Good writers or people with marketing taste who can find a handful of local or small-business clients.

The Truth

Social content for small businesses is a real, steady need, but it is also one of the most crowded freelance services, and many owners now use AI themselves. The $2,000 figure and the time savings are the creator's own claims. Generic AI posts are easy to spot; the value is in your editing, the business's real details and good images. You will need to pitch businesses directly to get clients. The creator pushes a free Sunday workshop (name and email required) and puts her prompts and outreach scripts behind description links; the video does not say what, if anything, is sold after the workshop. Her credentials ($10M in sales, Forbes feature) are self-reported.
