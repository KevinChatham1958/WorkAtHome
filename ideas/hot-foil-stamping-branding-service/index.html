Hot Foil Stamping Service

Offering hot foil stamping as a premium-finishing service for other brands' products and packaging

What It Is

Buy a hot foil stamping machine that presses metallic foil onto a surface using heat, and offer it as a finishing service to other small brands: leather brands with unbranded wallets, coffee brands with plain boxes, wedding invitation makers, and similar businesses that want a premium gold/silver-foil logo added to their existing product, charging per finished piece.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as costing a few cents in foil per item while letting the client charge substantially more once the product looks premium, working on leather, wood, paper, plastic, and even bread or leaves, arguing the appeal is selling brands 'the feeling that something is expensive,' not a physical product.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable doing small-batch finishing work for other businesses' existing products rather than manufacturing anything themselves.

The Truth (as we see it)

Hot foil stamping is a real, established finishing technique used across packaging and stationery industries. No specific personal revenue case study is given; pricing and use cases are the presenter's general framing.
