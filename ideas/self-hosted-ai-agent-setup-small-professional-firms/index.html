AI Agent Setup for Small Firms

Installing and managing a self-hosted AI agent (Hermes Agent) for small professional firms such as CPAs and specialty law firms

What It Is

Many small businesses want an AI assistant but cannot or will not use externally hosted tools and do not know how to set one up. You install Hermes Agent, a free open-source agent that runs on the client's own computer and works through Telegram, Discord, Signal, iMessage and voice messages, and configure it as an assistant, a marketing helper or a lead finder. You then stay on as the firm's outside "AI person" for a monthly fee. The guest suggests small white-collar firms such as local CPAs, accounting firms and specialty law firms (he estimates about 20,000 US traffic-law firms), with fees discussed around $5,000 a month.

The Pitch

The host points to people charging $5,000 to $20,000 to set up agents for businesses. The guest, a venture investor, likes the space, citing a rule of thumb that every $1 of business software sold has historically created about $5 of services work, and expects the same to happen with AI. He advises picking a vertical that is sophisticated enough to need help but not so sophisticated it does the job in-house.

Who It’s Best For

A technically comfortable person who enjoys setting up software and can explain it to non-technical professionals.

The Truth (as we see it)

The services-around-software argument is reasonable, and choosing one professional niche is sound advice. The $5,000-a-month figure is the guest's off-the-cuff suggestion, and the setup fees quoted come from other people's claims shown on screen; none are verified. Installing an agent with access to a law or accounting firm's data carries real security and confidentiality responsibility, so you need to know what you are doing. The AI-setup consultant pattern is common among work-at-home creators, and the tools change fast. The episode also contains a sponsored segment for a paid agent platform aimed at agencies.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
