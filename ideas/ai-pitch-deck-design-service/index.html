What It Is

You design presentation and pitch decks for executives, consultants and small businesses using AI slide tools: Gamma turns a one-line prompt into a full deck with AI images in chosen styles, Manus produces illustrated whiteboard-style slides, and ChatGPT can copy a style you show it. You deliver three options and let the client pick one to avoid endless revisions.

The Pitch

The creator says businesses pay $2,000-10,000 for pitch decks and $5,000-15,000 for keynote-quality decks, and quotes students who got a $2,000 tip and $1,500 for a single Manus deck. She recommends posting sample slides on LinkedIn to find clients and using AI to write quotes.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a good eye for layout and clear messaging who can find business clients, often through LinkedIn.

The Truth (as we see it)

The tools really do produce attractive decks in minutes, which is also why prices may fall as more people offer this. The price ranges and payouts are the creator's and her students' claims; most new freelancers will start far lower. Clients pay for clear thinking and story, not just looks. The creator uses affiliate links for the tools and sells an AI consultant certification and events.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
