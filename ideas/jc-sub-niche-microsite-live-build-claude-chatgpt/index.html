Sub-Niche Lead Microsite

Sub-niche lead microsite built live with Claude Code and ChatGPT (sewer line replacement example)

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (domain about $11-12, hosting under $1/month, AI plan from about $20/month)

Originally published Aug 18, 2026 — Jesse Cunningham

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jesse Cunningham · How I Build AI Microsites to Take Over Google ↗

What It Is

You pick a high-ticket sub-niche of a trade rather than the whole trade (sewer line replacement in Dallas instead of general plumbing), check it with a niche scoring tool, and build a full lead-generation site from a single prompt. ChatGPT is used to find top national sites for design inspiration; Claude Code builds about 46 pages (core, service and 26 location pages), validates links and schema, and fixes thin pages over roughly an hour while you do other things. Leads arrive by phone (answered by an AI agent) and web form, and you sell them to a local contractor.

The Pitch

The creator says one prompt can produce a site "better than 90% of local business websites," for the cost of a domain. He stresses that niche selection decides success, since a general plumbing site will not beat established competitors, and that your real focus should be choosing niches and finding buyers for the leads. He adds that people strapped for cash should start with faster income first.

Who It's Best For

Suits beginners who want to see the full build process and are willing to fact-check AI copy and then sell leads.

The Truth (as we see it)

The live build is a useful honest demonstration, including its flaws: the AI invented SVG illustrations, a "licensed and insured" claim, financing offers and fake testimonials, which he flags must be fixed; publishing such claims would be deceptive. Building is the easy part; ranking and selling leads are where most effort goes. He promotes his own paid Skool community (the Rank Expander Academy, 7-day free trial, then paid), and the build blueprint zip file he relies on is only available inside it. He also promotes a free niche-scoring tool tied to it.
