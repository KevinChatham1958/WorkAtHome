Live Events for High-Ticket Courses

"Intimacy marketing" via live events for selling high-ticket courses

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost

Originally published Aug 3, 2026 — Olly Richards - Business

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Olly Richards - Business · How to sell a disgusting number of courses in 2026 ↗

What It Is

A marketing framework for selling high-ticket online courses or coaching, built around replacing direct, constant sales emails with free or low-cost live events designed to build a real sense of connection with an audience before ever pitching the main product.

The Pitch

The presenter argues that email fatigue and AI-generated content have made direct, frequent sales pitches far less effective, and recommends a hub-and-spoke promotional calendar where live, interactive events, free or low-cost, sit around a central high-ticket product, each one designed to create a sense of closeness with the audience before a sale is ever made. The actual sale then happens either live during the event or in a follow-up email to attendees afterward.

Who It's Best For

Someone who already has an audience and an existing course or coaching offer, looking to improve conversion rather than someone starting from zero.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is explicit that this approach is not passive and requires ongoing live engagement, a notably honest departure from passive-income framing common elsewhere in this space. No specific before-and-after revenue figures are given for this exact framework; the argument is presented as reasoning and pattern-observation, not a single case study.
