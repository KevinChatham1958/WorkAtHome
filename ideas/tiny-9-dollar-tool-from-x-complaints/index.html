$9 Monthly Tiny Tool

A one-page $9-a-month web tool built with AI to fix a task freelancers complain about on X

AI & automation · Very low startup cost (AI agent subscription, domain; free hosting on Cloudflare)

What It Is

An AI agent reads posts on X where freelancers and small business owners complain about a recurring chore, shortlists five problems, then builds a one-page tool with a Stripe payment link, hosted free on Cloudflare. The creator's example, Invoice Bot, lets users make invoices free and pay $9 a month for AI follow-up reminders to clients who haven't paid. The agent also drafts daily replies to people posting about the problem, with a link to the tool. He projects about $95 a day.

The Pitch

The creator says charging $9 a month for a painful daily problem is easier than selling big-ticket services, and that the agent handles finding problems, building, checkout and promotion. He shows an earlier AI-built app that made its first $9.63.

Who It's Best For

Someone curious about building small software who will test the tool properly and handle support and billing questions.

The Truth (as we see it)

The only real revenue shown is $9.63 from an earlier app; the $95-a-day figure is a projection. Automated replies to strangers on X promoting your link can be treated as spam and get the account restricted, so manual or organic promotion is safer. Tools that email people's clients must handle data and deliverability responsibly. The creator sells one-click templates of these bots in his paid community.
