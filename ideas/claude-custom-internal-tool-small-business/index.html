Building Simple Custom Internal Tools (trackers, Logs, Booking Sheets) for Small Businesses to Replace Paper or Overpriced Software

What It Is

Build small, single-purpose internal tools for small businesses under about 20 employees — an employee attendance/clock-in tracker, a cleaning-company job log, a service-appointment booking page, a tutoring-center attendance sheet — using Claude to "vibe code" a simple, tailored system that replaces a paper process or an over-featured, overpriced piece of HR/operations software, then sell samples via cold outreach to businesses matching that specific operational pain point.

The Pitch

The presenter's framing example is an employee attendance tracker sold on Etsy as a simple, color-coded Google Sheet with automated formulas and real reviews from small businesses choosing it over a clipboard or a $100/month HR platform — the broader opportunity being that most small businesses under 20 employees are stuck between manual paper processes and software built for a much bigger company, and Claude can build a scoped-down tool for a specific repetitive task (not a full enterprise dashboard) in an afternoon, which is then demonstrated via cold email with a working sample attached.

Best For

Someone comfortable describing a business process clearly enough for Claude to build a working tool without needing to code by hand, and willing to build unsolicited sample tools and do cold outreach to land the first few clients.

The Truth

The underlying gap — small businesses caught between manual processes and enterprise-priced software — is a real and often-cited market niche, and scoped-down single-purpose tools are a genuinely practical use of AI-assisted ("vibe coded") development. The presenter is upfront this takes real early effort (building unsolicited samples, cold emailing) rather than being a quick, passive listing, and results will vary heavily by how well a given tool actually fits a business's specific workflow.
