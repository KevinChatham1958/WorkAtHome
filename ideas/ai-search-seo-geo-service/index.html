What It Is

You help businesses show up in traditional search results and, increasingly, in answers from AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude. Work involves producing targeted content and positioning the brand so AI tools recommend it. Clients pay a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Dang describes AI assistants as the new search engine and says few companies have adapted. He suggests targeting companies that already blog for SEO but show no sign of AI-search work, then messaging them about not appearing in Claude or ChatGPT answers. He quotes $1,000 to $3,000 per month and tells of a client who charged $3,000 a month for an app, grew its traffic dramatically, and was raised to $5,500. He scores it 22 of 25 and warns against mass-producing low-quality AI content.

Best For

Fits analytical people who like research, writing and measuring results, and who are willing to keep learning as AI search changes.

The Truth

There is genuine business interest in AI-search visibility, but the field is new, the methods are not well established, and results are hard to measure or guarantee, which can make retention difficult. His warning that spammy content gets caught is fair. The client story (including a claimed 1,000x traffic increase) is an anecdote with no data shown, and he notes clients in his paid program do this work. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
