What It Is

You build hands-on fluency with AI tools by using them daily for brainstorming, writing emails, editing content and automating repetitive tasks, without needing to code. The creator uses Claude for writing and research and Canva AI for design, and points to free AI courses from Google and Coursera. The skill is then sold to businesses that want to work faster, through roles or freelance work such as AI prompt specialist, automation assistant, digital operations specialist or AI workflow consultant.

The Pitch

The creator says AI dramatically increases productivity, so businesses need people who can put AI tools into their workflow, and she calls these roles well paid and doable from home. She frames tech literacy as insurance against being replaced by AI and outsourcing.

Who It's Best For

Curious, organized people who like trying new software and explaining it to others, including career changers without a technical degree.

The Truth (as we see it)

The video offers no specific service package, pricing or client-finding steps, so turning the skill into income is left to the viewer. Demand for practical AI help is real, but the field changes quickly and many people are offering similar help. The claim that these are "very high-paid" jobs is the creator's own and is not supported with figures. No paid product of hers is pitched in the transcript.
