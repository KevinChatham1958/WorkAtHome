Vertical SEO and AEO Agency

Agent-run SEO and AI-answer optimization agency for one industry, built on a free open-source SEO tool

What It Is

You run search engine optimization and "answer engine" optimization (getting a business named in ChatGPT-style answers) for clients, using Open SEO, a free open-source alternative to paid tools like Semrush and Ahrefs, with AI agents doing much of the routine work. The video's refinement is to pick one industry, such as orthodontists, mechanics, auto dealers or law firms, and sell a monthly package to that niche rather than to anyone. The host cites a founder who offered agent-run SEO for $1,000 a month on X and later raised prices. A side variation discussed is using the same tool to grow your own content sites paid by affiliate or performance commissions.

The Pitch

The host says the tool removes the expensive software bill that agencies normally carry, and that clients will pay a lot if you can actually move their rankings. His guest, a venture studio founder, says a vertical focus is what turns this into a real business, citing a marketing agency for orthodontists that sells $5,000 packages, and notes that almost any US vertical has thousands of potential customers.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who already understands SEO or is willing to learn it properly, and who is comfortable selling a monthly service to one type of business.

The Truth (as we see it)

The pairing of a cheap open-source tool with a narrow customer niche is sensible, and the guest's point that results, not the tool, decide whether clients stay is the honest core of it. The headline revenue figure for the founder on X was questioned on air even by the hosts, and no client results are shown; treat all numbers as unverified. Whether the open-source tool is as good as paid suites is left open in the video, and AI-answer optimization is still a young, hard-to-measure field. You still have to find and sell clients yourself. The episode also contains a sponsored segment for an agent platform aimed at agencies.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
