What It Is

A 5-phase roadmap for building a solopreneur consulting or service business: identify a marketable skill, validate demand through direct conversations, then build and sell an offer using a structured framework called the '6 Ps', Person, Problem, Promise, Plan, Product, Price.

The Pitch

Rather than guessing what to build, this approach validates demand through 10 to 20 real conversations with potential clients before building anything, aiming for a minimum offer price of around $2,000.

Who It’s Best For

Anyone considering a consulting or service-based business, regardless of the specific skill, this is a general launch process, not a specific idea.

The Truth (as we see it)

Presented as the creator's own general model and timeline, not a personally verified case study for this exact path. The direct-conversation validation step is a genuinely sound, low-risk practice worth borrowing for almost any service idea.
