What It Is

Ten AI-buildable digital product types, each demonstrated with a specific tool workflow: (1) printable activities (coloring pages, bingo games) made with any AI chatbot's image generator; (2) themed PDF planners optimized for GoodNotes, built with an AI coding agent (Claude Code) that autonomously assembles the layout; (3) guides/ebooks written by an AI chatbot, marketed with AI-generated fashion-model videos; (4) clip art packs, generated image-by-image or automated via an AI agent connected to a multi-model media generator; (5) printable wall art, sold as single high-resolution images (requiring an AI upscaler tool since generators don't output print-resolution by default); (6) customizable Canva business resource bundles for a specific professional niche (real estate agents, therapists, hairdressers), using Canva's frame feature for easy customer customization; (7) education resources sold on Teachers Pay Teachers (not just for teachers), using an AI image generator for worksheets; (8) customizable PowerPoint trivia/party games built and animated in Canva; (9) custom AI-generated niche videos (e.g. AI music videos) sold as Fiverr gigs; (10) simple apps (e.g. a budgeting app) built with an all-in-one AI 'vibe coding' platform that both builds and hosts the app.

The Pitch

For each category the presenter cites real, verifiable Etsy stores and sales figures as proof of demand: a printable-activities store with 239,000 sales in 3 years, a clip-art-only store with 12,000 sales in 9 months, a wall-art store with 22,000 sales in a year, an education-resources marketplace (Teachers Pay Teachers) that has paid out over $2 billion total, and a Fiverr AI-music-video gig with 14 orders queued at $560 each.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable using AI chatbots and at least one AI image generator, with an AI coding agent (like Claude Code) unlocking the more advanced formats (optimized planners, automated clip art batches, simple apps).

The Truth (as we see it)

All ten product categories and their cited Etsy/Fiverr/Teachers Pay Teachers sales figures are drawn from real, publicly viewable stores and platforms, giving genuine third-party grounding rather than unverifiable claims, and the specific AI tool workflows demonstrated (Claude Code, Canva, image upscalers, vibe-coding app builders) are technically accurate and current. The video includes two paid sponsorships (a domain registrar and an AI media-generation platform) plus several of the presenter's own affiliate links, so those specific tool recommendations are promotional even though the underlying product ideas and demand data are independently verifiable.
