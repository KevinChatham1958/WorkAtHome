One-on-One Zoom Coaching

One-on-one Zoom coaching in a skill you already have

Local & professional services · Near-zero cost (a payment link and a video-call account)

What It Is

You take something you are already good at, such as public speaking, fitness, exam subjects, piano or a professional skill, and sell it as paid one-on-one sessions over Zoom. You set a price per session or package and find clients by posting on LinkedIn or Instagram and messaging people directly. The creator's students charged amounts like £400 for a 90-minute session, £1,500 for a six-session package, or £100 for a month of fitness accountability. Hitting $1,000 usually takes only a handful of clients at these prices.

The Pitch

The creator says selling one higher-priced service to a few people is far easier than selling a cheap product to a hundred, which he illustrates with a simple price-times-people chart. He argues you don't need to be an expert, just "a couple of steps ahead" of the person you are helping. He points to students who reached $1,000 within weeks through coaching, including one who later quit her job.

Who It's Best For

People with a teachable skill or professional experience who are comfortable on video calls and willing to reach out to their network for clients.

The Truth (as we see it)

Coaching is a genuine, low-cost way to earn from home, and the price math the creator shows is accurate. The hard part is finding paying clients, which here meant personal outreach, posting on social media and getting on calls, so expect real sales effort. The student results are the creator's own examples and are not verified, and the creator runs a paid academy that one highlighted student later joined. Being only "a couple of steps ahead" can work for accountability-style coaching but is risky in areas like health or finance where real qualifications matter.
