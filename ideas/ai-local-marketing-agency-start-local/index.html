AI Local Marketing Agency

AI-assisted local marketing agency (start with two clients)

Marketing & content · Low startup cost

Originally published Sep 7, 2026 — Adam Enfroy

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Adam Enfroy · How to Build a 6-Figure Creator Business with AI (FREE 2 Hour Course) ↗

What It Is

Start a small marketing agency serving local businesses such as realtors, dentists, law firms or salons. Offer a short list of monthly services like local SEO and simple city-based blog pages, Google Business Profile and Maps management, social media and email content. Use AI to write your positioning, one-page website, service pages, pricing packages and standard operating procedures, then find leads through AI-assisted outreach and people you know.

The Pitch

Enfroy says this is the fastest path to income because you get paid monthly by clients instead of waiting for content to grow. His target is $2,000 a month times five clients for $10,000 a month, and he says just two clients ($4,000 a month) is enough to prove it works. In his example, an AI prompt produces packages from $1,500 to $3,500 a month. He says local clients pay $500 to $1,000 a month, higher-end ones like law firms $4,000 to $5,000, and over time $5,000 to $10,000 per client is possible when you move up to software companies and bigger brands. He tells his own story: going from a $9-an-hour pizza job to about $40,000 a year with two local clients, and says he used the same model again when he left a corporate marketing director role. His advice is to promise deliverables, not results, and to hire help after the second or third client.

Best For

Business-minded people who don't want to build their own audience, are comfortable with sales outreach and managing clients, and want recurring income sooner than content can provide.

The Truth

The model is long-established, and Enfroy's personal story is specific. Even so, the claim that "AI is doing 80% of the heavy lifting" and that you need no marketing experience is optimistic, and it is still client service work, as he admits. The video is a two-hour funnel for his paid programs: it promotes his free AI master class, a sale on AI Income Blueprint 2.0, his Launch to 10K agency program, and Thinkific affiliate links. Some segments appear to be recorded earlier (they mention older AI models and an early version of the course), so some tool references may be dated.
