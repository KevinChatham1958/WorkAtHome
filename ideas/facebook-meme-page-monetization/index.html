Facebook Page Monetization Via AI-scheduled Meme and Content Posting

Content & personal brand · Low startup cost (Post Planner subscription)

Originally published Aug 28, 2026 — Anji Martin

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Anji Martin · This Facebook Page Automation Tool Makes $1,000+/Mo (By Posting Memes) ↗

What It Is

Build a Facebook page around a niche (Martin's example: Christian content) posting text, photo, and meme-style content multiple times a day, using an AI-assisted scheduling tool (Post Planner) to find already-viral content in the niche, recreate it as original AI-generated images, and pre-schedule 6-10+ posts per day across Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and Twitter at once.

The Pitch

Martin says she grew her own page from zero to over 370,000 followers and as many as 49 million views in a single month using this process, and reports her best-performing single meme earned $500 through Facebook's content monetization program (with a student earning $400 on one post). Beyond direct platform payouts, she also monetizes by linking affiliate offers and digital products in post comments once a page has an engaged audience.

Best For

Someone comfortable picking a specific content niche and posting consistently multiple times a day, who's willing to use an AI tool to speed up content creation rather than making everything by hand.

The Truth

The core mechanic (studying proven viral content in a niche, recreating it as original AI-generated content, and mass-scheduling it) is genuinely how a lot of high-volume content pages operate today, and Facebook's content monetization payouts are real. That said, growing to hundreds of thousands of followers and tens of millions of monthly views, as Martin describes for her own page, took real time and consistency; the $500 and $400 per-post figures represent standout individual posts, not typical daily earnings, and the video promotes a specific paid tool (with the presenter's own discount code) and her paid mentorship community alongside the free advice.
