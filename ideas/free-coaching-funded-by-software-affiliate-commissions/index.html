Free Coaching Funded by Affiliates

Offering free coaching/mentorship funded entirely by affiliate commissions from the software tools your students sign up for anyway

Digital & subscription products · Very low (an AI website builder like Base44, a Claude/ChatGPT subscription, Loom for support)

Originally published Sep 7, 2026 — Nathan Nazareth

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Nathan Nazareth · how I make $1,800,000/mo from 1 AI business (just copy me) ↗

What It Is

Pick a niche where people already need to sign up for paid software to succeed, build a completely free coaching/mentorship program teaching people to start a business in that niche, direct students to sign up for the necessary tools through your own affiliate links so the software companies pay you a commission per user, and reinvest those commissions into running daily coaching calls and support, keeping incentives aligned with actually getting students results.

The Pitch

The presenter says his current version of this model made $1.8 million last month with over $1 million in pure profit, walking through the four-piece tool stack and the specific unit math for a beginner version: 50 new clients a month, half activating one tool paying $40-100 commission, already produces thousands of dollars a month in recurring income before charging students anything.

Best For

Someone willing to pick one specific niche with an obvious set of paid tools attached, and comfortable running real, hands-on coaching/support rather than a one-time course sale.

The Truth (as we see it)

Software affiliate commissions funding free coaching is a real, structurally sound business model that aligns the coach's incentives with student results better than a flat-fee course. His own revenue figures are self-reported, and the video is itself a lead-in to his own free 30-day program, which applies the same model to recruiting more people into this exact business.
