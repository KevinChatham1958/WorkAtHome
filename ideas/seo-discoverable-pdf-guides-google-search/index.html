SEO-discoverable PDF guides found through Google search rather than social media

Frank Garrett · I Just Used Google + PDFs To Make $32,000 in 30 Days Online ↗

What It Is

Writing a narrow-topic PDF guide with ChatGPT, formatting it as a real product in Google Docs, listing it on a free storefront, then building a simple, genuinely useful web page around the same topic and submitting it to Google Search Console so it can be found organically through search rather than social media.

The Pitch

The presenter walks through the full system live: use Google's autocomplete and People Also Ask results as free market research to find a narrow, already-searched-for problem, write the book with ChatGPT one chapter at a time using a detailed prompt (a named reader, their specific problem, and a no-fluff rule) rather than asking for the whole book at once, personally edit and fact-check the AI draft, format it properly in Google Docs before exporting to PDF, write a store page that names the exact problem, then build a free Google Sites page with real, useful content on the same topic and submit it for indexing in Google Search Console.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to treat this as a genuinely long-term, compounding project rather than expecting fast results, and comfortable doing real editing and fact-checking on AI-drafted content rather than publishing it as-is.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is one of the more transparent videos in this database on setting realistic expectations: the presenter says outright this is not fast money, shares that his own first book sold only three copies, and is explicit that indexing and ranking are not instant or guaranteed. The headline revenue figure is shown via a self-reported dashboard screenshot without independent verification, and it's not clear whether that figure reflects the same slow, compounding process otherwise described or a separate result. A paid program is promoted at the end.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
