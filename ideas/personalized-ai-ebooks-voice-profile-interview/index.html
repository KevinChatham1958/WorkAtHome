Personalized AI-Assisted Ebooks

Personalized ebooks written with AI using your voice profile and an interview

What It Is

You write and sell ebooks on topics you know, using AI to draft them in your own voice: first you give the AI writing samples to build a voice profile, then you have it interview you for your own stories before it writes. The creator demonstrates a 30-page relationship ebook that ChatGPT structured around a method it named, after asking her personal questions.

The Pitch

The creator says generic AI ebooks are junk, but ones built on your voice and stories read like you wrote them, which makes them worth buying. She notes viewers suggested dozens of sellable topics during the live session.

Who It's Best For

Someone with real experience or stories on a topic and an audience or channel to sell to.

The Truth (as we see it)

The voice-profile and interview approach genuinely improves AI writing, but the video doesn't cover marketing or sales numbers for ebooks, which is the hard part in a crowded market. Readers and platforms are increasingly wary of AI-written books, so honesty about AI use matters. The creator sells AI training programs and events.
