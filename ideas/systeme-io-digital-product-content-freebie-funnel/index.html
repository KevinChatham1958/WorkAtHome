Digital Product Funnel on Systeme.io

Own-audience digital product funnel built on Systeme.io instead of selling on Amazon

  • Free to start

What It Is

Instead of publishing a digital product (ebook, template, guide) directly to a marketplace like Amazon and hoping people find it, build a small owned funnel using the free all-in-one platform Systeme.io: create content that surfaces a specific problem, offer one genuinely useful free resource in exchange for an email address, then use an automated email sequence to introduce and sell a small digital product that solves the next step of that problem.

The Pitch

The presenter's five-step version: (1) find a specific problem rather than starting with a product idea, (2) create content on that problem before building anything, treating comments and analytics as free market research, (3) build exactly one useful freebie (not several), (4) connect landing page, email, checkout, and product delivery in Systeme.io's free tier, and (5) let audience behavior (what gets watched, downloaded, replied-to) dictate what small paid product to build next, rather than disappearing for months to build a large course upfront.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to build and grow their own content/audience first rather than relying on marketplace search traffic, and who prefers a single consolidated free tool over stitching together several paid platforms.

The Truth (as we see it)

Owning the customer relationship (email list) instead of a one-time marketplace transaction is a sound, well-established principle for repeat digital-product sales, and Systeme.io's free tier genuinely covers landing pages, email, and checkout in one place. The video is fairly generic and doesn't walk through a specific product, niche, or real numbers the way some similar funnel pitches do, so treat this as a solid structural framework rather than a proven, numbers-backed case study.
