Pet Sitting and Dog Walking

Dog sitting and walking through apps like Rover

Local & professional services · Free to start (just a Rover profile)

What It Is

Sign up on Rover (or similar pet-sitting apps) to offer dog walking, drop-in visits, and overnight sitting, building a client base one booking at a time and earning holiday-rate premiums during peak travel periods.

The Pitch

The presenter documents earning $794 in just over a week from a mix of one multi-day overnight stay ($282), two drop-in visits ($70), a cash tip ($50), and a holiday-rate booking ($294), rating it 5/5 for ease of entry and 4/5 for speed to first dollar.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with pets, available for flexible hours including early mornings, in an area with enough pet-owner demand.

The Truth (as we see it)

Pet sitting via Rover and similar apps is a real, widely-used gig platform with genuine demand, and the presenter's week of earnings is a plausible, itemized real example rather than a vague claim. Income is inherently variable and location-dependent, and building a steady client base takes time and good reviews.
