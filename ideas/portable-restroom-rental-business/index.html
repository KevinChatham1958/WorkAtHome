Portable Restroom Rentals

Running a portable restroom rental business for construction sites and events

Local & professional services · Moderate to high (purchasing multiple portable restroom units)

What It Is

Buy portable restroom units and rent them to construction sites, events, and fairs on a per-event or per-month basis, cleaning them on a scheduled route and collecting payment each rental cycle rather than selling anything outright.

The Pitch

The presenter cites the global portable restroom rental market at $20.71 billion, projected to reach $34.86 billion by 2030 per Grand View Research, framing the business's unattractiveness as precisely what keeps competition low.

Best For

Someone with capital to invest in multiple units and willing to manage a scheduled servicing/cleaning route.

The Truth

Portable restroom rental is a real, established equipment-rental business model with genuine recurring demand from construction and events, and the cited market-size figures come from a named third-party research source. No specific per-unit or per-operator revenue example is given in this segment.
