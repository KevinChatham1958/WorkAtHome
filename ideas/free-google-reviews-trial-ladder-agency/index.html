Review-First Local Agency

Phone-only agency for home-service companies: free review trial, then rankings and websites

Marketing & content · Near-zero cost (a phone and a lead list; a review-request tool once you have clients)

Originally published Nov 3, 2026 — Balint Adorjan

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Balint Adorjan · If I Had $0, I'd Do This to Hit $5K/Month (No Website, Just Calls) ↗

What It Is

Pick one home-service trade (plumbers, roofers, HVAC, pest control) and cold-call owners, ideally on their mobile numbers, after a 10-second look at how many Google reviews they have and where they rank. Open with "I noticed you only have 17 reviews, are you new in town?" and offer to get them 25 reviews free using a system that asks their past customers. Turn the trial into a low monthly fee ($97 to $149), then sell Google Business Profile optimization for rankings at around $499 a month, and finally a website, stacking services for the same clients. The creator's math is 10 clients at $500 for $5,000 a month.

The Pitch

The creator, who says he runs a $40,000-a-month agency while traveling, argues beginners waste months on websites, logos and funnels when the only thing that makes money early is talking to business owners, and a free, fast result earns the trust to sell the next service.

Who It's Best For

People willing to make lots of cold calls and handle rejection, who want a simple, step-by-step local marketing business.

The Truth (as we see it)

Review generation as an entry offer is a common and sensible agency tactic, and the step-up path is realistic. Cold calling takes many reps before it works, and some jurisdictions restrict cold calls or texts to mobile numbers, so check calling rules. Platforms prohibit incentivized or fake reviews, so the system must only ask real customers. The creator sells his scripts and frameworks in a paid community with live calls.
