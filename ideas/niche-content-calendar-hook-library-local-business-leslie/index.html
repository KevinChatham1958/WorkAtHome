Niche Content Calendars

Ready-made content calendars and reel hook libraries for a specific type of business

What It Is

You create done-for-you social media planning products for one type of business owner. The creator's examples are a 30-day content calendar for wedding photographers and an Instagram Reel hook library for local businesses, designed to turn viewers into booked appointments. Buyers download them and fill in their own posts, saving planning time.

The Pitch

The creator says these sell because they connect to making money (more appointments) and saving time on a task business owners know they must do, and that narrowing to one niche tells the right buyer immediately 'this was made for me.'

Who It's Best For

People with social media marketing experience in a particular industry who can write hooks and post ideas that actually fit it.

The Truth (as we see it)

Content calendars and hook lists are popular and easy to make, which also means there's plenty of free competition, so a narrow niche and proven examples matter. Results for the buyer depend on their own follow-through, so claims about turning viewers into appointments should be kept modest. The creator is a co-founder of MiloTree, a platform for selling digital products, and the video promotes its free AI product finder and building inside MiloTree, so this is partly a product pitch.
