AI-Designed Print-on-Demand Tees

Print-on-demand t-shirt store with 100 ChatGPT-made, print-ready designs

E-commerce · Low startup cost (AI subscription, store fees; ads are optional but used by the creator)

Originally published Sep 15, 2026 — Meg Heckman

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Meg Heckman · Stop Wasting Hours on T-Shirt Design. Use This AI Process Instead. ↗

What It Is

You run a print-on-demand t-shirt store, with the creator's rule being to launch with 100 designs, and generate every design in ChatGPT's image model. Her process is three steps: start from a concept flipped from a bestselling shirt, write a prompt with a 'design equation' (print look, art style, mood) plus print specs (4500 x 5400 pixels, 300 dpi, transparent PNG, sRGB, no copyrighted characters or logos), then generate from the prompt, from a breakdown of a reference image, or by applying a concept to a second image's style. Files upload straight to Printify, which prints and ships each order.

The Pitch

The creator says one shirt made this way has earned her over $8,000 in two months and cost under $25 to make. She argues the idea sells the shirt, and that a reusable prompt removes the need for Photoshop or Canva cleanup, so reaching 100 designs quickly is realistic. She says ChatGPT's newest image model is currently the best for print-on-demand art.

Who It's Best For

People with a sense of humor or a feel for a niche audience who can turn out lots of design ideas and are patient while a store finds its sellers.

The Truth (as we see it)

Print-on-demand is a real business, and her practical prompt specs and advice on avoiding copyrighted material are useful. The $8,000 figure is her own unaudited claim about one hit design; most designs in a 100-design store sell little, and she mentions running ads, which adds ongoing cost. Borrowing the look of a bestselling shirt can still drift into copying, so the originality of the concept matters, as she herself stresses. She references her paid community (the 'W scale community') and promotes a free blueprint that leads into her wider training.
