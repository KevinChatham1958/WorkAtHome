What It Is

You take over a business's email newsletter: writing the emails, nurturing subscribers and turning the list into sales. AI is used to study successful newsletters and draft much of the copy. The business pays a monthly fee, sometimes with a percentage of sales the emails generate.

The Pitch

Dang says many businesses collect large email lists but never write to them well or monetize them; he himself has tens of thousands of subscribers and writes his own emails. His outreach method is to subscribe to lists in niches with weak marketing, then send the owner sample rewritten emails. He estimates AI can do 70 to 90 percent of the drafting and quotes $1,000 to $5,000 per month, or a revenue share. He calls it underrated and scores competition low.

Who It’s Best For

Good for people who write clearly, understand basic selling, and like working on long-running client relationships.

The Truth (as we see it)

Email remains one of the more reliable sales channels, and a spec-sample pitch is a reasonable approach. The hard part is not writing emails but proving they make money, which requires copywriting and offer skills beyond what AI drafts provide. Results depend heavily on the client's list quality and product. The fee range is his estimate, and his offer to hire viewers doubles as engagement bait. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
