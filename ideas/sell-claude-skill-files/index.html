Selling Claude Skill Files

Packaging expertise as sellable Claude skill files

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost

Originally published Jul 14, 2026 — Sandy Lee AI and Ryan Doser

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Sandy Lee AI and Ryan Doser · Realistic Claude AI Side Hustle for Beginners ↗

What It Is

Take specialized professional knowledge, in this case marketing skills the creator uses for paying clients, and package it as a Claude "skill" file (a structured instructions/reference document Claude can use), then sell that file as a digital product through a simple Stripe checkout, rather than selling one-on-one consulting time.

The Pitch

Doser says this specific skill-stack product has generated over $10,000 in passive revenue through Stripe, requiring no ongoing delivery work once it's built, and frames the underlying idea simply: package your subject matter expertise into a sellable format rather than trading hours for client work.

Who It's Best For

Someone with real, applied professional expertise in a specific area (marketing, in this example) who's comfortable formatting that knowledge into structured, reusable instructions rather than personalized one-on-one service.

The Truth (as we see it)

The $10,000 figure is shown via a Stripe dashboard on screen, a stronger transparency signal than a stated-but-unshown number. This is a genuinely new product format compared to other digital-product entries in this database (ebooks, PDF guides, templates), specific to how Claude's skill system works, though that also means the audience able to actually use the product is narrower, limited to other Claude users comfortable setting up skills, not a general audience.
