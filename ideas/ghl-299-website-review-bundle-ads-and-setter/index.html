Home Service Website Subscription

$299/month website and Google reviews bundle for home service businesses (Facebook ads, sales calls and a commission setter)

Kai Stone · From $0 to $10,000/mo in 4 months selling ONE website Template ↗

What It Is

You sell home service businesses (contractors, trades and similar) a monthly subscription built entirely in GoHighLevel: an AI-built website, Google review requests, missed-call text-back and Google Business Profile optimization, with social media posting (three posts a week) as an upsell. The guest charges $299 a month and reports 43 active subscribers. Leads come from a Facebook ad (his first was a 'free website' offer) to a landing page and booking page with a video sales letter; people who don't book get messaged by an AI agent and called by a setter, and he closes on a sales call. After payment the site is built in three days, followed by two onboarding calls, the second focused on getting the client to collect reviews with a QR code.

The Pitch

The guest says he went from zero to about $10,000 a month in recurring revenue in three and a half months by copying the host's model, starting with $2,000 in ads. He credits a commission-based setter (paid $350 a month plus $5 per booked call that shows) with lifting his lead-to-booking rate from 42% to over 60%. His advice to beginners is to sell websites only, keep one offer and one lead source, and aim for '20 no's' a day.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable on sales calls and running paid ads who can invest a few thousand dollars and treat this as a full-time business with a small team.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a student-success interview on the host's channel, and both say it was built by following the host's paid community, so it doubles as a pitch for that program; the revenue and subscriber figures are the guest's own and unaudited. The guest had prior e-commerce experience, a business partner who handled the software, an office, 12-hour days and three hires, which is a long way from a solo side hustle. Their most credible point is about churn: they refuse to promise leads and push clients to call leads fast and collect reviews, because low-priced website bundles commonly lose clients who expected guaranteed results. Expect real ad spend before the first sale, ongoing software costs, and plenty of rejection.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
