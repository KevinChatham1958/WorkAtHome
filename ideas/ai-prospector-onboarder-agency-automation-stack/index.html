AI-Automated Client Onboarding

Using two connected AI tools to automatically find local businesses needing a website/AI system, then onboard them into a white-label recurring software stack in under a minute

  • Services from home
  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

From Jason Wardrop

Startup cost: Low (a white-labelable AI software platform subscription) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Jason Wardrop · I Set Up A Client's Entire AI System In 60 Seconds (LIVE Demo) ↗

What It Is

Use an AI 'prospector' tool connected to Google Maps to search any industry and location, automatically pulling contact info, review counts, and whether each business already has a website, then filter for businesses without one; copy that business's Google Business Profile link into an AI website builder and generate a complete demo website from a single prompt, no design/coding skill required. Once a business agrees to become a client, use a second AI 'onboarder' tool: create a white-label sub-account, paste in their Google Business Profile link, and run a pre-built 'skill' file that automatically fills in the needed custom values and sets up their full system (website with lead capture, AI chat/voice agents, booking calendars, follow-up automations, review requests) without manual configuration. Sell the completed package as a white-label monthly subscription under your own brand, illustratively priced around $209/month per client.

The Pitch

The presenter, who built and grew his own software company to over 14,000 paying clients between 2015-2021 (showing real Stripe screenshots of $15,000-20,000 daily recurring revenue as evidence), demonstrates the entire prospecting-to-onboarding workflow live and uncut in about 60 seconds, framing the traditional version of this work (a week of manual setup agencies charge $1,500-2,000/month for) as now fully automated; he cites several first-name-only individual results as illustrative rather than typical, explicitly disclaiming any income guarantee.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable operating within a white-label AI software platform and willing to handle the actual client relationship and sales conversations, since the AI tools handle prospecting and technical setup but not closing.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI tools that scan Google Maps for businesses lacking a website and auto-generate a working demo site, plus automated white-label sub-account onboarding via a pre-built skill file, are real, current capabilities demonstrated live on screen rather than just described. The presenter's past software-company revenue is backed by shown Stripe screenshots, though the specific client success stories cited are first-name-only and not independently verified, and the video gates a fuller paid/free workshop behind engagement actions, so that next step is promotional even though the demonstrated core workflow is real and replicable.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
