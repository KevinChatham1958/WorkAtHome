Used Phone Flipping

Buy, fix and resell used smartphones (the resale flip)

What It Is

Buy used smartphones cheaply, fix minor problems like a dead battery, and resell them for more. The video's example is buying a phone for $60, spending $15 on a fix, and selling it for $120, about $45 gross profit before fees. It can be started from home with a few hundred dollars of inventory.

The Pitch

The video points out that many people want newer tech but can't afford new prices, which keeps demand for good refurbished phones steady.

Who It’s Best For

People handy with small repairs who are comfortable buying and selling on marketplaces and checking devices for locks or damage.

The Truth (as we see it)

Phone flipping is a real and common side hustle, and the example margin is plausible on the right deals. The risks are buying blacklisted, carrier-locked or stolen phones, hidden faults, and marketplace fees eating the spread, so learn to check IMEI status and test thoroughly. Sourcing good deals is the hard part and may mean meeting local sellers. The video covers it in a few sentences without method details.
