Educational How-To YouTube Channel

Building a YouTube channel around educational how-to videos to earn AdSense revenue, rather than entertainment content

Content & personal brand · Very low (just a camera/phone and editing software)

Originally published Sep 6, 2026 — Greg Gottfried

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Gottfried · How I Built 10 Streams Of Income In My 20's ↗

One of 9 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Create short, specific educational how-to videos (the presenter started with individual chapters/lessons from his print-on-demand knowledge) and post them to YouTube to qualify for AdSense once subscriber/watch-time thresholds are met; deliberately choose educational content over entertainment content, since advertisers pay more for ads on educational videos and these videos get sustained organic search traffic over time (ranking on Google) rather than needing constant new uploads to keep view counts up.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a real example unrelated to his own channel — a general 'how to back up a trailer' tutorial with 400,000-500,000+ views — to illustrate how evergreen educational content can accumulate large view counts purely through organic search discovery, arguing this is the core mechanic that makes an educational channel more sustainable than an entertainment one.

Best For

Someone with genuine how-to knowledge willing to create clear, helpful videos rather than chase trends, and patient with the AdSense qualification threshold before monetization begins.

The Truth (as we see it)

YouTube's AdSense program, its subscriber/watch-time eligibility requirements, and the described pattern of educational content earning higher ad rates and more evergreen organic search traffic are accurate and match well-documented, common creator-economy observations. No specific personal revenue figures for this particular stream are given.
