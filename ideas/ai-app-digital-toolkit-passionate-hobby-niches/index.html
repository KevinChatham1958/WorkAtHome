What It Is

Pick a niche where people have an urgent or obsessive problem and sell a low-priced digital product, around $27, that includes a simple AI app that does the job for them rather than just a PDF. The creator's examples include a trading-card toolkit that checks whether a card is fake and what it's worth, a fantasy football planner that suggests weekly picks (with a monthly plan for the season), a first-30-days puppy plan with an 'ask the trainer' chat, and an interview kit that rewrites a CV for each job and runs mock interviews. He also suggests higher-priced versions for real estate agents (a seller-leads course plus an AI that writes listing posts) and coaches on LinkedIn (a course plus an AI that interviews them monthly and writes posts in their voice).

The Pitch

The creator says his team has sold over 20,000 digital products in 12 months and that most products fail because nobody was looking for them, so he starts with niches where people already spend money and feel the pain. He argues an app that does the work feels worth far more than the price, even if buyers could technically do it in ChatGPT.

Who It’s Best For

People who can build simple AI tools or are willing to learn no-code app builders, and who like researching what a specific audience will pay for.

The Truth (as we see it)

Bundling a working AI tool with a guide is a sensible way to stand out from plain PDFs, and the niche reasoning is sound. The sales figure is the creator's own and unverified, the video doesn't explain how to build, host or market the apps, and running AI apps has ongoing costs (API usage) and support demands. Niches like card authentication and job applications also carry accuracy expectations a quick AI app may not meet. The creator references his own products and tools (a niche library, a Pokemon card app and a singing-invitation service called SendASinger) and points viewers to more of his videos; he says plainly that this is information, not a promise of income.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
