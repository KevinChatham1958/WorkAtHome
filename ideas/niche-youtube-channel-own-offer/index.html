Niche YouTube Channel With Own Offer

Face-on niche YouTube channel monetized with your own offer instead of ad revenue

Content & personal brand · Low startup cost

Originally published Jun 30, 2026 — Patrick Dang

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Patrick Dang · Claude + YouTube = $124,000/Month ↗

What It Is

You start a YouTube channel where you show your face, in a niche you care about that has enough viewers and money in it, and plan from the start how you'll earn: sponsorships early on, then your own course, paid community or high-ticket service sold through calls or webinars. AI helps with ideas, scripts and thumbnails.

The Pitch

Dang argues that faceless AI channels are easy to copy and hard to trust, while a real person can sell their own offers. His niche test is passion first, then whether the market is big enough, then how you'll make money within 30 to 60 days. He shares that one of his videos with about 400,000 views earned around $3,700 in ad revenue, far less than he makes from his own programs. His examples include his early tech-sales channel selling a roughly $500 course, and his client Sandy's paid community at $77 a month with 181 members.

Who It's Best For

People with a real interest or skill they can talk about on camera week after week, who are willing to sell something of their own.

The Truth (as we see it)

The advice to plan monetization early and not rely on ad revenue is sound, and his ad-revenue figure is a useful reality check. Growing a channel still usually takes many months of consistent uploads, and most channels stay small. The title's '$124,000/Month' refers to Dang's own claimed income, and the client income figures are his claims. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield and promotes his free skill, live training and coaching calls.
