Underserved-Niche Lead Sites

$11 lead-generation sites for underserved local niches, found and built by an AI agent

Marketing & content · Near-zero cost (about $11 per domain plus a few dollars in agent credits)

What It Is

You have an AI agent find cities where demand for an unglamorous service outstrips supply (crane rental in Laredo, shower doors in Brownsville), buy the domain, build a simple SEO-friendly site and connect analytics. When leads arrive, you give the first few free to local providers, see who closes them, then rent the site exclusively to one provider for a monthly fee.

The Pitch

The creator says his $11 crane rental site brought three leads in a month worth roughly $3,000, $9,000 and $23,000 to the provider, and the agent sends him weekly ranking reports. He argues everyone builds sites for plumbers and roofers, so odd niches have less competition, and suggests charging around $800 a month and raising it as leads grow.

Who It's Best For

Someone patient with SEO who is comfortable calling local business owners to hand over leads and negotiate a monthly fee.

The Truth (as we see it)

Lead-gen sites are an established model, but three leads from one site in a month is an early, unverified anecdote, and ranking can rise or fall. You need a provider willing to pay, and nothing is earned until you convert free leads into a paid arrangement. The creator sells an AI consulting program (Playmakers AI).
