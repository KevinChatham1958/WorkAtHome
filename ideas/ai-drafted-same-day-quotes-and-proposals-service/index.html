Same-Day Quotes and Proposals

Same-day quote and proposal writing for small businesses

What It Is

You write the quotes and proposals a small business keeps putting off and get them sent the same day. Using the business's pricing and job details plus your rules document, AI drafts each quote and you check and finish it. The business uses you like an outside back office for this task and pays per finished result or a monthly fee.

The Pitch

The creator says owners often have "five quotes sitting in my email that I have never sent," which means lost jobs. Being the firm that turns them around the same day solves a problem owners already feel, and she says this is boring work worth real money.

Best For

People with sales-support, estimating or office admin experience in a trade or service industry.

The Truth

Slow quoting does cost service businesses work, so the need is real. The video offers only a one-line description, so pricing, workflow and how you get job details are up to you. Mistakes in prices or scope can cost the client money, so careful review matters more than speed. You will need to pitch businesses directly. The creator pushes a free Sunday workshop (name and email required) and puts her prompts and outreach scripts behind description links; the video does not say what, if anything, is sold after the workshop. Her credentials ($10M in sales, Forbes feature) are self-reported.
