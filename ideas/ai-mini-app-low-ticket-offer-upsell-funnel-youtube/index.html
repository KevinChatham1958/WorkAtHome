Low-Ticket AI Product Funnel

A $27-47 AI-built digital product (mini-apps or tools plus a short workshop) sold through an upsell funnel, with YouTube as the traffic source

Digital & subscription products · Low startup cost (CRM or funnel software subscription, AI tools)

Marissa Romero · After $1.3M in Digital Products, I CHALLENGE You: Make Your Next $10K in 30 Days ↗

What It Is

You build one low-priced "impulse buy" product, priced $27, $37 or $47, that bundles practical tools such as a calculator, mini-app or AI assistant (her examples: an AI pet-care assistant, family organizer, travel concierge) with a short tutorial people can finish in under an hour. AI writes the sales page copy and a vibe-coding tool builds the apps; she uses GoHighLevel, a CRM with a built-in app builder. Order bumps and several upsells raise the average order to around $100, so $10,000 takes about 100 buyers instead of 271 at $37. Traffic comes from a YouTube channel built around two or three content pillars, posting about eight videos a month, promoting the product from the first video; paid ads come later.

The Pitch

The creator says she has made $1.3 million from digital products and that a recent AI product earned $3,300 in under two weeks. She argues low impulse prices beat subscriptions and $300 offers for strangers, and that upsells let customers "say yes several times", lifting order value. She says organic YouTube traffic compounds like planting a money tree and lets you validate an offer before spending on ads.

Who It's Best For

Someone with a skill or topic people will pay to shortcut, willing to post YouTube videos consistently for a year and learn basic funnel software.

The Truth (as we see it)

Her $1.3 million figure and recent launch numbers are her own and unaudited, and she already had a 200,000-subscriber channel; her sister's example shows a year of posting to reach 1,600 subscribers. The video is largely a pitch for her paid "AI Product to Profit Builder" and she is a GoHighLevel speaker, so the software recommendation may involve an affiliate relationship; other funnel tools work. Stacking many upsells can raise order value but can also feel pushy and increase refunds. The "$10K in 30 days" challenge framing is ambitious for someone starting without an audience.
