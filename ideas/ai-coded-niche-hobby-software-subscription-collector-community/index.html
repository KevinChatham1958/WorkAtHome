Niche Hobby Software App

AI-coded niche software for an underserved hobby community (sold by subscription)

  • AI & automation
  • Low startup cost

Video published Oct 15, 2026 by Ryan Pineda

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Ryan Pineda · Russell Brunson: I Replaced 250 Employees With One AI ↗

What It Is

You pick a hobby or niche you know well where existing software is poor or missing because no company would invest in such a small market. You build the tool you want yourself with an AI app builder, describing features or uploading screenshots instead of writing code. Russell Brunson's example is a catalog app for rare-book collectors to scan and track their library. Once it works, you share it in the online groups you already belong to and charge a small subscription (he suggests $10-$20 a month).

The Pitch

Russell argues that big one-size-fits-all software is giving way to small custom tools, and that hobby niches are underserved because nobody would pay a development team to build for them. He says if a passionate insider shares a tool in 30 Facebook groups, around 1,000 users at $10 a month could make it a roughly $120,000-a-year business with no ads.

Who It’s Best For

Hobbyists or niche insiders who are active in their community's groups and willing to learn AI app-building tools.

The Truth (as we see it)

Building small tools for a niche you know is a real and increasingly practical opportunity as AI coding tools improve. Russell's figures are a projection for an app he hasn't launched yet, not a result, and he says himself that software prices are racing toward zero, which makes charging a monthly fee harder over time. The app builder he recommends, Over Skill, is his own company, so treat the tool advice as promotion and compare alternatives. Support, bug fixes and data privacy for users' collections are ongoing work the conversation doesn't cover.
