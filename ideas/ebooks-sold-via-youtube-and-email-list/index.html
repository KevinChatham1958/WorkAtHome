Ebooks via YouTube and Email

Selling your own ebook or digital product through a YouTube channel and email list instead of Amazon or Etsy

  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Startup cost: Near-zero cost (a phone, a free checkout platform tier and AI writing tools)

Molly Keyser · How selling ebooks with AI changed my life (DON’T sell on amazon!) ↗

What It Is

You pick a niche you know well, post one YouTube video a week modeled on proven outlier videos, offer a free download to build an email list, survey subscribers about their biggest problem, then pre-sell a digital product that solves it through a simple checkout page and deliver it afterwards. AI tools help draft the ebook, course scripts, sales copy and emails.

The Pitch

The creator says a 13-page, $59 ebook for photographers did over $56,000 in sales, that she reached $10,000 months with under 5,000 subscribers, and that she now profits about $400,000 a year from long-form YouTube alone. She argues Amazon and Etsy don't let you own your customer list and push you to pay for ads.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with real expertise or a hobby skill who is willing to appear on video regularly.

The Truth (as we see it)

Owning your audience and pre-selling before building are solid, widely recommended practices, and the creator is candid that paid ads are hard and that short-form content converted poorly for her. Her income figures are her own and come after years in business. She sells a $99-a-month membership (Freedom Creator Club), promotes an AI course as an affiliate, and earns from her checkout-platform link.
