Editable Wedding Invitation Templates

AI-designed editable wedding invitation templates sold on Etsy

What It Is

You use AI image tools to create invitation designs, then turn them into editable Canva templates where buyers fill in names and dates. Couples buy the template as a digital download on Etsy and print it themselves.

The Pitch

The presenter says revenue in this niche is unusually strong, citing an Etsy shop he says has close to $700,000 in lifetime sales from wedding invitation templates and another shop with a single listing that made thousands of sales. He says no design skills are needed: prompt tools like ChatGPT, Nano Banana Pro or Ideogram with something like 'minimalist wedding invitation, watercolor florals,' pick a design, and add text boxes in free Canva. He says a design can be generated in about a minute.

Best For

People with a decent eye for design who don't mind handling buyer messages and want a product with steady seasonal demand.

The Truth

The shop figures are the presenter's own references and were not shown in detail, so treat them as unverified; top-earning shops are outliers in a crowded category. He is candid that Etsy sellers handle all customer service, including printing problems, color changes and refund requests, and that Etsy charges listing fees. Wedding stationery is a competitive Etsy niche where listing quality, photography and SEO matter more than the AI generation step. He promotes his own KDP coaching program and book tool elsewhere in the video, though not for this idea.
