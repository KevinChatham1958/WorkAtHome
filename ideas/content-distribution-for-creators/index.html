What It Is

You take a creator's or founder's long-form YouTube videos or podcasts and turn them into content for platforms they aren't using yet: LinkedIn posts and carousels, newsletters, blog posts and short clips. Claude works from the transcripts to find the best material and draft the posts; you edit and publish. Pay is a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Dang says many YouTubers have no presence on LinkedIn or other platforms and are leaving money on the table. Your pitch is to turn each video into several posts or clips elsewhere. He quotes $750 to $5,000 a month, possibly more for strong results, and says he himself is looking to hire someone to do this for his channel. He rates it 3 of 5 for ease, since you need to know what good content looks like on each platform.

Who It’s Best For

People who know several social platforms well and can rewrite one piece of content to fit each one's style.

The Truth (as we see it)

Repurposing long-form content is an established service with steady demand, and AI genuinely speeds up the transcript work. It's also a crowded market, so results on the new platform are what win and keep clients. Dang mentioning that he'd hire for it is a real demand signal but not a job offer. The video is sponsored by Hostinger and promotes his free skill, live training and coaching calls.
