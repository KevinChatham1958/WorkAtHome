AI Agency Middleman

Running an AI-agency 'middleman' brokerage connecting developers to high-ticket local service businesses

Local & professional services · Very low (Lovable for a simple landing site, GoHighLevel/voice agent tooling paid per client, developer fees paid as a % of each deal, no coding required)

Originally published Jun 27, 2026 — Angelo Turri

Share on Facebook Link copied

Angelo Turri · My Business is Boring, But Makes me $48,000/Month ↗

What It Is

Position yourself as a sales-and-consulting 'middleman' for AI automation in one high-ticket local-service niche (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, painting, electricians), qualify leads via Google Maps plus Google Ads/review signals, close deals using a three-step 'pain point prioritization' sales framework (identify, quantify, prioritize problems before ever mentioning AI or price), then hand off the entire technical build to a vetted freelance developer (paid 15-20% of the deal) so you never touch the code yourself.

The Pitch

The presenter says this 'boring' middleman model makes him $48,000/month and cites several of his own students landing $8,000-$20,000 in their first 60-90 days using the same framework. He gives away the full sales script and qualification process, framing the business as low-risk to start since you're the closer/consultant, not the builder, with developers found and vetted through AI-agency Discord communities.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable making cold calls and running structured sales conversations, willing to build relationships with freelance developers rather than build the automations personally.

The Truth (as we see it)

The underlying model (broker AI automation deals for high-ticket local trades, delegate the technical build) is a real, sound structure that several agency operators use, and the sales framework itself (quantifying pain points in dollar terms before pitching) is standard high-ticket consultative selling, not AI-specific. Specific income figures (his own $48K/month and student results) are self-reported and unverified, and the video ends by pitching his own paid one-on-one mentorship program.
