Short-Run Product Label Printing

Short-run product label printing for small brands like coffee roasters, hot sauce makers and candle companies

Business Dash · If I Had to Start a Label Printing Business With Little Money, I’d Do This ↗

What It Is

You print and cut roll labels in small runs of a few hundred to a few thousand for small product brands, which need new labels every batch, flavour and season. You start with one compact colour label printer at home and grow the equipment only when customers demand it.

The Pitch

Business Dash says the real business is repeat orders from small brands, not one-off stickers. It works through a $450 job for 3,000 hot sauce labels that leaves about $128 after film, ink, labour and delivery, before equipment and other costs.

Who It’s Best For

Detail-oriented people who like print work and can win and keep business customers.

The Truth (as we see it)

The $128 is before equipment, maintenance and your other overheads, as the video stresses. Professional printers run about $4,000 to $8,500 and finishing equipment around $25,000, so buying too much too early is the main risk. Colour accuracy and deadlines decide whether brands reorder. The channel's video descriptions link to machines and supplies through AliExpress affiliate links.
