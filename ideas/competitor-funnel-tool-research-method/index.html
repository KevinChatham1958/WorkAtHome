Reverse-engineering Competitors' Online Business Strategy, Customer Funnel, and Tech Stack Without Copying Their Content

Marketing & content · Free (using free tools like builtwith.com, viewing page source, and signing up for competitors' free offers/email lists/webinars)

Originally published Sep 12, 2026 — Cindy's Got Systems

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Cindy's Got Systems · An online business education money can't buy ↗

What It Is

Identify at least three competitors already doing what you want to build, follow them everywhere they're active, sign up for their free lead magnets, webinars, and email lists, and pay attention to the overarching strategy (which platforms they use, whether they run ads or go organic, whether they use upsells) rather than copying specific content; use a screen recorder to document and narrate the full customer flow you experience, and use free tools like builtwith.com or a website's page source to identify a competitor's exact tech stack, so you can build your own comparable system without paying for expensive courses.

The Pitch

The presenter, a former middle manager turned online-business educator, says she built her entire early business education this way because she had no money for courses or coaches, explicitly distinguishing this from content theft: she never copies competitors' sales copy, courses, or onboarding content directly, only the underlying strategic structure and tools.

Best For

Someone starting an online coaching, course, or solopreneur education business with little to no budget for paid training, comfortable doing methodical, unglamorous research rather than following a packaged course.

The Truth

This is a legitimate, ethical competitive-research methodology, distinct from and explicitly contrasted against copying another creator's actual material, and tools like builtwith.com and page-source inspection are real, free, publicly available research methods. No specific revenue outcome is claimed; it's a research process, not a guaranteed path to any particular income level.
