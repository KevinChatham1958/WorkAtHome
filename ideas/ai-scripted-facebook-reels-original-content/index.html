What It Is

Once a Facebook page or professional profile qualifies for the platform's content monetization program (roughly 5,000 to 10,000 followers, plus meeting watch-time and originality rules), post short original Reels daily built through an AI pipeline: an AI chatbot generates the niche, video ideas, hooks, and scripts using strict formatting rules (no greeting, no intro, a strong first line, short spoken sentences), an AI voice tool reads the script, and a video editor assembles captions and background footage, with the creator's own voice, footage, or face required somewhere in the video to satisfy Facebook's originality rules.

The Pitch

The core lever emphasized is niche selection for RPM (earnings per 1,000 views), not just view count: general entertainment content from lower-paying regions might earn $0.30 to $2 per 1,000 views, while finance, business, and tech content reaching US/UK audiences can earn $8 to $20 or more per 1,000 views for the same effort, so picking an advertiser-friendly niche upfront matters more than chasing raw views. A specific prompt-chaining workflow is demonstrated step by step: generate niche options ranked by advertiser value, generate 30 video ideas with hooks under 12 words, turn a chosen idea into a tightly-ruled script (each line delivering new information every 5 seconds, ending in a way that loops back to the opening line to boost rewatch time), then feed a week's worth of performance screenshots back into the AI to identify what the best-performing videos have in common.

Best For

Someone willing to post daily and stay in front of the camera or use their own voice/footage (a bare AI-generated video without any personal element risks violating the platform's originality rules), and who's comfortable iterating on scripts and formats based on real performance data rather than guessing.

The Truth

The presenter shows their own real earnings dashboard on screen (over $14,000 for a single month), a stronger transparency signal than a stated claim alone, and is direct about a real risk: Facebook rewards content with a genuinely personal element and can demonetize an account that relies entirely on generic AI-generated video, sometimes only once the account has scaled, which is a real, worth-knowing platform risk rather than a minor caveat. The video promotes the presenter's own paid "Facebook Income Method" program throughout, a financial incentive separate from whether the free workflow shown works on its own.
