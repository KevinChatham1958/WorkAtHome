Lead Microsites Plus Agency Work

Lead-generating microsites that open the door to paid website and SEO work for the contractors you send leads to

Jesse Cunningham · How I Would Make Money Online in 2027 (If I Had to Start Over) ↗

What It Is

You build a small AI-made website for one service in one town, get it ranking, and sell its exclusive leads to a local contractor. Once you've built a relationship by sending them reliable leads, you offer to rebuild their own website or run their SEO for a flat fee or monthly retainer, which he calls the real cash flow. It's all done from a laptop.

The Pitch

Jesse says this is how he'd make money online in 2027 if starting from zero with a laptop and a few hundred dollars. He says a site costs about $12 to make, that some of his sites have held number one for almost two years, and that one community member built 300 microsites.

Who It’s Best For

People who enjoy building websites and can hold a straight sales conversation with tradespeople.

The Truth (as we see it)

The money comes only after a site ranks, which can take months and may never happen in a crowded niche. Selling leads and agency work both mean cold-calling contractors. The 'two years at number one' claim is his and isn't shown in detail. Jesse runs the Rank Expander Academy, a paid Skool community with a 7-day free trial, and promotes it throughout the video; the build files he mentions are inside it.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
