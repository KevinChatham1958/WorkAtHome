Affirmation and Quote Books on KDP

Daily affirmation and quote books published on Amazon KDP

What It Is

You create a themed book of affirmations or quotes, for example 365 affirmations for confidence or gratitude, laid out one per page in a Canva template with a simple cover, and publish it through Amazon KDP. Amazon prints and ships copies and pays royalties.

The Pitch

The presenter calls it the easiest product on his list, something that can be made in an afternoon by asking ChatGPT or Claude for 365 affirmations on a theme. He says gift demand is evergreen, with purchases for birthdays, Mother's Day, New Year and graduations. He positions it as a good first product to get a first sale and build confidence, with KDP handling printing, shipping and customer service at no publishing cost.

Who It’s Best For

Complete beginners who want a simple first publishing project to learn the KDP process.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter himself warns that because it is so easy, many people do it, and he expects affirmation books to be flooded; he cannot guarantee sales. Purely AI-generated text with little design effort is hard to differentiate and Amazon requires disclosure of AI content. Treat it as a learning exercise rather than an income source. The video ends with a pitch for his paid KDP coaching program via a free discovery call, and he also promotes his own AI book tool.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
