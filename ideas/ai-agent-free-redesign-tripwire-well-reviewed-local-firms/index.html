What It Is

Have an AI agent search a city for one type of local business, such as tree services, that has many good Google reviews but no website or an outdated one. The agent builds a custom website for each prospect, which you offer for free as a 'tripwire' to open a sales conversation. Once the owner accepts, you sell paid follow-on services such as AI automations or ongoing marketing.

The Pitch

The host says businesses without websites usually ignore pitches, but a ready-made custom site given away free gets a solid response rate. An agent can find the prospects and build the sites at scale, so the cost of each free site is small compared with the value of a paying client.

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable with sales conversations who want to start a small AI or web services agency for local businesses.

The Truth (as we see it)

A free, finished sample is a known way to get attention in cold outreach, and good reviews are a sensible sign that a business has money and customers. Free work only pays off if you can actually close follow-on services, and the video shows the prospecting, not the closing rate or revenue. Businesses that have managed without a website may still say no, and you are responsible for the quality of anything the agent builds. The video is sponsored by Zapier.
