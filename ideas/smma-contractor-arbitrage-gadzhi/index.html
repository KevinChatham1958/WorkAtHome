SMMA With Overseas Contractors

Social media marketing agency (SMMA) delivering services through lower-cost overseas contractors

What It Is

You sell marketing services such as paid ads, SEO, websites, social media management or lead generation to businesses for a monthly fee, and hire contractors to do the work. Gadzhi's example of 'contractor arbitrage' is paying a contractor in a lower-cost country about $500 a month per client while charging the client $1,500 to $2,000. You handle prospecting, outreach, sales and managing both the team and the clients.

The Pitch

Gadzhi, who started his own career with an agency, calls SMMA one of the best beginner models because it needs almost no money and you control delivery. He frames it as a lifestyle business that can reach $5,000 to $20,000 a month, but says growing past that adds so much client and staff complexity that 'the extra juice just isn't worth the squeeze.'

Who It’s Best For

People willing to do steady cold outreach and sales calls and to manage contractors and client relationships every day.

The Truth (as we see it)

Agencies run from home are a real, low-cost business, and Gadzhi is fair that it means daily client and team management, not passive income, and that competition has grown. The $500-cost, $1,500-$2,000-price example is an illustration, not a typical result; margins shrink fast if contractor quality slips and clients leave. Gadzhi built much of his fame selling SMMA courses (Agency Navigator, Agency Accelerator), and in this video he uses the agency model as a stepping stone toward digital products on Whop, a platform he co-owns.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
