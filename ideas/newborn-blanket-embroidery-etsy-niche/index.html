Personalized Newborn Blankets on Etsy

Personalized newborn blanket embroidery micro-niche on Etsy

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (print-on-demand embroidery via Shopify, no machine required to start)

What It Is

Sell personalized embroidered baby blankets in a focused micro-niche on Etsy, using a print-on-demand embroidery service (via Shopify) rather than an embroidery machine, syncing the store to Etsy, Amazon, and Instagram, with designs and fonts sourced from tools like Canva.

The Pitch

The presenter points to three real Etsy stores in this exact niche: one making over $44,000/month, one over $24,000/month, and a third, only 3 months old, already over $5,000/month, all selling embroidered baby blankets.

Best For

Someone drawn to a specific, narrow product niche within the broader baby/gift market, who wants to start without owning embroidery equipment by using a print-on-demand fulfillment service instead.

The Truth

Micro-niches like this can genuinely support real revenue, as the cited store examples suggest, and starting via print-on-demand removes the equipment barrier entirely. That said, these are the standout examples from a large marketplace, not typical results, and success likely depends heavily on design quality and marketing within a fairly specific, competitive gift category.
