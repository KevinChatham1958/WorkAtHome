Jewelry Cleaning Service

Ultrasonic jewelry cleaning service from home with drop-off and bulk shop orders

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (about $70 cleaner plus $10 cleaning liquid)

What It Is

You buy a small ultrasonic cleaner (about $70), fill it with water and cleaning solution, and clean rings, chains, watches, glasses and coins in about three minutes each. The creator suggests charging $5 per piece or $20+ for a bag of jewelry. Customers come from neighbors and local Facebook groups, and you can offer bulk cleaning to eyeglass shops, pawn shops and small jewelry stores.

The Pitch

The creator says a bottle of solution lasts dozens of jobs, so nearly everything earned is profit, and 15 customers paying $10 over a weekend pays back the machine. He says shops like it because their stock looks better with no effort on their part.

Who It's Best For

People wanting a tiny, low-risk local side income who are comfortable promoting themselves in their community.

The Truth (as we see it)

Ultrasonic cleaners do work and are cheap, but many jewelry stores already clean customers' pieces for free, which limits what people will pay. The creator's warning about pearls, opals and soft stones is important; damaging a customer's item is a real risk, so insurance or clear terms help. The weekend earnings example is illustrative, not a reported result. The creator sells a paid 'roadmap' guide for starting a home-based machine business (supplier info, budgets, calculators, outreach templates), linked in the description.
