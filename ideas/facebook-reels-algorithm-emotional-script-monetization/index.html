Facebook Page Growth Formula

Growing a Facebook page to monetization using keyword-optimized page setup and an emotion-structured Reels script formula

Content & personal brand · Low (a keyword research tool subscription, an AI chatbot subscription)

E'Calm · How I Make $1,024,937 In 90 Days Posting Facebook Reels (Full System) ↗

What It Is

Set up a Facebook page optimized for discovery: research high-search-volume keywords in your niche using a keyword tool (VidIQ), generate unique page name options combining your name with those keywords via an AI chatbot, verify the final keyword choice has high search volume but low competition, complete the page bio, and set the page type to 'Creator.' Structure every video script around four beats: a scroll-stopping 3-second hook, a message delivering real value, a 'metaphor' that makes the concept visually sticky enough to rewatch or screenshot, and a looping conclusion — since the presenter argues Facebook's algorithm rewards likes, comments, saves, and shares rather than watch-through completion. Post at least 3-5 times a day, and grow organically rather than through paid ads.

The Pitch

The presenter shows real, live (not screenshotted) dashboard data across three consecutive months on his main page: $49,000 in April, $37,000 in May, and $43,000 in June from content monetization alone, plus a second page earning $19,000-21,000/month, attributing results to an audience that's 95% from top-ad-rate countries.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to post consistently multiple times a day and comfortable structuring scripts around a specific emotional/narrative formula.

The Truth (as we see it)

It's important to flag clearly: the video's own title claims '$1,024,937 In 90 Days,' but that exact figure is never shown, explained, or connected to any data anywhere in the actual video — the real, live-demonstrated dashboard figures total roughly $129,000 across three months on the main page, a genuine and still-substantial result, but nowhere close to the headline, which reads as unsubstantiated title exaggeration. Facebook's Reels monetization program and its engagement-based reward structure are real and accurately described, and showing a live dashboard rather than a static screenshot is more credible than most similar videos, which makes the title/content gap even more worth noting.
