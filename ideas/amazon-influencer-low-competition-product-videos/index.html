Amazon Influencer Videos From Your Orders

Finding low-competition, high-revenue products for Amazon Influencer videos using your own past Amazon order history

Content & personal brand · Low (free Amazon Influencer Program signup; a product-research tool like ViralVue)

Originally published Sep 6, 2026 — Greg Gottfried

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Gottfried · How I Built 10 Streams Of Income In My 20's ↗

One of 9 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Join the Amazon Influencer Program and create short review videos of products you already own, which can then surface in a product's own listing page under 'Amazon Influencer Videos,' earning a commission when a viewer purchases after watching; rather than guessing which products to review, use a research tool that scans your own Amazon order history and ranks past purchases by opportunity (high monthly product revenue combined with very few existing competing Influencer videos), surfacing genuinely low-competition, high-traffic products to review first.

The Pitch

The presenter, who started this stream just a couple months before the video, reports making about $70 in profit the first month doing general reviews, then jumping to roughly $1,400 in profit the second month after using the research tool to target specific low-competition, high-revenue products (citing real examples found this way selling $20,000-75,000/month in product revenue with only one to three competing videos).

Best For

Someone with an existing Amazon purchase history to draw from, comfortable making brief on-camera or voiceover product reviews rather than needing a full YouTube channel or website.

The Truth

The Amazon Influencer Program and its video-placement mechanics on product pages are real and accurately described, and the specific before/after progression ($70 to $1,400) is a concrete, plausible self-reported result tied to a clear tactical change (using the research tool) rather than a vague claim. The presenter explicitly says he can't guarantee similar results for viewers.
