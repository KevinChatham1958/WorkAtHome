Semi-Automated Print-on-Demand

Semi-automated print-on-demand apparel using Claude Design and an AI storefront builder

  • Low startup cost

Joshua Mayo · The NEW Claude AI Side Hustle Nobody's Talking About ↗

What It Is

A semi-automated print-on-demand apparel business built around simple, repeatable text-based t-shirt designs, using Claude Design to build a custom bulk-design-generator tool, an AI website builder for a standalone storefront, and Printful for automated fulfillment.

The Pitch

The presenter argues simple text-based shirt stores succeed through volume, hundreds or thousands of listings, rather than one viral design, and shows how Claude's design mode can build a working custom software tool, not just generate individual images, that bulk-produces dozens of on-brand shirt designs from a template phrase across many niches at once. Also demonstrates using an AI website builder to build a complete, functional e-commerce storefront from a single prompt, connecting Printful for automated fulfillment to both that storefront and an Etsy shop simultaneously, and using Claude to reverse-engineer SEO-optimized titles and tags from already-successful competitor listings.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to build and maintain a large volume of simple listings rather than expecting a few designs to succeed, and comfortable stitching together several different tools into one connected workflow.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is explicit this is not a get-rich-quick scheme and still requires consistency, good design taste, and patience, a more grounded framing than some AI-business pitches. No personal revenue figures are given for the presenter's own store using this specific method; the examples of successful stores shown are third-party Etsy listings, not the presenter's own results.
