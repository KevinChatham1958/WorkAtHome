GoHighLevel Agency for Government

GoHighLevel digital marketing agency targeting government contracts

What It Is

Build a digital marketing agency on the GoHighLevel platform starting with local small-business clients to build real, provable results, then use those results to register as a government contractor and bid on federal, state, and county marketing contracts, which Bonds says routinely pay far more than equivalent private-sector work with much less competition.

The Pitch

Bonds cites a specific example from a fellow marketer, Tim: a $25,000-a-month, $300,000-a-year government digital marketing contract that only had two competing bidders, for essentially the same dashboard-reporting service Bonds sells local clients for $500 a month. Her core argument for why government contracts are less competitive is structural: government agencies have to spend their allocated marketing budgets by the end of the fiscal year or lose them, so there's real pressure to find and pay a vendor, and once a budget is posted, there's no cold-calling or price negotiation involved, you either bid or you don't.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to start with the unglamorous work of landing and delivering for local clients first, to build a real track record, before attempting the more involved process of registering as and winning business as a government contractor.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is explicitly a documented-in-progress journey, not a completed case study: Bonds discloses she's only a couple of months into running her own agency and is currently being walked through government-contractor registration by Tim and his team, she has not personally landed a government contract at the time of this video. The video also funnels into a paid workshop with her coach and a waitlist for a future interview with Tim, worth knowing given how much of the pitch rests on Tim's specific $300,000 contract story rather than Bonds' own verified results.
