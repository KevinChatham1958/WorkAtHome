Local Business Social Post Batches

Batches of 30 branded social posts for local businesses (ChatGPT spreadsheet + Canva Bulk Create)

What It Is

You pick a local business such as a barbershop and have ChatGPT write a 30-row spreadsheet of post ideas with image prompts, headlines, captions and hashtags, downloaded as a CSV. You generate the images in Canva's Magic Media, build one branded template with the shop's name, address and phone, then use Canva's Bulk Create to produce all 30 matching posts in one click. You sell the batch to the business as a month of social content. The creator puts the going rate for a batch of 30 custom posts at about $70 to $80, based on Fiverr pricing tiers.

The Pitch

The creator says nearly every town has businesses like barbershops that need posts and aren't making any, and that once the template exists each new batch takes little time. He says one $70 job covers Canva Pro for about four months. He suggests choosing businesses nearby or in your own country so communication is easy.

Who It’s Best For

Beginners with some design sense who are comfortable approaching local business owners and want a low-cost service to start with.

The Truth (as we see it)

The workflow is concrete and the Bulk Create steps are accurate as described, including the honest note that the feature needs paid Canva Pro. The $70 to $80 price is a marketplace estimate, not a track record, and at that price you need many clients to earn meaningful money. The video shows how to make the posts but says little about finding and closing clients, which is the harder part. AI-made generic posts are easy for competitors to copy, so personal service and local knowledge will matter more than the tool.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
