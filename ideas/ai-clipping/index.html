AI Clipping for Creators

What It Is

Cut creators' long-form videos into short clips using AI editing tools, post them to TikTok and Reels, and get paid per view through marketplaces like Content Rewards.

The Pitch

Big creators have hours of unused footage sitting around, and short clips are the easiest way for them to reach new fans, so they'll pay you for posting clips that gain traction.

Who It's Best For

Someone looking for the lowest possible barrier to entry, more as a way to learn content and AI tools than as a long-term income plan.

The Truth (as we see it)

Tilbury is direct about this one: competition is very high, because it's this easy to start, everyone jumps in. Most people quit within a week or two, so simple consistency puts you ahead of most, but the ceiling on income is modest.
