Seasonal Holiday Affiliate Pages

Seasonal Halloween affiliate pages targeting long-tail costume and decor searches, boosted with press releases

Marketing & content · Low startup cost (a simple website; optional paid press releases at about $100-600 each)

Originally published Oct 16, 2026 — affiliatemarketingmc

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

affiliatemarketingmc · Fastest Ways To Make Money With Affiliate Marketing? ↗

What It Is

You use Google Trends and keyword tools to find specific, lower-volume searches that spike before a holiday (such as a particular costume or 'last-minute group costumes for six adults'), build one focused page per problem, and link to the matching affiliate offer: Amazon or Spirit Halloween for costumes, Home Depot or higher-paying decor offers for decorations. You boost traffic with Pinterest, short videos and a newsworthy press release.

The Pitch

The creator, an affiliate marketer since 1999, says Halloween searches jump hundreds of percent and friends of his have made a full-time living from Halloween offers alone. He suggests pairing low-commission costume links (about 3-5%) with higher-payout offers, such as a decor item paying $50.

Who It's Best For

Someone patient with keyword research and writing who plans ahead for seasonal spikes.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator repeatedly says most affiliates make nothing and that $10,000 in two weeks is possible but improbable: it would take about 1,800 Amazon costume sales. Pages need time to rank, so starting weeks before the holiday is late; he recommends building for next year. Follow trademark and affiliate rules (such as Amazon's link rules) and disclose affiliate links. He offers paid notes and reports at his own site.
