The TAP Framework for Validating Digital Product Ideas, Plus comment-to-DM Automation

Tanya · How to Sell Digital Products Online (FULL TUTORIAL AFTER $11M IN SALES) ↗

What It Is

A three-part idea-validation test for a digital product before building it, Trouble (does the audience have a big enough pain point), Appetite (do they actually want a solution enough to pay for it), and Purchasing Power (can they actually afford it), paired with a specific client-acquisition method: post a short video with a comment-triggered keyword call to action ("comment MEAL PLAN and I'll send you the details"), then use a chatbot automation tool to capture the commenter's contact details, add them to an email list, and automatically deliver the product plus a follow-up email sequence.

The Pitch

The comment-to-DM automation technique is presented as a lower-cost alternative to tools like ManyChat, which can get expensive as a contact list grows, folded instead into an all-in-one platform. The presenter also outlines seven digital product formats with rough price and creation-time ranges (from $14-47 digital downloads taking a few hours to make, up to $500-1,000 coaching packages taking about a week to set up), giving a rough map of effort versus price point across formats.

Best For

Someone who wants a structured way to pressure-test a digital product idea before investing time building it, and who's comfortable using short-form video with a clear call to action to drive leads.

The Truth

Worth knowing directly: this video is essentially a full demonstration of the presenter's own paid all-in-one platform (Digital Creator Studio), with every step, idea generation, validation, pricing, and the DM automation itself, shown running inside that specific tool, and the presenter openly says "I created the tool, I'm biased." The underlying TAP validation questions and the comment-to-DM marketing concept are genuinely tool-agnostic and could be replicated with other chatbot or CRM tools, but this entry overlaps significantly with other digital-product idea-validation and pricing frameworks already covered elsewhere in this database.
