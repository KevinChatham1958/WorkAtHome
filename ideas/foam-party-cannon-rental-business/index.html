Foam Party Rentals

Running a mobile foam-party rental business with a professional foam cannon

Local & professional services · Low (a foam cannon setup runs about $1,500; needs only a standard electrical outlet and water source nearby)

What It Is

Buy a professional foam cannon setup, book backyard party events, and turn a yard into a bubble party for a couple of hours per booking using just a hose hookup and an electrical outlet; scale by running multiple cannon setups across simultaneous events.

The Pitch

The presenter cites Tim Carstensen, an elementary PE teacher with no business background who turned a foam cannon into a $150,000 side hustle in one year, working roughly 4 months out of the year around his teaching job and scaling from one cannon to nine simultaneous setups. Package pricing sits around $450-$550 for a 2-hour party, so the machine can pay for itself after the first weekend of bookings.

Best For

Someone with weekends/summers available for event work, comfortable transporting and setting up equipment at different locations, in a market that doesn't already have this service established.

The Truth

The low equipment/licensing barrier and event-based pricing described are plausible and match how novelty party-rental businesses typically operate. The $150,000/year figure is one presenter-cited example, and the video's own advice to keep backup equipment (after a mid-event cannon failure) signals a real operational risk to plan for; demand and pricing will vary heavily by local market and season.
