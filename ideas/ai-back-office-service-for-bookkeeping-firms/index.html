AI Back Office for Bookkeepers

AI-agent back-office service for small bookkeeping firms (month-end cleanup, document chasing)

What It Is

Pick one industry, such as small bookkeeping firms, and sell them one annoying job done with AI agents: cleaning up month-end for their messiest clients or chasing clients for missing documents. You run an intake agent, a preparer agent and a reviewer agent, and a person approves every result before it goes out. Each firm you serve teaches you how the business works, and the creator presents it as the path to eventually buying one of those firms.

The Pitch

The creator says doing real work for many firms over 6-12 months builds both reliable agents and trust with owners, so when one wants to retire you are the obvious buyer. He argues AI drafts are now good enough that checking work takes far less time than doing it.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with AI tools and basic bookkeeping concepts who can win and keep business clients.

The Truth (as we see it)

Getting the first firms will take outreach and a sales conversation, and firms will want strong accuracy and data-security assurances. Keeping a human approval step, as the creator insists, matters for client trust and liability. The approach is described in outline rather than with pricing or client numbers. No paid product is promoted.
