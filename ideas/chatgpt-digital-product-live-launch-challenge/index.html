London Leffler · I Tried Selling a Digital Product With ChatGPT to Make $1,000 in 7 Days ↗

What It Is

A fully documented, real-time attempt to build and sell a $180 digital guide from scratch: using ChatGPT for market research and niche selection, Codex (ChatGPT's coding-focused tool) to turn a rough AI-generated PDF into something genuinely worth the asking price, Lovable to build a Shopify-connected website in a few hours, an AI UGC-ad tool (Arc Ads) to generate spokesperson-style video ads, and paid Meta and LinkedIn ads to drive traffic.

The Pitch

Rather than a polished after-the-fact success story, this is shown warts and all across eight real days: zero sales on day one and two of running ads despite good click-through rates, then a pivot (dropping the price from $180 to $80, rebuilding the site specifically for mobile after noticing most traffic was mobile) that turned the campaign profitable, hitting a 6.25 return on ad spend on the best day. The specific ad-performance metrics tracked throughout (CPC, CAC, ROAS, checkout completion rate) give a genuinely concrete picture of what running a real paid campaign looks like day to day, not just the end result.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable spending real ad money and willing to iterate based on data (price changes, mobile optimization) rather than expecting the first version of a product or ad to work.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is one of the more transparent entries of its kind: the presenter openly states he missed his stated goal ($1,000 in 7 days), shows the exact math behind a genuine failure period, and documents the specific changes that eventually made the campaign profitable, a stronger honesty signal than most similarly-framed challenge videos. Total profit over the documented period was $319, a modest but real, fully itemized result rather than a projected or rounded-up figure.
