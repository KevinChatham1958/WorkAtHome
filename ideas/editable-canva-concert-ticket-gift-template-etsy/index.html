What It Is

You design an editable Canva template of a concert ticket that buyers personalize themselves (often as a gift or surprise reveal) and sell the template link as an Etsy download. Because buyers edit it themselves, there's no custom work per order. The creator found a listing estimated at $538 a month; the seller's whole shop, about a year and a half old, was estimated at $1,096 a month.

The Pitch

The creator says $538 a month from a template that takes about an hour to make is "really decent revenue," and that it's genuinely passive because the seller doesn't customize anything. She notes the seller uses Etsy's optional personalization box only to invite questions, which may help visibility.

Who It's Best For

Beginners comfortable in Canva who want small, quick-to-make template products.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a smaller earner, and the creator thinks the shop's spread-out product mix holds it back, so focusing on one type of customer matters. Template products are easy to copy and Canva template rules apply to what you can sell. Expect occasional buyer messages asking for editing help. Revenue figures come from Profit Tree's estimates, not the sellers' own records. The creator uses an affiliate link for Profit Tree (50% off a year of its pro plan), sells Canva templates herself, and promotes her own Etsy launch videos.
