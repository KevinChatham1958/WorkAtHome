Relationship-First Review Automation

Low-tech, relationship-first Google review automation for local businesses

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (GoHighLevel subscription)

Kevin Patrick · This Father of 2 Built a $20k/mo GHL SaaS with a Full Time Job... ↗

What It Is

A simple, single-offer GoHighLevel service (mainly automated Google review requests and reputation management, occasionally paired with website fixes) sold to local home-service businesses through deliberately low-tech, personal outreach: brief cold calls fit into small pockets of a full-time work day, a follow-up Loom screen-recording sent by text and Facebook Messenger showing specific, personalized fixes for that business's Google profile, and physical mail (printed Google review cards, a box of brownies after a client signs up).

The Pitch

The presenter built this to $20,000 a month in recurring revenue while working a full-time teaching job, using roughly one to two hours of actual prospecting a week, mostly cold calls squeezed into a car during school drop-off and pickup windows. He's candid that he still doesn't know GoHighLevel's more advanced features well, relying on a paid support service for anything technical, and that his real edge is treating every call like a genuine conversation between two homeowners and neighbors rather than a sales pitch, including deliberately mentioning his own kids, local landmarks, and other local businesses he's worked with to build quick trust.

Who It's Best For

Someone with very limited available hours (a side hustle around a full-time job or family responsibilities) who's comfortable with brief, personal phone conversations and physical follow-up (mailing cards, remembering personal details) rather than a highly automated or technical approach.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific numbers given (roughly 40 clients this year, 10 cold calls a week producing 2-4 good conversations and 1-3 new clients a month) are concrete and specific to a real, ongoing part-time business, a good transparency signal. This uses the same GoHighLevel platform as other entries in this database; the distinguishing element here isn't the technology but the deliberately low-tech, high-personal-touch sales approach and the very limited weekly time commitment.
