AI Voice Agents for Local Businesses

AI voice agents for small local businesses

What It Is

Building and installing AI voice agents for small local businesses, plumbers, landscapers, restaurants, that handle inbound appointment booking and outbound confirmation calls, sold as a monthly service.

The Pitch

The guest argues voice AI has crossed a real threshold in the last couple of years, from noticeably laggy and awkward to fast and natural, making it a genuinely usable tool now rather than a future promise. He recommends charging $200 to $500 a month per small business client, using existing plug-and-play platforms rather than building from scratch, and closing the sale by calculating the exact dollar value of missed calls for that specific business, missed calls per day, times close rate, times average job size, before mentioning price.

Best For

Someone comfortable with basic sales conversations with small business owners, since the actual technical setup is accessible via existing no-code platforms rather than requiring deep technical skill.

The Truth

This is real-time podcast brainstorming, not a personally run case study from either host. The guest cites that he personally knows several people charging in this range, but no account or client verification is shown. The core argument, that AI voice agents have recently become genuinely usable and that missed-call recovery is easy to justify with simple math, is a reasonable and internally consistent argument.
