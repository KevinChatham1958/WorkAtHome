Truck Driver Recruiting

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (ad spend only)

Originally published Aug 14, 2026 — Johnny & Sergio

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Johnny & Sergio · 9 One-Person Businesses With More Demand Than Competition ↗

One of 5 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Run Facebook ads to find qualified CDL drivers and owner-operators looking for better freight or routes, vet them for a valid license and clean record, then place them with trucking companies for a per-driver placement fee.

The Pitch

Trucking companies lose real money, cited at around $4,000 a month per truck, for every truck sitting empty without a qualified driver, which is why carriers will pay a placement fee of up to $4,000 for every qualified driver who gets hired and stays on. The presenters describe this as one of the purest "laptop style" businesses on their list, since you never touch a truck, own a truck, or need labor, inventory, or a lease, just a Facebook ad account.

Best For

Someone comfortable running paid ads and doing consistent outreach, since this is a placement/staffing model rather than a subscription, meaning you're always selling and refilling the pipeline, and a slow month directly hits revenue.

The Truth

The presenters are candid that this ranks mid-list because of that staffing-model instability, not because the underlying opportunity is weak, calling it stressful when the pipeline gets thin. The $4,000-per-truck and per-placement figures come from a source the presenter says they spoke with directly, a specific and checkable-in-principle number, though it's a single secondhand account rather than an audited industry statistic.
