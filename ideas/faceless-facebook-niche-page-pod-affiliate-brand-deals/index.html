Faceless Facebook Niche Page

Faceless Facebook niche page monetized with payouts, print-on-demand, affiliates and brand deals

Content & personal brand · Near-zero cost (free tools like Canva; print-on-demand has no upfront inventory)

Originally published Oct 16, 2026 — Anji Martin

Anji Martin · Monetize a Facebook Page FAST in 2027 (7 Step Guide) ↗

What It Is

You start a Facebook page about one interest (the example is a dog-lover page) after studying five to ten successful pages in that niche. You post three times a day at first and build up to six to eight posts a day, mixing memes, heartwarming content, tips and engagement questions such as "Does your dog sleep in your bed?", and you manage it all through Meta Business Suite. Money comes from Facebook's content monetization program, which you opt into, plus print-on-demand products made through Printify (stickers, engraved pet tags), affiliate links to products the audience uses, brand sponsorships, and simple digital products made in Canva.

The Pitch

The creator says she grew her own theme page to 100,000 followers in three months and over 300,000 within a year, and that this is her full-time income. She says some students got monetized at about 1,000 followers and some received brand offers at about 4,000. Her main advice is not to rely only on Facebook payouts but to build several income streams on top of the audience.

Who It's Best For

People who enjoy a hobby or interest enough to post about it many times a day and want a faceless business with low startup cost.

The Truth (as we see it)

The step-by-step method is reasonable and her warning against depending only on Facebook's payouts is sound, since platform payouts and reach can change without notice. Her follower and income results, and her students', are her own unaudited claims, and she says income is not guaranteed. Facebook's monetization program is not open in every country and acceptance can take a long time. Brand deals usually mean pitching companies yourself until they start coming to you. She promotes her own paid course and live mentorship community, and mentions she has worked with Printify since 2018.
