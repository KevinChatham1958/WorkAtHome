Microgreens for Restaurants

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (about $500-700 for a garage-scale rack with grow lights and shelving)

What It Is

Grow microgreens (sunflower, pea shoot, amaranth) in trays under grow lights in a garage or spare room, selling on standing weekly wholesale orders to two or three restaurant accounts rather than trying to build a broad consumer customer base.

The Pitch

Heavy-duty 10x20 trays start around $15, and a full garage-scale rack with shelving and grow lights runs $500-700. Real cost per tray (seed, medium, power, water, packaging, and labor at $20/hour) comes to $5-7. Sunflower trays retail $18-22, pea shoots $16-20, amaranth $25-35, with margins running 66-79%. Growers target $25-40/tray wholesale — 10 trays/week nets roughly $108, 50 trays/week around $490. The presenter warns that overwatering or dense sowing causes mold that can wipe out an entire tray (and miss a delivery) in a single afternoon.

Who It's Best For

Someone with reliable indoor growing space who's willing to build 2-3 solid restaurant relationships and treat consistent delivery as the core of the business rather than trying to sell broadly to consumers.

The Truth (as we see it)

The margins are genuinely attractive and the space/equipment requirements are modest, but the presenter is honest that the failure mode is both fast and total — a single contaminated tray from overwatering can take out an entire week's crop and cost you the account, so this rewards careful, consistent process more than volume.
