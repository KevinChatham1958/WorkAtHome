AI English-Practice YouTube Channels

What It Is

Use free AI tools to make simple videos of two AI-generated characters having an English-language conversation (with on-screen text), aimed at people learning English, then monetize through YouTube ad revenue and optional fan-funding features like memberships.

The Pitch

The entire production process is free: an AI chatbot writes the script, Google's free Flow tool generates the character images, and Microsoft's free Clip Champ generates AI voices and adds captions. Sarah cites one channel under four months old that gained 2.4 million views and over 90,000 subscribers in a single month, and confirms it's a monetized, revenue-generating channel because it has an active paid-membership feature (which YouTube only enables for monetized channels).

Best For

Someone comfortable being fully hands-off from the finished product's originality, since the format is intentionally simple and repeatable, and who's willing to treat this as a content-volume game rather than a single-video bet.

The Truth (as we see it)

The cited channel's actual monthly income isn't shown directly, it's estimated using YouTube's typical $3-7 per 1,000 views ad rate (RPM), which is a reasonable industry-standard estimate but not a confirmed number from the channel owner. The inference that the channel is monetized (based on it having membership features) is solid reasoning, clearly explained, rather than a guess presented as fact.
