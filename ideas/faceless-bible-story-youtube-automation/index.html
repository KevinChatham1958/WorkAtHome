AI Bible Story Videos

Creating faceless AI-generated Bible story videos for YouTube, monetized through AdSense, affiliate links, and digital products

Profit Stacking Club · How to Make Money Online Using The Bible - Without Guilt ↗

What It Is

Use ChatGPT to generate a list of Bible stories well-suited to short, 2-minute YouTube videos, then write an emotional, relatable script with a strong opening line and inspiring takeaway; feed that script into an all-in-one AI video generation tool (handling voiceover, visuals, subtitles, and music together), choosing a visual style and generating both horizontal and vertical versions to cross-post to Shorts/Instagram/TikTok. As a faster alternative, find an already-successful video on the same Bible story, paste its link into the tool's reference-video workflow, and let it generate a fresh, differently-styled version based on the same concept rather than a word-for-word copy.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a real, named faceless Bible-content channel pulling almost a million views a month without daily posting, making $5,000-10,000/month, and stresses three monetization layers beyond AdSense: affiliate links to the tools used plus Christian retailers, and eventually the creator's own digital products like scripture cards or printable journals.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable directing an AI video tool through prompts and choices rather than filming/editing manually, willing to layer multiple income sources rather than waiting on AdSense alone.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI video generation tools with the described capabilities are real and technically accurate as demonstrated live, and the cited channel's view counts support the core opportunity. The presenter's monetization figures for that other channel are relayed claims, not independently verified, and the reference-video generation method produces content closely following another creator's existing video's structure, worth being aware of even though the tool markets this as a legitimate feature.
