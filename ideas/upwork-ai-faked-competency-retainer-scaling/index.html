Upwork Retainers With AI

Landing Upwork retainer clients in a skill you don't have, using AI to fake and then actually deliver the competency

Local & professional services · Very low (a ChatGPT/Claude subscription, an Upwork account, Loom for video pitches)

Originally published Aug 2, 2026 — Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · How a Mom of 3 Makes $50K a Month With AI (No Experience) ↗

What It Is

Apply to Upwork jobs in a skill you don't actually have (e.g. SEO) by slightly tilting away from the most saturated keyword phrasing, record a short (2-3 minute) unedited Loom video pitching how you'd approach the client's problem using an AI-built workflow, win the retainer, then actually deliver the work by learning the skill just-in-time with AI tools (Claude Code for automation build-out), and increase the retainer price once results are proven by expanding scope.

The Pitch

The interview subject describes going from severe poverty to a $3,000/month Upwork retainer (later raised to $5,500/month with the same client) after just 7 job applications and zero prior SEO experience, using ChatGPT to build the actual automation and a confident, unedited Loom pitch video to win trust. She expanded into content creation, a paid community, sponsorships, and a self-built app as additional income layers once inbound interest picked up from her YouTube channel.

Best For

Someone comfortable pitching themselves into work outside their existing skillset and willing to learn just-in-time with AI tools under real client pressure; helps to have some video-comfort for the Loom pitch step.

The Truth

AI genuinely lowers the bar to producing passable client deliverables fast, and Upwork niche-tilting to reduce competition is a real, sound tactic. Most of this video is the subject's personal mindset framing and multiple loosely-connected income streams rather than a single step-by-step process, and her reported income figures and timeline are self-reported and not independently verified — treat this as one real example of the method rather than a guaranteed outcome.
