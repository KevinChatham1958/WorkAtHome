AI Sales Prospecting Service

What It Is

Feed Claude a prospect and a specific trigger event, a funding round, a new hire, a relevant post, and have it draft a short, non-pitchy outreach message that references the trigger directly, so a sales team spends less time hunting for leads and writing near-identical cold messages and more time on actual conversations.

The Pitch

Hawksworth's pitch is that this doesn't replace a sales team, it removes the boring research and first-draft-writing work that eats their week, while keeping the human conversation, which he argues is what actually closes deals, intact. He suggests charging around £1,500 to set the system up and £750 a month to keep it running and the CRM organized.

Who It's Best For

Someone selling into businesses that already have a sales team or process in place and just need the research and outreach-drafting work taken off their plate, rather than businesses with no sales process at all.

The Truth (as we see it)

The pricing figures (£1,500 setup, £750/month) are the presenter's own suggested starting points, not documented client outcomes. This kind of trigger-based, one-prospect-at-a-time personalization is a specific tactic within the broader AI-powered sales space that shows up across a lot of WAH creator content, and the underlying mechanic, feeding Claude a single prospect and trigger, is simple enough to test yourself before paying for training on it.
