Short-Form Video Editor

Short-form video editor for creators using mobile apps (CapCut, InShot)

Marketing & content · Near-zero cost (CapCut free tier or InShot)

What It Is

You edit creators' raw footage into YouTube Shorts, Reels and TikToks with clean cuts, captions and transitions using phone apps like CapCut and InShot. The creator says editors charge about $15-50 per short or $200-600 a month for a fixed package of clips. To get clients, you find creators who post publicly and send them a few seconds of their own footage edited better.

The Pitch

The creator says millions of people can talk to a camera but few want to edit, and short-form platforms demand a constant stream of clips, so creators hire help. He says speed and consistency matter more than brilliance: creators prefer an average editor who delivers on time.

Who It's Best For

Detail-minded people with a sense of rhythm who can deliver on a dependable schedule.

The Truth (as we see it)

Short-form editing is a real freelance market with plenty of demand, but also many competing editors, so low early rates are common. Sending unrequested sample edits is a cold pitch and works best when done thoughtfully. The prices given are labeled as estimates. No paid product is promoted.
