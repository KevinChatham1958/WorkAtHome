Selling a Cheap 'filter' Digital Product Paired With a High-touch Upsell of the Same Solution

Digital & subscription products · Very low (a free platform plan covering a few products with no credit card, taking a percentage of sales; a paid tier needed for upsells)

Jillian Leslie · This AI Prompt Turns a $27 Product Into $7,579/Month (Copy & Paste) ↗

What It Is

Sell a narrow $17-37 digital product ('filter product') that solves one specific problem, using the sale itself to identify buyers who are willing to pay for that solution, then offer a $297-997 higher-touch version of the exact same solution (adding access, customization, accountability, or done-with-you/done-for-you help) as an upsell on the thank-you page immediately after purchase; use an AI prompt to generate both offer descriptions together and score the idea on urgency, willingness to pay, and ease of creation.

The Pitch

The presenter argues a $27 product alone is a 'treadmill' needing about 370 sales/month for $10K, but reframes it as a customer-qualification filter: a buyer has proven real intent to pay, making them the right audience for a much higher-priced version of the same solution. She demonstrates the full setup live on her own platform, walking through the filter product, the AI-generated sales page, and the thank-you-page upsell.

Best For

Someone who already has (or can create) a narrow, specific digital product and is willing to design a higher-touch version of the same solution rather than build a second, unrelated product.

The Truth

The 'filter product plus higher-touch upsell of the same solution' structure is a real, sound customer-ladder concept, and the advice to sell the high-ticket offer before building it out is standard, validated lean-startup practice. The presenter is the co-founder of the platform being demonstrated, so the tool walkthrough doubles as a promotion for her own product, and no independently-verified case study of the full result is shown for any real business.
