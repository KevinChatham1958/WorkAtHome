AI Google Review System

Selling an AI-automated Google review request and reply system to local businesses

Jarid Orgeron · NEW $500/Day Google Review Software (Work From Home) ↗

What It Is

Set up a GoHighLevel-based system that connects to a client's Google Business Profile to automatically request reviews after a sale, send drip-paced review requests to avoid unnatural review velocity, and have an AI agent auto-reply to incoming reviews using a configured brand voice, with guardrails against risky language and holding sensitive/negative reviews for human review; sell this on the value proposition of winning the click in local search results, not just ranking higher.

The Pitch

The presenter, an online seller since 2003 and AI-agency operator since 2023, argues most agency pitches aren't things business owners wake up wanting, but nearly every business owner wants more five-star reviews, and demonstrates the full AI-agent setup live, positioning it as a $500/day business without needing cold calling to get started.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable setting up and configuring a SaaS automation platform for clients and explaining the local-search value proposition to local business owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core insight, that review count and velocity affect click-through and local ranking, is grounded in real local-SEO understanding, and the guardrails described for AI-generated review replies reflect real operational risk awareness. The presenter's own $500/day claim is not demonstrated with verified results in this video, and it promotes his own paid-adjacent free community and software affiliate link.
