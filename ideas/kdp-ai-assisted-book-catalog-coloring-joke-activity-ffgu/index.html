AI-Assisted KDP Book Catalog

Amazon KDP book catalog built with AI help (coloring, joke, puzzle, activity books and journals)

What It Is

You publish simple books through Amazon KDP, Amazon's free self-publishing program, by uploading a PDF manuscript and cover. The creator points to formats that need no credentials, such as kids' coloring books, activity and puzzle books, dad-joke books, trivia books, journals and trackers. Your daily work is coming up with book ideas, checking that similar books sell, making the book with AI speeding up the writing, and publishing under a pen name. Amazon handles printing, shipping, payments and customer service, and you earn royalties per copy; the plan is to build a growing library one quality book at a time.

The Pitch

The creator says the model is 'too boring to go viral', so competition stays lower than with dropshipping or social media, and Amazon brings millions of ready buyers so you do not have to build an audience. He says it costs nothing to start, needs no face or credentials, and one idea can become an ebook, paperback and audiobook.

Who It’s Best For

Beginners who want a low-risk, faceless project and enjoy creative work like designing puzzles or coloring pages. It suits people who can keep publishing for months before judging results.

The Truth (as we see it)

The basic model is real and widely used, but the video gives almost no detail on how to tell whether a topic will sell; the creator says that part is covered on a free 30-minute call that leads to his paid coaching program. His self-made millionaire claim and student results are his own and unaudited. Low-content categories such as coloring and puzzle books are very crowded on Amazon, so quality and niche choice decide results, and many books sell only occasionally.
