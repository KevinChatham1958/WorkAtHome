Pay-per-lead Agency Built Around a Single Niche, Offer, and Traffic Source

Local & professional services · Needs ad budget (scales with lead volume)

Originally published Aug 27, 2026 — Billy Robinson

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Billy Robinson · Everything I'd Do to Build a $350K/Month Agency Again ↗

What It Is

Build a pay-per-lead agency that generates qualified leads (or calls, or booked appointments) for businesses in a single niche and sells them for a per-lead price, using one paid-traffic channel, the presenter's is Facebook ads, as the acquisition engine. The presenter frames a 'rule of five ones' as the operating discipline: one target market, one product or offer, one conversion tool, one traffic source, all aimed at reaching $1 million a year before adding any complexity.

The Pitch

The presenter says he built his own agency to $350,000 a month using this exact discipline, after wasting real time and money (he cites tens of thousands of dollars) trying to run multiple lead types and a second channel (cold email) at once early on. The core argument: picking one narrow lane and mastering it produces better unit economics, working backward from a revenue target to cost-per-lead, cost-per-client, and ad spend, than spreading across many offers or channels.

Best For

Someone comfortable picking one specific niche and one paid-traffic channel and sticking with it through the slow, unglamorous work of landing a first client, then a second, then scaling only what already works rather than chasing new ideas.

The Truth

The presenter shows a real screenshot of his own agency's monthly numbers as evidence and names specific member results from his paid 'Pay-Per-Lead Nation Pro' community, though these are self-reported wins from his own paying community, not independently audited. The video functions as a funnel into that paid community. The core operating framework itself (pick one niche, offer, channel, and traffic source, validate with a first client, then scale what works) is explained in enough real depth to be useful independent of the community pitch.
