Bookkeeping Without Certification

Offering monthly bookkeeping services to small businesses without needing to be a certified accountant

Local & professional services · Very low (a laptop and basic accounting software knowledge)

Originally published Jun 30, 2026 — David Explains Money

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

David Explains Money · 20 BORING BUSINESSES That Make Money While You Sleep ↗

One of 3 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Offer monthly bookkeeping services to small businesses that need basic financial organization (not complex audits), charging $500-1,500/month per client; the presenter's math shows 10 clients at $1,000/month each generates $10,000/month with no office or employees needed.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as selling peace of mind rather than a credential, arguing small business owners hate bookkeeping and will pay to make the task disappear regardless of whether the provider is a certified accountant.

Who It's Best For

Someone with basic accounting/organizational skills comfortable working independently with multiple small-business clients on a recurring monthly basis.

The Truth (as we see it)

Bookkeeping-as-a-service for small businesses is a real, low-barrier recurring-revenue business, and the framing (selling relief from an annoying task, not certification) matches how many independent bookkeepers actually operate and market themselves. The specific $10,000/month example is illustrative math based on assumed client count and pricing, not a demonstrated case study.
