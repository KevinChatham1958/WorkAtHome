Selling AI-built (Lovable) Websites With a Rising Pricing Ladder

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (a Lovable subscription; the video itself promotes a $199-997 paid course as an add-on)

Originally published Aug 28, 2026 — Arlan Hamilton

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Arlan Hamilton · Change This ONE Thing & Sell Anything To Anyone ↗

What It Is

Build websites, landing pages, and sales pages for clients using the AI tool Lovable, and increase the average sale price over time as you gain experience and confidence — Hamilton's students reportedly start around $300 per site and move up to $500, $1,000, $1,500, and even $3,500 for a page-plus-maintenance package.

The Pitch

Hamilton frames the core sales lesson as shifting from "convincing" a prospect to "influencing" them by simply sharing that real people are making money this way, rather than pressuring anyone. She cites her own students' pricing progression as proof that the $300 starting point isn't a ceiling, and describes the AI tooling itself (Lovable) as now accessible enough that non-technical people can build something sellable within days, with the harder skill being the sales conversation itself.

Best For

Someone who's already comfortable with (or willing to learn) the mechanics of using Lovable to build sites, and who wants encouragement and a sales mindset shift more than new technical instruction.

The Truth

The pricing progression ($300 up to $1,000-3,500) is a genuinely useful data point for anyone already doing this kind of work, showing there's real room to raise prices as skill and confidence grow. That said, this video is largely built around promoting Hamilton's own paid "Lovable Income Challenge" course (priced at $199 in the video, with an explicit warning it's about to jump to $997), and the sales-philosophy framing ("influencing not convincing") is more of a mindset reframe than a concrete tactical technique — pair it with a more detailed how-to video (like her warm-network outreach method) for the actual mechanics of finding and closing clients.
