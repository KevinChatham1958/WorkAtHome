What It Is

You build a chatbot for a local business website that answers common visitor questions about hours, insurance, service areas and pricing, trained on the business's FAQ and price list. You use no-code tools such as Voiceflow or Botpress with Claude as the underlying model, charge a setup fee to put it live, and a monthly retainer to keep it updated.

The Pitch

The presenter says local businesses lose customers who visit the site after hours and get no answers, so a bot that responds at 9 p.m. on a Sunday solves a clear problem. He cites setup fees from about $500 up to $5,000 and monthly retainers of $500 to $2,500 per client. No coding is needed; it is mostly filling out forms in drag-and-drop tools. To start, pick one industry such as dentists, med spas or HVAC so you can reuse one base bot, build a working demo, and email 30 to 50 businesses a Loom screen recording of it with a proposed price.

Best For

People comfortable learning no-code tools and doing outreach, who are fine being on call for ongoing client updates.

The Truth

Website chatbots are an established service and the no-code platforms named are real. The fee ranges are from an unnamed source and the upper ends are optimistic for a beginner selling to small local businesses; many website builders now include chatbots, and many agencies sell this same service. The presenter notes the bot needs ongoing maintenance and clients on retainer will expect quick responses. Platform subscriptions and model usage costs are not discussed. No product is sold for this idea, though the video promotes his paid KDP mentorship.
