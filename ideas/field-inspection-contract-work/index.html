Field Inspection Contract Work

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (a smartphone and a car)

What It Is

Work as a contract field inspector for companies that need someone local to visit a property or business, take photographs (of a vehicle, building exterior, or specific condition), and submit them, typically for insurance, compliance, or verification purposes.

The Pitch

One presenter describes earning $75 for a roughly 20-minute inspection (a short drive plus photographing two company vehicles), working with a company called Metro Inspections. That specific company is no longer accepting new inspectors, but the presenter notes other similar inspection companies are actively hiring.

Best For

Someone with a reliable car and smartphone who wants flexible, quick-turnaround local gig work that doesn't require any specialized skill or equipment beyond basic photography and following instructions precisely.

The Truth

This is a genuinely low-barrier gig with fast per-job payouts, though per-job pay ($75 for 20 minutes in the example given) means meaningful income requires taking on a steady volume of jobs, and availability of inspection companies actively recruiting will vary by location and over time.
