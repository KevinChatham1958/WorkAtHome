$100 Thrift Resale Start

Starting resale with $100: buying underpriced items and selling them for more

What It Is

You use about $100 to buy items priced too low at thrift stores, on Facebook Marketplace or on clearance racks, then photograph, list and sell them for more. Profit comes from the gap between what you pay and what you sell for. You keep finding, buying and listing new items, because each sale empties your stock.

The Pitch

The creator says it trains a valuable eye for what things are worth and where others miss value, and can bring in quick cash starting today. He frames it as a learning stage rather than a lasting business.

Who It’s Best For

Beginners with little money who enjoy hunting for bargains and want quick, small wins.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator is frank that it builds no lasting asset: the income stops when you stop sourcing. The sourcing is done in person, and you store, pack and ship goods yourself. He gives no earnings figures, so this is an overview rather than a method. He ranks it lowest partly to set up his own number-one pick. He promotes his own paid program, Payments University, which teaches in-person payment-processing sales, in the video.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
