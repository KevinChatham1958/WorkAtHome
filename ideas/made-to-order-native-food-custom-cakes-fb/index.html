Made-to-Order Home Baking

Made-to-order Filipino native food and custom cakes sold through a Facebook page and groups

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (ingredients and basic baking supplies; a small rolling capital)

What It Is

You cook or bake to order at home and take orders through your own Facebook page and local Facebook groups. The creator suggests Filipino native foods such as puto, pancit and kakanin, plus customized and personalized cakes. You keep a small amount of capital for ingredients and turn it over as orders come in. Customers order ahead, and you prepare each batch for pickup or delivery.

The Pitch

The creator says it pays well if you have a passion for baking or cooking and that it works as extra income. She notes that only a small capital is needed and you can keep reinvesting it.

Who It's Best For

Good home cooks or bakers who can handle busy order days and have a local customer base reachable through Facebook.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling home-made food to order is a common, legitimate side business, and taking orders ahead keeps waste and upfront costs low. The creator gives no income figures and admits it is hard at the start and takes real time and effort when orders are large. Food made at home for sale may need permits or must follow local cottage-food and food-safety rules, so check what applies where you live. The video is a Filipino-language repost aimed at a Philippine audience, so market details may differ elsewhere.
