Automated Google-review Generation Service for Local Businesses

Local & professional services · ~$297/mo for a HighLevel software plan (covers unlimited clients)

Originally published Aug 26, 2026 — Adam Erhart

Share on Facebook Link copied

Adam Erhart · This ONE Offer Gets Clients to Say YES on the Spot ↗

What It Is

Sell local businesses one narrow service: an automated system that texts or emails every customer after a completed job asking for a Google review, plus a 'reactivation campaign' reaching a business's entire past-customer list for reviews they were never asked for. Pitched at a flat $297/month with a 'no new reviews, no pay' guarantee.

The Pitch

Built around a 'one-tool offer' framework: sell exactly one clearly-named result, guarantee the outcome to remove the buyer's downside, and remove all setup effort from the business owner. Cites the well-known 2000 jam-tasting choice-overload study and Kahneman's loss-aversion research as behavioral backing for a single narrow offer with a guarantee.

Best For

Someone comfortable selling a single, narrowly-defined service to local business owners, especially one who can offer a results-based guarantee to make the pitch an easy yes.

The Truth

The cited psychology research is real and widely known, though the jam study specifically has faced replication difficulties in follow-up research, so treat it as illustrative rather than settled proof of a guaranteed conversion lift. The underlying mechanic (automated review-request campaigns) is a real, commonly-sold local-marketing service. The recommended software platform is again the presenter's own affiliate link, and the price point is his suggested anchor, not a guaranteed market rate.
