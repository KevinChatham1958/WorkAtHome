One-person Digital-product Affiliate Business (lean, One-skill Model)

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (roughly $1,000-$2,000 to start, per the presenter)

Aaron Chen · This one person business is quietly making people millions in 2026 ↗

What It Is

A decision framework for picking a 'lazy'/easy one-person business over a 'hard' one: skip physical-product businesses (thin margins, must sell thousands of units), agencies (staffing, service delivery, deep expertise required), and capital-heavy models like Amazon FBA ($20,000-$30,000 to start) in favor of becoming an affiliate marketer for other people's already-built digital products. The only skill required is marketing — organic content or paid ads — with no product to build, no merchant account headaches, and no refunds or shipping to manage.

The Pitch

The pitch is that simplicity itself is the advantage: a business that requires you to be good at only one thing (marketing) is far more likely to actually get built and sustained than one with many moving parts (hiring, product sourcing, fulfillment, customer service). The presenter frames his own transition from a 14-person corporate management role to a solo, lean, high-margin business as proof this model lets one person earn without ever hiring staff.

Best For

Someone who wants to stay a true solo operator without hiring staff or building physical inventory, and who is willing to specialize in one skill (marketing) rather than juggling product creation, fulfillment, and support.

The Truth

This shares the same unverified '$3 million' claim as the presenter's companion video, and also funnels to a free masterclass. The comparison to harder businesses (restaurants, Amazon FBA, agencies) is somewhat one-sided — those businesses can also be run leaner than described here. The real substance is a decision framework (simple, affordable, one skill, high margin) rather than a step-by-step how-to.
