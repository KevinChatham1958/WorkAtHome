AI Agent Setup for Local Businesses

Setting up AI dashboards and agents for local businesses

What It Is

Build simple AI systems for local businesses and charge a setup fee. The creator suggests three: a dashboard that pulls sales, ad spend, payments and leads into one place to replace manual spreadsheets ($300 to $1,000 one-time); a speed-to-lead agent that calls or texts new leads instantly; and an AI customer-service agent trained on the company's rules to answer emails and messages, billed at hundreds a month. You find clients by making a list of local businesses and calling them.

The Pitch

He says he would have paid someone to set up the agents he now uses in his own company, and most owners lack the time to build them.

Who It's Best For

Tech-comfortable people willing to cold-call local businesses and support what they build.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator rates this B-tier: it takes real skill to build reliable agents, every client needs individual handling, and selling five to ten setups a month is hard work. The quoted stat that fast responders are seven times likelier to close is commonly cited but not sourced in the video. The video is a six-business tier list; the creator says results he mentions are not typical, and he promotes a free dropshipping course tied to a paid Shopify partnership.
