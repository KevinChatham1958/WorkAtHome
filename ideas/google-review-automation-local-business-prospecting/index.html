What It Is

Find local businesses (plumbers, roofers, dentists, salons, landscapers) with a visible review gap using Google Maps, checking five signals together: a decent but weak review count, no recent reviews, a rating below nearby competitors, negative reviews with no response, and evidence the business is active and can afford the service. Add the prospect to a CRM, run a quick review audit comparing them to their strongest competitor, then pitch a done-for-you AI system (built in GoHighLevel) that automatically texts customers a personalized review request after a job, follows up if they don't respond, and includes an AI-agent auto-reply for incoming reviews; price at $100-500/month.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as an easy first offer for a beginner specifically because it doesn't require explaining unfamiliar technology to a busy tradesperson, and positions it as genuinely recurring since reviews need to keep coming in; he shares his own real pricing ($100-300/month per client) and a specific outreach script highlighting the exact review-count gap versus a top local competitor.

Best For

Someone willing to do consistent local prospecting via Google Maps and comfortable setting up GoHighLevel automations.

The Truth

GoHighLevel's AI-assisted review automation and auto-reply agents are real, current capabilities, and the five-signal prospecting checklist is a genuinely useful, concrete filter. This general offer overlaps in concept with other GoHighLevel-based entries already in this database from different producers, though the specific checklist and script here are distinct; the video also promotes the presenter's own free community.
