Faceless Amazon KDP Publishing

Faceless digital publishing (Amazon KDP under a pen name)

What It Is

Writing and publishing nonfiction books under a pen name on Amazon KDP, using AI to draft and an optional ghostwriter to outsource entirely, then repurposing one book into ebook, paperback, hardcover, and audiobook formats.

The Pitch

Unlike service work, a published book keeps earning after the work is done since it stays listed on Amazon indefinitely, and one book can become several income streams (formats, translations, bundles) without additional writing. The presenter frames this as the most scalable of the five methods he covers precisely because it compounds with catalog size rather than hours worked.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to treat this as an ongoing catalog-building business rather than a one-off project, since the presenter is explicit the payoff compounds with more books published over time.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter says he's personally made millions this way over 10-plus years and cites students earning from hundreds a month up to over $100,000 in a single month, but these are self-reported without account verification, and he's direct that results aren't guaranteed and depend on effort. A paid AI writing tool is promoted alongside this pitch.
