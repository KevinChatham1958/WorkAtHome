AI Music YouTube Channel

AI music YouTube channel built with Claude for songwriting and an AI music generator

Content & personal brand · Very low startup cost (an AI music generation tool subscription, roughly $10/month, plus Claude access)

Originally published Aug 29, 2026 — Geekbot AI

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Geekbot AI · How to Make Passive Income in Day 1 from AI Music & Claude ↗

What It Is

Create an AI music channel by using Claude to generate song concepts, lyrics, and detailed genre/style prompts, feeding those into an AI music generation tool (the video uses one called Sondo) to produce a fully produced song and an AI artist image, then generating a complete AI music video synced to the song, publishing original (non-cover) music under a consistent AI artist persona to build a channel following.

The Pitch

The presenter cites real examples of small AI music channels earning six figures a year (an AI reggae channel around $250,000, an AI R&B artist around $229,000, an AI country act around $160,000) in premium-RPM niches like lowfi, cinematic, and ambient music, which pay $3-8 per thousand views, walking through the full production pipeline (song, artist, video) built in under an hour using Claude and a paid AI music tool.

Best For

Someone comfortable working entirely with AI tools for content creation, willing to treat idea/niche selection (not song quality) as the actual differentiator, and disciplined about following platform monetization rules.

The Truth

The specific YouTube policy rules covered (no mass-uploading near-identical tracks, no AI covers of existing artists' songs, labeling content as AI-generated, and only monetizing tracks generated while on a paid plan with commercial rights) are genuinely important and easy to violate accidentally, so this level of detail is more useful than most AI-content videos that skip the compliance side. The headline earnings figures describe standout channels, not typical outcomes, and the video also promotes the presenter's own AI-idea-scoring tool as a paid upsell.
