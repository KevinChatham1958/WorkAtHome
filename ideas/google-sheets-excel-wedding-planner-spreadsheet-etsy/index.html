Wedding Planner Spreadsheets

Wedding planner spreadsheets (Google Sheets/Excel) sold on Etsy

What It Is

You build an all-in-one wedding planning spreadsheet in Google Sheets or Excel (budgets, guest lists, timelines) and sell it as a digital download on Etsy. The creator found several such listings, one estimated at about $8,000 a month and another around $4,000, and recalls a spreadsheet-only shop estimated at $103,000 a month. A different seller with over 800 near-duplicate products was estimated at $6,451 a month.

The Pitch

The creator was surprised by how much spreadsheet sellers earn and highlights wedding planners as a strong-demand product. She stresses that prices around $17.99 are normal and warns against heavy discounting (one seller had a $6.89 sheet marked under $2), since Etsy listing and transaction fees leave little profit and it starts "a race to the bottom."

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable building tidy, user-friendly spreadsheets who want a higher-value digital product.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a competitive niche with many lookalike listings, and the $103,000 shop figure is from the creator's memory of an earlier video. A good planner spreadsheet takes real time to build and test, and buyers may need help using formulas. The creator's tip to vary titles, tags and listing photos so your own products don't compete with each other is practical. Revenue figures come from Profit Tree's estimates, not the sellers' own records. The creator uses an affiliate link for Profit Tree (50% off a year of its pro plan), sells Canva templates herself, and promotes her own Etsy launch videos.
