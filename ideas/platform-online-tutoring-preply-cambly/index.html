Platform Online Tutoring

Online tutoring through platforms like Preply and Cambly from a phone

Local & professional services · Near-zero cost (a phone with a good connection)

What It Is

You sign up as a tutor on a platform that brings students to you and teach over video calls. Cambly, focused on English conversation, has historically paid about $10 an hour; Preply lets you set your rate, commonly $10-30 an hour, while keeping a commission that can start around a third of earnings and shrinks as you teach more. Subjects beyond languages include math, test prep, coding basics, music and resume coaching. Twelve hours a week at $20 is about $1,000 a month before commission.

The Pitch

The creator says this is the most direct path to income because customers arrive already wanting to pay, and you need no product, inventory or audience. He is upfront that you must show up at set times.

Who It's Best For

Patient people with a teachable skill or fluent English who can commit to scheduled sessions.

The Truth (as we see it)

Tutoring platforms are legitimate and widely used, and the creator's rates and commission notes match the general picture, though platforms change pay and rules often. Income is tied directly to hours taught, and new tutors often start at low rates to win reviews. Sessions are live on video at set times. No paid product is promoted.
