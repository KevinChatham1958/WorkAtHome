Christian Facebook Theme Page

Faceless Christian Bible-verse Facebook page (AI-made image posts, Facebook payouts plus Gumroad products)

Content & personal brand · Near-zero cost (ChatGPT and free Canva)

The Blogging Rapper · How To Make $5,500+ Posting Bible Verses On Facebook (Step-by-Step) ↗

What It Is

You run a faceless Facebook page that posts Bible verses and faith-based encouragement as image graphics, earning from Facebook's content monetization and, like larger pages, from digital products sold on Gumroad. The creator studies big pages (one with 725,000 followers) to see which style gets the most shares, and checks whether they run ads through the page transparency section. He asks ChatGPT for lists of motivational scriptures by theme, then makes 1080x1350 images in ChatGPT or Canva, trying to avoid an obvious AI look. He posts three to five times a day and doubles down on whatever gets the most shares.

The Pitch

The creator says posting about your faith keeps you motivated for the long haul because you would be reading scripture anyway. He notes that image posts are quicker and easier than Reels, even though Reels pay more, and that shares are the strongest signal for higher Facebook payouts.

Who It's Best For

Christians who enjoy sharing scripture daily and want a low-cost, faceless page they can grow slowly alongside a job.

The Truth (as we see it)

The method is simple and cheap to try, and studying what works on big pages is sensible. The video never backs up the '$5,500+' in its title: the creator says his page has 50,000 followers and he is 'getting there' toward several thousand a month, so earnings are unproven here. Facebook's monetization rules and payouts vary by country and change often, and AI-generated, lookalike posts face growing competition and can be demonetized. No paid product is pitched in this video, though he mentions learning from mentors.
