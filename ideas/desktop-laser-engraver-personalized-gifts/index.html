What It Is

A desktop laser engraver that etches a name, phrase, or design onto everyday objects (tumblers, cutting boards, keychains, plaques) across more than 150 materials including wood, leather, acrylic, and painted metal, controlled from a phone and requiring no artistic skill.

The Pitch

A $5 tumbler engraved with a name can sell for $20, and an $8 cutting board with a phrase can sell as a gift for $35, since personalized items are described as rarely getting price-bargained by buyers. The customer base spans anniversary and corporate gifts to cafes and barbershops wanting branded signage, with predictable seasonal demand spikes around Christmas, Valentine's Day, and graduations.

Who It’s Best For

Someone looking for a low-skill personalization business with wide material versatility and genuine gift-giving-season demand spikes.
