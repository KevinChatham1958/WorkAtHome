Horoscope and Tarot Site

AI-built horoscope and tarot content site monetized with Google AdSense

What It Is

You build a content website about astrology, tarot and numerology, topics people search for constantly, and earn money from Google AdSense ads shown on each page. The creator uses Hostinger's AI website builder with a prompt to create a Spanish-language tarot and astrology site, then fixes broken links, adds article subpages (such as "What does yes or no tarot mean"), writes text and makes images with AI, and adds a privacy policy page. Because you are paid for ad views and clicks, the plan is to create many subpages so each visitor sees more ads. You then connect a domain, sign up for AdSense, paste its code into the site and wait days to weeks for approval.

The Pitch

The creator points to a horoscope site running since 2003 that they say gets about 1.4 million visits a month and around 10 million page views, earning several thousand dollars a month from ads alone (the exact figure is garbled in the transcript, roughly $9,000 to $10,000) based on AI-assisted research using SimilarWeb and Ahrefs data. The creator calls it one of the oldest ways to make money with Google and says they run several ad-monetized sites.

Who It’s Best For

Patient people who like writing about a popular evergreen topic and are willing to build up a large library of pages over time.

The Truth (as we see it)

Ad-supported content sites are a real, long-running model, but the video covers building the site and applying to AdSense, not the hard part, which is getting search traffic; a new site usually takes many months of steady publishing to earn meaningfully. The example site's traffic and revenue are third-party estimates gathered with AI, not verified earnings, and an old site that started in 2003 has advantages a new one won't have. AI-written astrology content is plentiful, and Google may rank thin or mass-produced pages poorly, and AdSense can reject sites with too little original content. The creator uses an affiliate link and discount coupon for Hostinger. The original video is in Spanish.
