AI Slide-Deck Production

AI-powered slide-deck production for companies that build presentations by hand

What It Is

You take over a company's routine presentation work, producing their decks with Claude and other AI tools instead of having staff design slides by hand. The company sends the content or brief; you deliver finished slides. Pay is a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Dang describes his client Shawn finding a company that paid people to spend their days making polished PowerPoints. The company had tried Claude itself without success, and now pays Shawn a couple of thousand dollars a month to produce the decks with AI. Dang's broader point is that any repetitive screen-based job a company pays a person thousands for is a candidate for an AI-powered service.

Who It’s Best For

People with an eye for clean slide design who are comfortable learning AI presentation tools and handling recurring client requests.

The Truth (as we see it)

Plenty of companies spend staff time on slide production, and AI tools have improved at it, so the opportunity is plausible. The details come from a single brief client story. The service is easy for a client to bring in-house once AI tools improve or someone on staff learns them, so steady quality and fast turnaround are what keep the client.
