Notion Templates on Gumroad

Building a niche-specific Notion template with Notion AI and selling it on Gumroad, driven by Pinterest traffic

Digital & subscription products · Low ($10/month Notion AI add-on with a free trial, a design tool with a free plan, free Gumroad/Pinterest accounts)

What It Is

Instead of copying a generic template (budget trackers, content calendars), build a Notion template around a niche you personally have real knowledge in so you know the exact fields/workflow that niche actually needs; build it directly inside Notion using Notion AI with a specific, detailed brief, then polish it visually with an AI design tool to create a cover, in-template graphics, and a lifestyle mockup listing image, list it on Gumroad (which auto-generates a title/description by scanning the product), and drive free traffic via Pinterest pins sized to Pinterest's recommended vertical ratio, posted to a niche-specific board.

The Pitch

The presenter made $2,200 in 2 months from this exact system using cold Pinterest traffic and zero pre-existing audience, no face-in-camera content, and no ad spend, crediting her personal niche knowledge (not the AI tool itself) as the real competitive advantage over generic templates, and reports over 34 million monthly Pinterest views across her accounts as evidence the traffic channel works at scale.

Who It's Best For

Someone with genuine hands-on knowledge of a specific niche/workflow, comfortable iterating conversationally with Notion AI and doing basic visual design work.

The Truth (as we see it)

Notion templates sold on Gumroad with Pinterest as a traffic source is a real, already-established niche within digital products, and the core insight (niche knowledge beats generic templates, Pinterest's search/save intent suits product discovery) is sound, non-obvious marketing logic. Her $2,200-in-2-months figure is self-reported from a single product, and the video includes a paid sponsorship segment that is promotional rather than neutral tool advice.
