Niche Utility Website

AI-built single-purpose utility website monetized with AdSense, affiliate links and a paid upgrade

What It Is

Use an AI prompt to brainstorm ideas from your work, hobbies and daily annoyances, then pick one simple tool that solves a specific searched-for problem; the creator builds a tea-steeping time and temperature site. An AI website builder (Hostinger's agentic builder in the video) writes the site from a plain-language description, and you connect a custom domain. You earn three ways: Google AdSense ads once the site is approved, an affiliate section promoting related products (a tea affiliate program here), and a paid monthly upgrade that unlocks premium extras such as recipes.

The Pitch

The creator points to odd-sounding sites that she says earn well, such as a cat-on-a-chair focus timer at $18,000 a month and a car-size comparison site she estimates at $32,000 a month from AdSense, and argues the 'stupid' idea works when it solves one clear problem. She says the AI builder makes this possible for beginners with no coding.

Who It’s Best For

Beginners comfortable with trial and error who will keep testing ideas and promote their sites, since a site nobody visits earns nothing.

The Truth (as we see it)

Small single-purpose tools can earn, but the examples shown are outliers and their earnings are estimates from third-party calculators, not verified figures. AdSense needs approval and a lot of traffic to add up, and the creator admits the hard part, driving visitors, is not covered here. The creator uses a Hostinger affiliate link and discount code and promotes her own community, and she is clear that results are not guaranteed. Check every feature the AI builds, especially anything that gives advice or analyzes photos.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
