Affiliate Site to YouTube Videos

Repurposing affiliate-website content into niche YouTube videos to earn affiliate commissions from day one, before qualifying for AdSense

Content & personal brand · Very low (a camera/phone; free to join Amazon Associates)

Originally published Sep 6, 2026 — Greg Gottfried

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Gottfried · How I Built 10 Streams Of Income In My 20's ↗

One of 9 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Take existing affiliate-website article ideas (or original ones) and record them as videos instead, posting an Amazon Associates affiliate link in each video's description; unlike AdSense, which requires hitting subscriber/watch-time thresholds first, affiliate-link earnings start from a channel's very first video and first viewer, since income comes from purchases after a click, not from ad placement eligibility. Focus on educational, question-answering videos, since Google increasingly surfaces video results (not just articles) for informational searches.

The Pitch

The presenter shows a real six-year-old video comparing flash drives for Tesla dashcams (a specific, once-niche need) with an affiliate link in the description, explaining that this exact video type let him start earning commissions immediately on a brand-new channel with no subscriber base.

Best For

Someone starting a brand-new channel who wants income before reaching AdSense eligibility, willing to make specific, question-answering videos rather than entertainment content.

The Truth

The distinction between AdSense's monetization threshold and Amazon Associates' day-one-eligible affiliate links is accurate, and Google increasingly favoring video results for certain searches is a real, documented trend. The specific example shown is real and verifiable in concept, though no personal revenue figures for this exact stream are given.
