What It Is

Instead of relying on platform payouts, you use AI to recreate viral faceless formats (slideshows, tips carousels, edutainment clips) on TikTok, Reels and Shorts and send viewers to something you sell: a mobile app, digital product or coaching. If you want ad revenue instead, she says only long-form YouTube pays meaningfully, and it shouldn't be fully automated.

The Pitch

The creator says short-form platforms grow fastest but pay almost nothing per view, so the money is in an offer outside the platform. She cites a mobile app earning thousands in monthly recurring revenue from automated TikTok slideshows.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who prefers not to be on camera and has, or plans to create, a product to promote.

The Truth (as we see it)

The distinction between platform payouts and driving traffic to an offer is useful and honest. Results from the cited app aren't verified, and copying viral formats can feel spammy and trip platform rules on AI-generated or repetitive content. You still need a product people want. The creator sells her own AI app.
