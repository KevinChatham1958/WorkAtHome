Federal Contracting Consultant

Government contracting consultant who helps small businesses find and win federal contracts

What It Is

You learn how federal buying works, using USASpending.gov to see what agencies buy and from whom, and SAM.gov to find solicitations and earlier 'sources sought' notices, then help existing businesses position for and respond to those opportunities as a consultant or freelancer. The video walks through this using laundry and dry-cleaning contracts as the example.

The Pitch

The host, a former military acquisitions officer, says that if you don't already run a business that sells to the government, consulting for companies that do is faster to revenue than bidding yourself. He says responding to sources-sought notices early lets businesses shape requirements before the formal solicitation.

Who It's Best For

Someone detail-oriented who enjoys rules and paperwork, ideally with procurement, government or proposal-writing experience.

The Truth (as we see it)

The host is candid that government contracting is the longest, hardest sales process in the US and that he can't promise wins. Consulting income figures aren't given, and clients will expect real expertise in regulations. The channel sells a paid government contracting program (GovClose). The laundry services themselves are on-site work and aren't part of this gig.
