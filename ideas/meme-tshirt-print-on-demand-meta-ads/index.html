Meme T-Shirt Print-on-Demand Brand

Funny meme t-shirt print-on-demand brand driven by Meta ads

E-commerce · Needs ongoing ad budget ($50-$150 a day in the video)

What It Is

You research t-shirt designs that are going viral on social media in categories like funny, nostalgic and sports, then make simple designs inspired by what's already selling, using AI to help. You upload the designs to a print-on-demand supplier (Tapstitch in the video), place them on blank garments, publish them to your own website, and the supplier prints and ships each order so you hold no stock. Traffic comes from paid Meta ads: the creator started with one $50-a-day campaign, then moved to a separate $50-$150 campaign for each shirt, and cut the design that didn't sell. Over three days of ads he reports about $500 in profit after ad spend and product costs.

The Pitch

The creator says he has done over $130,000 in sales with print on demand over four months on his own store, and that there is no upfront inventory risk. He argues shirts are easy to advertise because the design does the selling, and points to meme shirt accounts getting millions of views and thousands of sales.

Who It's Best For

People comfortable spending and managing an ad budget who have a feel for humor and trends, and who can accept losing money on designs that don't sell.

The Truth (as we see it)

Print on demand with paid ads is a real model, and the creator's day-by-day breakdown of ad spend, product cost and profit is more transparent than most. The $130,000 figure is revenue, not profit, and is unverified; ad results vary a lot, and many stores lose money before finding a winning design. Copying viral designs, especially ones that use logos, characters or other people's original jokes, can lead to takedowns or legal trouble. The print-on-demand supplier, Tapstitch, sponsored this part of the video, and the creator shares a discount code and affiliate link.
