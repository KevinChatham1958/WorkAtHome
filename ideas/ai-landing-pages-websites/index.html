What It Is

Build high-converting landing pages, single-page sites designed to sell one thing, for course creators, coaches, agencies, or local businesses, using AI website builders like Wix or Hostinger.

The Pitch

AI now handles most of the design work, so the real value you're selling is a page that actually converts visitors into customers, not just one that looks nice. Both creators independently rate this a reasonable, moderate-competition entry point.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who's comfortable pitching outcomes, more leads, more sales, rather than just design work, since that's what separates this from a commodity service.

The Truth (as we see it)

Gadzhi's independent framing puts it plainly: the mistake most people make is selling pretty pages instead of outcomes. Tilbury's version agrees the AI does most of the visual work, but making a page that actually converts is still a real skill the tool doesn't hand you automatically.
