Vertical AI Agent Service

Vertical AI agent that runs a recurring job for one industry (e.g. purchasing for indie restaurants)

AI & automation · Low to moderate startup cost (AI tools and development time)

Originally published Oct 6, 2026 — Greg Isenberg

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Isenberg · 13 Businesses for the Age of AI ↗

One of 8 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You build an AI agent that takes over one recurring job in one industry and sell it to businesses there. The creator's example is purchasing for independent restaurants: the agent checks stock, compares supplier offers, asks the owner for approval, and places the order. You earn a subscription or service fee from each business that uses it.

The Pitch

The creator admits people have been building vertical agents for about a year, but says there is still 'a ton of opportunity' because most industries have recurring jobs that no one has automated yet.

Who It's Best For

Technical builders, or people who know an industry's routine paperwork well, who are willing to sell to small businesses.

The Truth (as we see it)

A narrow, clearly defined job is the most believable way to sell AI to small businesses. Restaurant owners are busy and wary of letting software place orders, though, so expect sales effort and a trust-building period. Mistakes in ordering cost real money. The video offers the idea only, with no pricing or proof of demand. The video is a fast list of 13 business categories rather than a how-to, so there are no steps, prices or income figures to check, and the creator does not pitch a paid product of his own in this episode.
