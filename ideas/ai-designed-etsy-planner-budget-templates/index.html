What It Is

You find a template niche with proven sales on Etsy, such as ADHD planners, budget spreadsheets or wedding planners, then have an AI tool (Genspark here) design a fillable PDF planner from a detailed prompt and build spreadsheet templates with working formulas and charts. You give it a different angle from competitors rather than copying them, test every page, and list it on Etsy or your own store. The same tool writes the Etsy listing and SEO keywords, makes mockup images and creates Pinterest pins to promote it.

The Pitch

The creator shows Etsy examples she says sold over 20,000 times for more than $300,000 (an ADHD planner), $95,000 (a budget template) and $180,000 (a wedding planner). She argues simple digital products made once and sold repeatedly help regular people more than flashy AI agency models.

Who It’s Best For

Organized people who understand a specific audience's problem and can turn it into a genuinely useful template.

The Truth (as we see it)

The top sellers she shows are outliers in a very crowded template market, and their sales figures come from a third-party tracking tool, not the sellers. Using AI to make templates is easy for everyone, so a distinct angle and good listings matter more than the tool. The video promotes Genspark with an affiliate link and her paid community.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
