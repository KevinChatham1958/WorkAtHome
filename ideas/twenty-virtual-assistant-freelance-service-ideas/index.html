What It Is

Offer one or more remote freelance services grouped into three tiers: administrative (email management, calendar/appointment setting, data entry, travel research/booking, AI-assisted market/competitor research, survey creation and analysis, online community management, file/email organization); creative (blog writing with an AI-assisted first draft plus a human editing pass, graphic design, newsletter creation, Pinterest management); and specialized, higher-skill services (video editing overseeing AI tools like Descript, YouTube channel management, podcast editing and guest outreach, PR/media outreach, website design with an ongoing monthly maintenance retainer rather than a one-time fee, bookkeeping/tax prep). Identify which skills you're already using in your own life or a past job, start there, and where possible convert one-time projects into ongoing monthly retainers for recurring revenue.

The Pitch

The presenter, who has run this virtual assistant training business for nearly 10 years and manages a 140,000-member community, shares three real, named student results: one who signed a second client at her top package price within her first month, another who raised rates with two long-term clients via email, and a third who grew from a team of one to a team of six and from consistent $5,000 months to consistently over $15,000 months.

Who It’s Best For

Someone assessing what existing skills could translate into a paid remote service, willing to start narrow with 1-2 services rather than offering everything at once.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is an established, long-running business rather than a one-off video, which adds credibility, and the specific student results cited are named with concrete before/after numbers. The presenter is honest that AI tools assist several of these services now but still require human oversight, rather than claiming full automation. The video promotes the presenter's own free training on pricing and client acquisition as the next step, though the 20 service ideas stand on their own.
