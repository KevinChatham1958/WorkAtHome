Wedding Officiant

Local & professional services · Low to moderate startup cost

Originally published Aug 13, 2026 — Amber Gist

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Amber Gist · Don't mind if I DO ❤️ #buildinginpublic ↗

What It Is

Working as a paid wedding officiant, booking and performing wedding ceremonies for couples, and reinvesting early revenue into better equipment (a professional backdrop stand, drapes and decorations for photo backdrops, and a collapsible equipment wagon) to make the on-site experience feel more polished as the client base grows.

The Pitch

The creator set herself a concrete validation goal, book and officiate 25 married couples and see if she still enjoyed the work, before investing further, and documents that she passed that bar and is now formalizing her systems and buying real equipment rather than treating it as a one-off side gig.

Best For

Someone comfortable with public speaking and being the center of attention at events, who doesn't mind weekend and evening work built around other people's schedules, and who is willing to start small and low-cost before investing in equipment.

The Truth

This is a behind-the-scenes, equipment-unboxing style video, not a how-to guide, so it doesn't cover how to get licensed or ordained to legally perform marriages in your area, how ceremonies are priced, or how clients are actually found, all real open questions for anyone considering this. What it does show is a real, currently-operating practitioner with a specific, self-set traction goal (25 couples) she says she's already reached, a genuine, if narrow, validation signal that this can work as an actual local service business.
