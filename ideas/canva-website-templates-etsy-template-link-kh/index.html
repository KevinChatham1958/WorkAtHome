Canva Website Templates

Canva website templates sold on Etsy (wedding, portfolio, link-in-bio)

Kate Hayes · Create + Sell Canva Websites (Step by Step Canva Website Tutorial) ↗

What It Is

You design a complete website inside Canva's website editor, for example a one-page site for a social media manager with services, about, portfolio and a contact page with an inquiry form, and sell it as an editable template on Etsy. The creator walks through choosing colors and fonts, building each section, checking the mobile view and linking buttons to sections and pages. You publish the original as a live demo for the listing, make a copy, generate a Canva template link from the copy, and deliver a one-page PDF containing that link. Buyers open their own copy in Canva, edit it and publish it as their own site.

The Pitch

The creator says Canva websites command higher prices than printables because they solve a real problem for people who want a site without hiring a designer, and that competition is still relatively low. She shows a wedding website listing she says made 157 sales and about $2,747 in a month, and another with 99 sales and about $2,492. Once built, she says, a template can keep selling without more work.

Who It's Best For

People with a good eye for layout who already use Canva and want a higher-priced template product than printables.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling Canva templates by template link on Etsy is an established method, and the step-by-step build here is concrete and genuinely useful. The sales figures are the creator's own presentation of other sellers' listings and are not verified; most new listings sell far less. Canva Pro is required to create template links, and using Pro-only elements can break the template for buyers on free accounts, as the creator notes. She promotes a free starter guide and free masterclass that lead into her own digital product training, and links a Canva Pro trial.
