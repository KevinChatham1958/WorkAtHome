Local SEO Rank-and-Rent Sites

Local SEO microsite / rank-and-rent network using an AI coding blueprint

Startup cost: Very low startup cost (roughly $10 in domain cost plus ~$2/month hosting per site; AI coding tool subscription optional)

Source video

Jesse Cunningham · How I Built 30 Microsites to Take Over a City ↗

What It Is

Building a network of dozens of small, single-service-page local-SEO microsites targeting narrow, low-competition service-plus-location combinations in the suburbs around a city, then routing calls through an AI phone agent and monetizing via revenue-share or flat-fee lead sales to local service businesses.

The Pitch

The presenter runs a paid community providing a reusable site blueprint that's fed directly to an AI coding tool to auto-generate a full 40 to 50 page exact-match-domain website with almost no manual work. Core strategy: pick underserved suburb-level niches, avoiding city-center searches with a Google map pack which signal too much competition, register a cheap exact-match domain, let the site rank organically over 3 to 6 months without connecting it to Google Search Console, wire an AI voice agent to answer the phone 24/7, then monetize via revenue share, flat per-lead fees, or selling a bundle of built sites to a business owner outright.

Who It’s Best For

Someone patient enough to wait months for organic ranking rather than needing fast income, and willing to build and manage a network of many small sites rather than one polished website.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter's own numbers, six figures a month, sites costing under $10 to build, $150 or more per qualified lead, are self-reported without account verification shown on screen. Worth noting: in a companion live-build video, the AI-generated site included fabricated customer testimonials and a false licensing claim that the presenter himself flagged as mistakes needing correction before real use, a useful reminder that AI-generated site copy needs a real editing pass, especially anything implying licensing or certifications the business doesn't actually hold. The video ends promoting the presenter's own paid community with a 7-day trial.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
