Live Etsy Digital Product Challenge

A live, transparent Etsy digital product challenge using an AI research-to-build platform

What It Is

A documented seven-day, budget-capped challenge using a single all-in-one AI platform (GenSpark) to research an under-served Etsy niche by cross-referencing listing counts against real demand signals on Reddit and Pinterest, build the actual product (a Google Sheets template) from a detailed plain-English prompt, generate a matching brand kit and listing visuals, publish the listing, and run paid Etsy ads, with every step documented on camera.

The Pitch

The research method is concrete and repeatable: rather than picking an already-saturated category, the AI is prompted to count existing listings across several product types, then cross-reference that against real people asking for the product on Reddit and Pinterest, surfacing a specific under-served niche (Google Sheets garden planners) with only a couple thousand listings but real, quotable buyer demand. The entire product, brand identity, and listing assets were built inside one platform without switching tools, which the video presents as a meaningful time and cost saving over stitching several separate AI tools together.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants a realistic, honest picture of a first attempt at this model before investing real time, since this documents a genuine early-stage result rather than a polished success story.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is one of the more transparent entries of its kind: the final result was a net loss of $6.97 (four sales totaling $25.56 against $32.53 in combined ad spend and tool cost), openly reported rather than reframed as a win, a genuinely honest outcome that sets realistic expectations. The presenter notes Etsy itself recommends 30 days to properly judge a new ad campaign, so a 7-day window may understate the format's real potential, a fair caveat rather than an excuse. The video links to the AI platform used, though the underlying niche-research method (cross-referencing listing counts against Reddit and Pinterest demand) is a transferable technique independent of that specific tool.
