AI Health PDF Guides

AI-written health PDF guides for older adults sold on Selar, with traffic from a Facebook health-tips page

COACH AWAJI · How I Made ₦41 Million Selling Simple PDFs With Claude AI (Free Traffic Only) ↗

What It Is

You paste a detailed prompt into a free AI chatbot to write a health guide aimed at people 45 and older (his example: "50 everyday habits that could be damaging to your health and simple ways to fix them"), clean it up in Google Docs and export it as a PDF. Canva's AI image generator makes the cover, and the PDF is listed on Selar, a free African digital-product marketplace, for about 15,000 naira. AI also writes the product description and the code for a landing page hosted free on Systeme.io. Traffic comes from a Facebook health-tips page that posts short reels of scenery with on-screen health tips, linking to the landing page.

The Pitch

The creator says the health niche sells easily because older people actively look for ways to stay healthy, and that the whole stack is free and can be done on a phone. He shows his Selar dashboard with over 9,000 sales and about 41 million naira in lifetime revenue across several currencies. He says the Facebook reels approach drives traffic without paying for ads.

Who It's Best For

A beginner, including in Nigeria or Ghana where Selar is widely used, who is comfortable posting daily short videos on Facebook.

The Truth (as we see it)

The sales dashboard shown is the creator's own; the naira total sounds large but is roughly in the tens of thousands of US dollars at current exchange rates, and how much came from this exact method versus his mentorship sales is not shown. His traffic method is to "model" viral accounts by reusing their audio and copying their on-screen text onto new footage, which risks copyright and originality problems on Facebook; write your own captions. AI-written health advice can be inaccurate, so facts should be checked and medical claims avoided. He sells a paid mentorship course and keeps the full prompts and step-by-step training behind it and his Telegram channel.
