Finding Your Freelance Skill With ChatGPT

ChatGPT-guided freelance skill discovery and client acquisition roadmap

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (free ChatGPT tier works)

Originally published Aug 15, 2026 — ENERGI

What It Is

A structured sequence of ChatGPT prompts that takes someone with no clear freelance skill from personal interests and constraints (what you enjoy, how many hours you can commit, what you refuse to do) to a specific freelance offer, a prioritized 80/20 sub-skill learning plan, a personalized curriculum of videos and articles to learn it, and finally a three-channel test plan for finding first clients.

The Pitch

The presenter, who built a six-figure freelance writing business without a college degree, walks through the exact prompts in sequence: first asking ChatGPT for five freelance skill options based on your specific interests and constraints (explicitly telling it to push back rather than accept the first suggestions), then asking it to map the minimum 20% of sub-skills that produce 80% of the paid results, then generating a learning curriculum around a stated weekly time budget, and finally asking for three distinct ways to find clients (in her case, Upwork, cold email, and social media) so a beginner can test which approach actually fits them.

Best For

A complete beginner with no existing portfolio or clear niche who wants a structured way to turn general interests into a specific, learnable, sellable skill rather than guessing.

The Truth

This is a general framework rather than a single business idea, and the presenter's own six-figure outcome isn't demonstrated step-by-step within the video, it's cited as background credibility. The core value of the method, using AI to convert vague interests into a specific, prioritized skill and learning plan, doesn't depend on any paid tool or program and is fully testable with ChatGPT's free tier.
