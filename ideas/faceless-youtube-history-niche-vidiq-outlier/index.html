Faceless History YouTube Channel

Building a faceless YouTube automation channel in the history niche using VidIQ outlier scoring and an all-in-one AI video generator

Content & personal brand · Low (a VidIQ subscription, an AI video-generation tool starting around $29/month for ~9 videos)

Originally published Aug 26, 2026 — E'Calm

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

E'Calm · How I Created a $30,000/month Faceless YouTube Channel Using AI Tools ↗

What It Is

Pick the history niche specifically, create and optimize a channel with VidIQ-researched keywords, find video topics by checking VidIQ's 'outlier score' (aim for 20+, ideally 50+) and views-per-hour on existing videos, especially older videos still pulling steady views years later, rather than asking an AI to invent topics; feed the chosen topic plus a detailed master prompt into an all-in-one AI video tool that researches, scripts, voices, edits, and generates thumbnails/descriptions automatically, then unlist each video briefly to let YouTube auto-generate multi-language captions/dubs before publishing.

The Pitch

The presenter shows real channel dashboards claiming $40-47K and $240K+ from faceless channels, walks through his own documented failures (a compilation channel that couldn't monetize, an early history attempt where generic AI scripts killed retention) before landing on this process, and demonstrates a new channel monetized with only 4 videos.

Best For

Someone willing to do real topic research rather than relying on AI to invent ideas, comfortable using a paid all-in-one AI video tool rather than hiring freelance editors.

The Truth

The failure mode described, generic AI scripts killing retention even with initial views, matches real YouTube algorithm mechanics, and the outlier-score research method is a genuine, commonly-used technique. The presenter's own figures are self-reported screenshots; monetization thresholds referenced are accurate as of publication but subject to change.
