High-Commission Digital Affiliate

Affiliate marketing for other people's digital products, which often pay 50% commission or more

Richard Yu · how i made $1,204/day selling boring pdfs using claude ↗

What It Is

Instead of making your own product, you promote someone else's PDF, course or template to an audience that wants it, and earn a share of each sale. Digital products cost nothing to copy, so creators can pay far higher commissions than physical-product programs.

The Pitch

Richard says he made $36,130 in the last 30 days, about $1,200 a day, this way. He says products of $50 to $500 often pay 70% to 95% commission, versus 3% to 10% on normal affiliate programs.

Who It’s Best For

People with an audience or a way to reach buyers who'd rather sell than create.

The Truth (as we see it)

His income comes with an existing audience; without one, affiliate links rarely sell. Some high-commission products are low quality, so check what you promote, and you must disclose affiliate links. Richard says he has built systems for over 3,000 people who sell digital products, and his videos promote his own paid programs.
