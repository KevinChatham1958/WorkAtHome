Native Ads for Coaches

Native-style paid ads specialist for coaches and course creators

Marketing & content · Low startup cost

Originally published Aug 21, 2026 — Martin

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Martin · this stupid simple way of running ads made us $50,144 in 7 days ↗

What It Is

Offer paid social media advertising as a specialized service to online coaches and course creators, built around a specific technique: making ads look and feel like ordinary organic content (a 'native' style) rather than obviously polished advertising, since viewers increasingly recognize and skip traditional-looking ads.

The Pitch

Martin walks through the exact structure he used for a client who generated over $50,000 in seven days: finding proven hook formats by studying the client's direct and indirect competitors' best-performing organic content, matching the ad copy's complexity to the audience's actual awareness level (based on real sales call and DM data, not assumptions), and systematically testing combinations of hooks, value messaging, and calls-to-action across multiple ad variations to find the highest performer. He also gives specific target benchmarks to judge a campaign by (cost per profile visit, cost per qualified follower, show-up rate, and so on).

Best For

Someone with genuine copywriting and campaign-structuring skills who's comfortable doing real diligence (analyzing sales calls, competitor content, and audience awareness) rather than treating ad creative as a generic template.

The Truth

The $50,144-in-seven-days result is attributed to one named client campaign with a specific structure explained in real detail, more transparent than a vague results claim, though it's still a single case study, not an audited or repeated result across multiple clients. The video ends with a pitch for Martin's own done-for-you agency service, so there's a clear incentive to make the underlying method sound like it requires more infrastructure and expertise than a motivated beginner might actually need to get started with the basics.
