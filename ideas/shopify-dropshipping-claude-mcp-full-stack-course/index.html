A Full Shopify Dropshipping Build Using Claude MCP Integrations for Product Research, AI Ad Creation, and Email Marketing Automation

E-commerce · Low (free trials for a supplier tool, a free Claude account, a free email platform, an AI content tool)

Originally published Sep 2, 2026 — Jeremy Ray Holst

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jeremy Ray Holst · How To Actually Start Dropshipping In 2026 (FREE Course) ↗

What It Is

Build a complete dropshipping business end to end: (1) find winning products manually by training a dedicated 'burner' TikTok/Instagram account to surface viral product videos and validating demand in the comments, or automatically by connecting a supplier tool (Zenrop) to Claude via MCP and running a custom research prompt that has Claude scan and rank the best current products; (2) build the store using a free premium Shopify theme; (3) create AI-generated UGC-style video ads by recreating the structure (cuts, beat-drops, on-screen text) of an already-viral ad using an AI content tool, rather than filming original footage; (4) set up automated email/SMS marketing by connecting an email platform (Omnisend) to Claude via MCP and running a master prompt that builds out full campaigns from the product name.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as one single video covering the entire business from zero to a fully built, sales-ready store, walking through each stage live with real screen recordings of the actual tool integrations rather than just describing them, running several giveaways throughout tied to liking/subscribing/commenting.

Best For

Someone willing to learn several connected tools (a supplier platform, Claude MCP connectors, an AI video-editing tool, an email platform) and comfortable with a genuinely hands-on multi-step build process.

The Truth

Every individual technique described (algorithm-trained product research, MCP-based Claude integrations, AI-recreated UGC ad structure, automated email/SMS flows) is real and technically accurate to how these platforms currently work. The video is structured with multiple giveaways and promotional/affiliate links to the presenter's own free course and several tools, so those specific tool recommendations should be read as partly promotional even though the underlying techniques are legitimate.
