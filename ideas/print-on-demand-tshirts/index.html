What It Is

A print-on-demand apparel business built around niche-specific t-shirt designs, using a fulfillment partner for printing and shipping so you never hold inventory.

The Pitch

One case study claims over $1 million in revenue in its first year, though only after 10 earlier failed store attempts and a period of significant personal debt from other failed businesses.

Best For

Someone prepared for a real trial-and-error phase, this isn't presented as a first-try success story.

The Truth

The arc shown, many failures followed by success, suggests real survivorship bias in what's highlighted, the failed attempts aren't detailed. Worth weighing that against the low-effort framing implied by a headline like this.
