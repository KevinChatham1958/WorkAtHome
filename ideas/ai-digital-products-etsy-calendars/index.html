What It Is

Use AI to research digital product ideas, then create the products themselves, such as digital calendars, ebooks or guides, directly in ChatGPT and list them on Etsy or your own Shopify store. The creator shows Etsy listings with about 9,700 sales of a 99-cent digital calendar and 8,000 sales of a $17 one. To scale, he suggests short videos and even AI influencers to drive traffic.

The Pitch

He says digital products made most of his money, with no fulfillment cost per sale, and AI now makes creating them fast enough to test many ideas.

Who It’s Best For

People who can spot specific needs and are willing to test many listings and promote them.

The Truth (as we see it)

Digital products on Etsy can sell, but the market is crowded and the creator himself calls it extremely saturated; the hard part is traffic, not creation. Etsy sales counts on a listing don't show profit, and low-priced items need high volume. Using AI influencers to promote products without disclosure risks misleading buyers. The video is a six-business tier list; the creator says results he mentions are not typical, and he promotes a free dropshipping course tied to a paid Shopify partnership.
