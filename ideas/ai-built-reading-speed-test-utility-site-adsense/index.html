Utility Website With AdSense

AI-built single-purpose utility website (reading speed test) monetized with AdSense

What It Is

You pick a simple problem people search for and solve it with a one-purpose free web tool, like a typing test or the creator's reading speed and comprehension test for English learners. ChatGPT ranks niche ideas by search demand and competition, then writes the prompt you paste into Hostinger's AI website builder, which builds the tool, quizzes, a blog and a digital-product shop. You apply to Google AdSense, add its code and ads.txt file, wait 2-4 weeks for approval, and turn on auto ads. Money comes from display ads (and optional digital products) once the site ranks on Google and gets steady traffic.

The Pitch

The creator points to typingtest.com, which she says a verified Flippa listing shows earning about 42,000 euros a month from ads, without social media or a personal brand. She says AI has removed the coding barrier, so the main work left is finding a niche with enough searches but not too much competition, and following a ChatGPT-generated 30-day traffic plan.

Who It’s Best For

Patient people who like the idea of a faceless, low-maintenance website and are willing to learn basic SEO and wait months for traffic.

The Truth (as we see it)

Ad-supported utility sites are a real model, but the example site has been around for years; a new site earns very little until it ranks, and the creator herself says traffic "has always been pretty hard" and this isn't get-rich-overnight. Display ads pay small amounts per visitor, so meaningful income needs large traffic. The Flippa figure is the creator's report and was not independently checked. The video is sponsored by Hostinger and includes the creator's discount code, so the builder recommendation is paid. AdSense approval isn't guaranteed.
