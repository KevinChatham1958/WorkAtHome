Fast-Selling eBay Reselling

EBay reselling of fast-selling finds from thrift end caps, clearance sales and liquidation pallets

  • $500–$2,000 to start

What It Is

You buy underpriced goods and resell them on eBay, listing only items worth $20 or more that eBay sales data shows will sell quickly. The creator's sources include thrift-store end caps (vitamins, lotions, toothbrushes, water flossers for a few dollars), seasonal clearance sales for brands like Patagonia and The North Face, Facebook Marketplace deals and liquidation pallets of Amazon returns. He prices in the middle of the market, sends and accepts offers aggressively, aims for at least a 50% margin, and sends cheaper items to a flea market or a Whatnot live show. His October showed about $25,000 in net sales at an average price of $256.

The Pitch

The creator says pricing by demand data means many items sell within 24 hours, including a $12,600 sale of computer parts from a $770 pallet that sold six minutes after listing. He argues simple photos on a concrete floor are enough when the item is already in demand, and that seasonal clearance and overlooked end caps are reliable sources.

Who It’s Best For

Hands-on people who enjoy hunting for bargains, can store and ship inventory, and can spend time sourcing in person.

The Truth (as we see it)

The sales figures are the creator's own and unaudited, and he calls his $12,600 pallet sale a once-in-15-years event. He warns that liquidation pallets are like gambling: about half of each pallet goes to donation and only 10-15% is worth listing on eBay, so beginners should not buy them without cash reserves. Margins depend on finding underpriced goods, which takes regular trips to thrift stores, sales and sellers, plus packing and shipping. The $20 minimum and demand-data rules are sensible, practical habits for any reseller.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
