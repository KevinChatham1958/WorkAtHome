AI-Assisted Paralegal Service

AI-assisted paralegal service for law firms that can't fill a paralegal job

Local & professional services · Low startup cost

Originally published Aug 24, 2026 — Patrick Dang

What It Is

If you have paralegal experience, you offer law firms paralegal work done faster with AI, as a contractor or service rather than an employee. You find firms with an unfilled paralegal job posting and pitch your service as a cheaper, quicker alternative to a new hire.

The Pitch

Dang's client Zaidi, a long-time paralegal, first tried pitching law firms on 'AI audits' and got no replies. Dang had him instead look for firms that had a paralegal job open for months, and message them as an experienced paralegal who could solve that exact problem. On the call, Zaidi explained he was running his own AI-assisted paralegal service. Dang says replies started within two weeks and that Claude turns hours of paperwork into minutes.

Who It's Best For

Experienced paralegals and legal assistants who want to work for themselves and know legal workflows well enough to check AI output.

The Truth (as we see it)

Targeting firms that are actively trying to hire is a smart, concrete tactic, and the 'audits don't work, solve a visible problem' lesson applies widely. Legal work carries extra obligations: confidentiality of client data (check where AI tools store information), accuracy, and in many places rules about who may perform paralegal tasks and under whose supervision. The time savings and results are Dang's claims. The video is sponsored by .online domains and promotes his free skills, training and coaching calls.
