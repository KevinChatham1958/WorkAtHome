Multi-Network Affiliate Funnel

Multi-network affiliate marketing with a nurture funnel

Startup cost: Very low startup cost (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Aaron Chen · How I Built a $25K/Month Affiliate Business (No Product Needed) ↗

What It Is

Rather than creating your own product, become a skilled marketer for other people's digital products (courses, ebooks, software), sourced from affiliate networks like Digistore24, ClickBank, and WarriorPlus, earning a 50-80% commission per sale. Instead of dropping a raw affiliate link, build a simple funnel: an opt-in landing page collecting an email address, followed by a multi-day "nurture sequence" of short, genuinely useful content on the topic that builds trust before ever presenting the affiliate offer.

The Pitch

The core argument is that affiliate marketing removes the hardest, slowest parts of building a product-based business (six to twelve months of product development, pricing, merchant accounts, sales pages) since the product already exists and is already priced and tested. The presenter frames the funnel-and-nurture-sequence structure as the actual differentiator over just posting a bare affiliate link, since people buy from someone they've gotten to know and trust over several days of free value, not from a stranger's link. Traffic is driven through free organic short-form content (Instagram and TikTok, posting one to three videos a day), tracking which specific formats perform well and repeating those rather than constantly inventing new ones.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable being consistently on camera or creating short-form content, and willing to build out a funnel and email sequence rather than just posting links, since that infrastructure is described as the actual differentiator here.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is specific that commissions from these networks can be high (a cited $3,363 product paying an 80% commission), and that these are checkable, real figures visible on the platforms themselves, a good transparency signal. The claimed $25,000 to $30,000 monthly income and the $3 million lifetime figure are personal claims without independent verification, and the video ends by promoting a free masterclass, a funnel of its own worth being aware of, separate from whether the core affiliate-plus-funnel mechanic works.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
