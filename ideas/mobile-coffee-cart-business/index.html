Mobile Coffee Cart

Local & professional services · Low to moderate startup cost (a basic mobile cart and espresso setup, a couple thousand dollars to get fully equipped per the presenter)

Originally published Aug 13, 2026 — Austin Zaback

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Austin Zaback · 5 Side Hustles Making People RICH in 2026 (Low Competition!) ↗

One of 5 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Operate a mobile espresso/coffee cart at events, office parks, or high-foot-traffic locations, with no fixed storefront lease, selling coffee and espresso drinks directly to walk-up customers.

The Pitch

Zaback cites the founder of Crema Avenue, who reportedly sells about 550 coffees over a 10-hour day (~$4,400 revenue), netting around $3,000 profit after ingredient and labor costs, and frames the market opportunity around the mobile coffee cart market's projected growth from $1.8 billion to $3.8 billion. He contrasts the cart's low startup cost against the roughly $200,000+ typically needed to open a traditional coffee shop.

Best For

Someone comfortable with hands-on food service work and willing to secure event bookings or high-traffic locations, who wants a lower-capital entry into the coffee business than a storefront requires.

The Truth

The cost comparison to a traditional coffee shop is fair, and the low-overhead structure (no lease, no fixed location) is a real advantage. What the numbers don't show is the effort behind securing consistently good locations or event bookings, permitting/health requirements (which vary significantly by city and aren't addressed in the video), and that a 550-coffee, $3,000-profit day likely represents an already-established cart, not a first week of business.
