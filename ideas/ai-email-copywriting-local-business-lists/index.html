AI Email Copywriting Service

AI-assisted email copywriting that revives local businesses' unused email lists

What It Is

Offer to write short email sequences for local businesses that have customer lists they rarely use. The creator demonstrates a three-email sequence for a dentist's $49 first-time cleaning (a check-in, a reassurance about nerves, a final reminder), then feeds the AI a "humanize" prompt so the copy doesn't read like a robot. You sell the knowledge gap: many owners don't know they can do this themselves.

The Pitch

He argues good copy is in demand again because so much online writing reads like AI, and a list of 20,000 people should produce sales from even one sequence.

Who It's Best For

Good writers comfortable reaching out to local business owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

Email list reactivation is a legitimate quick win for many businesses, but the creator caps earning potential at roughly $3,000 to $5,000 a month and ranks it B-tier. Results depend on the list's quality and permission to email it. Sales outreach is the hard part. The video is a six-business tier list; the creator says results he mentions are not typical, and he promotes a free dropshipping course tied to a paid Shopify partnership.
