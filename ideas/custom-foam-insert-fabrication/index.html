Custom Foam Case Inserts

Custom foam insert fabrication for equipment and product cases

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (about $250-550 for foam stock and cutting tools)

What It Is

Cut custom foam inserts that hold tools, electronics, camera equipment, samples, or collectibles securely in place inside cases and shipping boxes, selling mainly to businesses (repair companies, photographers, tool sellers, sales teams) rather than casual individual buyers.

The Pitch

A beginner setup (foam stock, cutting equipment, adhesives, measuring tools, sample cases) costs roughly $250-550, using sharp knives, cutting guides, templates, or hot wire tools, with more advanced shapes outsourceable while a beginner handles measuring, prototyping, and assembly. The core challenge is precision: an opening too loose lets the product move, too tight and the customer struggles to remove it or the foam tears.

Best For

Someone with patience for precise measurement and prototyping work, comfortable pursuing recurring B2B clients (a single repeat business client can outweigh dozens of individual customers) rather than one-off consumer sales.

The Truth

The precision challenge described is real and not to be underestimated — foam insert work has a genuine skill floor around accurate measuring and prototyping, and the presenter's advice to start with simple rectangular/layered designs before attempting complex shapes is sound risk management for a beginner.
