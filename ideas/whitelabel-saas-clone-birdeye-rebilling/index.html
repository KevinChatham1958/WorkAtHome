White-Label Review Software

White-labeling a GoHighLevel-powered software clone of a named $500M SaaS company (Birdeye), with tiered pricing and usage rebilling

John Whitford · Steal This $500M Software Business Model ↗

What It Is

Study a specific, real, well-known SaaS company's product and pricing (the presenter uses Birdeye, a ~$500M reputation-management platform selling AI-powered review requests, business listing sync, and social/marketing tools) then recreate an equivalent branded software product using a white-labelable AI platform, building your own tiered pricing plans by turning specific features on or off per tier without any coding. Generate three layers of revenue: the monthly subscription fee itself, 'rebilling' (marking up the underlying per-use cost of things like text messages that get passed through to the client), and a one-time setup/onboarding fee ($1,000-2,000) that increases how long a client stays subscribed by making sure they're properly set up in their first 30 days.

The Pitch

The presenter, who says his own similarly-built white-label software reached over 1,000 paying customers and won the platform's top award, argues this beats time-based service businesses because clients treat a working software subscription like a utility bill they never think to cancel, and walks through cloning the specific competitor's feature set and pricing live.

Best For

Someone comfortable studying a competitor's product/pricing page and configuring a white-label software platform's tiers and rebilling settings, without needing to code anything.

The Truth

White-labeling an AI-powered local-business SaaS platform and reselling it under your own brand with tiered pricing and usage rebilling is a real, current business model already covered from multiple angles in this database, and the specific mechanics described (rebilling markup, setup-fee retention effect) are accurate to how these platforms work. The presenter's own 1,000-customer figure and the video's call to action (subscribe/like/comment for a free trial) make this a lead-generation video for his own affiliate-linked platform.
