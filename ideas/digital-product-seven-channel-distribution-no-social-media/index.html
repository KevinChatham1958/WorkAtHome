7 Channels to Sell Digital Products

Selling digital products through seven specific channels that don't require posting daily on social media

What It Is

Sell digital products using seven specific channels instead of daily social media posting: (1) marketplaces with built-in buyer intent (Etsy, Creative Fabrica, Teachers Pay Teachers, Amazon KDP); (2) Pinterest, used as a visual search engine rather than a follower-based platform; (3) an affiliate program following a 'WIN' framework — Worthy product, attractive Incentive (20-80% commission), and Necessities (ready-made graphics, testimonials, a tracking dashboard); (4) search-based YouTube videos that answer a specific question and link to the product in the description; (5) collaborations (podcast appearances, newsletter swaps, joint bundles), vetted for audience fit and partner reputation risk; (6) Google/SEO, including Etsy's own paid off-site ads; (7) email marketing, capturing an address at checkout and automating a nurture sequence to a list you own.

The Pitch

The presenter, a homeschooling stay-at-home military wife, reports $19,285.62 in revenue last month and $117,349.98 total from January through August 2026, all without relying on social media posting, and says her Etsy shop alone has made her family over $30,000 since opening.

Best For

Someone selling (or planning to sell) a digital product who wants to build multiple discovery channels rather than depending on one platform or daily content posting.

The Truth

All seven channels described are real, currently functioning discovery/sales mechanisms, and the specific mechanics (Etsy's off-site ads, Pinterest's search-driven traffic, the ownership advantage of an email list) are accurate. The presenter's own revenue figures are self-reported, and the video leads into her own paid community.
