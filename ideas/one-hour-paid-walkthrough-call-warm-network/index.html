Paid Audit Call

One-hour paid walkthrough or audit call sold to your warm network

Local & professional services · Near-zero cost (optional Stan Store about $20-25/month; PayPal or Stripe otherwise)

What It Is

You pick something you already know how to do that takes under an hour, such as a social media audit, LinkedIn profile review, website audit, pitch deck review or showing someone how to upload to YouTube or use Claude, and sell it as a one-on-one video call. The video's plan is to choose the offer in 10 minutes, write it up with a set number of spots and a price, set up a way to get paid (Stan Store, PayPal, Stripe or Square), then spend 30 minutes texting, emailing and DMing people who already know you, past customers and old leads. Pricing examples are $50 x 6, $100 x 3 or $150 x 2 to reach $300, with payment taken up front and the calls scheduled later.

The Pitch

The creator says this is the option most people should choose because there is somebody out there who doesn't know how to do what feels "normal and boring" to you. She argues you don't need an audience to find one buyer, saying 70%+ of the people she surveyed made their money from people they already knew.

Best For

People with a practical skill (social media, websites, software, a craft) and an existing circle of contacts who are comfortable asking them directly for a sale.

The Truth

Selling short paid calls to people who already trust you is a realistic way to earn first dollars, and the setup cost really is close to zero. The $300-in-an-hour framing depends entirely on having warm contacts who want the specific skill; many people will get no takers on the first try, and this is a one-off push rather than a steady income unless repeated. The survey figure and the student result mentioned ($105,000 since March) are the creator's own unaudited claims. The video repeatedly promotes the creator's paid "10K in Q4" challenge, and she also mentions her own Lovable training.
