FBA Wholesale With Small Brands

Amazon FBA wholesale with small brands (value-first pitch, then exclusive deals)

What It Is

You buy products wholesale directly from smaller brands and resell them on Amazon through FBA, where Amazon stores and ships orders. Using SmartScout, you look for brands in one repeat-purchase category doing under $3 million a month, with three or more Prime sellers, and where Amazon itself is in stock less than half the time. You find decision-makers' emails with Hunter.io, LinkedIn or Claude and send 30-50 emails a day offering value: a competitor revenue report, keeping their minimum advertised price, removing unauthorized sellers and improving their listings. You start with small test orders of 25-50 units, then reorder based on real sales, and later pitch your best brands to make you their only Amazon seller.

The Pitch

The creator says he has taken a business to over $50 million in total Amazon sales and now runs it about four hours a week. He argues small brands welcome resellers who help them, while big brands ignore small buyers. He says one brand deal from 2021 still nets him $20,000-$40,000, and that a student who won an exclusive deal grew one brand account from about $20,000 to $300,000 a month in sales.

Who It’s Best For

People with real money to put into inventory who are comfortable with email outreach, negotiating with brands and managing cash flow.

The Truth (as we see it)

Brand-direct wholesale is a real and established Amazon model, and the creator's step-by-step order (research, outreach, test orders, reorders) is sensible. It requires substantial inventory spending, steady cold outreach and sales calls, and margins can be squeezed by other sellers or Amazon itself. The revenue and student figures are the creator's own and unverified, and "$50 million" refers to sales, not profit. The video ends by sending viewers to his free training, which leads into his paid community and in-house tools.
