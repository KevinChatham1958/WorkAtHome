What It Is

You search the free Meta Ad Library for businesses that are already paying for ads but whose ads look low quality (his example is a roofing company), and get their contact details. You screen-record one of their existing ads, upload it to Higgsfield's Supercomputer chat with a prompt, and get several new variations built on their own script and message. You gift one or two free, then offer three new ads a week (12 a month) for $2,000 a month, about $170 an ad; five clients reaches $10,000 a month. He notes Higgsfield can also make posters and UGC-style videos and connect to ChatGPT or Claude to run ads on a budget.

The Pitch

The creator says businesses must keep testing new ads and agencies can charge thousands for a single one, so 12 ads a month for $2,000 is "an absolute steal." He presents it as the steady, monthly-paying alternative to one-off content work.

Who It’s Best For

People who follow ad performance closely and can handle ongoing client relationships and outreach.

The Truth (as we see it)

Targeting businesses that already pay for ads is sensible, and retainers are steadier than one-off jobs. But clients judge ad creative by results, the figures shown are a pricing example rather than client results, and obviously AI-made video can hurt trust-based trades like roofing. Many agencies and freelancers now use the same tools, so expect price competition. Gadzhi says he made a "sizable investment" in Higgsfield, the tool the video is built around, and he offers a free prompt pack and a MacBook giveaway for commenters.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
