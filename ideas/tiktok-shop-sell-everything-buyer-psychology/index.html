TikTok Shop Affiliate Selling

TikTok Shop affiliate selling across every product category using buyer psychology and a product-spy tool

Shop House podcast · $1M/Month On Tiktok Shop - How Flor Does It ↗

What It Is

Build a TikTok Shop affiliate business without committing to a single product niche, instead testing 2-3 new products daily across any category (skincare, electronics, household goods) using a competitor-spy tool to see which products and video hooks are already converting, and selling by describing the emotional transformation a product provides rather than its literal features.

The Pitch

The guest, an immigrant from Cuba who started TikTok Shop affiliate work about a year before this interview, reports over $10 million in GMV this year and $1-1.9 million in personal monthly commission at her peak, eventually buying a $1 million home. She describes her core sales approach as selling the feeling a product creates ("you'll never be sick again" instead of describing a water filter) rather than its specs, testing 2-3 new products daily using a spy tool to find videos and products already proven to convert, and has since built a paid academy teaching the content and psychology methods to over 3,400 students.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable posting high volumes of short-form video (the guest describes doing up to 20 videos a day at her peak) and willing to treat product testing as a numbers game across categories rather than specializing narrowly.

The Truth (as we see it)

The buyer-psychology framing (sell the transformation, not the product) is a genuinely transferable sales principle beyond just TikTok Shop, and the emphasis on testing volume over perfectionism reflects how affiliate content actually performs at scale. That said, the headline revenue figures are self-reported without independent verification, the described daily workload (12-14 hours during her early ramp-up) was intense, and the video is sponsored by the product-research tool being demonstrated (Shop Shark) — worth knowing going in.
