AI-built Flat HTML Websites for Local Contractors, Modeled on Top-ranked Company Designs

Local & professional services · Near-zero cost

Originally published Sep 14, 2026 — Jesse Cunningham

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jesse Cunningham · How I Build $11.57 AI Websites that Sell for $1,000s (No Tech Skills) ↗

What It Is

You use an AI model to build a fast, SEO-structured static (flat HTML/CSS) website for a local service business such as a pool builder or remodeler, borrowing design ideas from the websites of top-performing companies in that trade. The finished site is either sold to a local business owner or kept and used for rank-and-rent lead generation. Clients are local contractors who want a professional site that ranks on Google.

The Pitch

The presenter picks design inspiration from companies on the Qualified Remodeler Top 500 list, reasoning that their verified financials show their sites produce work, then tells the AI which sections and features to emulate (not copy one-to-one). His key tip is to generate a logo first and feed it to the model so it picks up a consistent color scheme, then have it pull free stock images from sites like Pexels. In his head-to-head test, Claude's model produced a near-finished homepage in about 15 minutes while the GPT model he tried did poorly. Because the site is flat HTML it can be hosted for free, so his stated cost is roughly $11.57 for the domain, and he says such a site can still sell to a contractor for thousands of dollars if you have a trusted relationship.

Best For

People comfortable prompting AI tools who can also find and win local business clients, or who want low-cost sites for rank-and-rent. Useful for someone with an eye for what makes a trade website convincing even without coding skills.

The Truth

The core mechanic is credible: AI models can now produce good-looking static homepages quickly, and static hosting is genuinely free or nearly so. However, the demo only shows a homepage, not a complete site with service pages, forms, and real images, and ranking it on Google is asserted rather than demonstrated. The 'sells for thousands' claim depends on sales skill and client relationships, which the presenter admits, and the work is becoming commoditized for exactly the reasons he describes. The video references his own 'microsite blueprint' prompt and closes by promoting his paid SEO academy community (7-day free trial), where the structure guide lives.
