What It Is

Creating personalized, custom AI-generated pet portraits, in a specific illustration style such as minimalist line art, from a customer's own photo, sold as a made-to-order digital product on Etsy.

The Pitch

The presenter walks through the reasoning live: personalized products avoid the saturation of generic AI-template shops since each one is made to order from a real photo. He identifies the typical buyer as someone buying a gift for a pet-obsessed friend or family member rather than pet owners buying for themselves, and demonstrates the actual creation process, uploading a customer's dog photo to an AI tool along with a style reference image, generating a finished portrait in under a minute, then using Claude to write the full Etsy listing from the finished image.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to do light per-order customization work for each sale, uploading a customer's specific photo, rather than a fully passive, pre-made product.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific pricing given, $18 to $28 per portrait, and market-size figures cited are stated without a named source in the video, so should be treated as illustrative rather than independently verified. The core creation process is demonstrated live and is genuinely as fast as claimed.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
