Career Expertise as an AI Offer

Turning your career expertise into a paid AI-assisted offer

AI & automation · Low startup cost

Originally published Aug 3, 2026 — The Calum Johnson Show and Edwina - Voice of AI

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

The Calum Johnson Show and Edwina - Voice of AI · The Mom Who Mastered Claude: The Fastest Way To Get RICH In The New Era Of AI (3 Step Framework) ↗

What It Is

Use a voice-to-text tool to dump everything you've actually done across your career into an updated resume, then feed that resume into Claude to identify the specific expertise you already have that businesses would pay for, and package that into a coaching or consulting offer rather than trying to become a generalist AI expert.

The Pitch

The guest, a former insurance broker who built her own book of business rather than working for someone else, argues most people already have 10-30 years of unrecognized experience buried in their job history, and that the real opportunity is packaging that specific expertise, not learning to code or becoming a technical AI consultant. She frames the math plainly: a handful of clients at $2,000 to $12,000 each is the realistic path to significant income.

Who It's Best For

Someone with real, specific professional experience (10+ years in a field) who's more interested in packaging what they already know than learning a new technical skill from scratch.

The Truth (as we see it)

This overlaps with other "package your expertise into an AI offer" entries already in this database; the general advice isn't unique to this video. What's distinct here is the specific resume-plus-voice-dump extraction method as a starting exercise, which is concrete enough to actually try. The $2,000 to $12,000-per-client math is presented as typical, not a guarantee, and no specific client names or account numbers are shown to verify the speaker's own results.
