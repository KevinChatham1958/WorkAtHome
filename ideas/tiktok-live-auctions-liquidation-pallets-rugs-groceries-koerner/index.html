TikTok Live Pallet Auctions

TikTok Live auction selling of liquidation pallets (rugs, Costco groceries) from home

What It Is

You buy liquidation pallets, such as rugs from B-Stock at about $12 each or Costco grocery pallets, and sell the items one by one in TikTok Live auctions from your home. The guest runs fast 'sudden death' auctions with no reserve: five seconds, highest bid wins, and a helper labels each sold item as he goes. He splits multi-packs into smaller lots, packs and ships from a garage-turned-warehouse, and uses TikTok's Collections by TikTok shipping, which he says cut his costs by about 40 percent. His first live, on a brand-new account with no followers, sold about $700 of rugs in an hour.

The Pitch

The guest says he reached about $14,000 to $15,700 a month in sales by his fourth month at 30 to 40 percent net margins, with a $2,000 grocery pallet bringing in about $6,000. He argues TikTok sends live viewers even to new accounts, and that items he couldn't sell anywhere else moved quickly in live auctions. He says faster payout tiers unlock as your selling history grows, letting you reinvest sooner.

Who It’s Best For

Outgoing people comfortable on camera who have space at home to store and pack inventory and some cash to buy pallets.

The Truth (as we see it)

Live auction selling on TikTok Shop is a real and growing channel, and the guest is open about his numbers, his margins and the setbacks. Expect a cash crunch early: payouts take about 30 days at first, and the guest sold his own car to keep buying inventory. Margins depend on buying pallets well and on shipping perks that TikTok can change at any time, and the work involves lifting, packing and long live sessions. The figures are the guest's own and unaudited, and the host sells a $19 business plan.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
