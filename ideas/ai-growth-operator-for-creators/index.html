Growth Operator for Creators

Becoming a 'growth operator' handling the business side (funnels, emails, webinars) for content creators who only want to make content

Marketing & content · Low (Claude subscription, an AI image tool for design)

Originally published Jul 31, 2026 — Patrick Dang

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

One of 10 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Work behind the scenes for a content creator, building and running everything needed to monetize their audience (landing pages, email sequences, webinar slide decks, ad campaigns) using Claude for copywriting and structure and an AI image tool for design, so the creator only has to show up and talk; find creators with a sizeable following who don't yet sell anything and pitch becoming the operational partner who builds that out.

The Pitch

The presenter, who employs his own growth operators rather than building his funnels himself, scores this 18/25 (ease 2, speed 3, validated 5, competition 3-4, long-term 5), calling it one of the most profitable opportunities on his list despite its higher skill requirement, and recommends charging a base fee plus 10-50% of resulting profit depending on negotiated value.

Who It's Best For

Someone with genuine funnel/marketing-systems skill willing to take on a business-operations role rather than a purely creative one.

The Truth (as we see it)

Creators increasingly outsourcing their business/monetization operations to a dedicated operator is a real, observable industry pattern the presenter describes from his own team structure. Pricing and difficulty ratings are the presenter's own framing, not independently verified, and the video promotes his free training and paid coaching.
