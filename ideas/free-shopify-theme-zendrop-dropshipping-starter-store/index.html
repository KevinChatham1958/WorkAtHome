Pre-Built Shopify Dropshipping Store

Pre-built Shopify dropshipping store with Zendrop auto-fulfillment

E-commerce · Low startup cost (Shopify $1/month for 3 months, Zendrop usage fees once selling, plus ad spend)

What It Is

The video walks through a free tool (linked in the description) that sets up a Shopify store in about five minutes: you pick a niche such as pets, choose colors and a banner, connect a new Shopify account and install the Zendrop app, which loads pre-selected "winning products" already linked to a supplier. When orders come in, Zendrop can fulfill them automatically from China or US warehouses, so you never handle stock. You then customize product pages and prices and drive traffic, typically with ads, earning the gap between your price and the supplier cost.

The Pitch

The creator says he wishes he had this software when he started seven years ago and calls it a cheap way to start an online business, especially now that AI tools can do much of the work. He says he broke even for three and a half months before it worked and urges people to stick with it.

Best For

Beginners who want to try e-commerce without holding inventory and who can afford some months of ad testing.

The Truth

Pre-loaded "winning products" are offered to many beginners at once, so your store may look like many others, and success mostly depends on product choice, ads and customer service rather than the setup. Shopify is cheap at first, but Zendrop charges once you scale (it shows up to $79 per 30 days, with a $179/year Plus upsell), and advertising is the main cost. Shipping times from China and refunds can hurt reviews. The store tool and Zendrop are promoted through the creator's links, which likely earn him a commission, and he also promotes his own Ad Library software.
