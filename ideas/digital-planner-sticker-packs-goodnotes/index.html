Digital Planner Sticker Packs

Digital planner sticker packs for iPad planner apps

Digital & subscription products · Near-zero cost (Canva or Procreate)

What It Is

You design packs of decorative "stickers" (small image files) that people drop into digital planners in iPad apps like GoodNotes and Notability. A pack is designed once in Canva or Procreate and sold as a download for about $3 to $15; top sellers offer packs with hundreds of stickers. There is no printing involved. Ongoing work means releasing fresh packs for seasons, moods and holidays.

The Pitch

The creator says planner fans are obsessive repeat buyers who purchase a new pack for every season and holiday, and once they like your style they keep coming back. He says top sellers move thousands of packs.

Who It's Best For

People with an eye for cute, clean design who enjoy making visuals and don't mind releasing new packs regularly.

The Truth (as we see it)

Digital planner stickers are an established niche with loyal buyers, and the startup cost really is close to zero. The creator gives no specific sales data for this product, and the space is very competitive, with low prices per pack, so income tends to come from a large, steadily updated catalog rather than a few listings. As he notes, it is not a make-once-and-forget product. The creator sells a one-on-one publishing mentorship promoted in this video.
