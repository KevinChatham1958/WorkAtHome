30-Day Personal Brand Launch

30-day personal brand launch system built on copying proven video hooks before creating original content

Content & personal brand · Free to start (just a smartphone; no equipment or editing software required)

Originally published Aug 24, 2026 — Sabrina Ramonov

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Sabrina Ramonov · How I'd Start a 1-Person Business + Personal Brand with AI in 30 Days ↗

What It Is

Choose a content niche using a three-circle framework (something you enjoy, something you're good at, and something people already spend money on), then for the first 17 videos, deliberately copy the exact opener/hook (word-for-word script, visual style, and delivery) from other creators' already-proven, high-view videos in that niche — swapping in your own specific content after the hook — before transitioning to fully original content once you've built intuition for what makes an opener work.

The Pitch

The presenter, who grew to 3 million followers and sold her first AI company for over $10 million, argues that copying proven openers isn't cheating but is how most successful creators actually got their first wins, since a hook already getting hundreds of thousands of views has effectively been "voted" successful by a large audience; she recommends no special equipment (a phone's front camera and the TikTok app's built-in teleprompter feature are enough) and to treat the first 17 videos explicitly as a practice period rather than expecting immediate original success.

Best For

Someone willing to be on camera and comfortable being explicitly imitative early on as a deliberate learning strategy, rather than feeling pressure to be original from video one.

The Truth

The specific, unusually candid method (systematically copying proven hooks for a defined 17-video practice period, not "be authentic" platitudes) is a genuinely more actionable strategy than most creator-growth advice, and treating early videos as practice rather than a launch reduces the pressure that stops many people from starting. Growing to 3 million followers and an eight-figure exit represent an extreme outlier outcome, not a typical result, even if the specific tactic described is legitimately useful on its own.
