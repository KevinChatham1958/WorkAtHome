Mini Tart Shell Supply

Supplying mini tart and pie shells to bakeries and caterers using a shell-forming machine, rather than selling desserts directly

Physical & print products · Moderate for the list (average starter machine price around $650, one of the more expensive, but plannable under $1,000)

What It Is

Use a tartlet/mini pie shell forming machine to press dough into consistent, uniform tart or pie shells, then sell the empty shells wholesale to bakeries, cafes, catering kitchens, dessert shops, and event suppliers who want to sell finished desserts but don't want staff time spent hand-shaping shells, rather than building your own retail dessert brand.

The Pitch

Presented as an unglamorous machine most beginners skip, with the actual opportunity being becoming the boring, reliable B2B supplier behind bakeries rather than competing with them directly for retail customers.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable approaching bakeries and catering businesses directly to become a reliable wholesale supplier, rather than building a consumer-facing dessert brand.

The Truth (as we see it)

Wholesale supply of semi-finished baked components to bakeries and caterers is a real, established B2B food-industry practice. No specific revenue example is given; food-safety/hygiene compliance requirements for wholesale food supply will apply and vary by location.
