What It Is

Instead of selling small AI tools to many clients, you aim for one client paying around $10,000 a month: an owner-run business doing $1-10 million a year that wants to grow. You use the knowledge from your current or past job to spot the gap holding the business back, position yourself as a growth partner, and show a clear return on the investment. You don't build the AI systems yourself; you hire implementation partners and, the creator says, keep over 70% profit per project, while making sure the results happen so they become case studies.

The Pitch

The creator says his businesses generate over $6 million a month delivering AI services, and that people with work experience have an unfair advantage over young beginners because clients pay for judgment, not labor. One big client is easier to run alongside a 9-5 than five small ones.

Who It’s Best For

Mid-career professionals with real industry experience and contacts who can hold confident conversations with business owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling high-value projects on industry expertise is a sound approach, and the advice to focus on one well-chosen client is sensible. But landing a $10,000-a-month deal with no track record is hard, and outsourcing delivery means you carry the risk if a partner underperforms. His revenue figures are his own and unaudited. He offers one-on-one partnership calls with his team, so the video is also a pitch for his program.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
