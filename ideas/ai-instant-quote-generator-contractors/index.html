What It Is

Convert a contractor or quote-based business's real pricing structure (base rates, labor, add-ons, often scattered across the owner's head and a personal spreadsheet) into a document Claude reads as pricing rules, then build a short 5-6 question customer-facing intake form whose answers feed those rules to generate a clean, branded estimate delivered by email same-day, with a booking link embedded directly in the estimate so the customer can convert immediately — sold to contractors, roofers, remodelers, landscapers, movers, and law firms who quote work before doing it.

The Pitch

The sales math: a contractor sending 12 quotes a month and closing 3 who speeds up quote delivery from days to same-day can realistically close 2 more of those 12 (going from 3 to 5), which at a $2,500 average job is an extra $5,000/month — meaning a $2,000-4,000 build pays for itself within weeks. The underlying insight is that slow quote turnaround (the classic "I'll get back to you" that becomes a kitchen-table typing session at 9pm) loses jobs the contractor mistakenly attributes to being outbid on price.

Who It’s Best For

Someone targeting service businesses where quotes are genuinely customized (not fixed-price) and slow turnaround is a believable, provable pain point, comfortable extracting an owner's informal pricing logic into a structured document.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is customer-facing (the end customer fills out the form and receives the estimate directly), which differs from a similar existing entry in this database built as an internal tool for a business's own employees to use — different mechanic, different buyer experience. The $5,000/month projected gain is the presenter's illustrative math from assumed conversion improvements, not a verified case study with a named client's actual before/after numbers.
