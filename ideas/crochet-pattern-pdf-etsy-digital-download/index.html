Crochet pattern PDFs sold as Etsy digital downloads

What It Is

You design a crochet project, such as a small stuffed doll, write up the pattern and sell the PDF as an instant download on Etsy. The creator found a bestselling pattern listing estimated at about $3,544 a month, in over 20 buyers' carts and not on sale. That shop has only six products (four patterns, two physical items) and is estimated at about $3,800 a month in total.

The Pitch

The creator says a pattern is a good way for crafty sellers to add passive income alongside physical goods "without increasing the amount of time you actually have to spend working on your shop." She reads the full-price sales and simple title as signs of strong organic demand and less competition.

Best For

Experienced crocheters or textile crafters who can design original projects and write clear instructions.

The Truth

One hit pattern carrying a tiny shop is unusual, so treat it as a best case, not a norm. Writing a pattern that strangers can follow takes real skill and testing, and some buyers will message with questions. Patterns are easily shared, so expect some loss to copying. Revenue figures come from Profit Tree's estimates, not the sellers' own records. The creator uses an affiliate link for Profit Tree (50% off a year of its pro plan), sells Canva templates herself, and promotes her own Etsy launch videos.
