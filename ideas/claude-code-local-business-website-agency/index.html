Free Websites With Maintenance Fees

Rebuilding local-business websites with Claude Code, given away free with a recurring maintenance fee

Source video

Jesse Cunningham · How I Make $5,000 in Two Hours with Claude Code ↗

What It Is

Find local businesses (via a plain Google search) with an outdated or poorly built website, use Claude Code connected to WordPress to rebuild a genuinely better custom site in a few hours, then offer it to the business owner for free, monetizing not through an upfront project fee but through an ongoing monthly hosting-and-maintenance retainer, with additional local-SEO "microsites" offered as a paid upsell.

The Pitch

The video is structured as a live, timed demonstration responding to a skeptic's comment that nothing worth $5,000 can be built in two hours: starting from a real local roofing company's existing site, Claude Code is walked through scraping the public sitemap, drafting a full new site structure (service pages, location pages for each metro the business serves), and building a working WordPress site on a real theme framework in under an hour of hands-on time. The monetization pitch is specific: give the finished site away for free (removing the buyer's biggest objection, price) in exchange for a modest recurring fee (the video suggests $500 a month) for hosting and changes, which is easier to sell than a large upfront project fee and builds more durable, recurring revenue.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with genuine comfort directing an AI coding tool through a multi-step build process, who's willing to do cold outreach to local businesses rather than waiting for inbound interest.

The Truth (as we see it)

The live demonstration format, with a visible timer and a real (if unnamed) business's actual site used as the starting point, is a stronger transparency signal than a purely hypothetical pitch, though the video never shows an actual completed sale, so the "$5,000 in two hours" framing is the presenter's opinion of the finished site's fair value, not a verified transaction. The give-it-away-free-then-charge-recurring strategy for winning local-business clients is a general approach that shows up in more than one form across WAH creator content, worth knowing if you're comparing several similar-sounding pitches. The video also promotes the presenter's own paid course community throughout, a financial incentive separate from whether the underlying technique works.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
