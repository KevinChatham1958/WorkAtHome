Backflow Valve Testing

Running a legally-mandated commercial backflow valve testing service

Local & professional services · Very low (certification costs a few hundred dollars and takes days)

What It Is

Get certified as a backflow tester and test the backflow prevention valves that restaurants, hospitals, and office buildings with sprinkler systems are legally required to have inspected once a year in nearly every state, or the water utility can shut the property's water off entirely; each test takes under an hour.

The Pitch

The presenter charges $75-400 per device, with a mid-size commercial building often having 3-4 devices on site, framing this as selling the only thing standing between a property owner and the water company shutting their building off, meaning customers don't haggle.

Best For

Someone willing to get a quick, inexpensive certification and comfortable managing a recurring annual testing schedule across commercial properties.

The Truth

Backflow testing requirements are real, codified legal obligations in most states, and the described low-cost, low-barrier certification path is accurate to how this trade actually works. No specific personal case study or revenue figure is given beyond per-device pricing.
