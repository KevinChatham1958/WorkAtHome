Etched Tumblers and Glassware

Personalized tumbler and glassware etching business marketed through process videos

Physical & print products · Very low startup cost (roughly $115 for a portable engraving tool, under $500 to start overall)

What It Is

Buy blank tumblers (or glass, metal, or other surfaces) wholesale, personalize them with a name or design using a laser engraver or a portable high-speed engraving pen, and sell them primarily by posting the etching process itself as short-form video content.

The Pitch

Zaback profiles Stephanie Stroman, who sold 102 cups in one week ($2,550) using a laser engraver and posting the process on TikTok, turning roughly $10 blank tumblers into $25-60 personalized pieces (60-80% margins by his estimate). He frames the process video itself as free marketing, since viewers are drawn to watching the personalization happen, not just seeing the finished product.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable posting short-form video of their own hands-on work process, who wants a low-cost physical product business where the content and the product creation are the same activity.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core economics (cheap blanks, high per-unit margin) are genuine and don't require a full laser setup — Zaback notes cheaper handheld tools work too. The business is heavily dependent on the process videos actually getting traction on social media, which isn't guaranteed and isn't really addressed in the numbers shown; one good week from one creator isn't evidence of a repeatable income stream without that visibility.
