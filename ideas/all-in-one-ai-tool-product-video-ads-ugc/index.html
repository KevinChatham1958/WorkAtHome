AI Product Video Ads

AI-generated product video ads and UGC-style clips from a single all-in-one tool (affiliate link)

AI & automation · Very low startup cost (paid credits on an AI video platform)

Originally published Oct 19, 2026 — Tiger Insight™

Tiger Insight™ · This Ai Website Makes me $10,000/Month ↗

What It Is

You upload a photo of a product to an all-in-one AI video platform (the creator uses TopView AI), tell its video agent you want an ad, and it writes a script, generates key frames and renders a short product video. The same tool can make UGC-style clips where an AI 'creator' unboxes or talks about the product, choose length (up to a few minutes) and aspect ratio for TikTok or Reels, and edit just one section of a clip without regenerating it all. The money comes from using these videos for your own faceless content channel or selling video ads to product sellers who want promotional clips.

The Pitch

The creator says he made over $10,000 in August from his first faceless AI channel, run entirely with AI automation, and that one tool can now handle video creation, editing, motion video and UGC ads. He shows a perfume photo turned into a polished ad and an unboxing-style clip in a few minutes, arguing anyone can do this without filming or editing skills.

Who It's Best For

People who want to make short promotional videos without being on camera and are comfortable experimenting with AI tools and finding their own buyers or audience.

The Truth (as we see it)

The demo shows the tool working, but the video is essentially a product walkthrough with a sign-up link in the description, which is likely an affiliate link, so the creator benefits when viewers join. It never explains how the claimed $10,000 was earned (ad revenue, client work or affiliate commissions), and that figure is unverified. AI-generated ads of real brand products raise trademark and disclosure questions, and AI 'testimonials' from people who never used a product can mislead buyers or break platform rules. Earning from this still requires finding paying clients or building an audience, which the video does not cover.
