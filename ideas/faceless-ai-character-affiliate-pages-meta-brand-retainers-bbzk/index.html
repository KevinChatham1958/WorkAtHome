AI-Character Affiliate Pages

Faceless AI-character affiliate pages on Facebook and Instagram (commissions plus brand retainers)

AI & automation · Low startup cost (Claude, AI image and video credits; later optional virtual assistants)

Originally published Sep 17, 2026 — Eric Swanson

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Eric Swanson · Unfortunately, Making Money With AI Really Is This Simple ↗

What It Is

You use Claude to research a problem that a large, already-spending audience has (the example is foot pain among people 45 and older), including how it feels day to day, and to design a believable AI 'authority' character for that audience. You generate the character's image (Nano Banana Pro via Higgsfield), animate it speaking an AI-assisted script in 8-second clips (Google Flow), stitch the clips into roughly 45-second videos and post several a day on Facebook and Instagram. Viewers who comment a keyword get an automated DM with an affiliate link, and you earn Amazon or brand commissions, sometimes 50% paid directly by the brand, plus monthly retainers for a set number of posts. The creator later hires overseas virtual assistants to generate and edit the videos while he writes scripts.

The Pitch

The creator says this pays him over $19,000 a month in profit from four tools and six posts a day. He argues Meta platforms, unlike TikTok, tolerate AI content and reach an older audience with money and health problems, and that brand retainers cover tool and assistant costs while commissions grow.

Best For

People comfortable with AI video tools and persuasive copywriting who want an on-camera-free content business.

The Truth (as we see it)

The income figure is the creator's own and unaudited, and the brand retainers he describes come through AI Creator Lab, a program he repeatedly pitches with an application link, so the video doubles as an ad for it. He admits he was banned from Amazon Associates, and his first two months produced only two sales. Selling health products through invented AI 'people' and 'drowning' viewers in pain-focused copy carries real risk: platforms and regulators expect disclosure of AI personas and paid endorsements, and health claims are scrutinised. Treat this as a high-volume content-marketing business, not an easy one.
