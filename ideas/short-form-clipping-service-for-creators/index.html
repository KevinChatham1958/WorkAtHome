What It Is

You turn creators' long videos, podcasts and livestreams into short clips for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube Shorts. AI tools find the best moments from transcripts and make first-pass edits, then you polish them. Creators pay monthly by volume, sometimes with a share of resulting revenue.

The Pitch

Dang says every long-form creator, himself included, needs short-form help. His suggested pitch is to make a few clips for a creator who lacks a short-form team and send them over. He quotes around $1,000 per month and up, with possible profit shares if your clips grow their business. He scores it 20 of 25 and acknowledges clipping agencies and clipping courses already exist.

Best For

Fits people who already edit video or want to learn, and who follow short-form trends closely.

The Truth

The demand is real and spec-clip pitching is a sensible entry, but competition is significant, including low-cost freelancers and AI clipping software that creators can run themselves. Dang himself questions how long the opportunity lasts as AI makes clipping easier to bring in-house. Basic editing software may cost a modest subscription. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
