Shrimp Breeding for Fish Stores

Neocaridina shrimp breeding business selling to local fish stores

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (about $150-300 for a single-tank starter setup with livestock)

What It Is

Breed Neocaridina shrimp (a popular, colorful freshwater aquarium species) in a home tank setup, selling to local fish stores as a consistent wholesale buyer and to aquarium clubs/online sales at higher per-unit prices but lower volume.

The Pitch

A 10-gallon setup with starter livestock runs $150-250, or a 20-gallon all-in-one kit for $250-300. Low-grade shrimp sell $1-3 each, mid-grade $3-5, high-grade $5-8, and breeding females $8-15. The presenter frames this explicitly as supplemental income that compounds slowly, not fast money, and warns that failing to cull low-grade offspring causes color quality to degrade across generations, drifting an entire colony back to plain wild-type coloring (and losing the price point) within a year.

Best For

Someone with patience for a slow-building, low-maintenance supplemental income stream, who's willing to actively cull a colony to maintain color quality rather than letting it breed freely.

The Truth

The presenter is upfront that this is slow supplemental income, not a primary income replacement, which is a more honest framing than most livestock-breeding side hustle content — the ongoing culling discipline required to maintain value is a real, easy-to-skip maintenance task that determines whether the business stays profitable.
