AI-built Lead-capture Websites for Local Service Businesses (WhatsApp Click-to-chat, Monthly Fee)

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (AI builder usage, about $7 per finished site according to the creator)

Luna Vega · ChatGPT Astra Built Me a Digital Product That PRINTS Money! ↗

What It Is

You use an AI model with built-in hosting (the creator uses OpenAI's newest model and its 'sites' feature) to build a simple one-page website for a local service business, such as a residential cleaning company: headline, services, pricing, testimonials and a contact form. The key addition is a green 'Message us on WhatsApp' button using WhatsApp's free wa.me click-to-chat link with a pre-filled message, so enquiries land straight on the owner's phone (a free form service like Web3Forms is the fallback). You find prospects on Google Maps by searching a trade plus a city and picking businesses with no website or a broken one, build them a demo with their real name and services, and send them the live link. Instead of a one-time build fee, you charge around $100-150 a month to keep the site running and the lead capture working.

The Pitch

The creator argues that local owners don't want flashy 3D sites; they want customers to find them and contact them, so a site that delivers leads justifies a monthly fee. She says building is now cheap and fast (about $7 per finished project) and that sending a ready-made demo with the owner's business name on it is a warm pitch rather than a cold one.

Best For

People comfortable reaching out to small business owners by message or email who want recurring client income from simple web work.

The Truth

Building a basic one-page site with click-to-chat is genuinely easy now, and the WhatsApp link trick works as described with no API needed. The hard part, which the creator admits, is selling: you must contact many businesses, and many will ignore unsolicited demo sites. The $100-150/month price and the $7-per-site cost are the creator's figures; you also need to handle a custom domain, hosting reliability and basic updates to justify a monthly fee. WhatsApp-first contact works best where customers use WhatsApp heavily (common outside the US). The video ends by promoting the creator's free training, which is a funnel toward her paid offers.
