What It Is

You build a bundle of everything one person needs for a single life event or project, such as a wedding planning kit with a budget spreadsheet, guest list tracker, timeline checklist and vendor comparison sheet. The creator uses Claude to list the four or five pieces a buyer needs, build each one, give them a matching look and add a 'start here' overview page. The finished kit is delivered as one folder or link and sold on Etsy as a single product. The same format works for a new baby, a house move, starting a small business or planning a trip.

The Pitch

The creator says bundles pay better because one sale is worth four: instead of a $15 spreadsheet you sell a $49 kit, and the buyer is happier not having to hunt for the other pieces. He shows Etsy research data suggesting wedding planner listings bring in thousands a month, including a month-old listing he says is growing fast. His framing is that at $50 a product, about 20 sales a month clears $1,000.

Who It’s Best For

People who are organized, like building practical planning tools and are willing to research Etsy niches before building.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling planning bundles on Etsy is a real and established digital product category, and pricing a kit above single templates is sensible. The revenue figures come from a third-party estimate tool, not from the sellers, and are the creator's presentation rather than verified results. Wedding planning in particular is crowded, so a narrower audience or a clearly better kit is likely needed to get noticed. The creator says Claude builds it in minutes, but buyers on Etsy compare many near-identical AI-built listings, so niche choice and presentation matter more than build speed. The creator sells a paid program (a 20-product-type module plus traffic training), gives away the prompts in exchange for an email address, and repeatedly promotes the Everbee research tool through a referral link.
