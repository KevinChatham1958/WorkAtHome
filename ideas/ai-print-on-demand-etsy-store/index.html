What It Is

Use free AI chatbots to brainstorm clothing designs and Google's free Flow tool to generate the actual artwork, then list the designs on print-on-demand apparel through Printify, sold via an Etsy storefront. Printify charges the manufacturing cost per sale; Etsy pays out the sale price minus its own fees once the item ships.

The Pitch

Sarah runs this business herself and is candid that it isn't entirely free to run, despite the AI tools themselves being free: Etsy has a one-time $15 setup fee, and because Etsy doesn't release the sale proceeds until after the item ships, you need to be able to front the manufacturing cost (roughly $9 on a $20 shirt in her example) before you're reimbursed. For anyone without even that small amount of float, she points to Printify's own free pop-up storefront tool as a genuinely $0 alternative to Etsy, which pays out as a royalty after Printify covers its own production cost.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with basic AI image generation and willing to either front a small amount of production cash on Etsy, or accept a smaller, simpler storefront through Printify's free option instead.

The Truth (as we see it)

Sarah discloses she runs a successful version of this business herself, which is a real credibility signal, but also means she has a direct financial interest in recommending the specific paid tools she promotes alongside this content (a Canva Pro affiliate link and her own paid course). The core mechanics of the business (AI design plus print-on-demand fulfillment) don't require either of those paid products to work.
