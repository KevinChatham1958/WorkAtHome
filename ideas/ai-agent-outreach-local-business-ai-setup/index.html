AI Agent-Powered Client Outreach

Finding AI-implementation clients with an agent that filters, audits and demos for local businesses

AI & automation · Near-zero cost (an AI agent subscription; optional gift baskets for top prospects)

What It Is

To win clients for AI setup work (AI receptionists, missed-call text-back, booking pages), you have an agent comb Google Maps for one niche in one city: 4+ stars, 50+ reviews, no online booking, owner never replies to reviews. It tests their after-hours phone and contact-form response times, writes a one-page audit for the best prospects, and builds a working demo with their logo. You record a short Loom walking through the audit and send it.

The Pitch

The creator says this narrows 100 businesses down to the few that can afford you and clearly need you, so you pitch nine likely buyers instead of a hundred strangers. He recommends unusual niches with fewer competing pitches and offers $500 to the first viewer who lands a paying client this way.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants to sell AI setup services to small businesses and can follow up personally once the agent has done the research.

The Truth (as we see it)

The filtering and audit idea is sensible and cheaper than blind cold outreach, but you still have to sell, deliver and support the AI systems you promise, which takes real skill. Auto-filling dozens of contact forms and calling businesses to test them may annoy owners or breach some platforms' rules. The creator sells an AI consulting program (Playmakers AI), which this pitch leads into.
