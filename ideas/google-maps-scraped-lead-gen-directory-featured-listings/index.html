Local Lead-Gen Directory

Single-niche, single-state lead-gen directory built from scraped Google Maps data and sold as featured listings

Jesse Cunningham · How to Build AI Directories on Easy Mode ↗

What It Is

You build a website that lists every business in one boring, high-ticket local niche across one state (for example, custom pool builders in Florida), with state pages, city pages and individual listing pages. The data comes from a paid scrape of Google Maps, which you filter, clean and enrich with AI, then publish as a site designed to rank for 'near me' and city-level searches. Local businesses in that niche pay you for top placement, featured listings or the leads the site generates.

The Pitch

Jesse lays out a seven-step process: find a niche, scrape Google Maps, clean the rows, enrich them with attributes, build the pages, rank over time, then monetize. For niche research he suggests filtering 'near me' keywords in Ahrefs to keyword difficulty under 20 and looking past page one at low-volume terms others ignore. He uses Outscraper (about $100 for a typical scrape, paid per row), notes that only around 5-6% of raw rows survive filtering, and has AI read reviews to tag each business with attributes and write descriptions; he estimates the full AI-coded pipeline (Outscraper, Claude Code, Crawl4AI, Claude Vision, Supabase) at about $250 per directory, and argues AI coding makes things like 4,800 city pages with local FAQs a one-prompt job versus painful in WordPress. He cites featured listings selling for roughly $29 to $500 a month each, plus selling leads, and his specific angle is pairing the directory with your own small lead-gen websites in the same niche so you can pitch a business owner on both directory placement and exclusive leads.

Who It’s Best For

Suits someone comfortable with SEO and AI coding tools who is willing to wait months for rankings and then do outreach to local business owners. It fits best for people already running small lead-gen sites in a niche who want another asset in the same vertical.

The Truth (as we see it)

Building local directories from scraped Google Maps data is a real, long-standing SEO model, and the steps and tool costs described are concrete and plausible. However, this video is largely a plan rather than a result: Jesse says he has not yet built this directory and is borrowing the framework from another creator, so the $29-$500 monthly listing prices and the 'still pays every month' example are secondhand and unverified. Ranking a new directory takes time, many niches already have strong directories and aggregators like Yelp or Angi, and selling listings or leads requires sales outreach to business owners. Republishing scraped data and mass-generated pages also carries search-quality risk if the enrichment does not add genuine value. The video repeatedly promotes Jesse's paid SEO academy community (7-day free trial, 90-day money-back guarantee), where he says the build will happen.
