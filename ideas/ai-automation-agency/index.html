What It Is

Build backend automation systems, beyond just phone calls, things like workflow automation, data syncing, or repetitive task elimination, that replace manual work for businesses.

The Pitch

This is pitched as the higher-ticket version of AI services: rather than one tool doing one job, you're diagnosing a business's operational pain points and building a broader system, which supports charging more per client.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with more technical, hands-on implementation work than a simple one-tool service, and who enjoys diagnosing a business's specific bottlenecks.

The Truth (as we see it)

Gadzhi is candid that the real need is genuine and the upside is there, but execution and managing multiple moving systems for a client can get tricky in ways a simpler single-tool service doesn't.
