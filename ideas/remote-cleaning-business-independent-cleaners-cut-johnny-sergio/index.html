Remote Cleaning Business

Remote-run house cleaning business that dispatches independent cleaners and keeps a cut

Local & professional services · Moderate startup cost (about $1,000 for insurance, booking software and ads)

Originally published Sep 25, 2026 — Johnny & Sergio

Share on Facebook Link copied

One of 2 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You run a residential cleaning company without doing the cleaning. You market the service (Google Local Services ads, Facebook ads, Facebook and Nextdoor groups), take bookings, and send independent cleaners who bring their own supplies, car and gas; you keep about 40 to 50% of what the client pays. The creator says about one in three first-time deep-clean customers becomes a recurring client. As it grows, you add online booking, automated texts and a virtual assistant to answer the phone and recruit cleaners.

The Pitch

The creator calls this the best-scaling business on his list because you work on the business, not in it, from day one. He says the residential cleaning industry is about $40 billion a year and highly fragmented, that the cleaning business he built for his mother has passed $1 million in total sales and did $50,000 in August, and that students run large versions alongside a 9-to-5.

Who It's Best For

Organized people comfortable with marketing, phone calls and managing contractors who want a local service business without doing physical work.

The Truth (as we see it)

Brokering cleaning jobs to independent cleaners is a real model and the 40 to 50% margin claim is plausible for the owner's share, though ad costs, refunds and re-cleans eat into it. The creator is upfront that early on you are tied to your phone during the day for customer and cleaner issues. Quality control, cleaner reliability and properly classifying contractors (rules vary by place) are the real challenges. The income figures are the creator's own and unaudited. The creators run Home Service Academy, a paid one-on-one coaching program built around the remote cleaning model, and the video links a free training that leads to it.
