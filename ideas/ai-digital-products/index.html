What It Is

Use AI to help create and sell digital products, ebooks, templates, guides, or mini-courses, solving a specific, narrow problem for a specific audience.

The Pitch

Digital products can scale without any per-unit cost once built, and AI substantially speeds up the actual creation process.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who already has, or is willing to build, an audience or niche to sell into, since a great product with no one to sell it to won't generate income.
