Problem-Solving PDFs With a Sales Funnel

Selling pain-point-specific PDFs through a four-asset sales funnel with a thank-you-page upsell

Digital & subscription products · Very low (cost of building or buying four assets: a sales mock-up image, sales page, thank-you page, and the PDF itself)

What It Is

Build a $17 PDF that solves one narrow, specific, emotionally-felt pain point, then sell it through four assets: a scroll-stopping sales mock-up image, a dedicated sales page built entirely around that one PDF, a thank-you page shown only once after purchase offering a higher-ticket upsell ($97+) framed as a one-time-only offer, and the polished PDF itself.

The Pitch

The presenter says he's used this exact four-asset system since 2010 and walks through two live example sales pages, explaining that zeroing in on a specific, widely-felt pain point combined with a dedicated sales page removes the need to personally sell anything once traffic is driven to it.

Best For

Someone comfortable identifying a narrow, specific pain point within a niche and building or commissioning simple sales assets; no coding or advanced design skill required if buying pre-made asset templates.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core mechanics (single-offer sales pages outperforming generic stores, and thank-you-page one-time upsells using FOMO) are established, real direct-response marketing tactics. His specific revenue projections are illustrative math based on assumed conversion rates, not verified results, and the video promotes his own paid 'PDF Vault' of pre-made assets across niches.
