What It Is

Offer part-time senior leadership, CFO, COO, or CMO, to businesses that need executive-level guidance but can't afford, or don't need, a full-time hire. You work with several clients at once, each paying a monthly retainer for a set number of hours or specific deliverables.

The Pitch

Package your judgment into a recurring monthly fee, typically $5,000 to $15,000 per client, and take on 4 to 6 clients at once, which can add up to real six-figure annual income without hiring a team or building a product.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a genuine, provable track record of running or operating a business, not someone who has only studied the theory of business strategy.

The Truth (as we see it)

Heacock backs this up with his own real hiring experience, he's personally used a fractional CFO in his own businesses, so he's speaking from the buyer's side too, not just the seller's side. That's a real credibility signal. That said, landing the first client still requires real, sometimes slow, relationship-based sales, this isn't a fast-start idea, even if the eventual payoff is strong.
