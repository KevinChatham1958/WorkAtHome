Short-Form Video Strategist

Short-form video content strategist for small businesses

What It Is

Help small businesses, entrepreneurs, and coaches (a local gym, a coffee shop, an estate agent) grow their audience and attention on short-form platforms by developing their content strategy, hooks, and posting approach, without needing to be the on-camera talent or creator yourself.

The Pitch

Tilbury cites Noah Brilley, who studied what made content go viral on TikTok as a student, tested and refined that understanding, and made over $100,000 before turning 18 helping creators, tech companies, and even banks grow their audiences this way. The pitch centers on a real behavioral statistic: the average TikTok user opens the app roughly 20 times a day and spends around 90 minutes on it daily, framed as evidence that attention itself is the scarce resource businesses are competing for, and that understanding how to capture it is a sellable skill independent of being a creator personally. A suggested pricing structure is a flat monthly fee plus a performance-based view bonus when content goes viral, which aligns incentives between strategist and client.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a genuine, demonstrated eye for what makes short-form content work (hooks, pacing, trends) who's more interested in strategy and analysis than being on camera themselves.

The Truth (as we see it)

The TikTok usage statistics (20 opens a day, 90 minutes average) are attributed to TikTok's own claims, a reasonable but self-reported source rather than independent audited data. The Noah Brilley case study is specific but, consistent with the other examples in this video, comes from the presenter's own paid course platform where Noah is a featured instructor. The core idea, that strategy and understanding of a platform's mechanics is a genuinely separable, sellable skill from content creation itself, is a real and increasingly common freelance service, independent of this particular case study.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
