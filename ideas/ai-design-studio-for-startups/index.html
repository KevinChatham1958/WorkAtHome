AI-assisted Design Studio for Websites, Ads and Lead Magnets

What It Is

You act as an on-call designer for companies, producing ad images, website visuals, landing pages, slides, motion graphics and lead magnets using Claude and AI image/video tools. You do not need formal design training, but you need taste for what looks good. Companies, especially funded startups, pay a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Dang says companies need new designs weekly or monthly and that founders do not want to spend their own time operating AI tools, so they hire specialists. He demos a website hero and 3D graphic made in about five minutes with Claude Cowork connected to Higgsfield. He says he pays a solo designer who runs exactly this kind of studio for multiple clients, and quotes $1,000 to $5,000 per month, up to $10,000 for heavier work. He scores it 20 of 25 and suggests specializing in websites or ads to stand out.

Best For

Fits visually minded people who enjoy iterating on creative work and can build a portfolio quickly to show prospects.

The Truth

Demand for ongoing design is real, and his note that specialization matters is sound advice since general AI design is crowded and clients can increasingly run the same tools themselves. The demo outputs were admittedly rough and would need human cleanup, so basic design judgment still matters. The $5,000 to $10,000 monthly figures are best-case numbers with no evidence shown. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
