What It Is

Five specific, already-validated Amazon KDP book niches, each identified using Amazon's Best Sellers Rank as a live demand signal rather than guessing at a topic.

The Pitch

The presenter's core validation method, using Amazon's Best Sellers Rank as a live demand signal, is applied across five real example books currently earning $500 to over $4,000 a month each. He walks through why each niche has durable demand, what specific angle differentiates a winning book from a generic one in that niche, such as targeting a bill tracker specifically for couples instead of a generic tracker, and where trademark risk applies, such as avoiding terms like World Cup or real K-pop artist likenesses in titles and covers to avoid account-termination risk.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to research a specific niche and angle rather than copying an existing book's exact format, and for the more content-heavy niches, someone with relevant subject knowledge or design skill, or willing to hire it out.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter discloses using a paid research tool throughout but is explicit a free method, checking Best Sellers Rank directly on each book's product page or using a free Chrome extension, works just as well. The specific per-book daily-earnings figures shown are pulled from the same third-party estimation tool used elsewhere in this database, not confirmed royalty statements. The video closes promoting the presenter's own free coaching consultation call.
