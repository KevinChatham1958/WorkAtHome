What It Is

Instead of custom-building a different website for every client, build ONE repeatable GoHighLevel website system with the core pieces nearly every local business needs (homepage, service pages, lead form, chat widget, missed-call-text-back, 5-star review automation, text remarketing, mobile app control), then customize branding/content per client rather than rebuilding structure from scratch; price it as a $297/month system (no upfront fee) positioned as 'a website that captures leads and follows up faster,' not a design service. Find first clients via Google Maps searches for local businesses with a poor or missing website. Once a client has the website running, upsell to a $500-997/month local SEO add-on as the website becomes their marketing system's 'front door.'

The Pitch

The presenter cites the conversion math behind missed-call-text-back (responding within 5 minutes vs. the average 30-minute response yields roughly 21x higher conversion), shows real live examples across actual clients (pressure washing, roofing, junk removal, cleaning, a dance studio) all on the same repeatable template, and explains his pricing evolution from $1,500-5,000 custom builds to $297/month using an AI-assisted build prompt.

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to build and refine one solid template system rather than customizing from scratch per client, comfortable prospecting local businesses via Google Maps.

The Truth (as we see it)

GoHighLevel's missed-call-text-back, review automation, and mobile-app features are real, current platform capabilities, and the response-time-to-conversion statistic cited is a widely-referenced industry figure. This general offer type is already covered from multiple angles elsewhere in this database, though the 'one repeatable template, not custom per client' insight and concrete real-client examples here are a distinct contribution; the video promotes the presenter's own paid community and a proprietary AI prompt.
