What It Is

Creating AI-generated printable wall art sold as digital PNG or PDF downloads on Etsy, where the customer prints and frames the artwork themselves, using Claude to analyze a bestselling listing's images and generate prompts for an AI image tool to create an inspired-but-different set of designs.

The Pitch

The presenter shows real examples earning $145 to over $4,000 a month from printable wall art bundles, one shop having made about $600,000 total. He demonstrates screenshotting a top-selling listing, having Claude analyze it and generate several unique, not copied, image prompts, feeding those into an AI image tool, then using a custom cropping tool Claude builds on request to instantly generate all the different print-size dimensions customers expect, cutting what would normally take weeks of manual resizing down to seconds.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable directing AI tools iteratively to get a genuinely distinct result rather than a close copy, and willing to build out a full size range for every listing, since top sellers all offer multiple dimensions.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is explicit he doesn't want to just copy an existing listing and iterates the AI prompts specifically to make the result look different, worth noting as a good practice signal, though the underlying method, studying a competitor's exact images as the starting reference, sits closer to the line than pure independent design and is worth being thoughtful about. The specific revenue figures shown come from each listing's own public Etsy sales data.
