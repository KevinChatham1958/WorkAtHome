What It Is

Build support or sales chatbots for businesses to handle repetitive customer questions on their website or messaging channels.

The Pitch

A lower-complexity entry point into AI services compared to voice agents or full automation systems, since chatbot-building tools have become fairly plug-and-play.

Best For

Someone looking for the simplest, fastest entry point into selling AI tools to businesses, even if it means lower differentiation.

The Truth

Gadzhi is fairly blunt here: he considers this overhyped and easy to commoditize, meaning a lot of people offer a very similar product, which puts downward pressure on what you can charge.
