Custom AI Tools for Small Businesses

Custom AI operations tools for small businesses sold directly (no audience needed)

What It Is

Instead of selling AI mini apps to a broad audience on social media, you pick one specific operational problem a small business already solves badly and build a simple tool for it: a shift tracker for a cafe, a log of which cleaning team did which property and what supplies they used, an AI-updated Google Sheet, SOPs, a finance report or a knowledge library. The creator's example is her assistant, who rebuilt the internal AI systems she made for the creator's own business and offered them to other businesses. You start with one business you know (your coffee shop or hair stylist), use it as a first case study, then sell the same fix to similar businesses. Outreach is by cold email, LinkedIn, Facebook groups, Fiverr, Upwork, DMs or walking into shops.

The Pitch

The creator says her community members built good AI apps but couldn't sell them through Instagram, and that you don't need marketing skills if you hand a working solution to a business that already has the problem. She frames the price around time saved (for example, 45 minutes a week of scheduling) rather than how long the tool took to build.

Best For

People with knowledge of a particular industry's day-to-day workflows who are willing to approach business owners directly and keep at it for months.

The Truth

The core logic is sound: solving a specific, painful problem for a specific buyer is easier to sell than a generic app. The creator is unusually candid that it can take 6-12 months of steady work before the first dollar and that outreach is unavoidable. No prices or income figures are given beyond an unverified mention of a student earning $10,000 with a different method. The creator runs a paid membership community for building and selling these apps and promotes a free live training that leads into her offers.
