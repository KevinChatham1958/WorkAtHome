One-Day Digital Product Launch

One-day AI digital product launch using free tools and a simple storefront

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost

Originally published Jul 14, 2026 — Kathryne LaBella

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Kathryne LaBella · How to Make $300+ a Day With AI for FREE and Launch in ONE Day ↗

What It Is

Creating and launching a single, specific digital product, a guide, workbook, template, or planner, in one day using AI to draft the content and Canva to design it, then selling it through a simple creator storefront and free short-form content rather than paid ads.

The Pitch

The presenter, a mother of three, walks through a literal 6-hour launch plan: pick a narrow niche and specific audience, ask AI for 20 simple product ideas that solve one problem for that audience, generate a first draft with AI, explicitly warning against copy-pasting AI output without adding personal voice, design it in Canva using AI redesign prompts, set up a storefront, write sales copy answering who it's for, what problem it solves, what's included, and why buy today, then launch with five pieces of short-form content covering the problem, the solution, a tutorial, a personal story, and the offer. Also mentions PLR, private label rights, products as an optional shortcut instead of creating from scratch.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable posting short-form content consistently after launch, two to three times daily for 30 days recommended, as the primary traffic source, rather than someone wanting to rely on paid ads from day one.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter's self-reported $400,000-plus total figure is not shown with account verification on screen. The PLR shortcut mentioned, buying a pre-made rebrandable template rather than creating original content, is a mainstream, legitimate digital-product practice distinct from resell-rights schemes, though it does mean the product in that path isn't originally created by the seller, worth knowing if originality matters to a given reader.
