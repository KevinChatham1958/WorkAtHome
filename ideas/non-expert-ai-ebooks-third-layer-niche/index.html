Non-Expert AI Niche Ebooks

Non-expert AI-written ebooks in narrow 'third layer' niches, sold through micro-influencers paid only on sales

What It Is

You choose a topic three layers deep rather than a broad market (not 'fitness' but 'resolving knee pain during long runs for marathoners over 50'), check demand by browsing what already sells on Whop, Etsy, ClickBank and the Facebook ads library, and have AI research and write the product, starting with a cheap ebook. You don't need to be an expert or show your face; AI voice tools like ElevenLabs can narrate. You then find micro-influencers whose audiences match the topic and who earn little from them, and agree that they promote your product and are paid only on the sales they bring; once you have the first one to three sales, you expand the ebook into a video course.

The Pitch

Gadzhi frames this as 'Digital Products 2.0': each attempt costs almost nothing, so you can keep testing products like spins of a wheel until one sells, and a digital product can be sold to 10 or 10,000 buyers with no extra work. He points to faceless products such as a cybersecurity course and a woodworking plans product he says sold over 16,000 copies at $67.

Who It’s Best For

People who are willing to research narrow problems, keep testing until one product sells, and do steady outreach to small creators.

The Truth (as we see it)

Very narrow topics and creator-promoted sales are sound ideas, but 'AI is the expert' is a weak foundation: AI-synthesized advice on health, money or security can be wrong, and buyers and platforms are increasingly wary of low-effort AI ebooks. Each test is cheap in cash but not in time, since outreach to influencers is the real work. Gadzhi says on camera that he is a co-owner of Whop, the digital-product platform he recommends for selling digital products, so his platform advice is not neutral. He also mentions his own past paid agency courses (Agency Navigator, Agency Accelerator) and recommends AI tools such as Synthesize AI without saying whether he has a stake in them.
