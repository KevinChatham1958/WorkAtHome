Home and Closet Organizing

Home and closet organizing service with flat-rate packages for a specific client type

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (organizing materials are typically purchased by the client, not the organizer)

Originally published Aug 7, 2026 — David Explains Money

Share on Facebook Link copied

What It Is

Offer professional organizing services (closets, garages, pantries, home offices) charging for your time, system, and design work rather than for inventory, specializing in a specific client type (such as families who just moved, or older adults downsizing) so you can offer flat-rate packages instead of quoting every job individually, and building referral relationships with real estate agents and moving companies whose clients regularly need this service right after a move.

The Pitch

The presenter frames before-and-after photos as doing most of the marketing on their own, since documented transformations are the kind of content people naturally share, reducing the need for paid advertising if the work itself is well-photographed and shared.

Best For

Someone with an eye for organization and design who's comfortable specializing in one client type to standardize pricing, and willing to build referral relationships with real estate agents and moving companies.

The Truth

Specializing in one client type to enable flat-rate pricing (versus quoting every job individually) is a genuinely useful way to make this business easier to price and market consistently, and the real estate/moving-company referral channel is a logical, underused source of recurring leads for this specific service.
