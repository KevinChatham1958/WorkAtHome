Wholesale Premium Candles

Premium candle making sold wholesale to boutiques, spas, and Airbnb hosts

Physical & print products · Low startup cost ($77 budget setup, or about $267 for a full premium setup)

What It Is

Make premium candles using coconut or soy wax, wooden wicks, and weighted vessels with a real fragrance load (not basic Mason jar candles), and sell primarily through wholesale accounts with boutiques, spas, and Airbnb hosts who reorder monthly, rather than one-off consumer sales through platforms like Etsy.

The Pitch

A standard 8oz candle costs about $3.03 to make at bulk material pricing and retails $18-28; premium coconut wax versions push $20-35. The presenter breaks down a premium candle costing about $11.22 all-in, where materials (wax, wick, fragrance) are only about $1.08 of that — labor is $3.33 and overhead is $5 — framing the real product as time and positioning, not materials.

Best For

Someone comfortable pitching wholesale accounts to boutiques, spas, and short-term rental hosts, and willing to price based on positioning and labor rather than just materials cost.

The Truth

The materials-vs-labor cost breakdown is a genuinely useful reframe for anyone underpricing a craft product, and targeting recurring wholesale accounts over one-off retail sales is sound. The presenter doesn't address how to actually land those wholesale relationships, which is really the harder part of this business.
