What It Is

You set up a print-on-demand shop, fill it with designs, order samples of your own products to photograph, and promote them on Pinterest, where pins can bring visitors for months.

The Pitch

Kimberly says people think print-on-demand is too late but the numbers disagree, citing a global market above $12 billion, and shows the full setup with Gelato.

Who It’s Best For

Patient sellers willing to build a catalog and pin steadily.

The Truth (as we see it)

Market size doesn't mean easy sales; most new shops sell little at first. 'Passive income' still means steady design and marketing work. She uses an affiliate link for Gelato.
