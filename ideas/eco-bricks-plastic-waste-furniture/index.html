Eco-Brick Furniture

Eco-brick furniture and community-build business using packed plastic waste

What It Is

Pack clean, dry plastic waste tightly into plastic bottles to create dense "eco brick" building blocks, then finish assembled modules with plaster or mortar skins to build benches, ottomans, garden beds, and outdoor tables, sold both as finished products and as school/community workshops that supply materials and guide the build.

The Pitch

Set up collection points and standardize bottle size and packing density for consistent modules, market with clear impact metrics (bottles per item) as the core marketing hook, and pitch sponsored installations to local businesses and parks — blending direct product sales, paid workshops/education, and sponsorship into one model built around a visible community/sustainability story.

Who It’s Best For

Someone motivated by environmental/community impact work who's comfortable coordinating group builds (schools, community groups) in addition to producing finished pieces solo.

The Truth (as we see it)

Eco-bricking is a real, documented construction technique used in community and DIY building projects worldwide, and combining product sales with educational workshops and sponsorships is a sound way to diversify revenue around a single sustainability story. Structural durability and safety (for anything load-bearing like benches) depend on correct packing density and proper mortar/plaster finishing technique, which takes some practice to get right.
