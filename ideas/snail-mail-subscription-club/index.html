What It Is

A paid physical-letter subscription, around $10 a month, where a creator writes and mails personal letters, recipes, or art to subscribers each month, no email newsletter, actual physical mail.

The Pitch

One cited example grew to $60,000 in monthly recurring revenue within 7 months, built entirely through organic short-form video, with no pre-existing following required.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a genuine creative hook or personality who enjoys the tactile, personal-touch side of a subscription business.

The Truth (as we see it)

The numbers given are specific and credible, 2% churn, the first 1,500 customers coming from a single viral video, which is a good sign. Reliably mailing physical letters at scale, especially internationally, adds real cost and delivery-time considerations most digital subscriptions don't have.
