AI UGC Ad Remixing

AI UGC ad remixing service using viral outlier detection

What It Is

Find already-viral UGC ad creatives (using an outlier-detection tool that filters TikTok or Meta ads by view count and engagement), reverse-engineer the winning ad's script, visual style, and pacing, then use AI-generated characters and Claude-based editing to remix that same proven structure for a client's own product.

The Pitch

Massimo walks through the full pipeline: find an outlier ad performing well above an account's average, auto-analyze it into a scene-by-scene script breakdown, swap in a new product's details, generate a matching AI character (with adjustable ethnicity, wardrobe, and features), then hand the raw clips to Claude to auto-edit into a finished ad. He breaks down the cost transparently: about $2 to produce one full ad, or roughly $24 to generate 50 ad variations by reusing the same script body with different hooks.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable working through a genuinely technical multi-tool pipeline (ad research, AI character generation, AI video generation, Claude-based editing) who's targeting e-commerce brands that need constant fresh ad creative.

The Truth (as we see it)

Massimo promotes his own paid tool and Skool community throughout the video as the system that ties all these steps together, worth knowing since the specific workflow shown depends on that tool rather than being fully replicable with free alternatives alone. The underlying method, finding validated winning ads and reverse-engineering their structure rather than guessing at what converts, is a sound and independently applicable principle regardless of which specific tools you use to execute it.
