AI Partner for Local Businesses

Becoming an AI "partner" for local businesses using Claude Code

Nate Herk · How to Build a One Person AI Business (Using Claude Code) ↗

What It Is

Consulting work where you use natural language to build automations for existing businesses through Claude Code, framed around three value buckets: getting more customers, making each customer worth more, or cutting costs.

The Pitch

Herk argues the barrier to entry has collapsed because Claude Code turns plain-language descriptions into working automations; work that used to take him hours now takes 20-30 minutes. He recommends positioning yourself as an AI partner selling outcomes, not an AI builder selling features, and cites McKinsey's AI report showing 3-15% revenue uplift for AI-forward businesses.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to learn Claude Code specifically and comfortable pitching automation projects to local or small businesses, without needing a computer science background.

The Truth (as we see it)

Herk has a real track record, he scaled his own AI agency past $100,000/month before this. The three-bucket framework for scoping and pricing projects is genuinely useful, not just hype. This idea overlaps conceptually with the AI automation agency and AI client-acquisition roadmap entries already in the database; the differentiator here is the specific tool (Claude Code) and the concrete pitch framework. As with any consulting idea, the real bottleneck is finding and closing the first few clients, not the technical build.
