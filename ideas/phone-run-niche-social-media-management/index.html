What It Is

You take over a small business's social media: plan posts, design them in Canva, edit short clips in CapCut, schedule them in Meta Business Suite and reply to comments. Clients such as dentists, bakeries, gyms or plumbers commonly pay $300-1,500 a month each, depending on how many posts and platforms are involved. Four clients at $500 is about $2,000 a month.

The Pitch

The creator says many small businesses have nobody, or 'a nephew', running their social media, so the need is wide. He argues the people who do well pick one industry, learn what those customers care about, and become the specialist rather than another generic freelancer.

Who It’s Best For

Organized, reliable people who enjoy marketing and steady client relationships.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a real and common freelance service, and the creator's point that reliability is the hidden cost is honest: a manager who disappears loses clients. Getting clients usually takes direct outreach to business owners, and ongoing retainers mean regular deadlines and check-ins. Figures are labeled as estimates. No paid product is promoted.
