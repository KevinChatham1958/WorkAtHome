What It Is

Producing granola and supplying it in bulk to cafes and breakfast spots that reorder weekly, rather than building a packaged consumer retail brand.

The Pitch

Selling a $6 bag at 100 units is about $600 in gross sales before premium nuts, dried fruit, printed pouches, labels, retailer margin, labor, shipping, and breakage — the presenter stresses calculating cost per finished ounce rather than per baking tray. A breakfast cafe using 10 lbs of granola weekly cares about batch-to-batch consistency, not pouch design.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who treats this as a consistency and reliability business (the same taste every single batch) and pursues recurring food-service accounts over a consumer packaged-goods brand.

The Truth (as we see it)

The recipe itself is easy enough that almost anyone can enter this business, so the real competitive edge has to come from the buyer relationship or distribution channel, not the product formulation.
