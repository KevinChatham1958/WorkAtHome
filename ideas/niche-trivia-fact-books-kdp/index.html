Trivia and Fact Books on KDP

Niche trivia and fact books published on Amazon KDP

What It Is

You research and compile trivia or fact books for narrow audiences, such as trivia for seniors, sports or movie trivia, or football facts for dads, format them with chapters and some illustrations, and publish through Amazon KDP. Amazon prints on demand and pays royalties; many buyers purchase them as gifts.

The Pitch

The presenter calls this one of his favorites because it is boring and overlooked, and says many narrow niches have little competition. He argues that niching down several levels, like 'weird facts about football for dads,' leads to steady monthly sales, and that gift buying spikes around holidays. Unlike fiction, there are no characters or plot, and illustrations are optional extras.

Who It's Best For

Detail-oriented people who enjoy research and will fact-check carefully, and who like the idea of many small niche titles.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter correctly warns that general AI tools make up facts and repeat themselves, so every fact needs checking, which is the real work here. He uses this point to pitch Co-author, an AI book-writing platform his team built for KDP (linked in the description), so the problem framing doubles as a sales pitch; any AI tool or manual research works. Claims of low competition and steady sales are his own, unverified. He also sells KDP coaching via a free discovery call.
