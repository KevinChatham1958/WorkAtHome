Personal-Experience Digital Guide

Turning a personal experience into a low-priced digital guide with an AI business-builder tool, sold through social media

Digital & subscription products · Low startup cost (subscription to the AI tool shown)

Originally published Oct 7, 2026 — Kandace Alexis

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Kandace Alexis · $100k in 100 days with AI and Wifi 2026 (BiziBrain created my 6 figure digital business) ↗

What It Is

You tell an AI tool (BiziBrain in the video) about something you've overcome or know well, and it shapes it into a digital guide; the example turns a 50-pound weight loss into a $17-27 beginner guide. The tool drafts and designs the product, suggests a price, gives a seven-day content plan, and analyzes viral videos in your niche so you can model your own short videos to drive sales. Later steps add a custom AI chatbot product, affiliate offers and a community.

The Pitch

The creator says the tool turned her own business into about $100,000 in 90 days while working around two hours a day on her phone, and that her students average from $100 to thousands a day. Her pitch is that low-priced impulse products plus social media views can add up quickly.

Best For

Someone with a real story or skill people would pay to learn from, who is willing to post short videos consistently.

The Truth (as we see it)

This video works largely as a sales pitch for the tool: the creator promotes BiziBrain through her own sign-up link bundled with her paid social-media club and playbook, and even suggests reselling the tool itself as your offer. Her income and student results are unverified, and the $27,000-from-one-post example is hypothetical. Selling digital guides from personal experience is a legitimate, common model, but it doesn't require this particular tool, and health or weight-loss guides should avoid medical claims.
