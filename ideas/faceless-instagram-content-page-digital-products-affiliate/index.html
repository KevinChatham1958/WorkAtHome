Faceless Instagram Page

Faceless Instagram content page monetized with digital products and affiliate links

  • YouTube & blogging
  • Very low startup cost

Source video

Brandon Belcher · YOU SHY? copy my faceless Instagram business using one tool ↗

What It Is

Build a niche-focused Instagram content page, quotes, reels, viral content, without ever showing your face, using an AI content-automation tool (faceless.page) to generate weeks of content in minutes. Monetize the page's traffic either by selling a low-cost digital product (the presenter's example: a $27 PDF guide) linked in the bio, or through affiliate marketing (linking to a paid tool or service and earning a commission, such as a Shopify affiliate link on an e-commerce-focused page).

The Pitch

The presenter says he personally grew a faceless account (PLR Hustle) to 170,000 followers and over $1 million in revenue before ever revealing his face, and cites a named second example (Millionaire Mentor, run by a founder named Jason) reportedly making $1 million a month anonymously. He walks through the underlying math: at a 5-10% follower-to-buyer conversion rate on a $27 product, roughly 100-200 new followers a day is enough to hit $270 a day in sales.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who's shy or camera-averse but comfortable consistently posting content, or automating it, and picking one specific niche and target avatar to speak to.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is promoting his own tool of choice (faceless.page) throughout, and it's reasonable to assume some financial relationship with that platform, though he's transparent that he used to do the same process manually with ChatGPT and Canva before switching. The $1 million and $1 million-a-month figures are both self-reported, his own account and a secondhand account of another creator's, not independently documented, and the 5-10% buyer-conversion-rate math presented as achievable is on the high end for cold social-media traffic, worth treating as an optimistic case rather than a typical outcome.
