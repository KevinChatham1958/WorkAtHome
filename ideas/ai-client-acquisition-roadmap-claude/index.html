What It Is

This isn't a single business idea so much as a general roadmap for getting your first paying client for any AI-powered service, using Claude as the core tool. The framework covers picking a niche, building a personal brand through content (Dang recommends YouTube or similar), and reaching out to or attracting potential clients, whether or not you have any prior experience or portfolio.

The Pitch

Dang argues the opportunity is still early: by his account, less than 1% of the world's population has even downloaded Claude, so anyone building real skill with it now has a head start on the market. He points to one of his clients, Sandy, a full-time insurance salesperson with three kids and no AI background, who he says grew a YouTube channel to over 36,000 subscribers and built an AI side business earning over $30,000 a month within about 5-6 months, all while keeping her day job.

Who It’s Best For

A total beginner to AI consulting who's willing to invest in building a public presence (content, a personal brand) rather than someone looking for a purely behind-the-scenes, no-audience path to clients.

The Truth (as we see it)

Worth knowing upfront: this video is a funnel into Dang's own paid three-day live training program, and it includes a fairly heavy sales pitch for that program partway through, complete with a mention of live sales role-play coaching as part of the paid offer. The Sandy case study is specific and named, which is a good transparency signal, but it's a single example, not an audited track record across many clients. The core advice, pick a niche, build in public, use content to attract inbound interest, is genuinely sound and echoes what other creators in this database (Tilbury, Gadzhi) also recommend for AI service businesses.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
