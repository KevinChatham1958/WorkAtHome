AI Animated Explainer Reels

AI-animated stick-figure explainer reels, auto-posted with comment-to-DM automation

Content & personal brand · Very low startup cost (a few dollars of video-generation credits per reel plus Claude and a paid Blotato plan)

Originally published Oct 17, 2026 — Sabrina Ramonov 🍄

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Sabrina Ramonov 🍄 · How to Build $20K/Month Faceless Channel (Without Higgsfield) ↗

What It Is

You come up with a short explainer topic yourself, then have Claude (Cowork or Claude Code) connected to a video-generation service (fal.ai) design a recurring cartoon character, generate key frames, animate the clips, add a voiceover and stitch the reel, with you approving each stage. Claude then posts it to Instagram through Blotato and sets up an automation that DMs your link to anyone who comments a keyword.

The Pitch

The video opens with a stick-figure animation channel said to make over $20,000 a month, and the guest (Mark of Automate with Mark) says AI can now produce that style of content end to end for about $3-4 per reel. The comment-keyword DM sends viewers to your offer or longer content, building followers along the way.

Who It's Best For

Someone who likes explaining a topic simply and wants to grow a faceless channel that points to their own product or content.

The Truth (as we see it)

The workflow is shown working, but the $20,000-a-month figure belongs to an established channel that wasn't built this way, and no earnings are shown for the AI version. Setup involves API keys and connector settings that trip people up, and AI clips can need fixes. Blotato is Sabrina Ramonov's own app, so the channel has a stake in recommending it.
