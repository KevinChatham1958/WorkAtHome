Building functional, tool-embedded business websites with Claude Code

What It Is

Rather than generating a generic, purely decorative "AI slop" website, use Claude Code with a specific front-end design skill installed to build genuinely functional tools directly into a client's website: an appointment-booking flow that syncs to a real calendar and captures abandoned form data, an AI-powered remodel visualizer that lets a customer upload a photo and preview design changes, or a distance-based dynamic pricing calculator for a service business, then deploy the finished site through low-cost hosting.

The Pitch

The core argument, demonstrated with a real side-by-side comparison, is that a plain prompt like "redesign this website" produces a passable but generic result (missing the client's actual logo and images, filled with placeholder-feeling content), while installing a specific design skill and iterating with detailed, specific instructions (fixing mobile text stacking, adding a real contact form, pulling in actual Google review data) produces something a business would genuinely pay for because it does something useful, not just look nice. The full technical walkthrough, from local file setup through uploading to live hosting and fixing a real caching bug that came up during the demo, is shown step by step rather than glossed over.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with iterative, detailed prompting (the process involves many rounds of specific feedback, not one perfect prompt) and willing to learn basic file-hosting mechanics to actually deploy a finished site.

The Truth (as we see it)

Showing a genuinely broken first attempt at redesigning a real client's site, and then troubleshooting a real live bug (a browser caching issue) during the demo rather than editing it out, is a stronger transparency signal than a polished, error-free walkthrough would be. The video is sponsored by, and funnels toward, a specific hosting provider, a financial incentive worth knowing, though the Claude Code workflow itself doesn't require that specific host to work. This uses the same underlying AI-website-building tool as other entries in this database; the distinguishing focus here is specifically on embedding functional business tools (booking, calculators, visualizers) rather than on a particular pricing or client-acquisition strategy.
