One-Person KDP Ebook Business

One-person Amazon KDP ebook business drafted with Claude, built as a portfolio of books

What It Is

You find a book topic that already sells on Amazon by checking the Best Sellers Rank of competing self-published books (he targets books ranked under 30,000 with few reviews), read their reviews to find what readers wish was different, and plan a better or more specific book. Claude helps research, outline and draft it chapter by chapter, and you rewrite, fact-check and add your own examples. A professional cover and proper formatting follow, the book is uploaded free to Kindle Direct Publishing, and you gather a few honest reviews before marketing it. Income comes from royalties on ebook and print-on-demand sales, and the plan is to keep adding books so the catalog grows.

The Pitch

The creator argues this fits a one-person business better than dropshipping, freelancing, YouTube or courses, because Amazon brings the shoppers and handles payment, printing, delivery and customer service. Once a book ranks, he says it keeps selling while you work on the next one, so income compounds without the workload rising in step. He shares student results such as one going from about $1,200 to nearly $10,000 a month after reworking her catalog.

Who It’s Best For

Patient people with good English editing skills who like research and are willing to build a catalog of books over months rather than expect fast money.

The Truth (as we see it)

The demand-checking method and the warning that raw AI output won't sell are sound, and he says plainly that results vary and some books flop. The student figures are his own examples and are not audited. He sells the tools and help he recommends: the Co-author software (the niche research feature is part of a $65-a-month plan; a Chrome extension is free) and a paid one-on-one KDP mentorship. Amazon has been tightening its rules on AI-written and low-quality books, so the quality bar he describes matters more over time.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
