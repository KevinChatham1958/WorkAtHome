What It Is

You create ready-made Notion workspaces that a buyer duplicates and starts using right away, with linked databases, filtered views and a short how-to page. Examples include a content calendar for creators, a client and invoice hub for freelancers, a home management dashboard or a student study planner. The creator connects Notion to Claude, prompts it with the specific audience and the databases and properties needed, and Claude builds the template directly in Notion. You sell it on Etsy and deliver it as a shared duplicate link.

The Pitch

The creator says people find Notion powerful but intimidating, so they pay for a setup they do not want to build themselves. Delivery is just a link, with nothing to download or break. He points to Etsy listings only a few months old that a research tool estimates are earning thousands a month, and stresses picking a sub-niche such as freelancers or students.

Who It’s Best For

People who already use Notion and understand how a specific group (freelancers, students, creators) organizes its work.

The Truth (as we see it)

Notion templates are a genuine and long-running digital product, and delivering by duplicate link is how the category works. It is also one of the most crowded template categories, and many buyers can now ask an AI to build a workspace themselves, which may weaken demand for generic layouts. The earnings shown are third-party estimates, not seller-confirmed figures. The creator says Claude builds it in minutes, but buyers on Etsy compare many near-identical AI-built listings, so niche choice and presentation matter more than build speed. The creator sells a paid program (a 20-product-type module plus traffic training), gives away the prompts in exchange for an email address, and repeatedly promotes the Everbee research tool through a referral link.
