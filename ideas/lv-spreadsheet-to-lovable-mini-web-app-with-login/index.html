Spreadsheet-to-Web-App Builds

Turning a spreadsheet into a hosted mini web app with logins (Claude plans, Lovable builds)

What It Is

You take an existing spreadsheet (a tracker, checklist, budget, symptom log or meal-prep calculator) and convert it into a hosted web app with user accounts, sold for about $17-47. You upload the sheet to Claude chat and ask it to analyze it as a product and write a product brief, then ask what decisions must be made before any code is written and answer them. You then build in Lovable, which handles login, database and deployment, using seven separate prompts in layers: foundation, onboarding, core feature, dashboard, report/export, settings, then polish. Finally you test the whole user journey and fix all bugs in one batched prompt ending with 'Fix only what I listed.'

The Pitch

The creator says spreadsheets on Etsy earn six figures but generic ones are now a race to the bottom at $1-2, while an interactive app built on the same proven demand is unsaturated. She says the whole build took under an hour and that Lovable's version came out cleaner than the same tool built in Claude Code.

Who It's Best For

People who already have a useful spreadsheet from their work or life and want to turn it into a higher-priced product.

The Truth (as we see it)

The layered build and 'decide before you build' steps are good practice for any AI app builder. Unlike a downloadable file, a hosted app with logins has ongoing costs (Lovable and database usage), needs support, and stores customer data; a health or symptom tracker raises privacy responsibilities. Price points and Etsy figures are the creator's own claims, and selling access to a hosted app on marketplaces needs careful setup. The video promotes the creator's free YouTube course, which leads into her paid offers.
