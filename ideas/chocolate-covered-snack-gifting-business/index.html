Chocolate-covered Snack Business for Corporate and Event Gifting

Physical & print products · Low-moderate startup cost (small automatic chocolate tempering machines around $400-1,100; simple tabletop melting equipment costs less; full enrobing lines run much higher)

What It Is

Producing chocolate-covered snacks (such as pretzels) packaged into corporate or event gift boxes, selling the order before investing in tempering equipment.

The Pitch

A company ordering 200 small gift boxes at $12 each for an event is about $2,400 in gross sales before chocolate, pretzels, boxes, inserts, labor, and delivery. The presenter recommends selling the order first and only buying a tempering machine ($400-1,100) once temperature-consistency needs justify it, since chocolate is sensitive — temperature, moisture, fingerprints, bloom, cooling time, and hot-weather shipping can all ruin a premium-looking product.

Best For

Someone who lines up a real corporate or event buyer before automating, and who is prepared for chocolate's sensitivity to heat and humidity during both production and shipping.

The Truth

The perceived-value trick here (a cheap pretzel becomes a premium gift once dipped and boxed) genuinely works, but chocolate's fragility under heat and humidity means quality control is a bigger ongoing risk than with most of the other items on this list.
