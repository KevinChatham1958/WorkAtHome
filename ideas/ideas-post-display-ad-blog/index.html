Display-Ad "Ideas" Blog

Display-ad blog built on endless 'ideas' keyword variations

What It Is

Start a blog in a hobby or lifestyle niche like home decor, gardening, pet care, or fitness. Fill it with short, easy 'ideas' posts that each target one variation of a broad keyword, such as farmhouse living room ideas, small living room ideas, or cozy living room ideas, or 'how to plant' plus a crop, or a dog breed plus 'training.' The first goal is enough traffic to qualify for a premium display-ad network. Later, you link from those posts to buying guides and 'best of' posts that earn affiliate commissions, and you repost each article as several Pinterest pins for extra traffic.

The Pitch

Enfroy calls this the 'infinite keyword loop.' One broad keyword with 20 or more variations gives you a near-endless list of quick posts. He points to big sites whose articles are 'literally some pictures and like one paragraph for each idea.' His 90-day plan is 30 to 40 of these posts at two to three a week, aiming for about 10,000 monthly sessions, which he says is Mediavine's entry threshold. He estimates $15 to $30 per thousand sessions in a decent niche, or around $5 for something like tattoo ideas. That works out to roughly $150 to $300 a month at 10,000 sessions, $750 to $1,500 at 50,000, and $1,500 to $3,000 at 100,000. He recommends sticking to only three affiliate programs in the first 3 to 6 months, and making about three pins per post.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a genuine hobby or lifestyle interest who likes producing lots of simple, visual content, can keep publishing for 6 to 12 months before real income, and is fine with modest early ad earnings.

The Truth (as we see it)

Variation 'ideas' posts with display ads is a real, widely used playbook on recipe and home blogs. Ad income per session varies a lot by niche and season. Enfroy's dollar figures are estimates, not his own results, and Mediavine's traffic requirements have changed over time, so check them yourself. The model depends heavily on Google and Pinterest traffic. This is exactly the risk he warns about in other videos, where he says his own traffic fell after algorithm updates and that SEO alone is 'dead' for small bloggers. Thin image posts are also the kind of content AI search answers can replace. The video promotes his free 'AI income launch kit' and recommends Bluehost, Kadence, and Surfer SEO.
