What It Is

Use free AI tools to design custom presentation decks and slideshows for businesses, then sell the finished product as a freelance service, largely on platforms like Fiverr.

The Pitch

Sarah cites a real example of a Fiverr seller charging an average of $222 per gig with 12 orders already in their queue, over $2,000 in visible pipeline revenue for one operator. She attributes the demand to two groups: larger companies that outsource internal-meeting slide design, and AI startups that need investor pitch decks built quickly. Her free workflow uses Google's Gemini to generate a base slide template, then Canva's AI to turn that into a more polished, editable version with one-click animations.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a reasonable eye for visual design who's comfortable directing AI tools rather than building slides from scratch, and willing to sell the finished output as a freelance service rather than a product.

The Truth (as we see it)

The $222 average gig price and 12-order queue come from a real, visible Fiverr listing, a genuinely checkable data point, though it reflects one seller's results rather than a category-wide average. Sarah's workflow relies on Canva's AI features, which require a paid Pro account (she offers her own affiliate link to a free 30-day trial of it), so the described process isn't fully $0 the way some of her other ideas are, despite being framed alongside them as free.
