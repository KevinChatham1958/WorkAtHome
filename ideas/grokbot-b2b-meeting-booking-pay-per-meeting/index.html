AI Appointment Setting Agency

AI-run cold email appointment-setting service for B2B companies, charging a base fee plus a fee per booked meeting

What It Is

You book sales meetings for B2B companies. A Grokbot agent builds a lead list from the client's ideal customer profile and buying signals, pulls verified contact details from a data tool (never guessing emails), drafts a short personal email for each prospect, and runs daily checks on inbox health, sending volume and reply rates, flagging interested prospects. You approve the first batch, keep the lead list topped up and act as the agency director. The creator suggests $1,000 a month plus $250 per booked meeting, so six meetings a month is $2,500 per client.

The Pitch

The creator says companies already pay $150 to $500 per booked meeting when they outsource, because a sales rep costs about $80,000 a year to book around 10 meetings a month. He cites a cold email agency owner getting 80-100 positive replies a day and a published setup with a 38% reply rate, and says you can use the same system to land your own clients.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with sales, numbers and email deliverability who wants a results-based service.

The Truth (as we see it)

Pay-per-meeting lead generation is a real, established service, and the creator is fair in including a test that got only a 1.2% reply rate and in saying the bot cannot fix a weak offer. The impressive reply-rate examples are other people's posts and are unverified. Cold email has legal rules (opt-outs, honest sender details, and stricter consent laws in some countries) and deliverability problems that sink many beginners. Expect meaningful tool and data costs before the first client pays. The creator closes by promoting his own seven-day "land your first AI client" challenge.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
