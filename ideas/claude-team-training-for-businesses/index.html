What It Is

You run hands-on, usually virtual, training that teaches a company's team to use Claude (or Claude Cowork or Claude Code), tailored to a role such as marketing, finance or founders, and to how teams collaborate with it. The pitch is a one-day session that saves each person hours every week.

The Pitch

The creator says everyone wants to learn Claude but there are few team-focused tutorials or trainers, and suggests charging $10,000-40,000 per training. To get clients she recommends DMing people who comment on Claude videos and offering to demo a workflow as a guest on AI YouTube channels.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who knows Claude well and is comfortable teaching groups over video.

The Truth (as we see it)

Interest in team AI training is real, but the $10,000-40,000 range is aspirational for someone without a track record, and the opportunity depends on one company's tool staying popular. Many larger companies are locked into other tools such as Copilot. The creator sells her own AI app and offers free tutorials.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
