Wedding Officiant Business With Photo-op Prop and Backdrop Upsells

Amber Gist · I'm Building a Wedding Officiant Business... Here's What I Bought Next ↗

What It Is

Officiate weddings as a paid service, then increase revenue per event by offering optional add-ons like a photo backdrop and props that couples can add to their ceremony package, using a collapsible cart to transport supplies between events.

The Pitch

Gist documents building this business "in public," describing hitting a personal milestone of 25 officiated weddings before deciding to invest further, and is in the process of upgrading her equipment (a heavy-duty photo backdrop stand, drapes, and a collapsible supply wagon) to make the experience feel more elevated for couples and support the add-on photo-op package.

Best For

Someone comfortable speaking in public and being the center of attention at events, interested in the wedding/events industry, who's willing to build the business gradually and reinvest in equipment as bookings grow.

The Truth

This video is genuinely thin on business specifics — there's no pricing, revenue, or client-acquisition detail here, just an equipment update. The wedding officiant role itself is a real and accessible local service (usually just requiring a state officiant registration or ordination, which varies by location), and add-on photo props are a sensible way to raise the average sale per event, but anyone considering this should look elsewhere for the actual pricing and marketing playbook, since this particular video doesn't provide it.
