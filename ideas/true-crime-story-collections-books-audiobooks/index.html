True Crime Story Books

True crime story collections published as books and audiobooks

What It Is

You research real criminal cases and write nonfiction story collections, then publish them as ebooks, paperbacks, hardcovers, and audiobooks. Readers and listeners buy them on Amazon and audiobook platforms, and you earn royalties. The work is research and writing, plus arranging narration for the audio version.

The Pitch

The presenter calls this the highest-content niche on his list but the one with the biggest upside, because the same research can be sold in several formats. He says true crime fans are highly engaged and already consume podcasts and documentaries, so audiobooks fit them well. He cites a 176-story audiobook bundle he says earns $129 to $258 per day and a paperback bundle from the same author at $33 to $39 per day. He suggests focusing on lesser-known, regional, or cold cases, survival stories, or themed collections, and validating keywords before writing.

Best For

Suits strong researchers and writers who are comfortable with dark subject matter and can maintain a consistent voice across a long book. Fits someone willing to invest weeks rather than a weekend per title.

The Truth (as we see it)

Multi-format publishing does spread one piece of research across several income streams, and true crime has a large audience. The figures quoted are the presenter's estimates for an established author with a large back catalog, not what a new publisher should expect. He warns that real research and accurate case details are required, that AI should not be used without fact-checking, and that victims and families must be handled carefully; publishers also face legal risk from inaccurate claims about real people. Audiobook narration adds cost or effort that is not detailed in the video. The presenter promotes his paid KDP coaching program and a free discovery call with bonuses.
