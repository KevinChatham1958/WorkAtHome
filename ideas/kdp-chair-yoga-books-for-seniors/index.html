Chair Yoga Program Books for Seniors on Amazon KDP

What It Is

You write and self-publish illustrated chair yoga guides aimed at people over 60, usually structured as a 28- or 30-day beginner program. Books are sold on Amazon as paperbacks and other formats, and you earn royalties on each sale. Buyers are seniors themselves or family members buying for them.

The Pitch

The presenter calls chair yoga for seniors one of the best niches in the KDP exercise category, citing a 28-day program book he says makes about $54 per day from the paperback alone (roughly $2,000 a month across formats) and a chair-yoga-for-weight-loss book at a similar daily figure. He argues seniors are a strong book-buying audience who prefer paperbacks, and the audience is growing. Books run 80 to 150 pages with illustrated poses, priced around $15.99 to $19.99, and he says AI image tools can now produce usable pose illustrations. He suggests specific angles such as balance, mobility, or weight loss.

Best For

Suits people with fitness or yoga knowledge, or those willing to pay an expert to review the content. A fit for someone patient enough to produce a longer, accurate instructional book.

The Truth

Higher list prices do mean larger royalties per sale, and the senior fitness audience is real, but the income figures are the presenter's estimates of competitors' sales and are not verified. Because this is health content, accuracy matters: he warns that Amazon scrutinizes medical claims and recommends having a qualified yoga instructor review the poses, which he says can be hired on Upwork or Fiverr, adding cost and time. AI-generated pose images can contain errors that a careful publisher must catch. The presenter sells a paid KDP coaching program and promotes a discovery call for it in the video.
