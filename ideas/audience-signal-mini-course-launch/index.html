Building a Narrow Mini-course by Listening for Your Audience's Repeated 'how Do You Do That?' Questions and Launching it With a Public FOMO Campaign

Content & personal brand · Very low (a Canva document plus unlisted vertical videos, no expensive course platform required)

Originally published Sep 3, 2026 — Brian O'Neill

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Brian O'Neill · This $57 Digital Product Made $313,000 in 6 Days ↗

What It Is

Post content in a topic you're genuinely interested in until people start repeatedly asking a specific 'how do you do that?' question, then build a narrow, simple mini-course (a handful of steps, a Canva walkthrough document plus short unlisted videos) that answers exactly that one question, launch it through a waitlist with a closing deadline, and drive urgency by publicly documenting sales milestones and reminders across stories and posts throughout the launch window.

The Pitch

The presenter analyzes another creator's launch, in which a $57 mini-course reportedly made $313,000 in 6 days ($50,000 in the first 24 hours) after she pivoted her content to a specific interest, got audience questions about her video-editing technique, and turned those questions directly into a course.

Best For

Someone already building an audience or willing to start, patient enough to let genuine audience questions surface before deciding what to sell, comfortable launching publicly with visible urgency/scarcity messaging.

The Truth

The core principle, that repeated audience questions are a reliable buying signal and a narrow single-problem course converts better than an over-built one, is sound, evidence-based content-marketing advice. This is a third-party breakdown of one unusually large launch, not a guaranteed formula, and the presenter has no personal relationship with the creator and hasn't independently verified the revenue figures beyond what she posted publicly.
