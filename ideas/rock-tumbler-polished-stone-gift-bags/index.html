Polished Stone Gift Sets

Rock tumbler polished stones sold as bags and gift sets on Etsy and at markets

What It Is

You put rough stones, water and grit into a tumbler barrel and let it run about four weeks per batch until the stones come out smooth and shiny. Small bags sell for $8-15 and gift sets with a card and pouch for $20-30, so the creator says $20 of rough stones can become $60-100. Sales channels include Etsy, weekend markets, gift shops buying in bulk, school fairs and party favors.

The Pitch

The creator says the machine runs on its own while you do other things, making it one of the lowest-effort ways to build a steady side income. He suggests a second barrel or back-to-back batches so you always have stock.

Who It's Best For

Patient hobbyists who enjoy stones and crafts and want a slow, low-effort product side income.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator is candid that this is slow, and the income per batch is modest. Tumblers can be noisy and run for weeks, so placement at home matters. Polished stones are widely sold, so presentation and gift packaging are what make them sell. Figures are the creator's estimates. The creator sells a paid 'roadmap' guide for starting a home-based machine business (supplier info, budgets, calculators, outreach templates), linked in the description.
