Peer-to-peer Storage Tote Rental Service

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (roughly $100 for an initial batch of totes, expandable as demand grows)

Originally published Aug 13, 2026 — Austin Zaback

Share on Facebook Link copied

One of 5 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Rent out 27-gallon plastic storage totes to local customers who need extra storage during a move, listed on Facebook Marketplace, with each renter signing a rental agreement and card on file, rather than requiring an upfront purchase of a full inventory before getting a first customer.

The Pitch

Zaback profiles Chelsea Wade, who reportedly didn't buy a single tote until she had her first paying customer, then scaled to over 140 totes at roughly $5/tote (about $100 per 20 totes rented). He frames this as an almost zero-risk business model, citing a fragmented storage-rental market (independent operators control about 73% of it, no dominant national player) and Americans spending roughly $40 billion/year on storage.

Best For

Someone wanting a very low-risk way to test a local rental business idea before committing capital, comfortable managing customer agreements and tracking physical inventory locally.

The Truth

The "prove demand before buying inventory" approach is a genuinely useful, low-risk way to validate this kind of business, and the underlying mechanic is simple. That said, per-unit margins are thin ($5/tote), so meaningful income requires real scale (dozens to hundreds of totes) and consistent local demand, which will vary a lot by area — this isn't described as a business that replaces a full-time income on its own without significant growth.
