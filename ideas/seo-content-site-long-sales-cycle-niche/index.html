SEO Site for Long Buying Cycles

SEO content site targeting a niche's long research-and-comparison sales cycle

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (a domain and hosting; content can be AI-assisted)

What It Is

Build a content-driven SEO website in a niche where buyers go through a long research process before purchasing (the example given: peptide supplements), targeting the low-competition, long-tail search terms people use while researching (benefits, side effects, comparisons, expert opinions) rather than competing on high-competition, ready-to-buy keywords, and supplementing articles with useful interactive tools (calculators) and organized expert-opinion roundups.

The Pitch

The presenter shows a real example site (built by the same guest, Eddie) that grew to 1.1 million monthly visitors purely through SEO, using three content strategies: long-form comparison and how-to articles targeting research-stage keywords, free calculator tools relevant to the niche, and pages organizing what named industry experts have said about specific topics within the niche.

Best For

Someone willing to do real keyword and content-gap research and produce a genuinely useful volume of long-form content, rather than a handful of thin articles.

The Truth

The three-part content strategy (comparison content, useful tools, expert-opinion aggregation) is a solid, well-documented SEO approach that outlasts algorithm changes better than purely AI-generated thin content. Reaching 1.1 million monthly visitors is a standout result built over real time, not an overnight outcome, and the niche example used (peptides) is explicitly flagged by the presenter as controversial, which may not be a comfortable content area for everyone to build a business around.
