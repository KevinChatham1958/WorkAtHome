What It Is

You build an audience by posting content about AI news, tutorials, experiments and your own learning journey. Income comes later from sponsorships, ad revenue, paid communities, courses, coaching or consulting. Claude handles scripting and AI tools produce thumbnails, B-roll and graphics.

The Pitch

Dang argues there are still too few AI creators relative to the number of people wanting to learn AI. He advises studying three to five creators, using Claude to break down their scripts, and finding under-covered topics; he cites a client who grew from 300 to 5,000 subscribers in two weeks by focusing on Gemini. He says sponsors pay thousands per post, a paid community might charge $9 a month and high-ticket coaching $5,000, and he reports over $250,000 in lifetime YouTube ad revenue himself.

Best For

Suits people who enjoy learning in public, can stay consistent for many months, and are comfortable on camera or in writing.

The Truth

This is a long-term audience play, and Dang himself rates it slower and harder to start than his service ideas. His and his clients' growth and income figures are self-reported and come from a creator whose business is selling AI coaching, which is a survivorship-biased sample. The AI niche is getting crowded quickly, and income usually takes many months to appear. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
