Faceless Ghostwriting

What It Is

Writing books, newsletters, LinkedIn posts, or email sequences under someone else's name for pay, with the client taking public credit, optionally speeding up drafting with AI tools.

The Pitch

Cost of entry is just writing ability plus AI tools. The presenter suggests starting with nonfiction or short-form work, which is faster to produce than fiction, noting a full 10,000-word nonfiction book commonly starts around $500 with rates climbing as you gain experience and a track record.

Best For

Someone who already writes competently and doesn't need public credit for the work to feel worthwhile; fiction specialists should expect substantially more effort than nonfiction ghostwriting.

The Truth

The presenter promotes his own paid AI writing tool partway through this segment, so the ease-of-use framing should be weighed against that incentive. The $500-for-a-10k-word-book figure is a typical starting rate, not a guarantee, and actual freelance listings vary widely by niche.
