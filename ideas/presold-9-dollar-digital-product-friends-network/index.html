Pre-Sold $9 Digital Product

Pre-sold $9 digital product (sell it before you build it)

What It Is

You set up a payment account (Stripe or PayPal) and a simple storefront such as Gumroad or Stan Store, then list a small digital product priced at $9 before it exists, clearly labeled as a pre-order. You share the link with friends, family, group chats, LinkedIn and Instagram, and once someone pays you have about a week to build it and email it to them. Products from the creator's students included a weight-loss meal plan, an ebook on moving to Dubai, a guide to speaking up at work, a sleep and stress plan, and a simple Mac folder app. The next step is selling the finished product to ten more people to pass $100.

The Pitch

The creator says selling before building validates demand the way Kickstarter does, and gives you a deadline to actually finish the product. He frames it as the second rung of a "1K ladder" meant to build the habit of asking for money in small, low-risk steps. He says it is not a scam as long as buyers are told plainly that it is a pre-order.

Who It’s Best For

Complete beginners who have some personal knowledge or experience worth packaging and want a low-risk first step into selling online.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a sensible confidence-building exercise, but most of the student examples sold only to friends and family, and the creator openly says that is not a viable long-term strategy; one student noted he still had not sold to a stranger. The income examples come from the creator's own challenge and are not independently verified. Stan Store is mentioned with an affiliate free-trial link, the video is sponsored by an AI content tool, and the creator runs a paid lifestyle business academy that some challenge students later joined. Expect earnings in the tens of dollars unless you already have an audience.
