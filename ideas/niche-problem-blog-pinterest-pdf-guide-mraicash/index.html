Niche Blog With PDF Guide

Niche problem-solving blog promoted on Pinterest, selling its own AI-made PDF guide plus display ads

Startup cost: Low startup cost (cheap web hosting, AI subscription, optional Higgsfield plan) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

What It Is

You find a recurring problem people complain about on Reddit and Quora (his example is a baby who won't sleep without being held) and build a WordPress site around it on cheap Hostinger hosting with the Astra theme and the free Zip AI plugin. Claude, connected to Higgsfield, researches the problem and produces an illustrated PDF guide with checklists and trackers, which you sell for about $29 through a Lemon Squeezy payment link on a landing page. You then use a Claude skill to write SEO blog posts that mention the guide, publish them with the Yoast SEO plugin, and turn each post into four Pinterest pins scheduled about three hours apart. Income comes from guide sales and, once traffic is high enough, display ads on the blog.

The Pitch

The creator says sites like this earn twice, from ads and from a product promoted in every post, and opens with estimated revenues of $3,000 to $5,000 a month for unnamed sites. He argues the setup is done once, after which promotion takes under an hour a day with Claude doing most of the writing. Choosing a problem that renews every year (new parents arrive every month) keeps demand steady.

Who It’s Best For

Patient people comfortable with basic WordPress who can stick with daily posting for months before traffic builds, and who can check that the advice they publish is accurate.

The Truth (as we see it)

Blogs that combine Pinterest and search traffic with ads and their own digital product are a real, long-running model, and the steps shown are concrete. The opening revenue figures are estimates for sites the creator doesn't name or own, and he shows no income of his own. Pinterest and Google traffic usually take months to build, ad networks require a minimum traffic level before you qualify, and search engines are increasingly hostile to mass-produced AI posts. A topic like infant sleep is health-related, so AI-researched advice needs real checking. The Hostinger link is a discount link (he says it isn't sponsored), the prompts and skill are free on his blog, and the 'secret' for beating competition promised early on is never clearly delivered.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
