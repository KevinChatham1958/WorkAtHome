Seven-Step Shopify Tee Launch

A seven-step playbook for launching a Shopify t-shirt brand and chasing 100 sales in the first month with weekend catalog ads

  • Under $100 to start

Startup cost: Low startup cost (Shopify, AI subscription; about $50 per weekend in ad testing)

What It Is

You follow seven steps in order: pick a niche you belong to; write a one-paragraph brand 'why' with Claude and spend one to two hours researching bestseller patterns; make 100 designs (5 to 10 minutes each with ChatGPT); build a fast, mobile-friendly Shopify store with a flat shipping rate and free shipping over $75; upload all designs to the Gildan 64000 shirt in six to eight colors at $29.99, about 20 uploads a day; then test ten flat-lay mockups at $5 to $6 per ad set before running a catalog ad of the whole store at $12.50 a day, Thursday to Sunday. After that, every week you add 25 new designs by Wednesday, run the weekend ads, and spend 30 minutes on Monday reviewing which designs got cheap clicks.

The Pitch

The creator says her first store made only 52 sales in four months, her next hit 100 sales in nine days, and her current brand reached 100 sales in six days and over 200,000 sales in three years. She says 5 to 10% of designs become winners, so 100 designs gives you real chances, and that testing the whole catalog at once finds them in one or two weekends instead of months.

Who It’s Best For

Organized beginners who want an exact, week-by-week routine for a work-at-home t-shirt brand and are comfortable using AI for designs.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is the most detailed playbook in her channel, with specific prices, budgets and target numbers you can actually follow, and the weekly routine is realistic about the ongoing work. Reaching 100 sales in a month is her own unverified result from a seller with years of experience; a first-timer should expect slower progress, and the ad testing costs money whether or not anything sells. She repeatedly pushes her free 'Million Dollar Brand Blueprint', an email signup that funnels viewers toward her and her husband's paid WeScale course and community; the 'brand builder GPT' she uses is offered through that signup, and the 'Build My POD' Shopify setup tool she recommends is her own team's product.
