What It Is

You generate a realistic AI 'creator' image, upload it with a photo of the product, and write a prompt with the action and the exact spoken lines; the Seedance model inside Arcads produces a 30-second vertical video of the character using and talking about the product, with matching voice and lip sync. The same method makes product-only ads, app demos with a screen recording on the phone, clothing try-ons and skincare routines. You post the videos on social media or run them as ads to drive traffic to your own products or affiliate links.

The Pitch

The creator says a comparable video from a real influencer would cost a few hundred to a few thousand dollars and take days or weeks, while this takes about five minutes, and a month of the tool costs less than one human-made video.

Who It’s Best For

Product sellers and affiliates who need a steady supply of promo videos without hiring creators.

The Truth (as we see it)

The videos she shows are impressive, but she shows no sales or ad results from them. Presenting AI people as real customers giving testimonials can mislead viewers and may break platform and advertising rules on disclosure, so label them as AI. The video is effectively a sponsored walkthrough of Arcads, and she promotes her paid community.
