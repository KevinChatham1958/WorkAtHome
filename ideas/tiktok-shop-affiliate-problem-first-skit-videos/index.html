Problem-Focused TikTok Shop Videos

TikTok Shop affiliate videos built around one buyer's problem (skits and talking-head, no ad feel)

Content & personal brand · Near-zero cost (a phone and a TikTok account; optional paid product-research tool)

What It Is

You join TikTok Shop as an affiliate (not a seller), pick products from brands that are already selling and putting ad money behind them, and post short videos with your commission link; you earn roughly 15-30% of each sale. The guest's method is to write each video for one specific buyer and their problem: a hook in the first three seconds, the problem, relating to it, why the product is credible, what life looks like after, and only then the product. He recommends three to six videos a day (about two to four hours including product research) or two-hour livestreams, and uses a paid research tool (Kalodata) to find products with rising revenue.

The Pitch

The guest says he went from a $73K corporate job and $432 in the bank to his first $10,000 month within five months part-time, and around $30,000 a month since. He argues TikTok's ad system now rewards affiliates who choose brands with ad budgets, that ordinary-looking people convert well, and that a niche video reaching 200,000 of the right viewers outsells a million-view generic one.

Best For

Someone comfortable on camera who can post several videos a day for months without results, and who enjoys studying what makes short videos sell.

The Truth

The income figures are the guest's own and unverified; by his own account he earned about $100 in his first three months and his accounts were once banned for a month, which shows how uneven this is. Account bans for TikTok policy violations are a real risk he acknowledges. The guest sells coaching (Escape Society) and a script-writing app (ScriptKit) that the host promotes during the interview. TikTok Shop affiliate marketing is common advice among WAH creators, and availability depends on country.
