Coaching Community (3C Model)

The 3C model: turning expertise into a scalable coaching community (sell before you build)

  • Under $100 to start

Startup cost: Very low startup cost (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Source video

Sunny Lenarduzzi · The Smartest Way To Turn Your Expertise Into $1M ↗

What It Is

A framework for turning personal expertise into a scalable program that combines the best parts of one-on-one coaching, memberships, traditional courses, and cohort programs rather than picking just one: a tightly scoped Curriculum (a specific transformation for a specific type of client, not "everything about business"), Coaching at scale (live group calls that feel personal but serve many people simultaneously), and Community (peer accountability and connection), built using a specific validation method: interview real prospective clients about their exact struggle before building anything, then convert those same interviewees into your first paying cohort.

The Pitch

The presenter argues traditional pre-recorded courses have become much harder to sell now that AI and free content have made raw information abundant, so the real differentiator left is live, human coaching and community, things that can't be replaced by an AI chatbot or a YouTube video. A specific, named contrast is given: one client spent two years building a course alone before launching to silence, then rebuilt using this interview-first, sell-before-you-build method and scaled to $10 million in four years. The interview questions themselves (what are you struggling with, what would you need to solve it, what would need to happen) are described as directly generating both the curriculum content and, through simple end-of-interview referral asks, a pipeline of the program's first paying clients.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with genuine, hands-on expertise in a specific area who has been putting off building a course or program out of fear it won't sell, rather than someone looking for a topic to build expertise in from scratch.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific case study (two years alone to silence, then $10 million in four years using this method) is a real, if unverified, contrast the presenter offers as evidence for the model, one data point rather than an audited track record. The core interview-before-you-build validation technique is genuinely sound, tool-agnostic advice independent of this presenter's specific program, and echoes validation approaches recommended elsewhere in this database. The video promotes the presenter's own free guide and coaching program, a standard funnel structure worth knowing.
