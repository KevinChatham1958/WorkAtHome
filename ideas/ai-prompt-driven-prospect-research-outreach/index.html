Using a Detailed AI Prompt to Research Verified Local-business Prospects and Draft Outreach for an AI-consulting First Client

Local & professional services · Very low (an AI chat subscription like Claude or ChatGPT with web access)

What It Is

Give an AI model (with live web access) a detailed, rule-bound prompt that has it pick a business niche and offer, research and verify 20+ real local prospects against their actual websites (flagging anything it can't verify rather than inventing details), draft outreach messages built around a specific, checkable observation about each business, and produce pricing plus a build spec for a first prototype, giving a beginner with no clients or portfolio a concrete, individually-researched outreach plan to execute.

The Pitch

The presenter ran the identical prompt through two frontier AI models role-playing as his own less-experienced self from 18 months ago, then spot-checked the AI-identified prospects against their live websites, one model found a real broken booking form on an actual local business's site, exactly the kind of concrete 'leak' that gets a cold outreach message answered. He gives away the full prompt and stresses that the prompt itself, not which model runs it, is the real asset.

Best For

Someone starting an AI-consulting or 'find and fix operational leaks' service from zero, with no existing portfolio or client base, willing to personally verify AI-generated research before sending outreach.

The Truth

The core technique, a rule-bound research prompt that forces verification and flags unconfirmed claims, is a genuinely sound way to generate higher-quality, personalized outreach than generic cold pitches, and the presenter demonstrates real spot-checking rather than just trusting the AI's output. The video is explicitly a model comparison/review rather than a step-by-step gig walkthrough, both models' outputs are described as 'not a client' since no outreach was actually sent in the video, and it promotes the presenter's own paid School community and consulting services.
