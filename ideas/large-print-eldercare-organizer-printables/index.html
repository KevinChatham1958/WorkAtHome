Eldercare Organizer Printables

Large-print organizer printables for adult children caring for aging parents

  • Digital products
  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Video published Oct 30, 2026 by Frank Garrett

Startup cost: Near-zero cost (an AI chatbot, a design tool, a simple store page) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Frank Garrett · Old People + Free PDFs = Lawyer Salary (30k/mo) ↗

What It Is

Make printable, fill-in-by-hand PDF tools for families looking after elderly parents, aimed at the adult child (often in her 50s) who does the buying: a who-to-call sheet for by the phone, a where-everything-is record, an appointment question list, a medication list the family fills in, a downsizing checklist, a caregiver handover sheet for siblings, scam-awareness and smartphone basics guides. Find problems by reading top posts in caregiving subreddits and Facebook groups, build each tool with an AI chatbot told never to give medical, legal or financial advice, and design for paper: 16-18 point text, black on white, plain fonts, page numbers. Promote with calm, useful Facebook posts and Pinterest pins, and offer free printables to senior centers, libraries and caregiver groups. He suggests pricing around $47.

The Pitch

The creator argues this is a market almost nobody builds for well: the buyer has money and urgency, competition is low, and simple competence and honesty stand out in a space full of scams.

Who It’s Best For

Careful, empathetic people who understand eldercare logistics and are willing to test their documents with real older readers.

The Truth (as we see it)

The niche insight and the strict "organizational tools only, no advice" rule are genuinely thoughtful, and large-print design is an easy edge. The $30,000-a-month figure is the creator's overall PDF income, not this niche specifically, and he notes Facebook and Pinterest take months to build. Many caregiver groups ban promotion, so read their rules. The video promotes his paid PDF course.
