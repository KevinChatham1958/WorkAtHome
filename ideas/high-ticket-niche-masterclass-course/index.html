Creating a Detailed, High-ticket Masterclass Course Teaching Your Own Proven Business System in Full Depth

Digital & subscription products · Low (time investment is the real cost; no special tools required beyond video recording/editing)

One of 9 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Take a business system you've already proven works for yourself (in this case, building niche affiliate YouTube channels) and package it into one comprehensive, detailed, hand-holding paid course — a step above a quick free tutorial — covering every resource, trick, and detail you know about the topic, sold for several hundred dollars per student.

The Pitch

The presenter is candid that this specific stream took roughly three months to create (versus days for a shorter free tutorial) and, despite students paying hundreds of dollars, hasn't been a great financial decision for him personally, because he feels obligated to over-help paying students in a way that conflicts with the low-stress, passive lifestyle he otherwise built; he still sells it today because of the genuine value inside, but doesn't actively promote it.

Best For

Someone with a genuinely proven system willing to trade significant upfront creation time for a premium price point, and prepared for the higher support obligation that comes with high-ticket customers.

The Truth

The core tradeoff described, a high-ticket course commanding a premium price but creating a real ongoing support burden that undercuts passivity, is an honest, commonly-echoed observation from people who've actually built and sold premium courses, not a rosy oversell. No specific revenue figures for this course are given, only the time-cost caveat.
