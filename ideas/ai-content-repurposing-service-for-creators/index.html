What It Is

You take a creator's existing content, such as long YouTube tutorials, cut and adapt it into shorts and posts for TikTok, Instagram, Threads and other platforms, and set up AI systems to repurpose and publish it automatically. You charge a monthly retainer to run and maintain it.

The Pitch

The creator says most creators aren't system builders but all want to grow, so pitching 'more followers on new platforms with zero extra effort' beats pitching a single task like editing. Knowing AI automation gives you an edge most creators lack.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who understands social platforms and automation tools and can pitch creators by DM.

The Truth (as we see it)

Repurposing is a well-established service and the outcome-based pitch is sound advice, but promising follower growth is risky because results depend on the content and algorithms. Fully automated posting can produce low-quality clips. The creator sells her own AI app.
