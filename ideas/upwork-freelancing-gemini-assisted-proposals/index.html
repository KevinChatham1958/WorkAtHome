Upwork Freelancing With AI-assisted Proposals and Automation Delivery

What It Is

Create an Upwork profile and apply to jobs (particularly AI automation and workflow-building jobs using tools like N8N), using an AI tool (Gemini) to draft tailored proposals from your background and the specific job post, and to help write the actual automation code or copy being delivered.

The Pitch

The presenter says this is how he personally landed his first freelance income, eventually making over $3,000 on the platform with a 100% job success rate and a top-rated badge. He frames Upwork's advantage as reaching buyers who already know they have a problem and are looking for a solution (versus needing to convince a stranger they have a problem at all), and describes using Gemini to draft N8N automation code for clients' workflow requests.

Best For

Someone with some existing skill (automation, copywriting, or a technical background) willing to start with small jobs to build a track record, comfortable using AI tools to speed up both the pitch and the delivery.

The Truth

Upwork freelancing is a genuinely low-barrier way to get a first paying client, and AI-assisted proposals/delivery is a real time-saver, but the video also shows plenty of very low-budget job postings ($5-20) alongside a few outlier freelancers earning six figures — most income on the platform sits well below those outlier examples, and building a track record from zero still takes real time and effort.
