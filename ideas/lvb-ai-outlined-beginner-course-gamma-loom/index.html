AI-Outlined Online Course

AI-outlined online course built with Gamma slides and Loom, sold through an email funnel

What It Is

You use ChatGPT to outline a course with modules, lessons and learning objectives on a topic you know, then turn the outline into slides with Gamma. You record lessons over the slides with Loom (screen plus your face) or run them as live sessions that become recordings. You attract students by posting tips on Threads or LinkedIn, offering a free checklist or mini-course (or a low-price Etsy product) to collect emails, then sending a nurture sequence that offers the course. The creator suggests starting at $27, $47 or $97 and raising prices as testimonials come in.

The Pitch

The creator calls courses "where the real money is" because one course can sell for $97 to $299 or more and is built once. She says people prefer structured, step-by-step learning over piecing together free content. She argues an email funnel lets you sell without a large following.

Who It’s Best For

People with real expertise who are comfortable on camera or teaching live and patient enough to build trust through email.

The Truth (as we see it)

Course selling is a proven model, but this video gives no course revenue figures, and income depends heavily on trust and audience, which takes time. The creator herself notes people do not buy courses from strangers. You will appear on video or in live sessions in her method. The video promotes her free prompt guide and references her own students.
