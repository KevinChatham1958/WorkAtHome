What It Is

You build a simple calculator or spreadsheet for one specific type of buyer, such as rental property investors, and sell it for around $10, marketing it to that audience through content they already follow.

The Pitch

London rebuilds a zero-sale product and says the rental property calculator is 'not bad' because it serves a clear buyer, but that it failed because nobody knew or trusted the seller.

Who It’s Best For

Spreadsheet-savvy people who belong to the audience they're selling to.

The Truth (as we see it)

His main point is that most of these products don't sell, and his rebuild isn't shown making money yet. A calculator used for financial decisions must be accurate, and it doesn't replace professional advice. London also runs a marketing company, Evolvant, which he mentions in his videos.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
