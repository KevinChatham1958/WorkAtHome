What It Is

You build an online presence around a skill, hobby or life experience by posting on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest, or in written form on X, Substack or a blog. Claude or ChatGPT helps you choose a niche through an interview-style prompt and then generates content ideas, hooks, scripts and images in your voice, and a scheduling app posts the content automatically. Once you post consistently, you earn through affiliate links to products you use, your own digital products like guides and ebooks, online coaching, brand partnerships and ad revenue.

The Pitch

The creator says this is the side hustle that has made her the most, over six figures a year, and that it made over $10,000 by month two with zero followers. She argues you don't need to be famous or an expert, and points to seven-figure brands about painting, gardening and learning activities for kids as proof it works across niches.

Who It’s Best For

People with a skill or passion they can talk about for months, who want several income streams and can be patient while an audience grows.

The Truth (as we see it)

Monetizing a personal brand through affiliates, digital products and sponsorships is a proven model, and AI can speed up the content work. The creator's income claims, including over $10,000 by month two, are her own and unaudited, and the examples she shows are top performers, not typical results. Most personal brands take a long time to earn anything meaningful, and heavily AI-written content can feel generic. The creator promotes her paid community and a free checklist, and the video is sponsored by an AI video-creation tool.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
