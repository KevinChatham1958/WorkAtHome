Jordan Welch · How I Used Claude AI To Make $102k In 90 Days ↗

What It Is

Build an entire Shopify e-commerce store, including product images, website copy, and design, using AI tools rather than hiring designers, copywriters, or photographers. Welch used Claude for the site's code and written copy, and an AI video/image generation tool (Higgsfield) for product visuals and marketing videos.

The Pitch

Welch frames this as a case study: he says he took a 17-year-old subscriber with less than $100 to their name and helped them build a store that did over $100,000 in sales within 90 days, and over $200,000 within six months, using only about 30 minutes of work a day. He argues that where store-building once required real skill in web design, copywriting, and content creation, AI tools now handle most of that work, leaving execution and consistency as the main differentiators.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable spending real time experimenting inside AI tools daily and willing to treat this as an ongoing store to manage, not a one-time setup.

The Truth (as we see it)

A few things worth flagging: Welch is promoting his own free course and a pre-built AI Shopify store template alongside this video, so there's a clear incentive to make the process sound as simple and fast as possible. He also has a long, established e-commerce career (he mentions a personal store portfolio doing over $10 million a year), so his starting skill level isn't really comparable to a true beginner's. Unlike the AI-built dropshipping model covered elsewhere in this database, this video doesn't specify a product-sourcing method (no mention of a dropshipping supplier tool), so it's not clear if this specific case study is dropshipping, print-on-demand, or another model, worth digging into before assuming it works the same way.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
