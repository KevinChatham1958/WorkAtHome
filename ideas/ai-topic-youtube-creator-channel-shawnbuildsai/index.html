What It Is

You make YouTube videos helping people use AI, appearing on camera, and earn from YouTube ads, tips and memberships (such as Patreon), affiliate deals, brand sponsorships and your own merchandise. He uses AI tools of his own to write hooks and plan video flow. The core work is finding in-demand topics, packaging them to stand out, and explaining them clearly.

The Pitch

The creator says several of his friends are doing this successfully and there are many ways to monetize. He shows his own growth from videos with under 150 views to several above 20,000, arguing it is a learnable skill.

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable on camera who enjoy learning and explaining new tools, and who can keep publishing for many months before it pays.

The Truth (as we see it)

Running an educational YouTube channel with several income streams is a real model, and the creator is honest that it is a hard skill that took him time. AI how-to content is one of the most crowded niches on YouTube, and he shows no income figures for his channel. Expect a long ramp before ad revenue or sponsors arrive. He offers free downloadable AI skills (behind a name-and-email form) and free one-on-one coaching calls in the description; free calls like these are a common lead-in to paid offers, though the video names no paid product.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
