What It Is

You set up a system for a professional firm that asks clients for missing paperwork, tracks what has arrived, summarises it and flags anything odd for staff to review. You charge per file or a small monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Kimberly says chasing clients for paperwork is the most painful, expensive and boring job at these firms, and that people charge per file or a small monthly retainer for this. She cites Upwork data showing demand for AI integration work up 178% in a year.

Who It’s Best For

Organised people with some tech comfort who can talk to small-firm owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

Client documents are sensitive (tax, insurance, immigration), so privacy and security are serious obligations. No earnings are shown. Selling to firms takes outreach. The video has a sponsored segment for Xero.
