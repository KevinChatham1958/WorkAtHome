What It Is

Sell other companies' products for a commission, through social media, a blog, or direct referrals, using programs like Amazon Associates or similar affiliate networks.

The Pitch

A straightforward, low-barrier way to earn without creating your own product, you're paid for driving a sale, not for building anything from scratch.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with direct selling or persuasive communication, since results depend heavily on your own reach and credibility.

The Truth (as we see it)

Presented generically with no specific income figures, this is a well-established model, but success varies enormously based on audience size and niche, results here are far from guaranteed or typical.
