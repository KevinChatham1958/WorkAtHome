Domain Investing and Lead Microsites

Domain investing and instant lead-gen microsites for underserved local searches

What It Is

Speculatively buying inexpensive, often-overlooked domain names, either to build instant, AI-generated lead-generation microsites for underserved local-service search terms, or to hold and resell premium address-based domains to developers and businesses.

The Pitch

The guest argues .com domains are still wildly underpriced relative to their potential value. He demonstrates the method with a pressure-washing example: search a service-plus-city combination, and if there's real search volume but only one or two competitors rather than a crowded market with paid ads and a map pack, buy a cheap exact-match domain, use AI tools to quickly build a generic lead-gen site, then wait. Once inbound leads start arriving, give the first few away free to a real local business to build trust, then start charging per lead once traffic proves out. A parallel version: use AI to generate lists of the most common street names and building-address numbers in a market, buy the matching domains in bulk for a few dollars each, and hold them to resell later to real estate developers or property buyers who want that exact address online.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with a low-cost, patience-based numbers game, many cheap domains, most going nowhere, a few paying off, rather than someone wanting a single fast-moving business.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a live, off-the-cuff brainstorm between two marketing hosts and a serial entrepreneur guest, not a personally tested case study with real revenue numbers from either idea, so treat the specific dollar figures as illustrative reasoning rather than demonstrated results. The core logic, buying long-established, still-undervalued domain inventory, is presented as a real and repeatable opportunity independent of the specific example given.
