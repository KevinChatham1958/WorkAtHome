Lead-Capture Websites for Local Businesses

Building websites and lead-capture automations for local businesses that don't have one, with a monthly hosting retainer

What It Is

Use an AI tool to scrape Google Maps or business directories for local businesses (especially trades like plumbers, roofers, landscapers, and salons, where up to 45-56% still lack a website) that have no website or only a broken/basic one, then pitch and build them a simple site with a lead-capture form or AI-driven automation (e.g. a WhatsApp/form-based agent), charging around $500 upfront plus a recurring $50-75/month hosting and maintenance fee since you retain control of their hosting.

The Pitch

The presenter argues the real opportunity isn't a saturated $80 Fiverr website gig but a mid-market position ($500, above the race-to-the-bottom price but below $5,000 agency rates) sold on the basis of "you never have to think about this again," using AI to build a targeted prospect list (business name, contact info, missing-website status) from a given postal code rather than cold-searching Google Maps by hand, then reaching out with a Loom video demo before ever discussing price, and adding a small monthly hosting fee to turn a one-time job into recurring income.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with direct outreach (email, DM, or cold call) to local business owners, and willing to build a simple sample website or automation on spec to demonstrate value before pitching price.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core premise — a meaningful share of small local trade businesses genuinely lack a website — is broadly plausible and the recurring-hosting-fee structure is a real, sound way to turn a one-time build into repeat income. The presenter doesn't cite an independent source for the specific 27%/45-56% figures, so treat them as approximate rather than verified statistics, and landing the first few clients still requires real outreach and sales skill that the video doesn't walk through step by step.
