AI "Mini Movie" Ads

AI-generated "mini movie" viral ad creation service for e-commerce brands, tested across three outreach channels

  • Under $100 to start

Startup cost: Very low startup cost (ChatGPT plan plus an AI video tool like TopView, roughly $20-50/month; a $1 domain)

Source video

Mr. AI Cash · I Gave ChatGPT 7 Days to Make $1,000 ↗

What It Is

Use the Facebook Ads Library to find products with long-running ads (a proxy for "this is working") that solve a clear problem with a strong "wow factor," then use ChatGPT connected to an AI video-generation tool (via an MCP integration) to produce a short, funny, narrative "mini movie" style ad (not a testimonial-style UGC ad) for that product in three 15-second clips, build a branded portfolio website and $1 domain entirely with ChatGPT, and pitch the finished samples to e-commerce and dropshipping brands running similar ads.

The Pitch

In a documented 7-day test, the presenter tried three client-acquisition channels side by side: automated cold email outreach (found leads and their contact info via AI, sent messages daily) produced replies but zero closed sales; posting the sample ads organically on Instagram with a call-to-action produced one $57 sale by day six; and direct outreach via WhatsApp links found in the Facebook Ads Library (using a wa.me search filter to find brands already used to messaging customers there) produced the fastest results — the first sale plus an $810/month upgrade after the client saw one finished video. Total revenue was $987 against real, disclosed costs, netting $572.20 profit for the week.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with a genuinely technical AI tool chain (ChatGPT plus a connected video-generation platform) and willing to test multiple outreach channels rather than betting on just one.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is unusually transparent for a challenge-format video: the presenter explicitly reports the method that didn't work (automated cold email, zero sales) alongside what did, and gives an honest final tally that fell short of the stated $1,000 goal rather than declaring an inflated win. The comedic "mini movie" narrative format is a distinct creative style from the testimonial-style UGC-cloning approach covered by other similar entries in this database, and results depend heavily on finding genuinely funny, product-appropriate concepts — not every product lends itself to this format as well as the example shown.
