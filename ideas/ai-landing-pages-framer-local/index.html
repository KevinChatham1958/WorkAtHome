AI-Built Landing Pages

AI-built landing pages for local businesses (Framer and Claude)

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Ben Hawksworth · 7 AI Businesses You Can Start This Weekend (Just Claude) ↗

One of 7 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Build a modern landing page for a local business, a restaurant, gym, dentist, or estate agent with an old or confusing site, by describing the business to an AI website builder (the video uses Framer, alongside Claude and Claude Code), then doing the human work of sharpening the message and call to action.

The Pitch

Hawksworth is explicit that AI removes most of the technical build time, generating a full landing page live in the video, but argues the real value was never the code, it's understanding who the customer is, what the offer is, and what's stopping them from buying, which is still work a person has to do. He suggests charging around £1,500 per page, plus ongoing support to keep it maintained.

Best For

Someone with a decent eye for what makes a page convert, a clear offer, a strong call to action, who's willing to do that judgment work on top of what the AI generates, rather than just shipping the AI's first draft.

The Truth

The £1,500 price point is the presenter's own suggestion rather than a documented sale. This is a similar underlying mechanic to other AI-website-building content in this space, with Framer as the specific tool of choice here, worth trying a couple of AI website builders yourself to see which workflow you prefer before committing to one.
