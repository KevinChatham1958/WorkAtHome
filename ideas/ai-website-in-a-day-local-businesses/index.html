AI Websites for Local Businesses

AI-built websites in a day for local businesses, with monthly upkeep retainers

What It Is

You find local businesses with no website or an outdated one, through Fiverr and Upwork or by searching Google Maps (for example "landscaper near me"). You design homepage and inner-page mockups in ChatGPT, have Claude Code turn the mockups into a working mobile-friendly site, and deploy a demo to Vercel's free plan. You send the owner the live link with an offer to put it on their domain, then charge to build it plus an optional $50 to $100 monthly retainer for updates and hosting.

The Pitch

The creator says he built websites for a living before YouTube and that it let him leave his job; with AI a site that took weeks now takes an afternoon. He argues that showing a finished demo is far easier to say yes to than a sales pitch, and that 10 clients at $100 a month would be $1,000 a month in recurring income.

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable reaching out to small-business owners and managing ongoing client relationships.

The Truth (as we see it)

Spec-website outreach is a well-known model, and AI tools do make building a demo fast. The hard part, as the creator admits, is finding clients: many owners ignore unsolicited messages, and those who reply will expect edits, domain setup, hosting and support. A site built by AI still needs checking for broken links, accessibility and basic SEO, and the $1,000 monthly figure is an illustration, not a reported result.
