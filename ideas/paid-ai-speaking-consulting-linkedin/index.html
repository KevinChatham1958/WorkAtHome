Paid AI Speaking and Consulting Sourced Entirely Through LinkedIn

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (time to build a LinkedIn presence)

Originally published Aug 16, 2026 — Sandy Lee AI (guest: Shen)

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Sandy Lee AI (guest: Shen) — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=81BBqOVaWsI

What It Is

Build visible expertise in applying AI to a specific field (Shen's background is financial services and fintech) through consistent LinkedIn posting and in-person networking events, then convert the inbound interest that generates into two separate paid income streams: paid speaking engagements and AI consulting or upskilling contracts, all without doing any cold outbound.

The Pitch

Shen describes going from unpaid, entry-level speaking slots to bookings that cover first-class travel and hotel, with some speakers she knows charging $10,000 to $100,000-plus per engagement, and describes her own consulting day rate as roughly $1,600, with some clients paying $20,000 a day for larger upskilling contracts. She's explicit that every one of her deals, including her own startup's first customers, came through inbound LinkedIn messages and networking event relationships rather than any outbound sales process.

Best For

Someone with genuinely deep, specific expertise (not just AI-curious) who's willing to consistently show up in public, on LinkedIn and at industry networking events, over a period of months to years before results compound.

The Truth

Shen is upfront that this took real time to build: her first paid speaking gig came well after a period of doing free events, and building both the consulting and speaking pipelines took roughly two and a half to three years of consistent effort. Her figures are specific and self-reported from her own deals, not third-party verified, but the specific mechanic, deliberately avoiding cold outbound in favor of consistent public content and in-person relationship building, is a clear and independently testable strategy regardless of the exact dollar figures.
