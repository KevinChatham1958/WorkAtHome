What It Is

Instead of chasing trendy digital product launches, create products in categories that solve a dull, recurring problem: (1) legal/contract templates, $27-150/pack; (2) spreadsheet templates and calculators, $15-35 simple trackers, $37-97+ niche calculators; (3) resume/LinkedIn kits (ATS-friendly), $10-39; (4) digital planners and printables, $5-20 printables, $20-45 hyperlinked planners, with dated planners creating natural yearly repeat purchases; (5) Canva template packs, $12-30 single sets, $30-67 bundles; (6) Lightroom presets and video LUT packs, $6-25 starter packs, $30-80 themed bundles; (7) Notion templates and systems, $15 simple up to $79+ premium bundles; (8) website/funnel templates for Framer, Webflow, WordPress, or Shopify, $19-350+ depending on platform; (9) business SOPs, checklists, and swipe files, $27-150, with proven playbooks reaching $97-297.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the common thread across all nine as: each solves a problem that doesn't go away and refreshes with new buyers every month, arguing boring, reliable demand beats a trendy product that dies off after launch hype fades, and demonstrates bundling multiple products into one checkout to increase revenue per customer.

Who It’s Best For

Someone deciding which type of digital product to create, looking for a category with durable, non-trend-dependent demand rather than chasing whatever's currently viral.

The Truth (as we see it)

All nine categories are real, established digital-product types with genuine ongoing buyer demand, and the specific pricing ranges given are consistent with what similar products commonly sell for. No personal revenue figures are given; it promotes the presenter's own checkout platform and a separate paid course as next steps.
