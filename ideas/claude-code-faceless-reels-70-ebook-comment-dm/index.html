AI-Edited Reels Selling an Ebook

Faceless AI-edited reels made with Claude Code, posted as many trial reels, selling a $70 ebook through comment-to-DM automation

  • $100–$500 to start

Startup cost: Very low startup cost (about $100 a month in tools: Claude Pro, Epidemic Sound, X Premium, TweetHunter, ManyChat)

What It Is

You find short-form edits you like on Instagram, feed them to Claude Code with detailed notes, and have it generate similar minimalist, faceless videos (white objects, moving text, sound effects from Epidemic Sound). Each video is posted many times as Instagram trial reels with different music, plus once on X, with a call to action to comment a keyword. ManyChat (Instagram) and TweetHunter (X) automatically DM every commenter a Stripe payment link for a digital product, here a 120-page ChatGPT-written ebook on the editing method priced at $70. In the creator's 24-hour test, one of 20 trial reels reached about 780,000 views and the posts produced 27 sales ($1,890) against about $101 in monthly tool costs.

The Pitch

The creator says Claude Code can now edit and generate videos from one prompt, so you no longer need editing skills or a paid editor, and the work stays faceless. He argues trial reels are 'free attempts to go viral' because followers don't see them, so posting the same video 10 to 20 times costs nothing. He also notes X brought in over a quarter of sales from about 5% of the views.

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable with AI tools and social media who want to sell a simple digital product without showing their face, and who can tolerate many posts that flop.

The Truth (as we see it)

The setup and costs are shown on screen and are plausible, but the sales figures are the creator's own and unaudited, and he says he has been doing this for about three years with billions of views, so a brand-new account should not expect similar results quickly. Results depend on one video going viral, which he admits takes luck at first. Selling a ChatGPT-generated ebook for $70 is a thin product; he mentions refunds, failed payments and abandoned checkouts, and buyers may feel shortchanged. The video promotes his free course as a funnel into a one-on-one coaching program (done-with-you and done-for-you options) sold by application and call, and it runs a giveaway to drive engagement.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
