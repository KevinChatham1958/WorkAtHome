Problem-Solving Ebooks and Audiobooks

Problem-solving nonfiction ebooks on Amazon KDP, also sold as audiobooks

What It Is

You publish full-length nonfiction books that solve common problems, such as gardening, mental health, dating, cleaning or cookbooks, on Amazon KDP, and turn the same books into audiobooks for Audible. The process in the video has five steps: find a topic people are searching for now with low competition, research what readers need and use ChatGPT or Claude to draft chapter by chapter, get a professional cover made by AI or a freelancer, upload and price the book, and repeat to build a portfolio. The presenter avoids low-content books like journals and coloring books because she says the margins are too thin.

The Pitch

She says she started in January 2019, quit a $4,000-a-month job by July 2019, and reached $20,442 in one month on KDP by October 2020, with income compounding as older books keep selling. Early on she earned about half from KDP and half from Audible, and she says Q4 holiday shopping roughly doubles sales.

Best For

Patient people who like research and can publish steadily over many months. It works for parents and retirees because the work is flexible and entirely at home.

The Truth

The dashboards shown are mostly from 2019-2020, when there was far less AI-generated competition on Amazon, so today's results may be very different. She stresses quality over 'AI slop', which matters because Amazon has tightened rules on low-quality AI content. Earnings are her own and unaudited, and her early books were written without AI. The video promotes the creator's paid Passive Publishing Challenge from acceleratedpublishing.com.
