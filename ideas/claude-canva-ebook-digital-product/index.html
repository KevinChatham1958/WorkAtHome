What It Is

Ask Claude for trending ebook topic ideas within a niche you know something about, have it write the full ebook content, then copy the text into a free Canva ebook template to format and design it, producing a sellable digital product with minimal writing time.

The Pitch

Presented as a low-cost, low-effort way to enter the digital products space, framed as a growing category over the next 5-10 years, with the presenter offering a free course covering distribution/selling specifics not shown in this video.

Who It's Best For

Someone with a topic they know enough about to add personalized value on top of AI-generated content, comfortable with basic Canva design work.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI-assisted ebook creation paired with a design template is a well-established, low-barrier method, and this specific video only covers the content-creation portion, not selling or distribution, which the presenter says is covered in a separate free course.
