What It Is

You turn a problem you've personally solved, like keeping up at university, into a structured online course for people now facing it, and sell it at a price that reflects the result. Your own experience is the research.

The Pitch

Richard says he was 21, with no credentials, when he built a $500 productivity course that students at Harvard, Stanford and Cambridge bought, and that it did over $500,000 in under eight months.

Who It’s Best For

People who have clearly solved a painful problem and can explain how.

The Truth (as we see it)

His first result came with an audience and timing most beginners won't have; he now cites over $30 million across his dashboards. A $500 course needs trust, which usually means months of free content first. Richard says he has built systems for over 3,000 people who sell digital products, and his videos promote his own paid programs.
