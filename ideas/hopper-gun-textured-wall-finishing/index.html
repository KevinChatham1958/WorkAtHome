What It Is

The Pitch

Skilled operators bill $300-500 per textured room and can complete 2-3 rooms in a day, targeting homeowners doing remodels, interior designers, boutique hotels, and general contractors looking to subcontract specialty wall finishing.

Best For

Someone willing to practice the spray technique to a professional finish quality, with the lowest capital barrier on this list.

The Truth

Hopper-gun textured wall finishing is a real, established specialty trade service with genuinely low startup cost. Pricing figures are general industry framing rather than a verified personal case study.
