Floor Mat and Uniform Route

Running a floor mat and work uniform exchange route for offices and restaurants

Local & professional services · Moderate (vehicle and initial mat/uniform inventory)

What It Is

Run a route where a driver visits offices and restaurants every two weeks, swapping dirty floor mats and work uniforms for clean ones, billed as a recurring B2B service contract.

The Pitch

The presenter cites Cintas, a $9.6 billion company built on exactly this simple exchange model, controlling nearly a third of a $20+ billion industry, framing it as proof that a genuinely boring, repeatable route business can scale enormously without any unique invention.

Best For

Someone comfortable with a driving/logistics route business and building recurring B2B service contracts.

The Truth

Cintas is a real, massive, publicly-known company validating this exact business model at scale. No specific small-operator case study or figures are given for someone starting from scratch in this niche.
