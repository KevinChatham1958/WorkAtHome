Vibe-Coded Interactive Products

Interactive AI-native digital products built with vibe coding

Digital & subscription products · Low startup cost (requires a CRM/vibe-coding platform subscription)

What It Is

Move beyond static digital products (PDFs, Canva templates) to interactive, AI-native tools built through vibe coding inside a platform like GoHighLevel's AI Studio: interactive workbooks that let a buyer voice-dictate answers and get auto-generated results, calculators (revenue, pricing, savings), client-tracking dashboards, bundles of custom marketing assets for a specific niche, and custom GPTs trained on your own expertise that a buyer can consult directly.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates building a functional interactive lead-magnet workbook live by talking to the AI tool in plain language, no code, describing toggles, autosave behavior, and design changes and having the tool implement them. She argues the appeal of building within a CRM-integrated tool specifically (versus a disconnected app builder like Lovable) is that the finished product connects directly to a payment processor and customer data, so it's sellable immediately rather than needing a separate integration step.

Who It's Best For

Someone already using or willing to adopt a CRM-style platform with a built-in AI app builder, comfortable describing what they want conversationally rather than writing traditional prompts or code.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter promotes her own affiliate link and a paid coaching community throughout the video, both disclosed, and several of the specific examples shown (a boxing coach's client tracker, a meal-prep business's marketing-asset bundle) are hers or her husband's own tools rather than independently documented sales. The core shift she's describing, static files versus interactive, buildable-by-talking tools, is a genuine and independently verifiable capability shift regardless of which specific platform is used to build it.
