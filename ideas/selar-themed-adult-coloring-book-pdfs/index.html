Themed Adult Coloring Books

Themed adult coloring books sold as PDF downloads or through Amazon KDP

Digital & subscription products · Near-zero cost (illustration or design tool)

What It Is

You create a set of coloring pages around a theme and an audience, such as travel, cozy self-care or faith, and sell the set as a PDF that buyers download and print, or as a paperback on Amazon KDP. The creator's example is a self-care coloring book for Black women featuring a recurring character, Rosie, doing self-care activities. The work is designing the pages around a planned 'experience' rather than stitching together random pictures.

The Pitch

The creator says adult coloring has become very popular because people want a screen break and a way to unwind, so you are selling an experience rather than information. She says a strategic theme aimed at a specific person is what makes a coloring book sell.

Who It's Best For

People who can draw, or design with illustration tools, and who want to make a product for a specific community they understand.

The Truth (as we see it)

Adult coloring books are a real and very competitive market, especially on Amazon KDP where many sellers mass-produce them. The creator's emphasis on a defined audience and a consistent character is a reasonable way to differ from generic books. No sales figures are given. If you use AI image tools to make pages, check the platform's AI-content rules and disclosure requirements. The creator is promoting a waitlist for her own paid annual membership, the Digital Gold Mine Club, which teaches these formats.
