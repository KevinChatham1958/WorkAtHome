AI Outbound Lead Generation

Running AI-assisted outbound lead generation (cold email/LinkedIn) for B2B companies in under-served niches

Marketing & content · Low (a Claude subscription for list-building and scripts)

What It Is

Build prospect lists and outreach scripts with Claude, then run outbound cold email/LinkedIn campaigns on behalf of B2B companies that need more sales calls but aren't skilled at outbound themselves; specifically target less glamorous, 'boring' niches (logistics, law, trades, and similar) where fewer people are already pitching the exact same outbound service, since the presenter notes outbound-lead-gen sellers ironically all compete for clients using outbound themselves.

The Pitch

The presenter scores this 18/25 (ease 3, speed 3, validated 5, competition 2, long-term 5), explicitly flagging competition as this idea's weak point since so many people sell outbound lead generation using outbound lead generation to find clients, and cites charging $1,000-5,000/month retainers plus commission, fully negotiable.

Who It's Best For

Someone with genuine sales/outreach skill willing to specialize in an under-pitched niche rather than the most obvious, crowded verticals.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI-assisted list building and cold outreach is a real, established service category, and the presenter's honest acknowledgment that this specific idea faces real competition (rather than presenting it as a blue ocean) adds credibility. Pricing figures are the presenter's own stated ranges, not independently verified, and the video promotes a free companion Claude skill and his paid training.
