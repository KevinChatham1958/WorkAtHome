Short-Run Screen Printing

Short-run screen printing for local business packaging

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (about $300-650 for a starter screen printing setup)

Share on Facebook Link copied

One of 10 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Screen print short runs (a few dozen pieces) of branded paper bags, fabric pouches, posters, boxes, and tote bags for small local businesses (cafes, bakeries, market vendors) who need only small quantities with their logo, rather than the large minimum orders typical of commercial screen printing.

The Pitch

A starter system (screens, a small press or hinge clamps, squeegees, inks, emulsion or reusable stencils, drying space, cleaning supplies) runs $300-650. The presenter emphasizes that this business gets stronger the more times a single screen is reused for the same customer's reorders, and warns against accepting a different complicated design for every tiny order, since setup and cleaning time can exceed the actual printing time.

Best For

Someone willing to focus on simple, one-color logo designs for repeat local business customers rather than chasing varied, complex one-off designs.

The Truth

The insight about reusable screens driving profitability on repeat orders (versus one-off custom work) is a genuinely useful operating principle that applies to most small-batch manufacturing, not just screen printing specifically.
