Planner App Sold via Meta Ads

AI-built planner/tracker app sold as a subscription via Meta ads

Digital & subscription products · Needs ongoing ad budget

Originally published Aug 22, 2026 — Alex

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Alex · how i make $800 per day selling boring digital products + claude ↗

What It Is

Using an AI niche-research tool to find a proven, in-demand planner/tracker niche (e.g. a Gen Z budgeting tracker), then an AI app builder to turn that idea into an interactive, mobile-optimized web app in minutes, and selling access as a monthly subscription through the creator's own website, driven by paid Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ad campaigns pointed at a dedicated sales page.

The Pitch

The creator walks through the entire pipeline live: validating a niche, building the actual app, standing up a subscription sales page, and configuring a real Meta ad campaign (budget, targeting, conversion pixel, ad creative), arguing that even if a batch of new subscribers only breaks even on ad spend in month one, the recurring subscription charge in later months is where the real profit comes from, so you shouldn't pull ads just because month one didn't turn a profit.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with the technical side of running paid Meta ad campaigns (pixels, conversion tracking, budgets, creative testing) in addition to using AI tools to build the product itself, since running ads well is presented as an ongoing, real skill this model depends on, not a set-and-forget step.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter shows a real, live run-through of the workflow end to end rather than only describing it, and cites third-party Etsy sales data (a competing shop with 59,000 sales in two years) as general market validation for the planner/tracker niche, though his own $800/day figure for this specific method is a self-reported screenshot, not independently verified. Running Meta ads well takes real, ongoing ad spend and iteration, this is not a zero-cost model, and the "break even on ads, profit from renewals" logic only works if you actually retain subscribers month over month.
