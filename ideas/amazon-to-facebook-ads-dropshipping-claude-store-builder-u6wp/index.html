Trending Amazon Products Dropshipping

Dropshipping trending Amazon products with Facebook ads and a Claude-built Shopify store

Startup cost: Needs ongoing ad budget (about $100/day test budget, Shopify plan, store app)

What It Is

You look for products that are newly selling well on Amazon or TikTok Shop, using a free scanner that filters Amazon listings from the last 30 days and looking for a high 'bought in past month' count with few reviews. You check the Facebook Ads Library to confirm few others are advertising it, and AliExpress to confirm it can be sourced. A store app connected to Claude builds the Shopify product page, homepage and bundle offers from the Amazon link, and you launch a broad-targeted Facebook sales campaign at about $100 a day split across three to five ad sets of video creatives. Winners are scaled by raising the budget; suppliers ship directly to customers.

The Pitch

The creator calls this 'traffic arbitrage': selling on Facebook what is already proven on another platform, ideally before competitors arrive. He shows past store dashboards and claims a $12,000 day at roughly 30% profit, and says AI now makes building the store take minutes instead of days.

Who It’s Best For

People with a few thousand dollars they can afford to lose on ad testing who are comfortable reading ad metrics daily.

The Truth (as we see it)

The sales screenshots are the creator's own and unaudited, and revenue is not profit; he estimates about 30% margin on his best day. Paid dropshipping testing burns money quickly at $100 a day, and many tests fail before one wins. His suggestion to download other people's TikTok videos for ads can raise copyright and platform-policy problems. He promotes his own free course, which works as a funnel to further offers, and links to the store-building app he uses.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
