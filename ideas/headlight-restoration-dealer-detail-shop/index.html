Headlight Restoration Service Selling to Dealerships and Detail Shops

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (about $125 for a restoration kit and rotary polisher)

What It Is

Restore cloudy or yellowed headlights, primarily by selling to used car dealerships and detail shops that need every incoming trade-in restored before resale, rather than marketing to individual drivers who only need this once every few years.

The Pitch

A Meguiar's heavy-duty restoration kit (about $26) plus a variable-speed rotary polisher (about $99) comes to roughly $125 total, with consumable cost per headlight under 50 cents. Restorations bill $40-160 depending on lens condition, with $65 as a common market rate and dealers benchmarking around $90. The presenter warns that underpricing (advertising a $30 restoration) signals to experienced buyers that a necessary coating step was skipped.

Best For

Someone comfortable pitching recurring B2B relationships with dealerships and detail shops rather than relying on individual homeowner jobs, which the presenter says aren't frequent enough to support a full-time income.

The Truth

The presenter is unusually candid that this doesn't work as a standalone full-time income selling to individual drivers — the real business is the wholesale/trade-in pipeline through dealers and detail shops, which requires building those specific commercial relationships, not just skill with a polisher.
