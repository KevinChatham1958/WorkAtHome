AI Agent Security Setup

AI agent access-control setup for accounting firms (via their IT providers)

AI & automation · Low to moderate startup cost (security know-how and tools)

Originally published Oct 6, 2026 — Greg Isenberg

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Isenberg · 13 Businesses for the Age of AI ↗

One of 8 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You help firms that handle sensitive data use AI agents safely by limiting what each agent can see and do. The creator's example is accounting firms: agents are allowed only to read invoices and draft entries, so data does not leak and systems are not exposed. He suggests selling through the firms' existing IT providers. You earn setup fees or ongoing service fees.

The Pitch

The creator expects more hacks and data breaches as AI agents spread, possibly including a major breach at an agent startup, and says businesses will pay to feel secure. He sees security as one of the few clearly lasting needs in the AI era.

Who It's Best For

People with an IT, cybersecurity or accounting-systems background who can win the trust of professional firms.

The Truth (as we see it)

Concern about AI agent security is real and growing, but this is specialist work: firms will expect proven expertise, and a mistake can be serious. Selling through IT providers is sensible but means building partnerships. The video gives only the example, with no details on tools, pricing or how to break in. The video is a fast list of 13 business categories rather than a how-to, so there are no steps, prices or income figures to check, and the creator does not pitch a paid product of his own in this episode.
