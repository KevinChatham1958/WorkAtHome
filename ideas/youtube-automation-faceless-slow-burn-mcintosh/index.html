What It Is

You run a faceless channel on YouTube, Instagram or TikTok where AI helps write scripts and paid AI tools generate the videos, so you never appear on camera. You publish frequently, ideally daily, to grow an audience until you qualify for platform monetization by hitting watch-hour and subscriber thresholds. Income comes from ad revenue once monetized.

The Pitch

The creator ranks this the slowest of seven AI business ideas but says it does work and lets you stay anonymous. He says AI makes production easy even if you would never go on camera.

Who It’s Best For

Patient people who want to stay off camera and can commit to publishing consistently for many months before earning.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is one of the more candid takes on faceless channels: the creator stresses it takes long, consistent effort, that AI video tools cost money, and that YouTube pays less per view than people expect. Platforms have also tightened rules on low-effort, mass-produced AI content, which can block monetization. No income figures are offered. Disclosure: the creator is co-founder and CEO of Buildy.ai, the tool he demonstrates and recommends throughout, and it also runs an affiliate program.
