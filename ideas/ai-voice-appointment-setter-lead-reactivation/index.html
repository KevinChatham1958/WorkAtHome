AI Voice Appointment Setter

AI voice appointment-setting and lead-reactivation agent for home service businesses

What It Is

Sell home service businesses (roofing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical) an AI voice agent that answers new leads within seconds, qualifies them, and books them directly onto the owner's calendar, plus a "lead reactivation" version of the same agent that calls through the business's existing list of old, cold, or unbooked leads to re-engage them with a promotion or special offer.

The Pitch

Turri, who says his own AI agency generates $40-50k/month, prices this system at an $8,000-15,000 setup fee plus a $2,000-4,000/month retainer, and cites installing it for a roofing company that generated $159,000 in additional revenue in 3 months, with call-response times dropping from hours to under 2 minutes and lead capture going from about half of calls to 100%. He frames the appeal to business owners as an easily quantifiable ROI and recommends also charging for ad management if the client doesn't already have enough lead flow to feed the system.

Who It’s Best For

Someone interested in selling and consulting rather than building software themselves — Turri's own model delegates all technical fulfillment to a developer for 10-15% of the client fee, so this fits someone comfortable with cold calling, sales conversations, and picking one home-service niche to specialize in.

The Truth (as we see it)

The mechanic (speed-to-lead AI voice agents, cold-lead reactivation campaigns) is a real and increasingly common offering in the AI agency space, and the ROI framing is a genuinely useful way to sell it. The $159,000 case study and pricing figures are Turri's own reported numbers from his agency, not independently verified, and the video is also a funnel toward his own paid mentorship program — success here depends heavily on the sales and client-acquisition skill described more than on the AI technology itself.
