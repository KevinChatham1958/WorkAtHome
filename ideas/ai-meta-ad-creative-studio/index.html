What It Is

You produce a steady stream of static, video and UGC-style ad creatives for brands that advertise on Facebook and Instagram. Claude writes the hooks, scripts and ad copy, and an AI image/video tool generates the visuals. Brands pay a monthly fee based on how many creatives you deliver.

The Pitch

Dang argues that brands need constant fresh creatives because winning ads burn out quickly, and AI now makes producing them fast. His suggested way in is to search Meta Ads Library for brands running very few ads (he shows a phone maker with only 26 active ads), build sample ads from their website images using Claude connected to Higgsfield, and pitch them. He says a sample set took about five minutes and that the work can bring $1,000 to $5,000 per month per client or more at higher volume. He rates it his top idea at 23 of 25.

Best For

Suits someone with a good eye for advertising who is comfortable doing cold outreach with spec work, and who can learn what makes an ad actually convert.

The Truth

Brands do buy ad creative at volume, and sending free samples is a known way to open doors. But generating images is the easy part; clients keep paying only if the ads perform, which requires real direct-response and testing knowledge the video does not teach. A brand running few ads may simply have a small budget rather than a creative gap, and many freelancers and agencies are now offering the same AI-creative pitch. Pricing figures are Dang's own estimates. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
