Printable Kids' Activity Packs

Printable kids' educational activity packs for parents, homeschoolers and teachers

What It Is

You make themed learning packs for a specific age group, such as a 'Preschool Ocean Adventure' pack for ages 3-5 or 'Middle School Math Detective' challenges for 10-12, and sell them on Etsy for $8-20. The ChatGPT prompt asks for 20 activities from basic to advanced, 10 printable worksheets, five hands-on activities using household items, three group activities, a parent guide explaining the learning benefits, and reward certificates. You design the final pages in Canva so they are printer-friendly and organized by difficulty.

The Pitch

The creator says parents feel guilty about screen time and teachers are short on prep time, so ready-made activities sell. She shows faith-based learning shops she says made over $80,000 and $178,000 in about 2.5 years, and suggests adding a parent guide to stand out and earn repeat buyers.

Who It’s Best For

Former teachers, homeschooling parents or anyone who understands what children of a given age can do.

The Truth (as we see it)

Educational printables have strong, lasting demand and reward subject knowledge. AI drafts often contain age-inappropriate or incorrect content, so careful review is essential, and good layout takes real time. The shop revenue figures are the creator's estimates. The creator offers the prompts free in the video description and does not pitch a paid product in this video.
