What It Is

You sell a 30-, 60- or 90-day content system built for one type of business owner, such as personal injury lawyers. It includes daily topics, hooks, scripts, captions, calls to action, email ideas, video topics and repurposing instructions designed to bring in leads and consultations. Claude drafts the plan from a detailed prompt, and you refine it before packaging it as a digital product.

The Pitch

The creator frames it as selling three months of planned marketing rather than a simple PDF, which he says makes it more valuable to busy professionals.

Best For

People with marketing experience in a specific industry who know what content works for those businesses.

The Truth

Tanner Chidester sells coaching for business owners through links in the description, and the $26,000-a-day figure is a hypothetical funnel calculation, not results from this product. Regulated professions such as law have advertising rules that generic AI content may break, so expert review is essential. No sales numbers are shown for this product, and buyers could generate a basic plan with AI themselves.
