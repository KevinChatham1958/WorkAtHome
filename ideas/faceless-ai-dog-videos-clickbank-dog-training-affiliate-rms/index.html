Faceless Dog Video Affiliate Channel

Faceless AI cartoon YouTube videos on dog behavior that promote a dog-training course through a ClickBank affiliate link

What It Is

You join ClickBank free and pick an offer, here the Brain Training for Dogs course paying about $40 a sale. With vidIQ connected to Claude, you prompt it to find faceless dog-behavior videos getting many times their channel's usual views, then to pick a title using low-competition search keywords (his example: 'Why Your Dog Barks at Everything'). Claude then writes a prompt for an AI video generator such as InVideo, which makes a cartoon-style narrated video ending in a call to action for the course. You put your affiliate link behind a simple Claude-built landing page hosted free on Netlify, shorten the link, add it to the video description, and mark the video as AI-generated when uploading.

The Pitch

The creator says dog owners are a huge audience (nearly half of US households) and AI cartoon dog videos regularly reach hundreds of thousands to millions of views on small channels. He argues that aiming for topics that are both outlier hits and still low-competition gives a new video a better chance of being found in search, and that viewers searching about problems like barking are ready buyers. He calls it 'lazy' because three prompts do most of the research and scripting.

Who It’s Best For

Patient people happy to publish many videos and track what gets views, who don't want to appear on camera.

The Truth (as we see it)

The research workflow is concrete, and he correctly warns that copying topics that already went viral is usually too late. He shows other channels' view counts but no sales or earnings of his own, so any income is unproven here; most faceless channels get few views at first and affiliate conversions from YouTube are uncertain. The video links his vidIQ, InVideo and ClickBank referral links and pushes ClickBank's own training, and he notes ClickBank profits when affiliates sell. YouTube has been tightening rules on mass-produced, low-originality AI content, so videos need real, useful advice to hold up.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
