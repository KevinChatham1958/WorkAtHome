AI Dropshipping Store With Video Ads

AI-built dropshipping store with auto-loaded products and AI video ads

E-commerce · Very low startup cost ($1/month Shopify promo, free AI store builder)

Jeremy Ray Holst · How to Build a FREE AI Dropshipping Store in 5 Minutes ↗

What It Is

Use a guided "free AI store builder" wizard to set up a niche-specific Shopify store in a few clicks (pick a niche, colors, and banner), connect the ZenDrop supplier app so trending, pre-vetted products get automatically loaded onto the store with fulfillment handled on autopilot, then generate product marketing videos using only a screenshot of the product and a text prompt through a tool called smplsocial.io.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates the entire build, from a blank page to a live, product-stocked store, in under 10 minutes on camera, emphasizing that no product ever has to be purchased, held, or physically touched to create realistic-looking marketing videos of it in use. The AI video step specifically removes the traditional friction of dropshipping marketing, no need to own the product, film it, or hire an editor, since a single screenshot and prompt produces a finished vertical video.

Who It's Best For

A true beginner who wants the fastest possible path to a live, functioning store and is comfortable letting an automated supplier and AI tools handle product selection and content creation rather than doing deep niche research themselves.

The Truth (as we see it)

The video is structured around the presenter's own free store-builder tool and a free course and community (dropshippingstarter.com) that it funnels into, both disclosed but worth factoring in given how central they are to the walkthrough. No revenue or sales results are shown or claimed for this specific store within the video, it demonstrates the build process only, not a proven outcome.
