AI Website Concierge Service

AI concierge for small-business websites, priced by the value of the leads it captures

AI & automation · Low startup cost (about $300-$400 a month in software plus AI usage once you have clients)

Originally published Nov 3, 2026 — Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast · Forget Building Websites. Local Businesses Pay $4K/Month for This ↗

What It Is

Install an AI "guide" on a small business's website that answers visitors' questions by chat or voice from a knowledge base built from the site, books appointments, and collects contact details from serious buyers. The 60-year-old guest builds a sandbox copy of the prospect's own site with the agent running so the owner can see it working, then installs it free for about four weeks before charging. He prices each client by what the extra leads are worth, from about $297 to roughly $4,000 a month plus a $1,000 to $5,000 setup fee for complex sites. He says about 26 clients, many in forklifts and pallet racking, bring in $25,000 to $30,000 a month, and each takes an hour or two a month to maintain.

The Pitch

The guest says high-intent visitors convert faster when they get instant answers instead of digging through FAQs, and one client's $13,000 engines drew 50 inquiries in a month. He argues the work fits around a full-time job and that even non-technical people can learn it.

Who It's Best For

People with some sales comfort and patience for tuning AI answers who can cold-call or meet small-business owners and understand their customers' questions.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI website assistants are a real and fast-growing service, and the guest's practical tips (build a demo on the prospect's own site, free trial, price on lead value, always say it's an AI) are sensible. Revenue figures are his own and unaudited, and he already owned two businesses and had industry contacts when he started. He reports a 30-40% close rate but admits clients sometimes saw no bump. The host's channel is sponsored by GoHighLevel, the software used, and the host promotes his paid AI agency community in the episode.
