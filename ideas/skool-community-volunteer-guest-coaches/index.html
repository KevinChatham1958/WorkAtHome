Paid Community With Guest Coaches

Paid Skool community with volunteer guest coaches and tiered pricing

What It Is

You run a paid online community on Skool around a skill you already teach (here, sales training). The course library is recorded as short straight-to-camera Loom videos; the transcripts are turned into written SOPs, and each module's transcripts are fed to AI to produce a course handbook. Instead of coaching everyone yourself, you recruit lesser-known coaches who run free weekly group sessions inside the community in exchange for being allowed to sell their own one-on-one services to members. Members pay a monthly fee, with a cheaper tier for content only and a higher tier that includes the live coaching calls.

The Pitch

The guest in this clip says he moved from a YouTube membership (which took about 40% and felt clunky) to Skool, rebuilt seven courses in about three days, and now has around 1,200 members paying $69 or $149 a month for about four hours of his own time per week. He says the volunteer coaches give members far more live sessions than he could run alone, while the coaches gain clients, and that Skool's own ad program brings in extra members for a cut. He credits a strong, irreverent group culture for 83% retention.

Who It’s Best For

People who already have an audience and a teachable skill, and who would rather organize a community and bring in other experts than coach every member personally.

The Truth (as we see it)

The structure is sensible and repeatable: record lessons quickly, charge monthly, and let guest experts supply live value in exchange for access to buyers. But the income figures are the guest's own, unaudited, and they rest on a large existing following in a niche where he is already well known; the free trial conversion rates he quotes come from that audience. Someone starting without an audience will find member growth the hard part, and Skool's ad boosting is not something a new community can count on. This is a short clip from a podcast, so details on pricing tests, churn handling and managing volunteer coaches are thin. Skool charges a monthly fee plus a cut on members it brings in, so margins will be lower than the headline numbers suggest.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
