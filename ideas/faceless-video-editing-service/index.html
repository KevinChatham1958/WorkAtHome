What It Is

Editing video content, short-form or long-form, for creators and businesses who post regularly but don't want to edit it themselves, working entirely behind the scenes through async communication like Loom and text.

The Pitch

The barrier to entry is low since free tools like DaVinci Resolve and CapCut cover most of the work, and a portfolio can be built using practice edits of existing public videos rather than requiring paying clients first. Presenter frames it as one of the fastest routes to first dollars: a typical 10-20 minute edit starts around $100, so 10 videos a month is $1,000 as a beginner, scaling with better clients.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants fast, tangible income from a specific skill and is comfortable trading hours for dollars rather than building a scalable asset right away.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is explicit that income stays tied to hours worked, so this doesn't become passive on its own. The $100-per-video, $1,000-per-month figures are presented as typical starting points, not guarantees, and rates vary significantly by niche and client.
