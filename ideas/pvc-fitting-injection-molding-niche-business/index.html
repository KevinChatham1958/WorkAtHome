PVC Pipe Fitting Molding

PVC pipe fitting molding business built around one chosen niche

What It Is

Injection-molding PVC pipe fittings (elbows, tees, couplings, irrigation parts, pool fittings) for one chosen niche — irrigation, pool repair, drainage connectors, hard-to-find adapters, or private-label fittings for a regional supplier — rather than trying to cover the full range of plumbing fittings.

The Pitch

Plumbers, farmers, and contractors constantly need fitting replacements, and hardware stores stock them because customers need them regularly. The presenter is explicit that every shape and size needs its own mold, so trying to become a general plumbing-fittings brand from day one adds cost fast — the smarter approach is picking one specific niche. A small setup runs $5,000-15,000 plus molds.

Best For

Someone willing to specialize narrowly in one niche rather than attempting to become a general-purpose plumbing-fittings supplier immediately.

The Truth

Accuracy is unforgiving here — a wrong-sized or weak fitting simply fails and the customer leaves — but a reliable niche supplier becomes close to indispensable, since a stalled job over one missing small part creates real urgency for the buyer.
