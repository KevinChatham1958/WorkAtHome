Seasonal Website Sales to Local Services

Selling AI-built websites to local service businesses, using seasonal cash-flow timing to pick when to target each niche

  • Services from home
  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Video published Sep 15, 2026 by Dylan Power

Startup cost: Low (cold email/lead-scraping tools, an AI website builder, GoHighLevel for hosting/upsells) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Dylan Power · F*ck It. Zero To $1,430/Day With AI Websites. (Full Course) ↗

What It Is

Build websites for local service businesses in proven niches (RV parks, tree surgeons, landscaping, while avoiding niches that look good on paper but aren't, like divorce attorneys, who are too busy and gatekept to respond) using AI to generate a finished site once a prospect shows interest, then sell it on a call for $1,000-2,000 plus $100/month hosting; critically, time outreach to when a seasonal niche has just finished its busy period (e.g. targeting landscapers in Q1, right after their May-August rush) so prospects have both cash reserves and available time; upsell existing clients afterward on SEO, review management, or ad management ($300-2,000/month) once trust is established.

The Pitch

The presenter shows a live Stripe dashboard with roughly £23,200 in sales over 3 weeks (~$1,430/day) from his AI website agency, and shares a cautionary story of a niche that looked perfect on paper but flopped because those prospects were too overwhelmed and gatekept to respond, arguing niche selection requires real operational nuance.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with cold outreach to local businesses and willing to research a niche's seasonal cash-flow cycle before choosing when to target it.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI-built websites sold to local service businesses is a well-established, frequently-covered business model, and this producer's specific contribution, the seasonal timing insight and the cautionary tale about a niche that looked good but wasn't, is a genuinely useful, non-obvious refinement. His own revenue figures are self-reported from a live dashboard screenshot, and 5 years of prior agency experience likely contribute to his execution speed.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
