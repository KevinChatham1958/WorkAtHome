AI Digital Products With Paid Ads

AI-built digital products (courses, ebooks, guides) sold with test-and-tweak paid traffic

What It Is

You create digital products such as courses, ebooks, training guides, audio, checklists or downloadable guides, using AI to build the product and its sales page. The creator's process is to send a small amount of paid ad traffic to the offer, see whether clicks turn into leads or sales, then adjust the page or offer and test again. He repeats this cycle until the product becomes profitable.

The Pitch

The creator calls this nearly his favorite model and says he has made tens of millions of dollars in revenue from digital products. He says building the product is now the easy part with AI, so the real focus should be sales and traffic.

Who It's Best For

People who are interested in marketing and willing to spend and learn on ad testing.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator is upfront that the hard part is getting buyers, and that first versions usually do not convert. Paid-traffic testing can lose money before anything works, so it suits people with a budget they can afford to spend while learning. His revenue figures are his own and unaudited. Disclosure: the creator is co-founder and CEO of Buildy.ai, which he recommends for building these products, and it also runs an affiliate program.
