Fresh Tortilla Supply Business for Local Taco Shops

What It Is

Supplying fresh tortillas daily to local taco shops and restaurants using a small commercial tortilla machine, rather than selling to individual consumers.

The Pitch

One small shop selling 300 tacos a day using two corn tortillas per taco goes through 600 tortillas before dinner is over; four similar accounts means roughly 2,400 tortillas a day in demand. Entry commercial tortilla machines currently run around $570-750, with larger automatic systems moving into the thousands — but that's only the machine price, not cooking equipment, packaging, freight, electrical work, a food-safe workspace, and delivery.

Best For

Someone willing to line up standing orders from several taco shops first (size, frequency, current pain points with their supplier) before buying equipment, and who can keep up reliable fresh delivery.

The Truth

The presenter is explicit that without confirmed repeat orders from multiple accounts, even a cheap tortilla machine just produces efficient leftovers — the business depends entirely on landing and keeping B2B supply accounts, which isn't addressed beyond identifying the target customer.
