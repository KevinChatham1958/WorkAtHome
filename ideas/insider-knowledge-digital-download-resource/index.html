Insider Knowledge as a Digital Download

Packaging your own insider industry knowledge into a small, low-cost digital download and selling it on a marketplace like Etsy

  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

What It Is

Distill specific, time-saving insider knowledge from a business you already run (the presenter's example: a curated list of the 10 best-selling print-on-demand T-shirt phrases, with a larger 100-phrase version also offered) into a small, focused digital download product, and sell it either through your own existing audience or on a marketplace like Etsy if you're starting without one.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as a genuinely passive, low-effort income stream built entirely from repackaging knowledge he already had rather than creating anything new, and is honest that it hasn't been a massive earner for him personally, positioning it as a reasonable side-addition rather than a primary business.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with real, specific insider knowledge from an existing business or skill, looking for a low-effort way to monetize that knowledge as a standalone product.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling small knowledge-based digital downloads via Etsy is a real, low-barrier product category, and the presenter is candid that this specific stream underperformed his other income streams rather than overselling it, which adds credibility.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
