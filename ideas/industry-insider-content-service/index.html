Video Coaching for Your Industry

Content and video coaching service sold to companies in your own industry

What It Is

You take an industry you already work in and sell its companies help with content: writing scripts and posts, shaping their videos, and coaching founders on how to come across well on camera. Claude does much of the research and scripting. Companies pay a monthly fee per client.

The Pitch

Dang's example is his client Brandon, a salesperson at a construction-glass company who loved making content. Instead of teaching content to a general audience, Brandon pitched glazing and construction companies on LinkedIn, arguing that one extra job worth $15,000 to $100,000 easily pays for a $2,000 to $3,000 monthly fee. Dang says Brandon signed three clients and went from about $750 a month on the side to roughly $10,000 a month within 30 days, while keeping his job.

Who It’s Best For

People with years inside a specific trade or industry who also enjoy making content and can talk to business owners in that industry as peers.

The Truth (as we see it)

The underlying logic is sound: insiders know a niche's customers and language, and high-value industries can justify the fee if content brings in even one deal. The speed and income figures come from one client story told by his coach and are not audited. Results depend on clients actually filming and posting consistently. Check your employment agreement before selling to companies in your employer's industry. The video promotes Dang's free Claude skill, free training and paid coaching calls.
