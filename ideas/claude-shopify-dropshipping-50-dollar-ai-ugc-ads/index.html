$50 Claude Dropshipping Test

Claude-built Shopify dropshipping store with AI-made UGC ads on a $50 Meta test

E-commerce · Low startup cost (about $50 in Meta ads, a $1/month Shopify trial, plus Claude and AI ad-tool subscriptions)

Originally published Sep 18, 2026 — Ac Hampton

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Ac Hampton · I Locked Myself In a Room Until I Made $1,000 With Claude ↗

What It Is

You find a product with proven demand by scrolling a fresh TikTok account, then check that the supplier price leaves at least a 2.5x markup (here an electric grill brush costing $10.15 that competitors sold for $39.99). Claude, briefed as an e-commerce strategist, researches competitor ads and writes the store copy, selling points and FAQ answers, which you paste into a basic Shopify store. An AI ad tool (Ecom Boss, connected to the same Claude project) generates about 15-18 UGC-style video and image ads, which run in a single Meta campaign-budget-optimization (CBO) campaign. The supplier ships orders straight to customers and you keep the difference between sale price and product plus ad cost.

The Pitch

The creator says he started from scratch to prove it was not luck and reached $1,029 in sales from 17 orders in about two days, claiming roughly 80% of that was profit. He argues the key was spotting a product whose demand was all flowing to TikTok Shop with no Shopify store competing, and letting Meta's CBO pick the winning ad (one still image) instead of guessing. His main lesson is not to touch a campaign while it is still learning.

Best For

Beginners who want to try dropshipping with a small, defined test budget and are comfortable running Meta ads and handling customer service for overseas-shipped goods.

The Truth

The step-by-step method (demand check, 2.5x margin rule, AI copy and ad creative, CBO testing) is a standard and reasonable dropshipping workflow, but the sales figures and the "80% profit" claim are the creator's own and unaudited; the full ad spend over two days is not clearly shown, and product, shipping and fee costs make an 80% margin hard to square with a $10 product sold near $40. One fast win with a seasonal (Father's Day) product does not show repeatable results. The video carries a Shopify trial link, an Ecom Boss affiliate code, a paid Melio sponsorship for paying suppliers by card, and a free product list from the creator's Supreme Ecom brand, which is a lead magnet. Overseas shipping times, refunds and payment-processor holds are common problems in dropshipping that the video does not cover.
