What It Is

Create small, highly specific digital files that solve one narrow problem: a practical guide (how to pass a particular exam, launch a specific business, or master one piece of software), a Notion template, a business spreadsheet or calculator, presentation templates, or photo-editing presets. Sell them on Gumroad, Etsy or your own site, and promote them across a blog and social media.

The Pitch

Enfroy says generic self-help ebooks and Canva templates are oversaturated, and that sellers win on specificity instead: a $47 guide to launching a YouTube automation channel will outsell a $10 generic business guide, and a $79 Notion template that saves a consultant 10 hours is an easy purchase. His example from his own business is a 15-page WordPress blog launch checklist, designed by a Fiverr freelancer, that he gave away free and used to grow his email list to 40,000 people. He says that list later produced about $250,000 in the first month of his first paid course. Revenue is a volume game: 100 copies at $29 is $2,900, 500 copies at $49 is about $24,000.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with real working knowledge of a niche audience who can turn that knowledge into a ready-to-use file, and who is willing to keep producing content to bring buyers in.

The Truth (as we see it)

Startup costs are low, but sales depend entirely on traffic, which Enfroy admits means putting out a lot of content across many platforms. His best result from this format came from a free PDF used to build an email list, not from selling it, so his own numbers show it working as a list-builder rather than proving it as a standalone income. The price-per-copy figures are illustrations, not results. The video promotes Enfroy's free "AI launch kit" lead magnet, which feeds his own paid programs.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
