Proprietary Niche Data Business

Proprietary niche dataset business (collect data AI tools can't get, sell access)

Digital & subscription products · Low to moderate startup cost (depends on how the data is collected)

Originally published Oct 6, 2026 — Greg Isenberg

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Isenberg · 13 Businesses for the Age of AI ↗

One of 8 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You collect useful information that people cannot get by asking ChatGPT or their personal AI agents, and sell access to it. At the large end, companies pay people for data and sell it to AI labs. The creator suggests a smaller solo version: pick a niche, build a dataset nobody else has, and sell it to businesses that need it.

The Pitch

The creator calls data 'the new oil' and says data-labeling companies selling to big AI labs have reached valuations in the billions. He believes a gold rush for acquiring data is only starting, and that a solo founder could build a business worth a few million a year from a valuable niche dataset.

Who It's Best For

Researchers, analysts or industry insiders who have access to information that is hard to find or put together.

The Truth (as we see it)

Data really is in high demand from AI companies, but the examples given are venture-funded firms, not one-person operations. The video does not say what data, who buys it, or how to price or deliver it, so this is a direction rather than a plan. Anyone collecting data needs to watch privacy law and the terms of the sites they collect from. The video is a fast list of 13 business categories rather than a how-to, so there are no steps, prices or income figures to check, and the creator does not pitch a paid product of his own in this episode.
