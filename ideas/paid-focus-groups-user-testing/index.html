What It Is

Sign up on platforms that pay everyday people for their opinions on products and services (the presenter names User Interviews, Respondent, and dscout), applying to daily interview opportunities, most conducted over video calls from home.

The Pitch

The presenter made $9,000 through this method, applying to about 5-10 opportunities daily even without expecting to be selected for most of them. Sessions pay $40-120/hour, selected slots fill within minutes so speed matters, and payment (via gift card or PayPal) typically arrives within 1-5 days.

Who It's Best For

Someone with reliable internet and video-call comfort who's willing to apply to many opportunities regularly, since selection isn't guaranteed and slots fill fast.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a genuine, low-effort way to earn supplemental income with no upfront cost, and the pay rates cited are real for this type of work. Income is inherently inconsistent since it depends on being selected for a limited number of daily opportunities, so it works better as a side income stream than a reliable primary one.
