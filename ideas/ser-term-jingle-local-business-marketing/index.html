Jingle Marketing for Local Businesses

"SER-term jingle" marketing service for local service businesses

Marketing & content · Low startup cost (requires either music production skill/equipment or the ability to hire a singer/producer)

What It Is

Create a short, catchy "earworm" jingle built around a specific, ownable search phrase (the business's "SER term" — search engine results term, e.g. "Google Pink Plumber") for a local service business (pest control, plumbing, HVAC), then run it as a cheap, geographically-targeted skippable YouTube pre-roll ad, licensing the same jingle concept and production process repeatedly to non-competing businesses in different markets (each business gets exclusivity in its own city/zip code), bundled with a broader "local internet presence" service (Google Business Profile management, high-converting website audits, content/podcast production).

The Pitch

The guest, a longtime musician and internet marketer, describes selling this exact jingle to over 100 pest control companies at $2,000 each (each getting exclusivity in their own market), plus an ongoing $500/month retainer for managing their broader local marketing (Google Business Profile, website conversion, content). One client's business reportedly tripled or quadrupled after running a jingle as a cheap, zip-code-targeted YouTube pre-roll ad, generating millions of ad impressions for a few hundred dollars total, since even people who skip the ad hear the jingle and phrase in the first few seconds.

Best For

Someone with (or willing to hire) music production skill, comfortable pitching and managing ongoing local business marketing relationships, and interested in a distinctive service that differentiates from typical digital marketing agencies.

The Truth

This is a genuinely novel, specific local marketing mechanic that doesn't overlap with typical "digital marketing agency" content — the combination of a memorable, ownable search-term jingle with ultra-cheap, geographically-targeted skippable pre-roll ads is a real, replicable insight, and the presenter has decades of documented client results behind it. That said, this requires either real music/production skill or the resources to hire it out reliably across many clients, which is a higher skill/coordination bar than most local marketing services on this list.
