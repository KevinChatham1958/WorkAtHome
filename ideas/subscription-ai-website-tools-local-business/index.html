What It Is

Building custom, AI-generated tools and dashboards into local business websites, such as ROI-tracking analytics, AI-powered booking systems, or before-and-after photo visualizers, sold as an ongoing monthly subscription with no contract, rather than a one-time website sale.

The Pitch

The presenter, a web agency owner, walks through several real client examples: a car-detailing site with a custom value-from-your-website dashboard calculating estimated revenue from bookings, a bridal shop with a custom booking and CRM system replacing a broken WordPress plugin, and a live, on-camera rebuild of a cookie business's homepage from a single set of Claude Code prompts, roughly 150 iterations, built around what would actually drive revenue for that specific business, not just visual design. Pricing is public and structured as a simple monthly fee, $250 to $450 depending on client, with no upfront cost and cancel-anytime terms.

Who It's Best For

Someone with real web design or AI-coding ability willing to build custom, business-specific tools per client rather than reusing one templated product, and comfortable with a no-contract model that trades short-term security for long-term retention.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific conversion and churn figures cited, over 90% conversion, under 2% monthly churn, and the claim of doubled revenue this year, are self-reported by the guest without account verification shown on screen. The demonstrated live build, from a URL and plain-language instructions to a finished custom homepage in one sitting, is shown directly and is a real, verifiable capability of the AI coding tool used.
