4 AI-Buildable Product Formats

Four AI-buildable digital product formats (action guide, prompt system, paid workshop, no-code app) plus a simple system for selling any of them

What It Is

Build one of four AI-assisted digital product formats, each matched to a specific customer and problem rather than made broad: (1) a short 10-20 page 'AI action guide' built section-by-section with Claude/ChatGPT using your own real knowledge, then designed in Canva; (2) an 'AI prompt and template system' bundling prompts/templates/checklists that solve one repeatable task for one type of customer; (3) a paid AI workshop that you can pre-sell before ever building it, letting real purchases validate the topic; (4) a no-code app built by describing the idea to Claude/ChatGPT to generate a detailed build prompt, then feeding that prompt to a no-code app builder, adding a paywall once it works. Sell any of these through a simple funnel: weekly YouTube videos about the same problem, a free related freebie, an email list, and a checkout page, pre-selling before fully building when possible.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the central lesson as: the specific product type matters far less than whether it solves a real, specific, painful problem for a specific customer, walking through the exact creation steps and honest pros/cons for each of the four formats.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to pick one narrow customer and problem rather than trying to build something broad, and comfortable using AI conversationally rather than expecting a single generic prompt to produce a sellable product.

The Truth (as we see it)

All four described build methods are real, current, technically accurate capabilities, and the pre-sell-before-building advice for the workshop format is sound, low-risk validation practice. No personal revenue figures or case studies are given; it's a structural breakdown that ends by directing viewers to the presenter's own separate free training.
