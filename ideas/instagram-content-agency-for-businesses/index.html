What It Is

You plan and produce Instagram content for businesses that already make money but have failed at social media, such as service firms or software startups. Claude handles research, copy, carousels and scripts; the client or their team still films. Businesses pay a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Dang says many companies, including funded software firms, want an Instagram presence and simply hire agencies to get one. He suggests finding businesses whose Instagram attempts clearly failed and offering help, and quotes $1,000 to $5,000 per month depending on whether editing is included. He notes he looked for such an agency himself before hiring in-house, and scores it 21 of 25 while rating competition higher than most of his ideas.

Best For

Suits people who understand Instagram formats and trends and can manage a few ongoing client relationships.

The Truth

Social media management is a well-established service with steady demand, but it is also one of the most crowded freelance markets. His own anecdote shows a common risk: clients often bring the work in-house once it matters. Results depend on the client actually filming, which AI cannot do for them. Fee ranges are his estimates. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
