Gourmet Popcorn Business

Gourmet popcorn production business built around bulk and event orders

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (basic commercial poppers around $50-100; small caramel kettle machines around $280-350; automated coating systems cost more)

What It Is

Producing and selling flavored gourmet popcorn in bags, built around landing bulk or event orders (branded bags for a company event) rather than waiting on individual retail customers.

The Pitch

Small premium popcorn bags commonly retail around $4-6; selling 100 bags at $5 is about $500 in gross sales before corn, oil, coating ingredients, bags, labels, event fees, labor, and stale inventory. The popping machine itself is cheap ($50-350) — coating, cooling, weighing, and sealing can actually be the slower bottleneck, and poor packaging ruins the product's crunch.

Best For

Someone who targets bulk/event buyers (a company ordering 200 branded bags) over individual retail sales, and who treats packaging and moisture control as seriously as the flavor itself.

The Truth

The popping equipment is one of the cheapest parts of this business — the real work and the real differentiation is in coating, packaging quality, and securing larger fixed-size orders rather than one-off bag sales.
