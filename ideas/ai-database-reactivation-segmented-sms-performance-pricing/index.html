AI-segmented Database Reactivation Campaign With a Performance-based Pricing Option

What It Is

Export a local business's list of past customers, unbooked quote requests, or old leads sitting unused in a booking system or inbox, have Claude segment that list by what each contact bought or asked about and how long ago, then write a short, specific win-back text sequence per segment (referencing the exact thing that contact previously booked or inquired about) sent out in small batches rather than all at once, so the business owner can review and adjust the message before it reaches everyone.

The Pitch

The sales math scales with list size and average ticket: a med spa with 400 past clients at a $200 average visit reactivating just 4% of them nets $3,200 from a list that was otherwise producing nothing, while a home services company with 5,000 old leads at a $3,000 average job reactivating 2% nets $300,000. Priced $2,000-8,000 depending on database size, or offered on a performance basis for newer operators: a smaller $3,000 upfront fee plus 5-10% of revenue the campaign generates, which is easy for owners to agree to since it's revenue they wouldn't otherwise have had. This is pitched as the highest-leverage of the five offers because a successfully reactivated database builds enough trust to upsell every other service on the list afterward.

Best For

Someone comfortable segmenting and messaging a business's existing customer list carefully (specificity and small batching matter for trust), and willing to offer performance-based pricing when starting out without a track record.

The Truth

The presenter is explicit that messaging must only go to people who gave the business explicit permission to contact them, with an unsubscribe option in every message — a real compliance requirement, not just a nice-to-have, since sending unsolicited bulk texts carries legal risk under anti-spam regulations. The cited revenue projections (4% and 2% reactivation rates) are the presenter's own illustrative assumptions, not documented results from a named client campaign.
