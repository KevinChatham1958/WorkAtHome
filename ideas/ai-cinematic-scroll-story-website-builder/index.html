Scroll-Story Websites for Brands

Building premium 'visual story' scroll-scrubbing websites for e-commerce brands using GPT-6 Astra and AI video generation

Digital & subscription products · Low (a ChatGPT Codex/Astra subscription, a Higgsfield account for AI video generation via Seedance 2.5)

Originally published Sep 7, 2026 — Bart Slodyczka

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Bart Slodyczka · Build a $10K Website With GPT Astra (No Code, Full Tutorial) ↗

What It Is

Use a custom multi-step prompt (built as an autonomous 'skill' for ChatGPT's Codex mode with GPT-6 Astra) to design and build a premium, cinematic e-commerce website: the AI interviews you about your brand, generates a scroll-triggered 'visual story' concept for your product, generates the actual video/image assets for that story via the Higgsfield MCP and Seedance 2.5, assembles it into a working website, then runs a second prompt to create a dedicated, separately-optimized mobile version of the same visual story.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this technique as capable of producing '$10K' quality e-commerce websites (demonstrating a smoothie brand, a luxury watch store, and a pizza brand, each with a distinct cinematic visual story) using free custom prompts he shares via GitHub, positioning cinematic scroll-based storytelling as the differentiator that makes a website feel premium.

Best For

Someone comfortable directing an AI through an iterative creative process who wants to offer premium web design as a service to e-commerce or DTC brands.

The Truth

AI-assisted website generation combined with AI video/image tools for scroll-based storytelling is a real, current capability, and the demonstrated results look polished. This video is a technical build tutorial, not a client-acquisition or pricing guide, so the '$10K' framing describes the perceived value of the output, not a demonstrated sale; turning this into an actual business requires separately figuring out how to find and sell to e-commerce clients.
