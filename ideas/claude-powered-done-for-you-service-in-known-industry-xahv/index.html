Claude-Powered Service for Your Industry

Claude-powered done-for-you service for an industry you already know (30-day launch plan)

AI & automation · Near-zero cost (Claude subscription and a laptop)

What It Is

You pick one industry you understand (examples: recruitment, accounting, estate agencies) and use Claude to research its most common time-wasting admin problems from reviews, Reddit and LinkedIn. You package a fix as an outcome-based service, such as cutting client onboarding from three hours to 20 minutes or four weeks of LinkedIn content done for you, and set up a simple profile, one-page explanation and booking link. You post content regularly and send 10-20 researched, personalised messages a day offering a quick win or short demo. Each client gets its own Claude project loaded with their details and tone, plus a growing library of templates for proposals, onboarding and reports, so one person can serve many clients.

The Pitch

The creator argues you do not need to build software to make money with AI; businesses pay to have one painful, repetitive job taken off their plate. He says Claude lets one person serve 20 or more clients instead of five, and that a first client in 30 days is realistic if you avoid hopping between tools and niches.

Best For

People with experience in a particular industry who are willing to do daily personalised outreach and post content.

The Truth

The plan is sensible but general: the creator gives no income figures, client examples or pricing. He promotes his own Titan Code OS system and invites viewers to book a call. Success depends heavily on outreach and on genuine industry knowledge, and he warns against handing your thinking over to AI. Expect the first month to produce conversations rather than income.
