What It Is

Document-heavy, recurring back-office services, bookkeeping, medical billing, or payroll, where AI now handles much of the document processing, letting one person serve more clients than before. No accounting license is required, just tool fluency and a chosen niche.

The Pitch

This work is recurring by nature, businesses need it every month, not once, and AI tools have lowered the manual workload significantly compared to a few years ago.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable learning bookkeeping or payroll software and who wants steady, recurring client relationships over flashier project work.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a solid, learnable option, though it's more operational than strategic compared to something like fractional executive work. It requires real comfort with financial recordkeeping, not just general business sense.
