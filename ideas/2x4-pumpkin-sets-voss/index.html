2x4 Pumpkin Sets

Sets of wooden pumpkins made from 2x4s, painted for fall porches

  • Things you make
  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Video published Aug 8, 2026 by WhosTheVoss

Startup cost: Low startup cost (box-store lumber and a few beginner power tools) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

WhosTheVoss · I made a set for my porch. Now everyone in town wants them! ↗

What It Is

You cut 2x4s into blocks, add dowel stems and paint them into small, medium and large pumpkins, then sell them as porch sets for autumn.

The Pitch

He says 12 sets cost $89.90 in materials, and at $72 a set that's $864. He also shows lower prices still leave good money, and says one session paid for the cheapest tools.

Who It’s Best For

Beginners who want a simple autumn product.

The Truth (as we see it)

Autumn décor sells in a narrow window, so plan stock for September and October. Sales mostly come through Facebook Marketplace, local pickup and Etsy, so you'll need to photograph, list and hand over orders. His profit maths leaves out your time, tool wear and fees unless stated. He gives his plans away free on his website.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
