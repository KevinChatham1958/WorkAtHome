Media-kit Sponsorships and Done-for-you Agency Services Layered on a Content Site

Local & professional services · Low incremental cost once a site or channel already has traffic — a monetization add-on rather than a standalone startup

Originally published Aug 24, 2026 — Adam Enfroy

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Adam Enfroy — https://youtu.be/nqSj6ewlRM0

What It Is

Once a content site or channel has real traffic or rankings, build a simple media kit and sell sponsorship inventory directly to brands (sponsored articles, ad reads, newsletter sends, or flat fees for guaranteed placement in a ranking article), and additionally pitch brands done-for-you services like running their PPC campaigns or content/SEO strategy for a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

The argument is to think of yourself as a media company rather than a small affiliate blog, opening a higher-margin revenue layer not tied to affiliate conversion rates or algorithm swings, since a brand already benefiting from your rankings is in a weak negotiating position.

Best For

Creators or bloggers who already have ranking content or an existing audience and are comfortable with direct B2B outreach, pitching, and negotiation.

The Truth

The underlying mechanic (brands paying flat fees or retainers for placement) is plausible and consistent with real media/affiliate negotiations, though the cited figures are unverified anecdotes. The pitch is somewhat oversold in its own framing — despite language suggesting this works 'even with no traffic,' the entire pitch to brands depends on already having an audience or ranking asset to sell against, making this a monetization upgrade for an existing site rather than an independent starting business.
