What It Is

Use a single all-in-one AI workspace to build every core piece of an affiliate marketing business via conversational or spoken prompts: an AI 'developer agent' codes a fully custom, mobile-responsive landing page with an email capture box from a plain-language description; the same workspace can research and suggest a profitable niche and affiliate product; build an interactive, web-app-style lead magnet (rather than a static PDF) with the affiliate link coded directly into its call-to-action button; generate a full email follow-up sequence and Pinterest pin graphics, all within the same connected project.

The Pitch

The presenter says she built this entire affiliate marketing business from scratch in under an hour using only conversational prompts, positioning the interactive lead magnet as a way to stand out since she hasn't seen other affiliate marketers using anything beyond standard PDF lead magnets, and provides a companion checklist with every exact prompt used.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable directing an AI tool conversationally to build web assets without needing to code, and willing to pick a real niche/product before starting.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI workspace tools that code full websites and lead magnets from plain-language prompts are a real, current capability, and the core affiliate-marketing structure described reflects standard, sound fundamentals. No revenue figures are given, this is a tool-and-workflow demonstration, and the video promotes her own referral link to the platform.
