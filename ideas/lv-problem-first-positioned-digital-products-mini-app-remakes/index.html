Remaking Best-Selling Digital Products

Problem-first digital products: remaking proven best-sellers with sharper positioning

Digital & subscription products · Near-zero cost (marketplace listing fees and existing software)

Luna Vega · I Made $1,000,000 Selling Digital Products - 6 Things You Are Missing! ↗

What It Is

You sell digital products (templates, spreadsheets, PDFs, mini web apps) on Etsy, Gumroad or your own page, but instead of inventing something new you find what already sells, read its reviews, and release an improved version aimed at a specific person and problem. The creator's examples include turning a crowded Google Sheets wedding planner into a phone-friendly web app, and making an app version of a best-selling PDF of texts to send after a breakup. The listing or landing page is written in a set order: the problem, what life looks like after, the product, then proof. With no reviews yet, you lead with your own story (for example, a budgeting tool by someone who paid off $47,000 of debt).

The Pitch

The creator says she has made over $1 million selling digital products over a decade and that whether a product sells depends on buyer psychology, not the product itself. She says her own 'too innovative' course flopped, and that buyers decide in seconds based on whether a product fixes their problem and was made for someone like them.

Best For

People who like writing and studying customers and want to sell low-cost digital products without inventing a brand-new idea.

The Truth

The advice (copy proven demand, lead with the outcome, use clear language) is standard and reasonable marketing guidance, and it applies to almost any digital product. The $1 million revenue claim is the creator's own and unaudited, and this video teaches positioning more than a step-by-step build. 'Improve what is already selling' needs care: copying designs or text too closely can break marketplace rules and copyright. The video closes by promoting the waitlist for the creator's paid AI Profit Lab membership.
