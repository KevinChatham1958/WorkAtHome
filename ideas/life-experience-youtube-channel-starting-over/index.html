Life Experience YouTube Channel

YouTube channel built on your career knowledge or life experience

Content & personal brand · Near-zero cost (webcam or phone)

Originally published Oct 21, 2026 — Shane Hummus

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Shane Hummus · 5 Jobs BEGGING For People Who Are Starting Over ↗

What It Is

You start a YouTube channel teaching or sharing what you already know: your former career, a skill, or what you are living through, like a layoff. Examples given include a laid-off tech worker filming his story, a nurse repeating advice she gives patients, an IT professional helping people get into cybersecurity, and a tradesman explaining trades careers. Income comes from AdSense plus coaching, newsletters, merch, tips and affiliate links.

The Pitch

The creator calls this the "ceilingless career." He says the laid-off tech worker's first video got 1.3 million views and earned over $6,500, and that the channel now makes about $5,400 a month in AdSense according to third-party estimates. He argues that what's common sense to you is valuable to others, and that you can work in 15 to 30 minutes a day.

Who It's Best For

People with a career, skill or life experience others want to learn from, who are comfortable being on camera and willing to post consistently.

The Truth (as we see it)

Building a channel around real expertise or a relatable story is a legitimate path, and the examples are real channels. The earnings shown are third-party estimates or student claims, and viral first videos are rare; most channels take many months to reach monetization. The creator is using this to promote free live trainings that lead into his paid YouTube coaching program, and several of the success stories are his own paying students.
