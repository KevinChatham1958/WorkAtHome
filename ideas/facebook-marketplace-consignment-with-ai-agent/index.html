Marketplace Flipping With an AI Agent

Facebook Marketplace flipping and consignment selling with an AI agent handling listings and messages

E-commerce · Low startup cost (money to buy items if flipping; none if selling for others)

Originally published Oct 7, 2026 — Christian Peverelli

Share on Facebook Link copied

Christian Peverelli · 5 Proven Ways to Make Money with Meta Muse ↗

One of 5 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You either buy underpriced items you understand on Facebook Marketplace and resell them at a fair price, or sell other people's unwanted goods for a cut (he suggests about 20%). An AI agent (Meta's Muse, in the video) searches for underpriced items in your categories, drafts listings and handles buyer messages, while you set the prices and approve key decisions. He suggests starting with $100-200 items and moving to higher-priced ones.

The Pitch

The creator notes Marketplace has about a billion users and millions of new listings a day, many priced by people who just want the item gone, and says consignment is a proven model that the agent makes nearly hands-off. He likes that the platform and the agent are owned by the same company.

Who It's Best For

Someone who knows the value of a product category (electronics, furniture, tools) and can store and hand over items from home.

The Truth (as we see it)

Marketplace flipping is well established, but margins depend on your product knowledge, and buying to flip ties up cash. The creator himself recounts a user whose agent accepted a lowball offer and gave out his home address, so keep the agent on a short leash and set prices yourself. Selling for neighbors involves collecting and storing their items. The creator offers free prompts through his community.
