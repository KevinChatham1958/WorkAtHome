End-to-end Faceless YouTube Production Pipeline Using ChatGPT's Browsing Model, Solo AI, and VidRush

Content & personal brand · Low startup cost (ChatGPT subscription with the newer model, Solo AI subscription, and VidRush at roughly $3/minute of finished video, i.e. about $27 for a 10-minute video, plus optional Fiverr video editing at $25-50/video)

One Person Business · How I Built a $32K/Month Faceless Channel With ChatGPT Astra ↗

What It Is

Use a newer ChatGPT model with live YouTube-browsing ability to research proven faceless-channel niches and title/thumbnail formats for an older, higher-RPM audience (Western viewers 40+), have it analyze specific competitor channels to extract title and content frameworks and surface lesser-known facts on the chosen topic, then run that research through Solo AI (an all-in-one toolset) to generate a script in a chosen channel's style, an AI voiceover, and thumbnail text/image, and use VidRush to auto-edit the script or voiceover into a full video by referencing another channel's editing style — optionally adding a Solo-built digital product (PDF guide) as a second revenue stream.

The Pitch

The presenter shows his own faceless channel (about $32,000/month in ad revenue after roughly 70 days) and walks through the full pipeline live: prompting the browsing-capable ChatGPT model to identify a niche and proven title formats, feeding it competitor channel links to adapt those formats, researching obscure facts on the topic so content doesn't read as generic, generating a script and voiceover through Solo AI, producing the video through VidRush by referencing an existing channel's editing style, and building thumbnail text and a companion digital product through the same toolset.

Best For

Someone comfortable stacking and paying for several AI subscription tools in sequence, willing to treat channel-building as a numbers game with real failure risk, and targeting a niche where longer-form, screen-narrated content (not on-camera presence) fits the format.

The Truth

The presenter is unusually candid that results vary enormously — he explicitly says he has abandoned channels that flopped and had a million-subscriber channel deleted by YouTube — and notes his own current channel's RPM ($4/1,000 views) is far below the outlier $31/1,000 figure some channels see, directly pushing back on hype from other faceless-YouTube content. VidRush is described as genuinely expensive (from $99/month, about $3/minute of output), and revenue figures for other channels come from third-party estimator tools, not verified statements, so all dollar figures here should be read as rough and highly variable rather than typical.
