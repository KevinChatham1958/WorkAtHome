5 Trending Etsy Niches

Five specific, currently-trending Etsy niches identified by studying young, fast-growing listings directly on Etsy itself, most fulfilled via print-on-demand

What It Is

Research trending Etsy niches by searching directly on Etsy itself rather than pulling trend signals from other platforms, since Etsy's buyer pool and taste are genuinely different; look for listings that are young (weeks to months old) but already showing real demand indicators (a 'sold X times in the last 24 hours' flame icon, items in many carts). Five specific trending niches demonstrated live: (1) 'old money' aesthetic products fulfilled via print-on-demand at roughly 20-30% margins; (2) whimsical/vintage-style products and specific hobby kits — hyper-targeted single-product listings with low competition, one example cited earning roughly $61,000-100,000 within a year; (3) wedding 'micro-niches' — pet-inclusive decor, destination-wedding items, passport-style invitations, one example cited at roughly $50,000 revenue in 7 months; (4) retirement-themed gifts, suited to a fully-niched shop identity. For each niche, cross-check whether the trending product already exists in a print-on-demand catalog to fulfill without holding inventory, and look for newly-added product options specifically, since being among the first to use a brand-new variation is a genuine open-market opportunity.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates the entire research process live on Etsy and Printify simultaneously, showing real listings with current sales-velocity indicators and specific dollar figures (a wedding pet-napkin listing selling 60 units a day), calculating real Printify production costs against observed retail prices.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable using a print-on-demand fulfillment model and willing to do live, current research on Etsy itself rather than assuming a trend from another platform will transfer.

The Truth (as we see it)

The core research methodology is sound, practical e-commerce logic, and the specific listings/sales indicators shown are real and live at time of filming, though trend-driven niches can fade and weren't independently reverified here. Revenue figures for other sellers are estimates based on visible demand indicators, not confirmed sales data, and the video includes a paid sponsorship segment for a specific Etsy research/AI tool.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
