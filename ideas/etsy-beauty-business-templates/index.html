What It Is

Creating business-facing digital templates, such as scheduling flyers, Instagram story templates, and training guides, aimed at nail techs, lash techs, estheticians, and similar beauty-service providers, sold on Etsy.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as a business-to-business niche within Etsy that's underused since most sellers default to consumer-facing decorative products. Direct experience in a beauty-service field, or just willingness to research it, is presented as a real edge, since a well-curated shop of 40 to 160 related templates can outperform much larger general shops.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with beauty-industry experience or willingness to do targeted niche research, comfortable designing repeatable templates that can be resold in color and style variations.

The Truth (as we see it)

The standout example, a lash-training guide reportedly earning close to $1,000 a month on its own inside a shop doing about $4,000 a month, comes from the same third-party Etsy research tool, not confirmed sales data. The presenter is candid that she doesn't personally sell in this niche and is evaluating it live in the video rather than reporting from firsthand selling experience.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
