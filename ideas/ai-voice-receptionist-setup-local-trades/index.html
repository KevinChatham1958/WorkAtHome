AI Phone Agent Setup Service

Setting up AI phone receptionists for local service businesses

What It Is

You build AI voice agents that answer a business's phone like a receptionist for plumbers, electricians, real estate agents, lawyers and similar businesses. Using a tool like ElevenLabs' agent builder, you pick a voice, paste in the business's hours, prices, service area, booking steps and how to respond, then link the agent to the business's phone number so it can take calls and collect booking details. You get paid as a freelancer to set these up, finding clients through Fiverr gigs, cold calls or emails with a demo agent, or social posts showing the agent working.

The Pitch

The creator says local businesses lose customers when they can't answer the phone, and an AI agent solves that around the clock. She points to Fiverr freelancers with many reviews on this exact service as proof of demand, and says no coding is needed.

Who It's Best For

Tech-curious people comfortable talking with small-business owners, and willing to test and fine-tune agents until they handle real calls reliably.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI voice receptionists are a real and growing service, and Fiverr demand can be checked directly. The video shows only the basics; real deployments involve phone-number routing, calendar or booking integrations, edge cases, and ongoing per-minute platform costs that someone has to pay. A badly configured agent that gives wrong prices or misses emergencies can cost a client business, so expect testing and support work after setup. The creator offers a free checklist of prompts and promotes her paid community, and the video is sponsored by an AI video-creation tool.
