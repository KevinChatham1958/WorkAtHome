AI-Agent-Run Affiliate Site

Affiliate site run on AI agent workflows for research, briefs, product tables and content audits

What It Is

An affiliate content site where repeatable jobs are handed to AI 'agents' (custom GPTs or Make.com, Zapier or n8n workflows). These jobs include filtering keywords, analyzing the top 10 competing pages, writing content briefs, pulling product specs and prices into HTML comparison tables, suggesting internal links, checking on-page SEO, and flagging old posts to update. You review the output, add your own product opinions and make the decisions.

The Pitch

Enfroy estimates competitor analysis drops from two hours to 10 minutes, table formatting from 45 minutes to two, and updating 50 articles from 10 hours to one. He shares prompt wording, such as filtering for keywords with over 1,000 monthly searches, difficulty under 30 and clear buying intent. Another prompt flags posts whose traffic fell more than 20% in 90 days. He says this lets one person publish five to seven quality posts a week, about 240 a year instead of 75. His roadmap: 10 to 12 posts in month one, 15 to 20 in month two, 35 to 50 live by month three, and 75 to 100 posts with perhaps 20,000 to 50,000 monthly sessions by month six. He suggests TranslatePress to rank in other languages and Google Trends for rising product keywords.

Who It’s Best For

An organized, systems-minded person who likes building repeatable processes and will still fact-check prices and add genuine first-hand opinions to every post.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a recent (2026) video, and the time savings and traffic numbers are projections ('could'), not reported results. Enfroy warns that AI can pull outdated prices and that unedited AI content will not build trust. The tools list (Claude, Make.com, Thirsty Affiliates, Rank Math, TablePress, VidIQ) is practical. The video promotes Hostinger's website builder with his discount link (85% off, $2.99 a month) and funnels viewers to his free AI masterclass and paid community, where he offers ready-made Make.com automations.
