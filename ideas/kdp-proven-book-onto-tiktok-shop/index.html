Proven KDP Book on TikTok Shop

Moving an already-selling KDP book onto TikTok Shop to lift its Amazon ranking

  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Startup cost: Low startup cost (an initial print run through a print-and-fulfillment partner; ads optional once videos convert)

Source video

Jonathan Nilsen · How I Sold $350K in Books on TikTok Shop and 10x'd My KDP ↗

What It Is

Take a self-published book that already sells on Amazon, in a topic with proven demand (his rule of thumb: the topic's leading books rank better than roughly 50,000 in Amazon's Best Sellers Rank), and list it on TikTok Shop with a print-and-fulfillment partner such as Acutrack printing, storing and shipping it. Make short videos that sell the idea before the book: open with a curiosity hook about the problem, build on it, then reveal the book as the answer. Copy one video structure already working in your niche and repeat it 10-20 times, using AI to write variations from your manuscript. Scale later with TikTok's automated ads (GMV Max) and affiliate creators once the book has about 100 sales.

The Pitch

He says his first book went from about $1,500 a month in KDP royalties to $9,000-$13,000 a month after he put it on TikTok Shop in February 2025, with its Amazon rank improving from about 27,000 to 2,000-3,000, and shows roughly $347,000 in 2025 TikTok Shop sales plus $113,000 in KDP royalties and $25,000 from Audible for the same book. His explanation: TikTok creates demand, some viewers then buy on Amazon instead, and that outside traffic lifts the book's Amazon ranking, so a handful of books can earn what would otherwise take dozens.

Who It’s Best For

A self-publisher who already has a book selling steadily on Amazon and will post short videos consistently while tracking costs closely.

The Truth (as we see it)

The spillover he describes (outside traffic lifting Amazon rank) is a real, widely reported effect, and his own warning is the most useful part: TikTok Shop is a full e-commerce operation with inventory, fees, promotions and affiliate commissions, and he says he has seen publishers do $40,000 in a month and keep $400. The sales figures are his own dashboards, the 'proven book first' rule means this isn't a starting point for someone without a selling title, and the video promotes his paid TikTok Shop coaching program.
