Logo-Printed Paper Bags

Small-batch logo printing on paper bags for local businesses

Physical & print products · Moderate startup cost (a compact automatic bag printer runs a few thousand dollars, plus blank bags)

Originally published Oct 7, 2026 — Business Dash

Share on Facebook Link copied

One of 3 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You buy a compact machine that prints a logo directly onto plain kraft paper bags and take short-run orders from bakeries, coffee shops and other small businesses that want a few hundred branded bags. One version is contract printing, where the customer supplies artwork and you charge per print; the other is a full package where you also source the blank bags and build margin into both bag and print. The creator's example is a 500-bag job at 40 cents per print, a $200 invoice before costs. You then swap the artwork and run the next customer's design.

The Pitch

The creator says this fills a gap between home-office printing and large packaging factories, which often do not want orders of only a few hundred bags. Branding turns plain packaging into part of a business's identity, which gives local shops a reason to pay.

Best For

Someone with a garage or spare-room workspace who likes hands-on production and is comfortable approaching local businesses for repeat packaging orders.

The Truth

The small-order gap is a real niche, but the example figures are revenue, not profit, as the creator himself stresses. He also warns that the very low Chinese wholesale price he found appears to be a deposit and that comparable machines cost a few thousand dollars, so get a written full quote before buying. Winning customers means approaching local businesses directly, and you will store blank bags and deliver finished orders. No income results are claimed and the creator sells nothing in the video.
