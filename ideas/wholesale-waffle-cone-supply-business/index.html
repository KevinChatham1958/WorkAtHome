Wholesale Waffle Cone Supply Business for Ice Cream Shops

Physical & print products · Very low startup cost (simple commercial waffle cone makers around $70-75; double-head units around $70-130; automatic production machines cost much more)

What It Is

Supplying fresh waffle cones to local ice cream shops so they don't have to make their own, testing delivery durability before pitching accounts.

The Pitch

An independent shop selling 150 cones on a busy day across six busy days works out to 900 cones disappearing in a week. The equipment itself is almost irrelevant at this scale ($70-130) — the presenter stresses testing the actual delivery route (making and boxing 100 cones, driving them across town, and counting how many survive intact) before approaching shops about replacing an employee's cone-making time.

Best For

Someone willing to solve the packaging and delivery-breakage problem first, since a cracked cone in the box is waste, not inventory.

The Truth

The machine's low price is misleading — breakage during delivery, not equipment cost, is the real variable that determines whether this works as a supply business.
