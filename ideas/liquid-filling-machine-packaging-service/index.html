Product Filling Service

Offering liquid and paste product filling as a service to small producers using a manual filling machine

What It Is

Use a manual liquid/paste filling machine to fill bottles or containers cleanly and consistently for products like honey, sauce, shampoo, lotion, or cosmetics, either packaging your own product line or offering filling-as-a-service to small home producers who currently fill bottles by hand; keep separate equipment/cleaning protocols for different product categories to respect hygiene rules.

The Pitch

Presented as an unglamorous but practical machine, with the underrated business model being the B2B filling-as-a-service option for small producers who already make a good product but have messy, inconsistent packaging.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable following hygiene/food-safety protocols and building relationships with small home-based producers who need packaging help.

The Truth (as we see it)

Contract filling services for small producers are a real, established B2B niche, and the hygiene/cross-contamination warning reflects genuine regulatory and safety concerns. No specific revenue example is given; success depends on finding enough small producers locally who need this specific service.
