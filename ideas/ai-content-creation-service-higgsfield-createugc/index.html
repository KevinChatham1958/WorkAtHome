What It Is

Help e-commerce brands and local businesses create short-form product videos, ad variations, and UGC-style content without them ever hiring a creator, using Claude to plan the strategy and script (angles, scenes, voice-over) and an AI video tool, either Higgsfield connected to Claude for more custom control, or Create UGC for a faster, more streamlined workflow, to generate the actual footage.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the sales pitch specifically: don't say "I make AI videos," instead reach out with something like "I saw your product and had a few quick video ideas that could work well for your TikTok or ads, I can even make you a sample," which performs much better than a generic pitch. The offer is packaged into simple tiers, a basic package of one video, a standard package of three, and a premium package of five with captions and basic editing, sellable on Fiverr or through direct outreach to e-commerce brands and local businesses.

Best For

Someone comfortable learning a multi-step AI video pipeline (connecting tools, writing prompts, approving and regenerating scenes) who's willing to do a bit of manual editing to combine AI-generated clips into a polished final video.

The Truth

The presenter discloses this workflow depends on paid tools (Higgsfield and Create UGC both cost money per generation, and regenerating a scene you don't like costs double), and promotes a separate video and free cheat sheet for the full walkthrough. The core sales positioning advice, specific and personalized pitches over generic ones, is sound and doesn't depend on any particular tool.
