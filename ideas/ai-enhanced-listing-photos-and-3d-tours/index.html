AI Listing Photos and Tours

AI-enhanced listing photos and 3D tours for small hotels, hosts and realtors

What It Is

You take the phone photos that small hotels, hostels, short-term rental hosts and real estate agents already have, and use AI to regenerate them so they look professionally lit and shot, then add an AI-generated 3D walkthrough as a bonus. You find clients on map searches and listing sites and email them samples.

The Pitch

The hosts say independent owners can't afford professional shoots, so bad photos keep their calendars empty. They suggest charging $150-300 per package, so ten clients a month brings $1,500-3,000, and say referrals between agents follow once you have a portfolio.

Who It's Best For

Someone with an eye for interiors and photo editing who is comfortable contacting local businesses.

The Truth (as we see it)

Making photos look better is valuable, but AI regeneration can subtly change rooms, furniture or views, and misleading property images can breach listing-site rules and, in real estate, advertising laws. Any edits must stay faithful to the real space. The income figures are the hosts' estimates.
