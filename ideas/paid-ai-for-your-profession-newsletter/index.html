What It Is

You pick the profession you already work in, such as law, and write a paid newsletter about using AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude for that specific job. Each issue covers one small, practical tidbit, for example how to use AI for one particular legal task, drawn from what you are learning yourself. She suggests one to three issues a week on Substack or Beehiiv. Readers pay a monthly subscription; her example is 200 subscribers at $50 a month for about $10,000 a month, with typical paid newsletters priced from about $20 to a few hundred dollars a month.

The Pitch

The creator says working in the profession gives you far more credibility than an outsider writing about it, and that saving readers 10 to 20 hours a month makes $50 a month an easy sell. She likes it because the income repeats every month as long as you keep delivering value.

Who It’s Best For

Working professionals who are already learning AI in their field and can write short, practical tips week after week.

The Truth (as we see it)

Writing for your own profession is a sensible niche, and the platforms really are free to start. The 200-subscriber, $50-a-month figure is a hypothetical, not a reported result; getting 200 people to pay is the hard part, and most paid newsletters need a free audience first. Advice on using AI in regulated fields such as law needs care around client confidentiality and accuracy. The creator runs her own AI software company and promotes her other videos, though no paid product is pitched for this idea.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
