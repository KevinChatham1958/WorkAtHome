AI-drafted Google Review Reply Subscription Service, Planned End-to-end With Claude Code

Local & professional services · Free to start (free Gmail, free Netlify hosting, free Stripe account)

What It Is

Offer local businesses a $199/month subscription service where AI drafts replies to their existing Google reviews (especially ones the business has ignored), with the owner approving each reply by email or WhatsApp before it's posted; the presenter uses Claude Code itself to generate the entire business plan, brand identity, and step-by-step setup instructions, then Stripe to handle subscription billing automatically.

The Pitch

The plan calls for finding 50 local businesses on Google Maps with visibly ignored reviews, messaging 10 per day with two sample replies already written using their real reviews, offering a free first week before converting to the $199/month plan — daily maintenance work is estimated at 15 minutes per client, with a projected path to $10,000/month (51 clients) around month six at a pace of 8-10 new clients per month.

Best For

Someone comfortable with cold outreach to local businesses and willing to use AI tools (Claude Code, Stripe, Netlify) to assemble the entire operation for close to zero upfront cost.

The Truth

The specific step-by-step setup (free tools throughout, a scripted cold-outreach message using the business's own real reviews as proof, a free-trial-to-paid-subscription conversion) is more concretely actionable than most similar pitches. The month-six, $10,000 projection assumes a steady 8-10 new clients per month with no churn, which is an optimistic scenario rather than a guaranteed outcome, and the video is sponsored by one of the AI tools demonstrated (Higgsfield) for the marketing/ad-creation portion.
