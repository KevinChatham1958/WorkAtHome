Online Storefront for a Farm

Running the online store and marketing for a farm that packs and ships the product

E-commerce · Low startup cost (domain, WordPress and Shopify, shipping software)

What It Is

Buy a domain naming a well-known regional product (the guest bought a Vidalia onion domain at auction for about $2,100), then approach growers who don't want to deal with direct-to-consumer sales and offer to build and run the online store for free. The partner farm handles boxes, climate-controlled storage and packing; you handle the website, marketing, social media, sales and customer support. The guest has run this on a handshake deal for 10 years using WordPress, Shopify and ShipStation.

The Pitch

The guest says many farmers find shipping to consumers "the biggest thorn in their side," so a partner who takes the online side off their hands is welcome, and the farm's existing packing and storage mean you never touch inventory.

Who It's Best For

Marketers who can build trust with a producer and are prepared to handle customer service during the shipping season.

The Truth (as we see it)

Partnering with a producer who fulfills is a smart, low-inventory way into food e-commerce, but it depends heavily on one partner and on a perishable, seasonal product, and customer support was heavy enough that the guest says it nearly broke him. The video gives no revenue figures for this project. The interview is with a guest on the host's highlights channel; figures are the guest's own and unaudited, and the projects took years to build.
