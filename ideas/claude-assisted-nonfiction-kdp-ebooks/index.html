Claude-assisted Niche Nonfiction Books Published on Amazon KDP

What It Is

You research evergreen nonfiction niches people search for on Amazon, such as specific cookbooks, hobbies and how-to topics, then use Claude to help outline and draft a book, which you edit and humanize. You publish it free through Amazon Kindle Direct Publishing as an ebook, paperback (printed on demand) or audiobook, and Amazon pays you royalties on each sale.

The Pitch

The presenter says Amazon brings buyers who are already searching with a card on file, so you need no website, traffic or client hunting, and there is no inventory or upfront cost. He says he has made millions selling books this way and sold his first catalog through Empire Flippers for $820,000. Claude makes the writing, once the hardest part, much faster than hiring a ghostwriter. He says individual books may earn tens to hundreds of dollars a month, or thousands if done well, and that a growing portfolio compounds as an asset that keeps paying long after it is written.

Best For

Patient people who are willing to research niches, edit carefully and build a catalog of books over months rather than expecting quick income.

The Truth

KDP is a real, free platform and low-content and nonfiction publishing does produce royalty income for some people, but the presenter's millions and catalog sale are his own unaudited claims. He is candid that one book rarely makes meaningful money and that poorly chosen niches may never sell. The market is now crowded with AI-generated books, Amazon has added disclosure rules and publishing limits for AI content, and success depends heavily on niche research, quality, covers and reviews. The presenter heavily promotes his paid mentorship program, Road to Hero, with discovery calls and a free niche cheat sheet, and this idea is clearly the one the video is steering viewers toward.
