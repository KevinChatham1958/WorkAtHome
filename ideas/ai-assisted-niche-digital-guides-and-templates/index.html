What It Is

You package information that solves one specific group's problem into a guide, template, checklist, short program or toolkit, using AI to research and structure the content and to write the store page, pitch and FAQs. You build it once and sell it repeatedly on digital marketplaces.

The Pitch

Iman Gadzhi calls digital products the highest-leverage of the five ideas because they scale infinitely, and says AI removes the years of expertise and team that creating them used to require. Marketplaces already have buyers searching.

Who It's Best For

Someone who can identify a specific audience's problem and is willing to market the product.

The Truth (as we see it)

Digital products can earn well, but this segment is brief and gives no numbers or sales method. Products assembled from what's 'already been written' by AI tend to be generic and hard to sell in crowded marketplaces; your own experience is what makes them valuable.
