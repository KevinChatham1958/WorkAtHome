Small Engine Repair

What It Is

Repair small engines, lawnmowers, generators, chainsaws, pressure washers, and line striping machines, for local landscaping crews, contractors, and homeowners, positioning yourself as the reliable shop with fast turnaround rather than a hobbyist who fixes engines occasionally.

The Pitch

The presenters argue this is a skilled trade that almost nobody under 50 is actively pursuing, even though every contractor with small engine equipment eventually needs it fixed and there's no national chain to call. The real opportunity, they say, isn't that nobody fixes engines, it's that almost nobody does it as a real, reliable business with an actual shop and same-day callbacks.

Best For

Someone younger who's willing to learn a genuine skilled trade and is comfortable with a business that gets noticeably slower in winter, since demand is seasonal for outdoor equipment.

The Truth

The presenters are upfront that this is one of the weaker "build a real business" plays on their list precisely because the labor takes real time to learn and the seasonality is a real constraint. It's framed as a good cash-flow trade, not an easy path to a scalable company.
