At-home Wash-and-fold Laundry Service Taken Through Laundry Apps

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (your own washer, dryer, detergent and utilities; gas for pickups)

What It Is

You sign up with a laundry-service app that sends you customers' orders, pick up their laundry, wash, dry and fold it in your own home, and return it, usually within 24 hours. The app supplies the customers and payment, so you don't have to find clients yourself; some people also build a few private regular clients. The video's example earns about $500 a month from two regular clients, with single orders around $90.

The Pitch

The creator ranks it as one of the more flexible options for stay-at-home parents because the work happens at home alongside chores you already do, can be done with kids around, and pays quickly once an order is complete.

Best For

A stay-at-home parent or retiree with a reliable washer and dryer who wants modest, steady income without finding customers.

The Truth

The creator flags the real costs herself: you pay for water, electricity, detergent and gas, so the net is lower than the order price, and you need to track those costs. It involves short local pickups and drop-offs, availability depends on the app operating in your area, and earnings are modest rather than a full income. The other ideas in this video were on-site shift and delivery apps, not home-based work. The creator also sells a field-inspection training program.
