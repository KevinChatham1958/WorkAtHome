What It Is

Use a free AI store-builder to instantly launch a Shopify store, source products via AutoDS (product research, competitor data, automated fulfillment), then connect Claude to AutoDS so it can research products, compare opportunities, and help run day-to-day store decisions conversationally.

The Pitch

Presented as one of three Claude-powered side hustles capable of reaching $500/day, emphasizing zero inventory or shipping since AutoDS's suppliers fulfill orders directly and Claude acts as a research/decision-support 'employee' inside the business.

Best For

Someone comfortable connecting multiple SaaS tools and managing an online store, willing to learn cold-start dropshipping basics.

The Truth

The described mechanics (AI store builder plus AutoDS drop-shipping fulfillment plus Claude as a research layer) are real and match a pattern seen from other producers around the same time, so treat it as a variant of an increasingly common AI-dropshipping approach rather than a novel method. No independent revenue example is given for this producer's own results, and the video funnels to the presenter's own free lead-magnet course.
