Building Niche-specific Affiliate Websites That Answer Search Questions and Link to Amazon Products Via Amazon Associates

Marketing & content · Very low (a website/hosting cost; free to join Amazon Associates)

Originally published Sep 6, 2026 — Greg Gottfried

Share on Facebook Link copied

Greg Gottfried · How I Built 10 Streams Of Income In My 20's ↗

One of 9 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Build a website of articles answering specific search questions within one niche (e.g. how to clean a specific car's seats), embedding Amazon Associates affiliate links to relevant products within helpful, genuinely useful content; when a reader clicks through and buys anything on Amazon within 24 hours, the site owner earns a commission, with search engines (not paid ads) driving the traffic.

The Pitch

The presenter shows one of his own sites with over a hundred pages of articles, and says he's built several similar niche sites, but is candid that this stream has become notably less effective in recent years as AI-generated content has flooded the same search results, making him uncertain whether handwritten content will eventually outcompete AI content again or not.

Best For

Someone willing to write (or have written) genuinely helpful, specific content and comfortable with an SEO-dependent business model that's grown more competitive.

The Truth

Amazon Associates and the affiliate-content-website model are real and accurately described, and the presenter's own honest caution about AI content flooding this space is a credible, current concern rather than an oversold pitch; this kind of transparency about a declining tactic adds to rather than undercuts the entry's trustworthiness.
