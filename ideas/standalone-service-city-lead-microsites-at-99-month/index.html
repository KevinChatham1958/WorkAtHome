Lead Microsites Rented to Contractors

Standalone service-and-city lead microsites rented to contractors at about $99 a month each

What It Is

You build many small, separate websites, each targeting one narrow local service in one city (for example sewer line replacement in Dallas), get them ranking on Google, and route the phone calls they generate to a local contractor. The contractor pays you a monthly fee for the leads, or you sell the leads per call. Calls are answered and qualified by an AI phone agent around the clock before being passed on.

The Pitch

Cunningham argues these sites are not the things Google penalizes: unlike doorway pages they are the actual destination where the visitor gets an answer and calls, unlike private blog networks they never link to each other, and unlike scaled content abuse each one is focused on a single service and location. He says each site should be built around one sub-niche service with four to six related services plus many neighborhood and city service-area pages, which he calls force-feeding Google a service and a place. His stated goal is $99 per month per site across 1,000 sites, and he points to community members claiming 300-plus leads over a long weekend and six sites ranking on page one for one search. He urges building quickly now because AI token costs are, in his view, subsidized and could rise roughly tenfold.

Who It’s Best For

Suits someone comfortable with local SEO and outreach to tradespeople who can manage many small sites and the contractor relationships behind them.

The Truth (as we see it)

The rank-and-rent model is real and long-established, and his compliance guidelines (unique standalone sites, no interlinking, a real business answering the phone) are reasonable risk reduction, but they do not guarantee Google will keep ranking hundreds of near-identical-format local sites; ranking, and therefore income, can disappear with an algorithm update. The $99-per-site target and the community testimonials are his own and unaudited, and the video shows no actual revenue from rented sites. Making money requires finding and keeping contractors willing to pay, which is sales work the video barely covers, and the AI phone agent adds an ongoing cost. The video is largely a promotion for his paid SEO academy (7-day free trial), whose site-structure template file is offered only to members.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
