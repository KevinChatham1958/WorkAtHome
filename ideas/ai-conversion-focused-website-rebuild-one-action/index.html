Conversion-Focused Website Rebuilds

Conversion-focused website rebuild built around one specific action, not a template

AI & automation · Low startup cost (Claude access to generate the site)

What It Is

Rebuild an outdated local-business website (the common pattern: an about page, services page, photo gallery from years ago, a phone number buried in the footer, zero bookings ever attributed to the site) by first defining the single action the page needs to produce for that specific business type, prompting Claude as a conversion-focused web designer with that goal and business type so it lays out section order around the goal before any design happens, then populating the copy from the business's real customer reviews rather than generic marketing language, with the booking link placed prominently.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the common failure mode as starting from a template, which means starting from someone else's goal rather than this business's one needed action — the fix is goal-first design, not prettier design. Priced $1,500-3,000, and pitched as a strong first-sale offer specifically because it doesn't require having prior client work to show (unlike offers that need an existing portfolio).

Best For

Someone new to selling AI-built services who needs a first client and doesn't yet have a portfolio, since this offer can be demonstrated with just the target business's own existing reviews and goal.

The Truth

The goal-first (not template-first) design principle is sound web-design practice independent of which AI tool builds the site, and using a business's real reviews as ad copy is a legitimate way to make a rebuild feel authentic rather than generic. This is a distinct market segment problem (an existing but non-converting website) from most other AI-website entries in this database, which more often target businesses with no website at all.
