Faceless Pinterest Account

Running a faceless Pinterest account to drive affiliate commissions or digital product sales

E-commerce · Free to start (just a Pinterest account and access to a brand's affiliate program)

Originally published Jul 18, 2026 — Diamond Chanel

Share on Facebook Link copied

Diamond Chanel · I Found 7 Boring Side Hustles You Can Run From Your Phone, 24/7 (NOT DoorDash or Uber) ↗

One of 6 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Pick a product category you already use and understand (skincare, coffee, shoes), find the brand's affiliate program through their website, get your unique tracking link, and post simple product-focused pins on Pinterest with no face or voice required, earning commission when pin viewers click through and buy; the same setup works to sell your own digital products in place of an affiliate link.

The Pitch

The presenter reports making over $2,000 using this exact faceless Pinterest setup, framing Pinterest's search-and-shop nature (rather than personality-driven content) as what makes a faceless approach genuinely effective there, rating it 4/5 for ease (AI can speed up pin creation) but only 2/5 for speed to first dollar since it takes time to build traction.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with basic visual content creation and willing to be patient, since Pinterest traffic and affiliate income typically build gradually rather than instantly.

The Truth (as we see it)

Faceless Pinterest affiliate marketing is a real, established content-to-commerce strategy, and Pinterest's product-search-driven user behavior genuinely suits affiliate and digital-product links better than most social platforms. The presenter's own $2,000 figure has no stated timeframe attached, making it hard to judge as a rate of income, and her own admission that it's slow to earn a first dollar is a useful, honest caveat.
