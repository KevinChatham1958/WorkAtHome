What It Is

You connect a business's tools and automate manual processes with platforms like n8n, Make or Zapier plus AI steps, then maintain them. To find early clients, the creator suggests applying to brand-new Upwork automation jobs within the first couple of hours with short, human, on-point replies, and DMing people who comment on automation videos.

The Pitch

The creator says the bar on Upwork is low: when she posted automation jobs, about 30% of applications were obviously AI-generated, so a quick, genuine response stands out. The core pitch is always saving time or making money, not the tools.

Who It’s Best For

Someone technical or detail-minded who enjoys troubleshooting and can handle messy client systems.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator is unusually frank that this is one of the hardest paths: client data and systems are messy, automations break often, and many creators who promote agencies earn more from selling courses than from the agency. Expect consulting-style discovery work and ongoing maintenance. The creator sells her own AI app.
