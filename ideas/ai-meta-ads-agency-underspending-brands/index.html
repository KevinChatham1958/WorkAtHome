AI Ad Creative for Brands

Selling AI-generated Meta ad creative to brands that are running too few ads compared to their competitors

Marketing & content · Low (Claude subscription, an AI image/video tool like Higgsfield)

Originally published Jul 31, 2026 — Patrick Dang

Share on Facebook Link copied

What It Is

Use the free Facebook Ad Library to find brands running noticeably fewer ads than their direct competitors (a signal they can't produce creative at scale); use Claude for ad copy, hooks, and angles, and an AI image/video tool (Higgsfield) for the visuals, feeding Claude a competitor's proven, long-running ad as creative inspiration to build sample ads for the underspending brand before ever contacting them; approach the founder or ad manager with several free finished samples already made, rather than pitching the idea cold.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates this live on a real brand (a keyboard company running only 20 ads worldwide versus a competitor's 700), generating finished sample ads in minutes and framing unsolicited, already-built samples as the strongest way to get a response; he scores this 14/25 on his own scorecard (ease 3, speed 3, validated 5, competition 3, long-term 5) and cites charging $1,000-5,000+ per brand depending on scale.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to learn ad strategy and AI image/video tools, comfortable doing unsolicited prospecting work (building free samples) before landing a client.

The Truth (as we see it)

The Facebook Ad Library is a real, free, public research tool, and AI-generated ad creative is a real, current capability demonstrated live. The presenter's own pricing and scorecard reflect his stated experience but aren't independently audited, and the video promotes a specific AI tool's affiliate signup and his own paid training/coaching.
