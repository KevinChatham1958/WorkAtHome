AI Voice Assistant for Realtors

AI voice assistant for real estate agents, prospected via Zillow with a reusable demo

What It Is

Build one reusable, generic AI voice assistant demo (using GoHighLevel's voice AI, trained on a fictional sample property) that answers buyer calls, captures leads, and demonstrates live-calendar showing bookings, then find real estate agents with multiple active listings on Zillow, pitch them with a short personal Loom video referencing their actual listing, and walk interested agents through the demo live over the phone before customizing the assistant with their real listings and calendar once they sign on (typically around $297-500+/month).

The Pitch

The presenter frames the core insight as building the demo once rather than a custom assistant per prospect — one phone number, one generic sample property, reused across every pitch — since agents lose real money when a buyer call goes unanswered and moves to a competing agent. The outreach method is simple: find 20 agents with active Zillow listings, send each a 30-40 second Loom video referencing their specific listing, and demo the AI live over the phone once they show interest, converting to a customized paid setup only after they say yes.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with a moderate technical build (GoHighLevel's voice AI interface and prompt writing) and direct video-based cold outreach, who wants a scalable one-to-many pitch rather than building a custom product per prospect.

The Truth (as we see it)

The reusable-demo-first architecture is a genuinely smart way to avoid the common mistake of over-building before ever landing a client, and the specific $297+/month pricing and outreach script are concrete and actionable. This is a well-covered idea category in general (AI voice agents for real estate), but this creator's specific pricing, prospecting method, and demo-first build process are distinct enough to stand on their own — the video is also a funnel toward the presenter's own paid GoHighLevel affiliate link and course bundle.
