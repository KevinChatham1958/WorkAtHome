Posting Text-on-video Content to Facebook's Older Audience and Funneling Views to a Single Higher-priced PDF, Not Chasing Ad-revenue Payouts

Content & personal brand · Very low (a phone or basic video editor to add text over a slow-moving background clip)

Originally published Sep 10, 2026 — Frank Garrett

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Frank Garrett · Facebook + PDFs = $38,474/mo (JUST COPY THIS) ↗

What It Is

Post short, vertical videos to Facebook consisting mostly of on-screen text over a slow-moving background, aimed at Facebook's older (35-65) audience with plain, practical information, posting twice a day consistently; treat Facebook's own per-view ad payout as a minor bonus, not the goal, and instead use a plain three-line caption plus a pinned comment to funnel viewers to a single, higher-priced ($50) PDF that solves one narrow problem in one sitting.

The Pitch

The presenter reports $38,474 from one PDF and 609,000 views in 30 days, but is explicit that Facebook's own ad payout was only $149 of that; he's candid about his own mistakes (never collecting emails behind a free lead-magnet, no second product or checkout add-on) and specific platform rules that get pages suppressed.

Best For

Someone comfortable posting consistently and willing to review weekly performance to iterate, targeting an older demographic with a practically useful niche.

The Truth

The platform mechanics described match publicly known information about Facebook's creator monetization, and the pricing psychology is sound direct-response reasoning. His own revenue and view figures are self-reported from his own dashboard, not independently audited.
