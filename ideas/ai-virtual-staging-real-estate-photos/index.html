AI Virtual Staging

AI virtual staging of empty-room listing photos for realtors

What It Is

Realtors send you photos of empty rooms and you use ChatGPT to add furniture and decor in a chosen style, while keeping the walls, windows, floors and lighting unchanged. The creator says services charge about $20 to $50 per photo, so a five-room listing at $40 a photo is $200. You can find clients through Fiverr or Upwork, or by finding Zillow listings with empty rooms, staging a sample photo, and sending the before-and-after to the agent. Happy agents send repeat listings and referrals.

The Pitch

The creator says physical staging can cost over $1,000, so virtual staging is a cheaper way to help buyers picture living in a home. He points to more than 1,300 Fiverr results and one seller with over 9,000 reviews as proof of demand, and rates the potential as medium, with consistent realtor clients as the path to steady income.

Who It's Best For

Detail-oriented people comfortable reaching out to real estate agents and delivering quickly when listings go live.

The Truth (as we see it)

Virtual staging is an established, paid service, and the demand evidence on Fiverr is real. It is also a crowded market with dedicated apps that charge only a few dollars per image, which pushes prices down. General AI image tools can subtly alter windows, floors or room proportions, which can mislead buyers; many MLS rules and some state laws require virtually staged photos to be labeled, so check every output carefully. The creator rates the effort as high mainly because finding clients takes real work.
