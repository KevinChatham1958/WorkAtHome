Cloning a Proven Winning Shopify Store's Page and Creative Assets With AI Tools, Then Drop Shipping the Product

E-commerce · Low-to-moderate (product-research tool subscription, a page-cloning tool like White Labeler, AI image/video generation tools, Shopify subscription; no inventory required for the drop-shipping test phase)

Originally published Sep 13, 2026 — Ali Akbar

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Ali Akbar · $950k/m Store Shopify Store Built With Claude Ai Employees (Full Reveal) ↗

What It Is

Use a product-research tool to find an already-proven, heavily-advertised e-commerce product (validated by high ad-account activity, scaling traffic, and a converting sales-funnel-style product page), clone that page's structure section-by-section with an AI page-mirroring tool (White Labeler), source the product from AliExpress/Alibaba/local suppliers to drop-ship-test it before committing to inventory, then use an AI-agent tool to generate your own branded product images and UGC-style ad videos by swapping your product into the same creative formats the original winning store used.

The Pitch

The presenter picks a specific pet-product example doing an estimated $546K-$950K/month and walks through the entire build live: page cloning, sourcing with margin data (60-78% depending on supplier), and generating on-brand images/videos with an AI assistant that gives step-by-step creative prompts. He frames this as proof that dropshipping is one of the most beginner-friendly online business models when building on top of an already-validated winner.

Best For

Someone comfortable running paid ads and managing a drop-shipping supply chain, willing to pay for multiple tool subscriptions (research, page-cloning, AI creative) and to iterate on ad creative regularly.

The Truth

Product-page cloning combined with a validated, already-scaling winning product is a real, established e-commerce tactic (not cloning brand names or copy, per the presenter's own caveat), and AI tools genuinely speed up creative-asset production. The specific revenue figures for the reference store are third-party estimates, not verified sales data, and this method inherently competes directly with an established, heavily-advertised incumbent, so success depends on real ad-spend and iteration skill, not just the initial clone.
