What It Is

You create and sell an organized library of AI prompts for one audience, such as '200 Claude prompts every agency owner needs', with sections for sales, hiring, marketing, customer research, content, operations, training, finance and client retention. Claude helps generate prompts designed to produce specific, useful outputs, and you test and organize them into a product buyers can use immediately.

The Pitch

The creator says the value is saving buyers hours of figuring out what to ask AI, and that a specific library stands out where generic '1,000 ChatGPT prompts' bundles are everywhere.

Best For

People who use AI heavily in a particular field and can tell which prompts produce useful results.

The Truth

Tanner Chidester sells coaching for business owners through links in the description, and the $26,000-a-day figure is a hypothetical funnel calculation, not results from this product. Prompt libraries are a crowded, low-priced category, and AI tools increasingly suggest their own prompts, so the product's lasting value depends on real testing and niche depth.
