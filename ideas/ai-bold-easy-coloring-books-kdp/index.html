AI-generated Bold and Easy Coloring Books Published on Amazon KDP

What It Is

You generate simple, thick-outline coloring pages with AI image tools, compile about 50 into a book sized to Amazon's specs, and publish it through Kindle Direct Publishing. Amazon prints each copy on demand, ships it, and pays you a royalty.

The Pitch

The presenter says bold and easy (large-print, simple) coloring books are trending and tap into self-care, gift and senior markets. He describes prompting for 'bold and easy coloring book page, simple line art, thick outlines' across themes, then sizing pages in a free Canva template. He stresses KDP is free to publish, has no listing fees, and Amazon handles printing, shipping, returns and customer questions, so you only need to keep publishing more books.

Best For

People who want a hands-off product and are willing to build a catalog of books over time rather than expect one title to sell well.

The Truth

Print-on-demand through KDP does remove fulfillment and customer service, which is a real advantage. However, AI coloring books are heavily saturated on Amazon, and he admits pages may need editing for AI errors; Amazon also scrutinizes low-effort AI content and requires disclosure of AI-generated material. No income numbers are given for this specific idea. The presenter sells KDP coaching and an AI book-writing tool, so his preference for KDP over Etsy is not neutral.
