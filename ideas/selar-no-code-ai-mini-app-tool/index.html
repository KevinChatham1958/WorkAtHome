No-Code Mini App

No-code or AI-built mini app that solves one small problem for a niche audience

What It Is

You use AI and no-code tools to build a small web app that does one job for your audience, such as calculating, scoring or generating something. The creator's example is a 'magnetic content planner' that creates a personalized, strategic content plan for creators, with options to make a first plan or the next one. You sell access as a digital product.

The Pitch

The creator says AI and no-code tools let ordinary people build apps without coding, and that some customers do not need more information at all, just a tool that does the task. She calls this the format she is most excited about and expects many creators to move into it.

Who It's Best For

People who understand a specific audience's recurring problem and are willing to learn AI or no-code app builders.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI app builders have made simple tools much easier to create, so this idea is credible, but a mini app needs testing, hosting and occasional fixes in a way a PDF does not. Many app builders charge monthly fees once you go beyond free tiers. The creator gives no sales numbers for her app. The creator is promoting a waitlist for her own paid annual membership, the Digital Gold Mine Club, which teaches these formats.
