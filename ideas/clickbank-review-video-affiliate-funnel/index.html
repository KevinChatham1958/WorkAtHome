ClickBank Review Videos

Promoting ClickBank affiliate products via faceless screen-recorded review videos, sent through an email capture funnel

Garrett Barry · Make $500/Day With This EASY Clickbank Method! (100% WORKING) ↗

What It Is

Create a free ClickBank account, filter the marketplace by 'gravity' and average dollar per conversion to find a promising product, record a faceless screen-recorded review video with the product name plus the word 'review' in the title to rank for that search term, place the affiliate link in the first line of the description, then improve conversion by sending viewers to an email capture page before the sales page instead of directly to the affiliate link, following up with automated emails.

The Pitch

The presenter frames this as a beginner-friendly, no-camera method, adding what he calls a rarely-shared tip: routing traffic through an email list first can lift conversion from roughly 1% (direct link) to 30% or more, before pointing viewers toward his own recommended 100%-commission affiliate program with an already-built funnel.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable talking over a screen recording without appearing on camera, willing to post consistently since results build as review videos accumulate search rankings.

The Truth (as we see it)

ClickBank affiliate marketing via review videos is a real, long-standing method, and the email-capture-before-the-sale conversion principle is standard, sound direct-response practice. No specific personal results or screenshots are shown despite the '$500/Day' title, and the video's main call to action is his own affiliate link to a different program, so treat this as a generic tutorial rather than a demonstrated result.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
