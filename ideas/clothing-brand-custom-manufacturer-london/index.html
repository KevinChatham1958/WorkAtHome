Small Clothing Brand

Starting a small clothing brand from scratch, with designs made by a custom apparel manufacturer

  • $500–$2,000 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Startup cost: Moderate startup cost (samples, a first production run and branding) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Source video

London Leffler · I Tried Starting My Own Clothing Brand With No Experience ↗

What It Is

You pick an audience and a name, design a few pieces, have a custom apparel manufacturer produce samples and a small first run, and sell them through your own online store and social media.

The Pitch

London documents building a brand with no experience, and studies existing brands his research tool estimates at about $2,000 a month in sales up to $50,000 to $104,000 a month.

Who It’s Best For

Design-minded people with a clear audience and money to invest in stock.

The Truth (as we see it)

Clothing is crowded and needs real marketing; the revenue figures are estimates for other brands. Trademarking a name can cost him about $2,000, he says. The video is sponsored by TapStitch, the manufacturer he uses. London also runs a marketing company, Evolvant, which he mentions in his videos.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
