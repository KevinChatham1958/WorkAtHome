AI-generated TikTok Shop Content System Using Proven "bottom of Funnel" Formats

E-commerce · Low startup cost (AI image/video generation tools like Google Flow/Nano Banana/Omniflash; a product research tool like Kalodata)

Originally published Sep 1, 2026 — ducrez

ducrez · How AI TikTok Shop Generates Me $342,927 GMV Per Month (just copy me) ↗

What It Is

Find already-viral, simple "bottom of funnel" TikTok Shop videos (short, low-production clips aimed at buyers who already know the product but haven't purchased yet), study their format and hook, then use AI image and video generation tools to recreate that exact proven format with a different product, without ever ordering, filming, or showing your face, taking advantage of TikTok's GMV Max ad system (which automatically pushes ad budget toward whichever creative gets the highest click-through rate) to scale winning content.

The Pitch

The presenter says their community has generated seven figures in GMV this year using entirely AI-generated content, and demonstrates the full pipeline live: using a product research tool (Kalodata) to pull a product's base image, generating a styled product image with Google Flow's Nano Banana, then an AI video clip with Omniflash, and explains that consistency of format (not constant creative reinvention) combined with rapid product-swapping is what makes this scalable, eventually delegating the repetitive content-generation steps to a hired VA running the same automation.

Best For

Someone willing to study and reverse-engineer already-successful TikTok Shop content rather than creating original formats, and comfortable using several AI generation tools together as a repeatable pipeline.

The Truth

The specific mechanics described (GMV Max's click-through-rate-driven budget allocation, and the value of testing many simple videos over polishing a few) reflect real, current TikTok Shop advertising behavior and are more concretely actionable than most AI-content pitches. The $342,927 GMV figure is the presenter's own reported result, not independently verified, and this is a crowded, fast-moving space where formats that work today may saturate quickly.
