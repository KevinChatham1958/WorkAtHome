Skill Tutoring Business

Skill tutoring that starts with local students and scales online

What It Is

Pick a skill people pay to learn, such as math, coding or music, and start teaching four or five local students for about $100 to $150 each per month. Once you have a method and some results, move lessons online over Zoom so you can take students from anywhere. The video presents it as one of the cheapest businesses to start because you are selling only your knowledge and time.

The Pitch

The video argues parents always invest in their children's education and adults keep looking to upskill, so demand survives technology changes and recessions.

Who It's Best For

People who already know a subject well and enjoy explaining it, and who can keep a regular lesson schedule.

The Truth (as we see it)

Tutoring is one of the most dependable low-cost side incomes, and the per-student prices are realistic in many places. Income is capped by your hours unless you move to group classes or courses, and lessons happen at set times, which makes it harder to fit around other commitments. Finding the first students usually takes word of mouth or a tutoring marketplace that takes a cut. The video's treatment is a short summary in a nine-business list.
