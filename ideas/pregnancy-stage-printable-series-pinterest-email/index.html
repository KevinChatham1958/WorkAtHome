Pregnancy Printables Series

Pregnancy-stage organizational printables series (Pinterest + timed email funnel)

What It Is

You make printable organizational tools for pregnant women, such as hospital bag checklists, birth plan templates with blank preference sections, appointment trackers, registry and first-year budget planners, and scripts for setting visitor boundaries after the birth. Product ideas come from reading top all-time posts in pregnancy subreddits and the three- and four-star reviews of Etsy best sellers, then pasting those complaints into Claude to build checklists and fill-in templates, including a black-and-white printer-friendly version. The products are planned as a four-part series that matches the stages of pregnancy, so one buyer can purchase again at roughly 12, 24 and 34 weeks. Traffic comes from tall Pinterest pins that use week numbers and trimester words, leading to a free hospital bag checklist, an email signup, and a short email sequence that offers each product when she is likely to need it.

The Pitch

The creator argues the niche "refills itself every single month" because new women become pregnant all the time, and the due date gives buyers a real reason to act now. He says he makes over $30,000 a month with this PDF business model, and $31,426 in the last 30 days. Mapping products to pregnancy stages turns one customer into several purchases.

Who It’s Best For

Organized people who are comfortable writing practical checklists and templates and will take care to stay clear of medical topics; it suits someone willing to wait months for Pinterest traffic to build.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator is careful about the main risk: he says never to give medical advice, avoid anything about symptoms, food, medication or warning signs, and add a "not medical advice" line to every product, which is sensible for this audience. His $30,000+ monthly figure is his own unaudited claim and refers to his overall PDF business, not proven results from this niche. He admits the obvious products like hospital bag checklists are crowded and that the business is slow, since Pinterest takes months to warm up. The video includes a sponsored segment for a paid workbook prompt library (300 prompts by Palo Grow), and he promotes his own paid course, the Passive PDF Method.
