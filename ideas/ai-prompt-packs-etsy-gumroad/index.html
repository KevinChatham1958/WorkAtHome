AI Prompt Packs Sold on Etsy and Gumroad

What It Is

You bundle a set of AI prompts built for one job, such as email copywriting, course creation, social media posts or image generation, and sell the pack as a digital download. Buyers on marketplaces like Etsy or Gumroad pay a one-time price for the pack.

The Pitch

The presenter says prompt packs cost almost nothing to make, are quick to package and need no fulfillment. He notes many people search Etsy and Gumroad for prompts, and that sellers did well when AI tools first appeared. He says the model still works and can earn money with the right angle.

Best For

People who already use AI tools heavily in a specific niche and want a quick, cheap digital product to test, without expecting it to be a main income source.

The Truth

The presenter himself does not recommend this one: he argues prompt packs lose value as AI tools get better at understanding plain requests and add built-in templates, and that Etsy is already flooded with large prompt bundles. That reasoning is credible, since a buyer can increasingly get the same result without a pack. Sellers also have to drive their own traffic or compete on crowded marketplace search. A narrow, specialist angle is the only realistic path, and income expectations should be modest.
