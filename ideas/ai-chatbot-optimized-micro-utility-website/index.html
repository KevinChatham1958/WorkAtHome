Websites Recommended by AI Chatbots

Building simple one-page utility websites specifically optimized to be recommended by AI chatbots, not just found via Google search

What It Is

Build a genuinely simple, single-purpose one-page tool or calculator website (the same broad concept as other 'micro-utility site' approaches), but specifically instruct the AI building it to add the technical elements that help AI chatbots (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) find and recommend the site when a user asks a related question in conversation, rather than relying only on traditional Google search ranking; since more people are now asking AI assistants questions directly instead of searching Google, a site optimized to be the answer an AI recommends can capture traffic that never touches a traditional search results page. Monetize with ad networks that approve and pay from day one (the presenter names Adsterra and Monetag as options), rather than waiting for a longer AdSense approval process.

The Pitch

The presenter cites several real, named one-page utility sites as proof of the underlying ad-monetized-utility-site model's ceiling (a sleep calculator earning roughly $36,000/month from 1.7 million monthly visitors with no blog or backlinks, a free online photo editor earning around $200,000/month on about $700/year in hosting cost, and a text-case-converter tool earning five figures monthly from 3.6 million visitors on about $20/month in hosting), framing a simple one-page tool as capable of running almost entirely on autopilot once built and indexed.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable directing an AI coding tool to build a genuinely simple, narrow tool, and willing to learn the specific technical steps that help AI assistants (not just Google) discover and recommend a site.

The Truth (as we see it)

The named example websites and their described monetization models (ad-supported, no blog/backlink strategy needed) are real, well-known, and independently verifiable one-page utility sites, and AI answer engines increasingly citing and recommending websites in conversational responses is a real, current, and growing traffic dynamic. The specific dollar figures for each cited site are the presenter's own claims about other people's businesses, not independently verified, and this specific technique shares its core 'simple ad-monetized utility site' concept with content already covered elsewhere from this same producer, with the AI-recommendation optimization angle being the genuinely new contribution here.
