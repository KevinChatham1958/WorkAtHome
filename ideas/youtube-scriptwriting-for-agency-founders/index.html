YouTube Scriptwriting for Founders

AI-assisted YouTube scriptwriting service for agency founders

What It Is

You come up with video ideas and write scripts for founders of agencies and other businesses that earn well but get few views on YouTube. Claude helps research topics and draft outlines; you add structure, hooks and the founder's voice. Clients pay a monthly retainer.

The Pitch

Dang says scriptwriting is one of the most time-consuming parts of his own YouTube work and that he would pay thousands a month for someone good. Running his business-idea Claude skill on this offer, he shows it estimating that about five clients would reach $10,000 a month. He finds prospects by asking Claude, connected to the sales tool Apollo, for businesses earning over $100,000 a month whose channels get little traction.

Who It’s Best For

Writers who understand what makes YouTube videos get clicked and watched, and who can capture another person's speaking style.

The Truth (as we see it)

Founders do pay for scriptwriting, but scripts alone rarely fix a channel; packaging (topic, title, thumbnail) and the founder's on-camera delivery matter as much. The five-clients-to-$10,000 figure is a projection from Dang's own AI tool, not a result. The video is sponsored by Apollo and promotes Dang's free skill, his program FounderX and coaching calls. It also repeats a layoff statistic that is overstated.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
