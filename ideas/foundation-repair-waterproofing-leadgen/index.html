Foundation Repair Lead Gen

What It Is

Partner with licensed foundation repair and waterproofing crews who already own the specialized equipment, hydraulic piers, drainage systems, and handle their marketing, scheduling, and sales conversations in exchange for a cut of every job you bring them, without doing any of the physical repair work yourself.

The Pitch

A cracking foundation or a flooding crawl space pushes homeowners to act fast, with jobs commonly running $5,000 to $15,000, and the presenters note there's no single dominant player controlling more than a small share of the market. Because you're leveraging crews who already own expensive equipment, the presenters say you can start with just a Google Business Profile and some before-and-after photos rather than any real capital.

Best For

Someone comfortable with lead generation and sales who wants to avoid buying equipment or doing physical labor, and who's okay with a business that's mostly one-time transactions per customer rather than recurring.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenters are upfront that the one thing keeping this from ranking higher on their list is that it's almost entirely one-time work, a homeowner's foundation gets fixed once, and then you're hoping for the next referral or lead, which they acknowledge can get stressful as you try to grow. They frame the urgency and dollar amounts as genuine, not exaggerated, since a worsening foundation is a real, escalating cost to the homeowner.
