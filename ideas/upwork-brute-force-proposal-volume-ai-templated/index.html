Upwork Retainers With No Reviews

Landing Upwork retainer clients with zero testimonials by brute-force applying using AI-templated proposals and Loom videos

What It Is

Pick one narrow, realistic service you can confidently deliver and defend in an interview (GoHighLevel setup work, in this example), build a simplified AI-templated system to quickly draft a customized proposal letter for each job posting, pair every proposal with a short Loom video pitch, and apply to a high volume of jobs daily (aiming for 100 proposals/day, though roughly 10-25/day proved realistic) with zero testimonials or existing client history, treating early rejections as expected volume rather than a signal to stop.

The Pitch

The guest, a busy mom with a full-time job and only 2-3 hours a day after her kid's bedtime, signed her first $8,000/month retainer client on her 23rd proposal within her first two weeks on Upwork, having started with zero followers and no prior freelancing experience, crediting confident presentation over polished credentials as the real deciding factor for clients.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with limited free time who can commit short, consistent daily blocks to job applications, comfortable being interviewed on video calls and confidently defending AI-assisted work as genuinely their own capability.

The Truth (as we see it)

High-volume proposal submission combined with a consistent, AI-accelerated application process is a real, sound way to overcome the lack of an existing portfolio on freelance platforms, and the specific numbers given are unusually concrete for a self-reported case study. This is one individual's outcome discussed inside the interviewer's own paid community, so some promotional framing should be expected, and results depend heavily on picking a service niche with genuine personal competency.
