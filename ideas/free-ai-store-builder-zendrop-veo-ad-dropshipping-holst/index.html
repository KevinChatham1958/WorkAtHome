AI Dropshipping Store

AI-built Shopify dropshipping store with a supplier app and AI-generated video ads

E-commerce · Low startup cost (Shopify $1/month for 3 months, then plan fees, supplier app and AI credits)

Originally published Oct 3, 2026 — Jeremy Ray Holst

Share on Facebook Link copied

Jeremy Ray Holst · Build a FREE AI Dropshipping Store in 10 Minutes ↗

What It Is

You use a free AI store builder to pick a niche, brand color and banner, then connect a new Shopify account and install a dropshipping supplier app (Zendrop) that pushes 'winning products' onto the store automatically. After taking the store password off, you market products on social media. The creator makes ads with an all-in-one AI image and video generator: he screenshots a product, uses a prompt helper, picks a video model (Veo 3), sets 9:16 at 720p and 8 seconds, and generates clips to stitch into TikToks. You earn the difference between your sale price and the supplier's cost when orders come in.

The Pitch

The creator says AI removes the old work of product research, store design, descriptions and ad filming, so a full store can be set up in under 10 minutes. He says keeping video at 720p makes AI ads look less obviously artificial, and that one tool covering many AI models saves paying for several subscriptions.

Who It's Best For

Beginners who want a low-cost way to try e-commerce and are prepared to spend most of their effort on marketing and testing products.

The Truth (as we see it)

The setup steps are real and cheap to try, but the video ends once the store is built and the first ad is made, with no sales or results shown. The store builder collects your name, email and phone number, the free course and community are a lead funnel, and the AI video tool is promoted with a free-credits offer, which suggests a sponsorship or affiliate deal. The example product (character-themed slides) may raise trademark problems, and auto-imported 'winning products' are usually the same items many other new stores sell. Dropshipping also brings slow shipping, refund and payment-hold issues, and most new stores need paid ads or a lot of content testing before they sell.
