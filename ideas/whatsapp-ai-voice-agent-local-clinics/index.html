WhatsApp/voice AI Agent Business for Local Clinics and Service Businesses

What It Is

Build an AI voice and WhatsApp text agent (using ElevenLabs, with Twilio for landline/phone numbers) that answers calls and messages for local businesses — the presenter's example is dental and aesthetic clinics — booking appointments and answering questions, then sell it to business owners after researching the most underserved local niches with Claude and building a free demo based on a real target business's actual website content before ever reaching out.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a $158,000 revenue result over about 3 months (9,161 calls, 85% answer rate) from this exact system for one client, and demonstrates the full workflow live: using Claude Cowork to research which local business niches lose the most revenue to missed calls, building a demo voice agent in ElevenLabs based on a specific real clinic's website, then using the Claude Chrome extension to find business owner names and contact details (via Companies House/public records) for targeted outreach, recommending in-person demos or a screen-recorded Loom video as the pitch.

Best For

Someone comfortable with a moderate technical setup (ElevenLabs, Twilio, prompt engineering) who's willing to do direct local outreach (in-person, cold email, or LinkedIn) rather than waiting for inbound interest.

The Truth

The specific tool stack and step-by-step demo-then-prospect workflow is more detailed and tactical than most AI-voice-agent content, and using AI research tools to target the most underserved local niches first is a genuinely useful de-risking step. The $158,000 case study figure is the presenter's own reported client result, not independently verified, and success still depends on the outreach and sales execution described, not just the technical build.
