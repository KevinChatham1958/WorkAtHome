Amazon Influencer Program video reviews (residual-payout model)

What It Is

Join Amazon's Influencer Program and make short product review videos of items already sold on Amazon; Amazon displays these videos to shoppers browsing those products, and pays a commission whenever a video influences a purchase — payouts can continue on already-uploaded videos even during long stretches with no new uploads.

The Pitch

The presenter describes continuing to get paid monthly on videos she hasn't updated since the previous October, having started the program after her son was born and posting inconsistently since. She rates it a 3/5 for ease of starting and a 3/5 for speed to first dollar, framing the appeal as ongoing residual payouts from a modest, one-time batch of videos rather than needing constant new output.

Best For

Someone with everyday Amazon purchases already on hand who's comfortable being briefly on camera, and who wants a low-maintenance income stream that doesn't require an existing following.

The Truth

The genuinely distinguishing point here is the residual-payout behavior on old videos, which is a real feature of the program's structure and not typical of most side hustles requiring constant new content. That said, per-video payouts are generally modest and highly variable, and this account gives no specific dollar figures for actual earnings, just the ongoing-payout mechanic itself.
