Revenue-Share Growth Operator

Growth operator who builds and runs creators' paid offers for a revenue share

Marketing & content · Low startup cost

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

One of 11 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You partner with a creator who has an audience but no products, and build their offer, funnel, emails, webinars, ads and sales process. Claude helps build landing pages and write copy. Pay is typically a base fee plus a percentage of the profits you generate.

The Pitch

Dang says many creators have followings but earn little beyond brand deals and want courses, coaching or digital products. He suggests finding creators with reach whose bios and websites show no booking link or training, then pitching them directly; he says a persistent cold-messaging operator eventually won his own business. He quotes around $5,000 per month base plus 10 to 50 percent of profit depending on how much you run.

Who It's Best For

Best for people with real marketing, funnel and sales experience who want a partnership rather than a simple freelance gig.

The Truth (as we see it)

Dang rates this the hardest of his list to start because it requires several skills at once, and that is accurate. Revenue-share deals carry real risk: if the launch flops you may earn little for months of work, and many creators' audiences do not convert. The percentages he cites, including rumors about large publishers, are unverified. It is a credible model for experienced marketers but not a beginner side hustle. The video is sponsored by Higgsfield, the creative tool he recommends throughout, and it repeatedly pushes his free Claude skill pack, a free 3-day live training, and calls with his team for paid coaching.
