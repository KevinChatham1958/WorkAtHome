Contractor Local SEO Agency

Local SEO and GoHighLevel retainer agency for home-service contractors, with review and referral campaigns to past customers

What It Is

Sell home-service businesses such as roofers, remodelers, electricians and concrete companies a monthly marketing package. The guest started at $500 a month with Facebook ads and GoHighLevel automations, then moved to $2,000 a month on 12-month contracts covering a new website, local SEO, blog and Google Business Profile posts and backlinks, first using a white-label SEO provider (about $400-500 a month) and later his own SEO staff. While rankings build over three to six months, he runs short-term campaigns on the client's old customer list: a review request campaign, then a referral drawing for a $200 Amazon card. He found his first clients by cold calling lists pulled from a scraping tool and is now adding a $497-a-month entry package (AI-built website, AI receptionist, directory listings and a ready-made ad campaign the client launches).

The Pitch

The guest says he reached $10,000 a month within four to six months and now runs about $20,000 a month with a team of seven, while traveling with his family. He and the host argue that contractors' past-customer lists are an untapped source of quick jobs, which keeps clients paying while SEO takes hold, and that a high-ticket job like a kitchen remodel can cover a year of fees.

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable with cold calling and sales calls who want to learn SEO and marketing automation for local trades.

The Truth (as we see it)

The structure is sound: pairing slow-building SEO with quick wins from a client's existing customer list is a sensible way to reduce early cancellations, and the guest had prior SEO agency and sales experience that most beginners won't. The revenue figures are his own and unverified, and the model depends on heavy cold calling and long contracts that many small businesses resist. The video is a testimonial-style interview from the host's paid community, where the guest is also a coach, and the host is a GoHighLevel affiliate, so both benefit when viewers sign up.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
