Agency Using Contractor Arbitrage

Online boutique marketing agency using "contractor arbitrage": pay overseas contractors about $500 per client per month and sell the service to Western clients for $1,500 to $2,500

What It Is

You run an agency that sells a marketing service, such as ads, email marketing, SEO or content, but you don't deliver it yourself. You hire a contractor in a lower-cost country (Romania, South Africa, Brazil, Colombia) at about $500 per client per month, then sell the service to clients in the US, UK, Canada, Australia or Western Europe for $1,500 to $2,500 a month, keeping the difference. Later, a trusted contractor can become your right-hand person running the agency.

The Pitch

Gadzhi says he built his agency in high school this way, earning $20,000 to $25,000 a month profit by 17, and by 2022 made $100,000 to $150,000 a month profit working 4 to 5 hours a week, because his former $600-per-client contractor became his CMO. He calls it the fastest and most scalable route to $10,000 a month.

Who It’s Best For

People confident pitching business owners who can find and manage reliable contractors.

The Truth (as we see it)

At the time Gadzhi ran an e-learning business teaching this exact model, which he names in the video, so it is his own product's method. His income figures are his own, unaudited, and came after years of work and a strong team. The model is real, but clients judge you on the contractor's work, quality varies widely, and margins disappear if you need to replace staff or redo work. This video was published in 2022, more than two years ago, so tools, platform rules, rates and income figures may have changed.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.

What Viewers Say

Mostly encouragement and thanks. The practical comments come from video editors, who say finding clients who will trust a newcomer, especially from another country, is much harder than the video makes it sound.
