$999 AI Tools Assessment

Local & professional services · Low startup cost

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Corey Gannon · The $1,000/hour Solo AI business (Full Course) ↗

What It Is

A fixed, productized service where you meet with a small business owner for about 45 minutes, ask structured questions to find where they're losing time, and then recommend three to seven off-the-shelf AI tools they can start using immediately. No coding or technical implementation required, you're prescribing existing tools, not building anything new.

The Pitch

Gannon charges $999 for a 45-minute assessment where he recommends off-the-shelf AI tools rather than building anything custom, backed by a money-back guarantee if he can't find at least five hours a week of time savings. He says the average client saves about seven hours a week, and the assessment opens the door to bigger, higher-priced implementation work afterward.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants the lowest-friction possible entry into AI consulting, no coding or automation-building, and who's comfortable running a structured 45-minute conversation with a business owner to diagnose their specific pain points.

The Truth (as we see it)

The pitch is explicit that this works because most small businesses (his stated sweet spot is 2-20 employees, $500K-5M revenue) are barely using AI beyond basic chatbots, a believable and checkable claim rather than a vague one. The money-back guarantee is a genuine credibility signal. As with every consulting-based idea in this database, the real bottleneck is still finding and booking that first assessment call, not delivering it once booked.
