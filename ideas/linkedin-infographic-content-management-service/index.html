LinkedIn Infographic Content Management Service for Personal Brands

What It Is

Create infographic-style LinkedIn posts on behalf of a business owner or personal brand, using an AI image-generation tool (Nano Banana, within Gemini) to produce the graphics from a topic prompt, with the goal of driving inbound leads through consistent, shareable content rather than outbound outreach.

The Pitch

The presenter points to an existing creator (Chris Donnelly) who built a company largely through LinkedIn infographic content produced by a team of content managers, and demonstrates generating his own LinkedIn infographics with Nano Banana from a custom prompt template, saying he's booked strategy calls directly from people who engaged with these posts.

Best For

Someone with an eye for what makes content engaging (or willing to learn it) who's comfortable pitching this as a done-for-you service to business owners and professionals who want a LinkedIn presence but don't have time to build one themselves.

The Truth

Generating the images themselves is genuinely easy with current AI tools, as demonstrated in the video, but the presenter is upfront that speed to first income is only moderate since there's real competition and clients want to see evidence the content will actually generate leads before hiring someone new. The named case study (Chris Donnelly) represents an already-established business, not a typical starting point.
