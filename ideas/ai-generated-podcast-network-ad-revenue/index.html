What It Is

Generate scripts with Claude and voice them with ElevenLabs' V3 model to produce fully AI-generated podcast episodes in evergreen niches (history, finance, fitness, religion, etc.), publish through an ad-enabled distributor like RSS.com that automatically splices in ads with no manual ad reads, and grow reach by testing the same content across multiple differently-named podcasts to find which framing/title performs best organically before doubling down on the winner.

The Pitch

The hosts say their own human-hosted podcast made about $1,057 in the last month from 24,000 downloads via automatic RSS.com ad splicing, and describe running several fully AI-voiced podcasts (ElevenLabs V3 narration) that also earn real money, with individual side shows pulling $250-$524/month from repurposed content. Their scaling trick: launch the same episodes under 10 differently-named podcasts, see which gains organic traction, then focus effort on the winner.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable picking an evergreen content niche and managing multiple podcast feeds, without needing to appear on camera or record their own voice.

The Truth (as we see it)

Ad-revenue-per-download from platforms like RSS.com is real but modest — the figures shown come from tens of thousands of downloads, so this is a volume game, not a fast path to income. The multi-podcast-name testing tactic is a legitimate organic-discovery strategy, but the host explicitly warns against using trademarked terms in podcast names (a friend's podcast got banned for this), and the video doubles as an ad for the presenters' own paid School community, which gates the more detailed guidance behind a $20/month membership.
