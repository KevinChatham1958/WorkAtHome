Kat Lewis · 4 Work From Home Claims Processing Jobs (Some Provide Equipment) ↗

What It Is

Apply to remote work-from-home insurance/medical claims processing roles, ranging from entry-level positions requiring just a high school diploma and basic computer skills (resolving denied/pending claims, correcting billing errors) to leadership roles overseeing a claims team (requiring a bachelor's degree or equivalent experience). The presenter reviews four specific current examples: an account resolution specialist at Sarnova (Medicare/Medicaid claims, no degree required, ~$20-27/hour), a claims customer experience team lead at Reserve (leadership role, $75,000-95,000/year, includes equipment), a claims correction specialist at OneOncology (medical billing correction, ~$21-31/hour), and a PIP/med pay claims associate at Allstate (auto insurance claims investigation, ~$21-23/hour, full equipment provided).

The Pitch

The presenter reviews each job's actual posted responsibilities and requirements directly from company careers pages, and since most listings don't publish salary, she researches and estimates realistic pay ranges herself rather than just repeating the employer's own vague framing.

Best For

Someone comfortable with detail-oriented administrative work, willing to learn insurance/medical billing terminology; entry-level roles need no prior experience, while leadership roles require a degree or equivalent industry background.

The Truth

All four named employers and job postings are real, verifiable current listings, and claims processing is a genuine, established category of remote employment. Salary figures for roles that don't publish pay are the presenter's own researched estimates, not confirmed by the employer.
