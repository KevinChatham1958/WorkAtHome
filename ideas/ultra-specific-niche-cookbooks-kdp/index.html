Ultra-Specific Niche Cookbooks

Ultra-specific diet and problem-focused cookbooks on Amazon KDP

Physical & print products · Very low startup cost (more if you hire a ghostwriter)

What It Is

Instead of a generic recipe book, you publish narrowly targeted cookbooks, such as a carnivore diet cookbook for beginners on a budget, a 30-minute Mediterranean air fryer cookbook, or an anti-inflammatory cookbook. You can draft with AI, write from your own experience, or hire a ghostwriter, then fact-check and edit before publishing through Amazon KDP. Amazon prints, ships and handles customer service, and pays you a royalty.

The Pitch

The creator says generic cookbooks are "dead" but ultra-specific ones "quietly sell like crazy," because a buyer with a particular problem picks the book that feels made for them, even at a higher price. He says Amazon already brings shoppers searching for those exact solutions.

Who It's Best For

People with real background in cooking, nutrition or health, even from personal experience, who are willing to edit carefully until the content is accurate.

The Truth (as we see it)

Narrow positioning is a sound way to stand out on KDP, and the creator is right to warn that health-related recipes must be accurate. He shows no sales figures for this product, and an AI-drafted cookbook with untested recipes risks poor reviews; Amazon also requires you to disclose AI-generated content when you publish. Hiring a knowledgeable ghostwriter, which he suggests, raises the cost considerably. The creator sells a one-on-one KDP mentorship ("Road to Hero") promoted in this video.
