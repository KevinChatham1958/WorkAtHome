Baddie in Business · EASY Amazon Side Hustle Selling Ai Coloring Books (HOW TO START NOW) ↗

What It Is

You use an AI tool to generate a complete coloring book, illustrations, layout, and cover, then publish it as a physical, print-on-demand book through Amazon's KDP platform, with no inventory to manage yourself.

The Pitch

The appeal here is speed and cost: the presenter walks through building an entire book in one sitting using free AI tools, meaning you can have a real, purchasable physical product live on Amazon the same day, for close to zero upfront cost.

Best For

Someone who wants a very cheap, very fast way to test whether they enjoy print-on-demand publishing, without committing much money or time to find out.

The Truth

The income claims in the video, $500 to $1,000 a month per book, aren't backed up with any account screenshots or sales data, they're stated, not shown. Coloring books are also a fairly crowded category on Amazon, so a lot of the real work is picking a niche or theme that isn't already saturated.
