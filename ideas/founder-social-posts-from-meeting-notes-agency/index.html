Founder Posts From Meeting Notes

Social posting agency for busy founders that turns their own meeting recordings into posts, published by an open-source scheduler

What It Is

Instead of interviewing executives for content, you pull ideas from their existing meeting recordings and note-taker transcripts (tools like Grain), have an AI agent spot the topics they were most animated about, and turn those into social posts in their voice. Posts are scheduled and tailored per platform with Postiz, a free open-source tool that can publish to about 34 networks and be driven by an agent. Clients pay a monthly retainer; the hosts discuss about $5,000 a month per client for a small one- or two-person shop with 10 to 20 clients.

The Pitch

The host argues that posts drawn from real meetings sound more genuine than interview-based ghostwriting, and that the whole workflow can be automated. The venture studio guest is lukewarm on it as a large company but says it can be a good lifestyle business with very low costs.

Who It’s Best For

A writer or marketer who can capture someone else's voice and is comfortable managing a few demanding executive clients.

The Truth (as we see it)

The guest's caution is the useful part: executives are hard customers who give little time and churn easily, and financial advisers and similar professions are slowed by compliance rules. The $5,000-a-month price and 10-20 client figures are a hypothetical discussed on air, not a reported result. Getting access to a client's private meeting recordings raises real confidentiality questions you would need to settle in writing. The software itself is free, so your value is judgment and voice, not the tools.
