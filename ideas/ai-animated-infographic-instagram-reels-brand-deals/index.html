AI Animated Infographic Reels

AI-animated infographic reels on Instagram (brand deals + own digital products)

What It Is

You research a niche topic with an AI chatbot (the video uses dog sleeping positions), turn the text into a vertical 9:16 infographic with an AI image generator, then use an AI video model to animate parts of the image while the text stays still. The finished 6-10 second clips are posted as Instagram Reels. The account earns money through paid brand sponsorships once it has a big following, or by linking to its own digital products such as ebooks or an app.

The Pitch

The creator says animated infographics are going viral on Instagram, pointing to one post with over 823 million views in three months. She says large accounts can charge brands roughly $5,000 to $25,000 per sponsored post, and that the workflow is simple once you know which AI models to use.

Who It’s Best For

People who enjoy researching and presenting facts visually and are willing to post consistently for months before any money comes in.

The Truth (as we see it)

The making of the clips is genuinely simple with current AI tools, but the money depends on growing a large audience, which most accounts never do. The 823-million-view example and the $5,000-$25,000 sponsorship range describe a rare top account, not a typical result. Facts produced by AI chatbots need checking before you publish them. The creator, Sarah of Wholesale Ted, sells a video course (The Ecom Clubhouse, offered with a $1 seven-day trial) and gives away a free ebook in this video, and she uses affiliate links for the tools she recommends.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
