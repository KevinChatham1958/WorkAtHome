YouTube Monetization Coaching

Coaching existing YouTubers on monetization and growth

Source video

Shane Hummus · How I Helped Sean Scale to Multiple 6 figures Per Month on YouTube ↗

What It Is

Coach existing YouTubers who already have an audience and expertise, in this case a creator teaching the Amazon KDP publishing model, on monetization, operations, and hiring, packaged as a paid course plus coaching program.

The Pitch

In a named, on-camera case study, Hummus describes taking his client Sean's channel from around 100,000 subscribers to over 300,000, more than tripling AdSense income to $22,000 a month, growing course revenue to over $500,000 a month, and landing a single affiliate commission of $73,000 along the way.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who already has real, demonstrable expertise in a specific area of business or content growth to coach others in, since this model depends entirely on already having that credibility.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is Hummus's own paying client, interviewed together on camera, which is a stronger transparency signal than a third-party retelling, but it's still a single promotional case study from the coach's own business, not an independently audited result. Building a coaching business requires you to already have real expertise in a specific niche, which isn't something this idea creates for you.
