Goodwill Bins Clothing Reselling

Reselling Goodwill Bins clothing across eBay, Poshmark and Depop with a daily listing quota

  • Under $100 to start
  • Jump to The Truth ↓

Startup cost: Low startup cost (by-the-pound bins inventory plus crosslisting tool subscriptions) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

Arejay136 · I FINALLY Hit $15,000 per month in Reselling and This Is How I Did it ↗

What It Is

You dig through the Goodwill Bins (outlet stores selling donated goods by the pound) for branded clothing such as Levi's 501 and 569 jeans, Carhartt, Dickies, Seven for All Mankind and occasional vintage or designer finds, often grabbing items other shoppers throw back. At home you photograph each piece with tag, measurement and flaw shots and list it, then use crosslisting tools (Spadeberry for posting, Nifty for relisting) to put it on eBay, Poshmark and Depop. The creator lists 42 items a day, Monday to Friday, keeps stock in a storage unit sorted by SKU, and ships about 20-30 orders on a busy day using a cheap scale, a label printer and the cheapest carrier option. He says this has grown the business to $15,000 a month.

The Pitch

The creator says the secret to reaching $15,000 a month is simply doing the work consistently: getting good items, listing good items and selling them. He says bins sourcing keeps his inventory full and that boosting his Depop store and crosslisting to several platforms keeps sales coming. He presents it as a business you can keep scaling by setting a listing goal, then fixing whatever breaks in the system.

Who It’s Best For

People who enjoy hands-on thrifting, can learn clothing brands and tags, and can keep up a steady daily photo-and-listing routine.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a day-in-the-life vlog rather than a tutorial, so you have to pick out the method from the footage, though the brands, tools and listing volume are concrete. The $15,000 a month appears to be sales, not profit, and is the creator's own unaudited figure; many of the sales shown are $9-$40 items. The workload is heavy: daily bins trips, 42 listings a day, packing and shipping, and the creator notes the strain and anxiety of running it solo. The method relies on Goodwill Bins outlets and US-centred platforms like Poshmark, so it fits best in the US.
