Sneaker Cleaning and Restoration

Sneaker cleaning and restoration service selling to resellers

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (about $35-150 for a cleaning kit)

What It Is

Clean, restore, and repair sneakers (deep cleaning, yellowing reversal, suede restoration, sole repainting), selling primarily to sneaker resellers who need every pair cleaned before listing it for resale, rather than relying on individual sneaker owners as a slow trickle of one-off customers.

The Pitch

A basic Reshoevn8r kit (about $35, cleans up to 50 pairs) or a fuller professional setup (about $110) covers most of the startup cost, with supply cost of $2-5 per pair. Real shop rates: deep clean $35-50, yellowing reversal $40, suede restoration $20, sole repaint $40, sole protector $48. One documented job billed a Jordan 1 restoration at $162 with about $100 profit. The presenter frames resellers as the real repeat customer, since cleaning adds $10-20+ to resale value on pairs they move by the dozen every week.

Who It's Best For

Someone who wants recurring volume from sneaker resellers rather than individual sneaker owners, and who's willing to learn proper cleaning technique to avoid damaging valuable inventory.

The Truth (as we see it)

The economics and target-customer insight (resellers over individual owners) are solid, but the presenter is explicit about a real risk: machine washing destroys 60-70% of sneakers and direct heat melts adhesive, meaning a mistake in a service business like this means buying someone a replacement pair — the margin for error with valuable resale inventory is genuinely low.
