What It Is

Use a tufting gun, a handheld tool that shoots yarn at high speed onto fabric stretched on a frame, to create custom rugs with a logo, character, or design, a process that would take weeks to weave by hand but takes hours with the tool.

The Pitch

A small rug costs around $20 in materials and sells for $60-250 depending on size and design, with a customer base spanning barbershops and streaming studios wanting branded entryway rugs to individuals wanting a rug with their pet's face or kid's name. The process itself is described as inherently viral content: posting a video of a rug being made tends to generate orders on its own without needing to actively find clients.

Who It's Best For

Someone who enjoys hands-on, visually satisfying craft work and is willing to post the process on video, since the content itself is described as doing much of the marketing.

The Truth (as we see it)

The cost and price figures are consistent with what's publicly listed for tufting kits and finished custom rugs elsewhere, a reasonable range. No specific named operator or verified sales figures are given, so the income potential is illustrative rather than demonstrated by a real case study.
