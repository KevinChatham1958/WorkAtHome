Statewide AI Micro Directory Used to Sell Featured Slots and Open Doors With Every Business

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (he says under $1 for a whole state's business data, plus domain and AI subscription)

Originally published Oct 6, 2026 — Jesse Cunningham

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jesse Cunningham · How AI Micro-Directories Are Taking Over Whole States ↗

What It Is

You build a directory covering every city in one state for one trade, with each city page carrying 500-800 words of local copy, ranked and enriched listings, paid featured slots, visible local FAQs and a "claim your listing" form. You publish it under your own name as the expert publisher. You then earn three ways: monthly fees for featured or top positions and "best of" badges, a reason to phone every business on the list (for example, congratulating them on being top three) to sell featured spots, exclusive leads or website work, and internal support for any lead-generation microsites you own in the same trade.

The Pitch

The creator calls the directory a "force multiplier": one build gives you passive featured-listing income, instant authority when you call contractors, and a way to boost your other sites. He says what once needed a team can now be built by one person in about an hour, and that businesses come to the directory wanting to be listed higher. He suggests testing niches with small sites first, then committing a directory to the trade that works.

Best For

Suits people who are willing to make phone calls to local business owners and want a cheap asset that gives them a credible reason to start those conversations.

The Truth

Using a niche directory as a door-opener for selling to local businesses is a long-standing, credible tactic, and the build-cost claims are plausible with current AI tools. The income depends heavily on cold calling and on the directory actually ranking, neither of which he puts numbers on. Charging businesses for higher placement in a "best of" list should be clearly labeled as paid to stay honest with readers. He promotes his own paid Skool community (the Rank Expander Academy, 7-day free trial, then paid), and the build blueprint zip file he relies on is only available inside it.
