AI Website Audit Service

AI-run SEO and AI-search (AEO) website audits sold to local businesses, with a monthly retainer

Marketing & content · Near-zero cost (Claude with the Chrome extension, or ChatGPT's)

Originally published Oct 7, 2026 — Alicia Lyttle

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Alicia Lyttle · The Ultimate AI Side Hustle: Get Started In 24 Hours ↗

What It Is

You open a business's website (a local salon, for example) and run a detailed prompt through the Claude Chrome extension that has it crawl the site and produce a client-ready report on search engine optimization, visibility in AI answers like ChatGPT and Claude, and conversion problems, ranked by revenue impact. You send part of the report as a teaser, then sell the fixes and a monthly re-audit and maintenance plan.

The Pitch

The creator says clients will pay for the report and then ask you to fix or rebuild the site, and suggests $500-2,000 a month for maintenance with small businesses and $1,500-5,000 with larger ones. She notes search is changing so fast that monthly audits are an easy sell.

Who It's Best For

Someone who understands websites and online marketing basics and can explain recommendations to business owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI can produce an impressive-looking audit quickly, but recommendations still need checking, and businesses judge you on results. The retainer prices are the creator's suggestions, not shown outcomes. Sending unsolicited audits works best when they're accurate and specific. The creator sells AI training programs and workshops and collected emails for the prompts.
