Personal-Brand Coaching Offer

Building a high-ticket coaching/mentorship offer around a personal brand, using a structured niche-finding and offer-formula process

Digital & subscription products · Very low (a phone/camera for content, an all-in-one platform for hosting courses/coaching/payments)

Originally published Aug 29, 2026 — Maddox Goforth

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Maddox Goforth · How To Make F*ck You Money With Digital Products ↗

What It Is

Find a sellable niche using a structured self-reflection exercise (what you love, what you're good at, what you can be paid for, what the market needs), then package it using an offer formula: 'I help [target audience] go from [current problem] to [desired outcome]'; price as high-ticket ($2,000-5,000) rather than low-ticket, since fewer sales are needed from a smaller audience; build a personal brand across five steps (message, target audience, profile optimization, content, scaling), posting value/proof/authentic content twice daily to attract inbound leads; once revenue supports it, hire an editor, then a DM-setter and sales-call closer.

The Pitch

The presenter, who cites 90,000 Instagram followers, 14,000 YouTube subscribers, and 277,000 TikTok followers, says he's generated over $200,000 through personal branding, structuring the video as a fairly complete playbook (niche exercise, offer formula, content strategy, hiring roadmap) directing viewers to a free questionnaire and ultimately his own paid mentorship application.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to consistently post content (twice daily is the stated benchmark) for several months, comfortable positioning themselves as a coach/expert in a specific niche.

The Truth (as we see it)

The high-ticket-coaching-via-personal-brand model is a widely-used, real business structure, and the specific frameworks given (the offer formula, the niche-finding exercise, low-vs-high-ticket math) are genuinely usable tools rather than vague motivation. The $200,000 lifetime figure is self-reported and not broken down by year or source, and the video funnels toward his own paid mentorship application.
