Using a Self-published Book Funnel (not Just Amazon) as a High-ticket Coaching Client Acquisition Engine

Marketing & content · Low (self-publishing costs plus a funnel/email tool; ad spend optional for driving traffic to the funnel)

Originally published Sep 12, 2026 — Brian Ellwood

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Brian Ellwood · Three Reasons Your Book Will Never Make Money (plus the fix) ↗

What It Is

Write and self-publish a short book positioned to solve one specific problem for your target coaching/consulting audience, sell it through your own checkout funnel (not just Amazon) so you capture the buyer's contact info, follow up with an automated email/video sequence that continues the book's 'transformational journey' and makes a clear offer with a deadline, converting a percentage of cheap book buyers into high-ticket coaching clients.

The Pitch

The presenter says he's made $2-3 million in high-ticket client revenue over nearly a decade using books as the entry point, currently 3-6 high-ticket clients/month, after his first self-published Amazon-only book made just $17. He names three specific failure points (poor book packaging, no 'transformational journey' inside the book, and selling only through Amazon which withholds buyer contact info) and credits learning this system directly from ClickFunnels founder Russell Brunson.

Best For

Existing or aspiring coaches/consultants/experts with a specific area of expertise, comfortable writing a short book and setting up a basic sales funnel with email automation.

The Truth

The core mechanics (own-funnel book sales for list-building, and structuring a book to drive a specific next action) are established direct-response marketing techniques already used across the coaching industry, and this database already includes similar book-as-lead-magnet entries from other producers. His own revenue figures are self-reported across nearly a decade and multiple niches, not from a single book.
