GoHighLevel/Merchant Review-automation Foot-in-the-door With Fast Upsell and Dynamic Pricing

Local & professional services · Low startup cost (a Merchant or similar Google Business Profile optimization tool subscription, roughly $23-99/month)

Source

Balint Adorjan (interviewed by ItsKeaton) · He Closes $1,500/mo SaaS Clients on the FIRST Cold Call (Beginner Friendly Method) ↗

What It Is

Cold-call local service businesses with a free "database reactivation" review-request blast (texting a business's past customers asking for a Google review) as a foot-in-the-door offer, deliver it fast (all requests sent within about two weeks instead of trickling slowly), then upsell the client onto an ongoing Google Business Profile management tool (the presenter uses Merchant, built so the business owner can approve photos/updates entirely from their phone) once they see early results, converting a free trial into a base $97/month product with no fixed price beyond that — pricing the setup and monthly fee dynamically based on cues from the conversation rather than quoting a set rate.

The Pitch

The presenter built this after moving away from a similar tool (Yext) whose reputation soured in the GoHighLevel community, discovering that clients who saw fast review-blast results (some going from zero to steady inbound calls within days) began asking "what else do you do?" unprompted, producing what he describes as a 100% upsell rate on everyone who completed the trial. Once experienced, he skips the trial period entirely and charges $500-1,500 upfront on the very first call, adjusting the price based on how the prospect responds to value cues during the conversation (a plumber overwhelmed with new business, for instance, cares less about the price than the outcome) rather than quoting one fixed number to everyone.

Best For

Someone comfortable with cold calling and reading a prospect's reactions in real time to set price dynamically, willing to front-load fast, visible results (an aggressive initial review push) before asking for a larger commitment.

The Truth

The core mechanic (a low-cost, fast-results free trial that upsells itself once the client sees value) is a sound, well-established sales sequence, and the presenter's own numbers (100% upsell rate on completed trials) are self-reported from his personal business, not independently audited. This uses the same category of Google Business Profile tool as another entry in this database with a much lower-tech, relationship-first sales approach — the distinguishing element here is the aggressive fast-trial-to-upsell sequence and dynamic, conversation-driven pricing rather than a fixed rate card.
