Christian Peverelli · How to Build a $10,000 Mobile App in 46 Minutes Using AI ↗

What It Is

Building a native iOS mobile app from scratch using a no-code AI app-building tool, describing the desired app in plain language and iterating through each build phase, then publishing it through Apple's App Store submission and TestFlight beta process.

The Pitch

The presenter walks through cloning the core concept of a well-known app as a demonstration case, feeding a chain of specific prompts one at a time into a no-code AI app-building tool: design and navigation first, then a key feature, then a backend database for data persistence. He also covers deploying a companion legal and landing page and the real mechanics of Apple's App Store submission process, including the developer fee, TestFlight beta testing, and the review wait time.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable directing an AI tool through an iterative build process and willing to navigate Apple's actual app-submission requirements, not just build a prototype and stop there.

The Truth (as we see it)

This video's tool recommendation is a paid sponsorship, which the presenter discloses directly, so the specific platform choice should be weighed with that in mind, though he does explain his reasoning, native hardware access like the camera, for picking it over alternatives. The example app is a demo and practice clone, not the presenter's own live revenue-generating business, so no income figures are given or implied for this specific build.
