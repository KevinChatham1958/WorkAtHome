What It Is

Build and sell ready-made AI automations for one specific business problem, for example a system that turns a company's blog posts into social media content, a lead-qualification flow that scores and routes sales leads, or a customer-service workflow for an e-commerce brand. These are built as custom GPTs, Make or n8n scenarios, Zapier workflows or API integrations, with clear setup documentation, and can be sold with an optional monthly maintenance fee.

The Pitch

Enfroy says most businesses know they should be using AI but don't know how, so the market is "pretty wide open" and you are selling the implementation, not the AI itself. He suggests prices from about $300 for a simple automation up to around $4,000 for a fully customized one, aimed at mid-sized businesses that wouldn't hire a development team but "wouldn't bat an eye" at $4,000 to $5,000. His example: 10 sales a month at $3,000 each is $30,000 a month. He names Claude and n8n as the main tools.

Who It’s Best For

People with some business or tech background who understand how a particular team works day to day and are comfortable building and testing automations and selling to businesses, for example on LinkedIn.

The Truth (as we see it)

Demand for AI implementation is real, but the $30,000-a-month figure is an illustration with no client results behind it. He describes it as a one-time build you then sell, but his own examples are specific to each business, so expect custom work and support, not a product you build once and resell. You also need real technical skill, and sales happen business to business. The video promotes Enfroy's free "AI launch kit" lead magnet, which feeds his own paid programs.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
