What It Is

Use AI to generate designs for shirts, mugs, and posters, then sell them through print-on-demand platforms with no inventory held.

The Pitch

Extremely low barrier to entry: no design skill and no upfront inventory investment required to get a product listed.

Best For

Someone testing the waters of e-commerce with essentially no money at risk, rather than expecting significant income from it.

The Truth

Worth knowing this one is rated at the bottom of the presenter's own list, he calls it brutally competitive with no real moat, closer to a gimmick than a serious business, despite how easy it is to start.
