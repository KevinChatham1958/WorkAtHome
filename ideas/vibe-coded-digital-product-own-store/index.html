AI vibe-coded digital products sold through your own branded online shop

What It Is

Research proven-selling digital templates and tools (using public sales-data tools to see exactly how many times a competing product has sold and for how much), "vibe code" a functional version using an AI coding platform like Hostinger Horizons, then sell it through your own branded .shop domain online store rather than a crowded third-party marketplace like Etsy.

The Pitch

The presenter shows real, specific examples to justify the approach: a finance-niche budget tracker template that's sold over 20,000 times for more than $400,000, and a life planner niched down specifically for neurodivergent people with ADHD that's sold over 22,000 times for more than $350,000, arguing that competitive niches (finance, health, productivity) are actually good signals of real demand as long as you niche the specific problem down far enough. She argues against selling on marketplaces like Etsy specifically because of listing and transaction fees, competition burying your listing, and the risk of account suspension, versus owning your own store with a .shop domain and full customer data control.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable using an AI coding tool with detailed, specific prompts (rather than vague requests) to build an actual functional product, not just a static file, and willing to set up their own branded storefront rather than relying on marketplace traffic.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific $400K and $350K product examples are shown as public sales data from other sellers, not the presenter's own results, and she doesn't demonstrate her own product's sales figures within the video. She recommends Horizons specifically because it integrates with her own store-building workflow and is, by her account, one of the least expensive AI coding platforms, worth noting as a product preference rather than a neutral recommendation.
