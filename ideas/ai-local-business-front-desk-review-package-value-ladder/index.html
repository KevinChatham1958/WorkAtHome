Local Business AI Package

AI front-desk and reviews package for local businesses, sold on a monthly value ladder

What It Is

You set up a bundle of AI and automation tools for local businesses such as landscapers, dentists, gyms and remodelers: a quick AI-built website, automatic AI replies to Google reviews, review-request campaigns, a chat bot trained on a ChatGPT-written FAQ document that books calls on the owner's calendar, voice AI that follows up with new leads, missed-call text-back, and "database reactivation" texts offering past customers a discount. Prospects are pulled from Google Maps with scraper tools such as Outscraper or Instant Data Scraper and approached with an outcome-based offer (for example, five new reviews in five days, or "I built you a new website, want to see it?"), then shown the work on a short video call. Pricing follows a ladder: about $150 a month for a basic site or missed-call text-back, $300-500 a month for the full AI suite, around $1,000 a month once ads or SEO are added, and roughly $2,000 a month as a full growth partner. The first month involves frequent check-ins so the owner gets used to the app, then monthly calls that double as upsell chances.

The Pitch

The guest presenter, an agency owner, says local businesses have thin margins, high labor costs and no time to learn AI, so they will pay someone to set it up. He says the tools make setup fast (a website in under an hour) and that wiring the business's forms and phone numbers into your system makes clients stay, so a client who pauses ads can be downgraded to a cheaper monthly plan instead of cancelling. He says he grew his agency from his mom's basement, runs it while traveling, and now makes over $100,000 a month across several businesses.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable selling to small-business owners on video calls who wants recurring monthly income and is willing to learn one marketing platform well.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a webinar from HighLevel, the company that sells the software the whole method runs on, and the guest is a HighLevel affiliate who promotes his own coaching community, bots and paid upgrades during it, so the tool choices are not neutral. The webinar appears to have been recorded in 2024 (it mentions free AI credits "if it's still 2024"), so prices, features and offers may have changed. The services themselves are real and widely sold, but the income figures and the $10,000 roofing example are the presenter's own unaudited claims, and the host himself says results vary and this takes hard work. The core of the job is sales: cold outreach, demo calls and ongoing client management, plus a monthly software bill you carry before you have clients. "Guarantee" offers such as five reviews in five days should be checked against Google's rules on soliciting reviews before you promise them.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
