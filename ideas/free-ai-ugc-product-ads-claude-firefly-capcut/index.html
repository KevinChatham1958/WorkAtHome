What It Is

You make short TikTok-style ads that look like a real person showing off a product, without filming anyone. In the demo, Claude (with a free "AI UGC" skill or prompt) takes an AliExpress essential-oil diffuser and suggests a price of $34.99 against a $7.54 cost, then writes three concepts, a voiceover script and scene-by-scene image and motion prompts. Adobe Firefly generates a consistent creator image and five 5-second clips, and CapCut stitches them together with text-to-speech voiceover and music. The finished ads promote products in your own dropshipping store, and the money comes from product sales.

The Pitch

The creator says the whole process can be done for free and that AI UGC lets sellers produce ad videos without hiring creators. For anyone with a small budget, he says Create UGC can make a similar video in about a minute for up to a dollar per video.

Who It’s Best For

Dropshipping or small e-commerce sellers who want cheap ad creative and are comfortable working through several AI tools.

The Truth (as we see it)

The workflow is real and free tools can produce usable short clips, but the demo shows typical flaws (odd hands, a strange ending clip) that needed trimming. To get past Firefly's free limits, the video creates accounts with throwaway email addresses, which likely breaks the tool's terms of service and could get accounts banned; waiting a day or paying is the safer route. Ads should also avoid false product claims, and the video's own AI flagged mismatched specs. The channel belongs to AutoDS, a paid dropshipping software company; the cheat sheet is given for comments, and Create UGC is promoted as the paid upgrade.
