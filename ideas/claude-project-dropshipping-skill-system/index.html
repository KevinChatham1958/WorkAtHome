AutoDS · I Used Claude AI to Make $5,439 in 7 Days Online ↗

What It Is

Load a set of six sequential custom "skills" into a Claude Project, find my niche, find my product, build my offer, open my store, improve my store, and market my product, so that typing a single word like "start" walks you through a structured, question-driven process from picking a niche based on your own interests and budget through to a built, branded e-commerce store.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates the niche-discovery step live: Claude asks what topics or jobs you already know, your starting budget (even $0 works, beyond the Claude subscription), how you'd prefer to make content, and what you want to avoid selling, then uses those answers to guide product research through an integrated winning-products database. He frames the appeal as structure: rather than facing the whole process at once, each skill hands off to the next in a fixed order.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who wants a fully guided, question-and-answer approach to starting a dropshipping store rather than researching each step (niche, product, store build, marketing) separately themselves.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter doesn't show his own specific winning product on camera, citing competitive reasons, so the $5,439-in-7-days headline figure from the video's title isn't demonstrated step-by-step within the video itself. The skill set and product database are distributed via a free cheat sheet that funnels into the presenter's broader AutoDS platform and paid product-sourcing tools, worth knowing since the workflow is built around that ecosystem.
