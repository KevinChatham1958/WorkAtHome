Shopify Email-Flow Audits

AI-assisted email-flow audits and monitoring for Shopify stores (Klaviyo)

What It Is

You offer online stores an audit of their automated emails: connect an AI agent to the store's Shopify and Klaviyo accounts, compare each email flow against actual sales, find missing flows such as abandoned-cart reminders, turn off weak ones and draft fixes. You then sell ongoing monitoring that alerts you when a flow's conversion rate drops. The creator suggests a $500 audit followed by about $500 a month.

The Pitch

The creator says around 70% of online carts are abandoned, so adding a single missing flow can noticeably lift a store's sales, and email agencies charge $1,000-5,000 a month, leaving room to undercut them with an agent doing the analysis.

Who It's Best For

Someone who understands e-commerce or email marketing basics and can explain results to store owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

Email-flow audits are a real, established service, and the pricing given is the creator's suggestion rather than results he reports. Clients have to grant access to their store and email accounts, so trust and data care matter. Finding stores to pay for it takes outreach. The creator offers free prompts through his community.
