AI-Researched Niche Ebooks

AI-researched ebook on a topic people already pay for, sold on Whop

What It Is

You use an AI agent to find a topic where people are already paying for guides, checking Reddit, X, search results and existing products, then have it write a sourced ebook and lay it out as a PDF with AI-generated images (he uses Higgsfield). His example is a 48-page container-gardening guide called "Grow Food in Pots," built after he found a similar $9.99 PDF selling on Gumroad. He lists it on a Whop store for $15. He says the build took 19 minutes and about $3 in credits, and the agent re-checks every source so facts point to real pages.

The Pitch

The creator says the trick is going into "a market that already has demand" and building "a better mousetrap" rather than guessing at topics. He presents it as a near-free, fast way to have a sellable product the same day.

Who It’s Best For

Suits people who want a low-cost first digital product and are willing to proofread and fact-check AI output before selling it.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling short, practical PDF guides is a real and common work-at-home model, and checking for existing paid demand first is sensible. The video shows the ebook being built but no sales, so whether it earns anything is unproven; getting buyers to a new Whop store with no audience is the hard part and is barely covered. AI-written guides can still contain errors even with source links, so you need to read the whole thing yourself, especially on health or food topics. He gives the prompts away in his free Skool group but uses the video to sell his paid "AI Systems Society" membership (with a money-back guarantee offer), and the whole video is framed as a cheaper alternative to Iman Gadzhi's $1,995 program.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
