Etsy Print-on-demand Business Using AI-generated Designs

Physical & print products · Very low startup cost (no inventory, pay only after a sale)

Originally published Aug 15, 2026 — Wisdom Speaks

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Wisdom Speaks — https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-Zmcd_ueGQ

What It Is

Sell print-on-demand products (t-shirts, mugs, posters) on Etsy by picking an evergreen, versatile product category, researching underserved niches and keywords using a tool like Alura to check search volume against competitor count, generating designs with an AI image tool like Midjourney, mocking them up in Canva, and connecting a print-on-demand supplier (the presenter uses Inkjoy) that only prints and ships once a customer actually buys.

The Pitch

The presenter, who's run print-on-demand since 2022, distills product selection into three criteria: evergreen (proven sellers like t-shirts and mugs that won't die out with a trend), profitable enough to be worth it ($8-10 per sale at a 20-30% margin is the target he uses), and versatile enough to carry designs across many different niches. He walks through a real example using Alura to check a competitor's fishing-t-shirt niche, finding it doing $3,000-4,000 in sales after 6-8 months with a manageable few thousand competing listings, and treats a competitor count under roughly 10,000 as a good opportunity signal.

Best For

Someone willing to do real upfront research (checking search volume and competition before committing to a niche) rather than guessing at designs, and comfortable using AI tools for the actual artwork even without traditional design skills.

The Truth

The presenter doesn't reveal his own specific niche, keywords, or exact numbers, citing competitive reasons, so the profitability framework and the fishing-niche example are demonstrations of the research method rather than his own personal, verified results. The specific tools recommended (Alura, Midjourney, Inkjoy) each cost money, roughly $10/month for the AI image generator plus Alura's own subscription, worth factoring into the very-low-cost framing.
