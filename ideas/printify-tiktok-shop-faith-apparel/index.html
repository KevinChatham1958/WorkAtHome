Print-on-demand Apparel Via Printify and TikTok Shop

Physical & print products · Very low startup cost

Originally published Aug 22, 2026 — Anji Martin

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Anji Martin · Watch the video ↗

What It Is

Use a free TikTok Shop account together with a free Printify account to sell print-on-demand apparel with zero inventory held. Research TikTok Shop listings that have sold around 1,000 units with only 50-100 reviews (a signal of real, still-fresh demand), use an AI t-shirt design tool (KD) to generate an original design inspired by, not copied from, what's already selling, then upload it to Printify, link it to your TikTok Shop, and publish. Printify prints, packs, and ships every order hands-off once a sale comes in.

The Pitch

The presenter points to one specific existing shirt listing that has sold 6,800 units at $14.55 each, roughly $100,000 in revenue for that single design, as proof the model works, and walks through building and publishing a new original listing in under 10 minutes for zero upfront cost. You keep the difference between Printify's wholesale cost and the TikTok Shop retail price you set.

Best For

Someone comfortable with basic on-platform product research (checking sales counts and review counts on existing listings) who wants a genuinely zero-inventory way to test a print-on-demand apparel idea.

The Truth

The $100,000/6,800-unit figure belongs to one specific, already-established listing being used as market validation, not a promise that a new design will replicate those numbers. The video frames this specifically as a way to sell faith-based apparel, but the underlying mechanic, validate an already-selling niche, create an original alternative design, and list it through Printify and TikTok Shop, applies to any product niche, not just faith-based designs.
