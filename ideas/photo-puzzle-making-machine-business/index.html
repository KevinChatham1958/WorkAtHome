What It Is

Convert customer-submitted photos into jigsaw puzzles by sublimation printing onto puzzle blanks or printing onto board and die/laser-cutting, offered in sizes from quick 48-piece gifts to 500-piece challenges, delivered as a boxed puzzle featuring the customer's own image (family, wedding, pet, vacation photos).

The Pitch

Sell through Etsy, a personal site, and gift shops, with seasonal bundles (holiday photos, baby's-first-year sets) to boost volume and corporate clients using branded puzzles for events — the presenter frames the combination of personalization, play, and keepsake value as support for premium pricing and repeat gifting.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with print production equipment (sublimation or laser cutting) and interested in a seasonal, gift-occasion-driven sales pattern.

The Truth (as we see it)

Photo puzzles are an established, proven personalized-gift category with genuine repeat-purchase potential around recurring occasions (holidays, weddings, births). Print/cut equipment represents a real upfront cost compared to some other items on this list, and quality consistency (image resolution, cut precision) directly affects both customer satisfaction and repeat business.
