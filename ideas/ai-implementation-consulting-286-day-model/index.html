Offering Weekly 'done-with-you' AI Implementation Sessions to Local Businesses on a $1,000/month Retainer

What It Is

Sell local business owners a recurring $1,000/month 'done-with-you' service where you sit down with them once a week and automate one specific task at a time using AI, starting with your first client from your existing phone contacts for free or cheap to build a testimonial, then acquiring further clients primarily by walking into local businesses in person (using an AI-generated prompt to build a targeted list of small, owner-operated, non-franchise local businesses in your city) rather than relying only on cold email or calls.

The Pitch

The presenter claims to be personally making $50,000/month within a few weeks of starting this model at scale, and breaks down beginner-level math showing that landing two clients a week at $1,000/month each nets $286/day for roughly 1-2 hours of fulfillment work per client, arguing you only need to 'stay one chapter ahead' of a typical business owner's AI knowledge.

Best For

Someone comfortable approaching business owners in person and willing to learn AI workflow implementation just ahead of each client's specific need.

The Truth

The core mechanics (warm-network first client, in-person local outreach, a low-commitment weekly retainer) are sound, low-risk ways to start a service business with minimal credibility. The presenter's own $50,000/month figure is self-reported and from a business with 1.3 million followers and an existing 30-person team, so his personal results reflect pre-existing infrastructure a true beginner won't have; the video is also explicitly a lead-in to a deeper paid podcast/course.
