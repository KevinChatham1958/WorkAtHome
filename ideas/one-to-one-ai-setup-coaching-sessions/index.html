One-to-One AI Coaching

One-to-one AI setup coaching sessions sold to busy people in your network

What It Is

You sit down with one person, ask about their work, and show them how to install, set up and personalize tools such as ChatGPT or Claude so they save time. Sessions are priced at roughly $100 to $500 each. She suggests finding the first clients by texting about 20 people you know who are drowning in busy work, offering to save them five to 10 hours a week, and posting on LinkedIn and other social media that you are now helping people use AI.

The Pitch

The creator ranks this the easiest way for beginners to make money with AI because people who binge AI content are already far ahead of most users, who still struggle to get useful answers from ChatGPT. She says you need no audience or website and could earn your first money within a week. She notes plainly that it trades time for money.

Who It’s Best For

Patient explainers who are comfortable reaching out to people they know and are a step ahead of their friends and colleagues on everyday AI tools.

The Truth (as we see it)

Low-cost, quick-to-start help with AI tools is a believable offer, and the warm-network approach is realistic for first clients. The $100 to $500 price range is her estimate, and repeat business may be limited once a client is set up. Income is capped by your hours, as she says. The video closes by recommending Blotato, a social media posting tool, so part of it works as promotion for that product.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
