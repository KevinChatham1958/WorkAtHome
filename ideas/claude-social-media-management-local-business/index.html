What It Is

Offer traditional social media management (captions, carousels, scheduling, light community management) to local businesses like salons, cafes, and fitness studios, but compress the traditionally 15-20 hour-per-week workload by feeding Claude a business's branding, voice, ideal customer profile, and goals to batch-write a month's worth of captions and content in a single sitting, then use templates, proven post frameworks per business type, or simple AI-built tools to produce visuals faster than building each post manually in Canva.

The Pitch

The presenter frames the old version of this service as brutal (a $500/month client eating 15-20 hours/week, leading most people to quit within months) and argues Claude changes the math by letting one person batch-write 30 days of on-brand captions and content in a single weekend session, then sell it three ways: teaching the client Claude-based content skills, a one-time content/template pack, or an ongoing monthly retainer managing everything. Client acquisition is via direct outreach (DM or email to ~10 local businesses in a chosen niche) with a sample post or Loom demo attached.

Who It’s Best For

Someone who already has a feel for social media content and copywriting (the presenter is explicit this isn't fully hands-off) and is comfortable both using Claude as a production tool and doing direct outreach to land clients rather than relying on inbound leads.

The Truth (as we see it)

The efficiency gain from batching AI-generated content is real and consistent with how many creators and agencies already use AI tools, and the presenter is upfront that this isn't easy and still requires genuine writing/social media skill to make the output sound human rather than generic. This is a crowded, well-established service category, so differentiation and client acquisition — not the content production itself — remain the harder part.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
