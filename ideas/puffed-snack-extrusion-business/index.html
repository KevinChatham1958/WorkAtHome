Puffed Snack Extrusion Business Starting From One Flavor and One Buyer

What It Is

Producing puffed corn, rice, wheat, or millet snacks using a small grain extruder, starting with one flavor, one shape, and one local buyer before treating capacity as a problem to solve.

The Pitch

5oz puffed corn snacks commonly retail around $2.60-3.90; selling 100 bags at $3 is about $300 in gross sales before grain, seasoning, oil, labor, retailer margin, and waste. The extruder itself is cheap relative to its output — at a rated 150kg/hour it could theoretically produce over 1,000 5oz bags in a single hour, which the presenter frames as proof that demand, not equipment, is the real constraint. Different dies let one machine produce multiple shapes.

Best For

Someone starting narrow (one flavor, one shape, one confirmed local buyer) rather than scaling production before demand is proven.

The Truth

Like several machines in this category, the extruder's rated output vastly outpaces what a new producer can realistically sell — the presenter's repeated point across this video is that capacity is not the same thing as demand.
