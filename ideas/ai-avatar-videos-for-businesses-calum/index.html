What It Is

You learn Google's AI tools, including image editing, video generation and digital avatars made from a scan of a face, and use them to make videos and images for businesses that don't know how. Typical jobs are short promo videos, product images and avatar explainer clips. It's done entirely on a computer.

The Pitch

Guest Paul Lipkin says most people focus on ChatGPT or Claude and overlook what Google offers. His advice is to just learn these tools and then bring them to businesses who don't know how to use them, which he calls low-hanging fruit.

Who It’s Best For

Curious people who enjoy trying new creative tools and can explain the results to business owners.

The Truth (as we see it)

No prices, client numbers or earnings are shown; the 'low-hanging fruit' claim is his opinion. These tools change fast and are getting easier for businesses to use themselves, so your edge may shrink. Some viewers and businesses dislike AI avatars, so check that a client is comfortable before using one.
