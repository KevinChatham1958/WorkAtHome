Your Average Tech Bro · The SOLO Dev's Guide to Building a Profitable SaaS While Working 9-5 ↗

What It Is

A roadmap specifically aimed at software engineers who already have a day job: build small consumer or B2B apps on the side, expect the first several attempts to make little or no money, and treat idea generation, building, and marketing as three separate skills to develop in sequence rather than all at once.

The Pitch

The presenter, who built 14 different apps over five to six years before one made meaningful money (his current app, a meeting-recorder tool, makes roughly $7,000 to $8,000 a month), is candid that most early attempts will underperform and that this is expected, not a sign to quit. His specific tactical advice for finding ideas: deliberately rebuild an app you already personally use and pay for with a small twist, since copying a validated idea is a legitimate way to make your first few hundred dollars, and treat any niche with existing competitors as a green flag (proof of real paying demand) rather than a reason to avoid it.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with real software engineering skills who's willing to accept that the first several apps they build probably won't make significant money, and who's building specifically around evenings and weekends rather than trying to go all-in immediately.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is unusually transparent about his own numbers, showing live revenue dashboards for a past app that peaked around $1,900 a month in revenue and now sits at $650 a month in largely untouched, ongoing revenue, real figures rather than vague claims. He notes he quit his own full-time job in October 2025 to go all-in, so his current framing is shaped by someone recently past the exact stage this video is aimed at, not someone speaking from years of hindsight.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
