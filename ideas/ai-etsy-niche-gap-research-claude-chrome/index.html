Etsy Niche Gaps With Claude

AI-driven Etsy niche-gap research using Claude for Chrome

Teanna Scott · Etsy Print on Demand Tutorial: How I Used Claude AI to Find a Niche Nobody's Touched (FREE PROMPT) ↗

What It Is

Using the Claude for Chrome browser extension to automatically research an Etsy niche, crawling listings, cross-referencing Google search demand, mining customer reviews for complaints, and identifying design or product gaps competitors haven't filled, then generating AI image-design prompts based on that research.

The Pitch

The presenter, a 4-year Etsy print-on-demand seller, argues most sellers simply copy whatever's already trending, which puts you permanently a step behind. Instead, a detailed two-part Claude for Chrome prompt has Claude autonomously browse Etsy and cross-reference Google search demand to find topics people are searching for that don't yet have good Etsy listings, mine reviews on existing top sellers for complaints and unmet requests, score the resulting opportunities, then generate several ready-to-use image-generation prompts for the best opportunity, which she runs through an AI design tool and fulfills via Printify.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with some existing Etsy print-on-demand experience who wants a more systematic way to find genuine market gaps, comfortable directing an AI browser agent through a fairly detailed, customized prompt.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a first-time-trying-it demonstration rather than a proven, repeated result; the presenter is candid this is a hack she's actively still exploring and plans to refine further in a future video, so there's no track record yet showing the specific product found in this video actually sold well. The underlying research method, cross-referencing marketplace trends against real search demand and review complaints, is a sound, verifiable research practice independent of whether this specific example becomes a bestseller.
