Window Screen Rebuilding

Window screen rebuilding service for property managers and landlords

What It Is

Rebuild torn or damaged window screens, selling primarily to property managers and landlords who accumulate a steady stream of damaged screens across multiple rental units at every tenant turnover, rather than marketing to individual homeowners.

The Pitch

A 100-ft roll of fiberglass mesh (about $210) covers roughly 40-50 screens, a spline roll is under $18, and the rolling tool is about $10 — a full setup for around $240 with per-screen material cost of $10-25. The national average charged per screen installed is around $322, with the low end starting at $147 and labor billing $50-80/hour. The presenter frames one apartment complex as a standing account, since every unit turnover generates more damaged screens.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable pitching directly to property managers and landlords rather than one-off homeowners, willing to build a recurring B2B account rather than relying on individual repeat customers.

The Truth (as we see it)

The economics check out and the barrier to entry is genuinely low, but the business depends entirely on landing property-management accounts rather than individual jobs — that's a sales and relationship-building skill, not just a repair skill, and isn't really addressed in the pitch beyond identifying the target customer.
