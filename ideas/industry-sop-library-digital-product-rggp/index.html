SOP Library by Industry

What It Is

You create a ready-made library of written procedures for one industry, such as agencies, gyms, home service companies, dental offices or restaurants. It can cover onboarding, customer support, sales, hiring, employee checklists, meeting structures, quality control and KPIs. Claude drafts the procedures from a detailed prompt, and you review and improve them before selling the library as a download.

The Pitch

The creator says thousands of small companies want to run more smoothly but do not know which processes should exist, so a done-for-you library gives them a starting point.

Who It's Best For

People with operations or management experience in a particular industry.

The Truth (as we see it)

Tanner Chidester sells coaching for business owners through links in the description, and the $26,000-a-day figure is a hypothetical funnel calculation, not results from this product. Generic AI-written SOPs are easy to produce, so the product's value depends on your real industry knowledge. Businesses usually need procedures adapted to their own tools and staff, which can lead buyers to expect custom help. No sales data is shown.
