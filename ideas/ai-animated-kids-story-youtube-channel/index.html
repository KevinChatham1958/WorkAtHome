AI Children's Story Videos

Creating AI-animated children's story videos for YouTube, with a structured story framework to avoid looking like mass-produced content

Content & personal brand · Low (an OpenArt subscription, with a free tier to experiment before committing to a paid plan)

Originally published Aug 12, 2026 — Joshua Mayo

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Joshua Mayo · How to Create AI Animated Stories That Make Money on YouTube in 2026 (Beginner Tutorial) ↗

What It Is

Build AI-animated children's story videos using a tool (OpenArt) that generates a consistent character across scenes and a 'Director' feature that turns a plain-language story description into a full animated video (visuals, narration, music) in one generation; structure every story around a simple three-part framework (a relatable problem, a turning point, an earned resolution) rather than generic prompted content, since YouTube has been actively removing channels that look mass-produced/templated; optionally blend in real stock footage alongside AI-generated scenes as a 'hybrid' style that reads as more clearly human-directed and lower-risk for removal.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates the entire build live and explains the monetization path: YouTube AdSense as the baseline (kids content has lower ad rates but high repeat-watch volume offsets this), then repurposing a successful character/story into an AI-illustrated children's book sold on Amazon, citing Super Simple, a real channel that grew into its own paid ad-free subscription app plus licensing deals with platforms like Amazon Prime Video.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to build an actual story rather than mass-generate templated content, and patient with a real production process rather than expecting instant volume.

The Truth (as we see it)

AI-animated kids content, the removal risk for templated/mass-produced channels, and the described monetization progression are all real and consistent with how YouTube's kids-content ecosystem works, and the Super Simple example is a real, verifiable case of this progression at scale. No personal revenue figures for the presenter's own channel are given; the video promotes his own affiliate link to the tool used throughout.
