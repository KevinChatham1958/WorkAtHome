Free Website, Paid AI Retainer

Free-website-as-a-gift client acquisition, upsold to a $297/month AI lead-system retainer

Startup cost: Low startup cost (HighLevel subscription ~$297/month unlimited-client plan, covered once first client signs; AI Studio site builder included)

Source video

Adam Erhart · I Give Local Businesses a Free Website. Then They Pay Me $297/Mo ↗

What It Is

Post a free-website offer in local Facebook community groups (or find businesses lacking a website via Google Maps or a prospecting tool), build a real, AI-generated site (HighLevel's AI Studio) in minutes on a temporary preview link, hand it over framed as a completed gift, then upsell the business owner to a $297/month retainer that keeps the site live and adds lead-capture automations (text-back, review requests, appointment booking).

The Pitch

The presenter argues the real barrier to landing local-business clients isn't skill but the price conversation up front, which gets cold outreach ignored; building the site first and framing it as an already-finished gift removes that friction, and a real deadline (a preview link that comes down in 7 days) creates urgency without hard selling. He provides five copy-paste scripts (the group post, delivery message, close, and outreach messages) and packages the whole HighLevel setup as a free bonus he calls Agency OS.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable posting in and monitoring local Facebook groups or running simple Google Maps prospecting, willing to do a few minutes of unpaid AI-generated site-building per lead before any commitment from the business owner.

The Truth (as we see it)

The mechanics (build first, gift-frame, real time-limited preview, upsell to a hosting/automation retainer) are sound and match how successful local-service agencies commonly operate. The presenter's claimed track record and the ~13% no-website hit rate from his own 692-business test are self-reported, not independently verified, and the video is structured as a lead magnet for his own paid HighLevel affiliate link and Agency OS bonus, so treat the tool recommendation as a sales funnel rather than neutral advice.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
