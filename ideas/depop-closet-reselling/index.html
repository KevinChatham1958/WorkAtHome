What It Is

Sell your own used clothing and accessories (or sourced secondhand items) on Depop by photographing them, listing them in a personal storefront, and using periodic storewide discounts to move older inventory.

The Pitch

The presenter sold $1,000 on Depop in one week, describing a simple process: take clean photos, list items, and run periodic discounts (she runs 35% off storewide) to clear stale inventory. She also resells cheap bulk-find accessories (70 bags bought for about $10 total, listed individually for around $5 each) alongside her own closet items.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with a closet of clothes they no longer wear, or access to cheap secondhand/bulk items, who's comfortable with basic product photography and customer shipping.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a genuinely low-barrier way to turn existing possessions into cash, and the mechanics (photograph, list, discount stale stock) are straightforward. Income will scale with how much inventory you have and how much time you put into sourcing and listing, so it's more of a supplemental income stream than a scalable primary business without significant sourcing effort.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
