Guided Journal Printables

Guided transformation journals sold as printable PDFs (Selar or Amazon KDP)

What It Is

You design a guided journal that walks a specific reader through a change over 30 or 60 days, with a daily prompt rather than blank pages. The creator's example is a 30-day prayer journal for Christian women entrepreneurs with weekly scriptures, daily reflection prompts, gratitude and action spaces. You sell it as a downloadable PDF on Selar (a digital product platform popular in Africa) or another store, and buyers print it themselves; or you list it on Amazon KDP, which prints and ships each order.

The Pitch

The creator says not every customer needs to read to get a result; some need to reflect, and a well-prompted journal delivers that. She says the value is in the daily prompts, so a focused journal for a clear audience is a beginner-friendly product you create once and sell repeatedly.

Who It’s Best For

People who enjoy writing reflective prompts and have a clear audience in mind, such as a faith, wellness or business group.

The Truth (as we see it)

Journals and planners are a crowded product type, and the creator's own point, that the prompts and the target audience matter more than the cover, is the honest key to standing out. She shows her own example but gives no sales figures, so how well it sells is unknown. Selling still takes marketing work to get the product in front of buyers. The creator is promoting a waitlist for her own paid annual membership, the Digital Gold Mine Club, which teaches these formats.
