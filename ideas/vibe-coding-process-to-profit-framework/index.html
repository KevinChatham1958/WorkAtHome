Same-Day AI Tools

Turning a repeated manual process into a same-day AI tool

What It Is

A framework for deciding what to actually build with AI coding tools like Claude or Lovable: rather than picking a business idea you find personally interesting, identify a specific process people already do manually and repeatedly (writing a press release, humanizing AI text, filling out a form), then build the smallest possible tool, ideally one input, one output, one screen, that automates just one step of that process, validated before building against three signals: real search demand, evidence people already pay to solve it, and public complaints about existing solutions.

The Pitch

The core discipline demonstrated live in the video is speed and restraint: rather than planning for weeks, the presenter picks an idea, validates it against the three-signal checklist in minutes using a keyword tool, then builds a working (if visually rough) prototype in under an hour using Claude and Lovable, deliberately resisting the urge to add more features. The specific example built on camera, an AI-writing humanizer tool, went from concept to a downloadable desktop app within the session, illustrating the "ugly and working" first pass discipline over polishing something that never ships.

Best For

Someone comfortable directing AI coding tools through iterative, specific feedback (rather than one perfect prompt) who wants a repeatable method for deciding what's worth building, rather than a single business idea to copy.

The Truth

The specific numeric criteria for validation (does it show up in search, is someone already paying for it, are people complaining about current options) are genuinely concrete and checkable, a real strength of this framework over vaguer "find a good idea" advice. The video is a long promotional session for the presenter's own paid workshop and existing tool ecosystem (Cliptor, AI Profit Scoop Elite, and others), a financial incentive worth knowing, though the underlying validate-then-build-small methodology doesn't require any of those specific products to apply.
