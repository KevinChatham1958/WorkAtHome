ClickBank via Pinterest Pins

Free-traffic ClickBank affiliate marketing via bulk Pinterest pins

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (free tools throughout, per the presenter)

Originally published Jul 19, 2026 — Real Money Strategies

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Real Money Strategies · How to Make Money With Clickbank Starting With $0 (Full Tutorial) ↗

What It Is

Promoting existing ClickBank affiliate offers using entirely free tools and free traffic, by bulk-generating dozens of Pinterest pins with Claude and Canva, linking each pin to an AI-built bridge page rather than the affiliate link directly.

The Pitch

The presenter demonstrates the full free workflow live: pick a proven, high-converting ClickBank offer, verify real demand using free keyword and ad-audience data, use a pre-made Claude prompt to bulk-generate pin copy and matching AI-image prompts, use Canva's bulk-creation tool to mass-produce dozens of pins from that data, generate the pin images for free in Google Gemini, and build a simple branded bridge page using another Claude prompt, hosted for free via Netlify, rather than linking to the affiliate offer directly, since Pinterest has removed some pins that link straight to an affiliate URL.

Who It's Best For

Someone willing to do a real, if largely free, technical setup and comfortable with a sales cycle where commissions can take days or weeks to land, since much of the buying happens through the vendor's own follow-up email sequence.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter discloses on-pin that AI-generated images are used, matching Pinterest's own content-labeling requirements, a good transparency signal. The specific conversion-rate and demand figures cited are pulled from the vendor's own affiliate marketing materials and third-party ad-audience tools, not independently verified by the presenter. No personal income figures are given for this specific method; the video demonstrates mechanics rather than reporting the presenter's own results.
