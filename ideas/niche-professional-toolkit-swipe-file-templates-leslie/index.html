Professional Template Toolkits

Niche toolkit or template pack that saves a working professional time

What It Is

You package templates, scripts and checklists that remove a task a specific professional already knows they have to do. The creator's examples include a sponsorship pitch kit for small podcasters, a digital product launch checklist for first-time coaches, a client onboarding toolkit for virtual assistants, a podcast guest outreach swipe file with pitches and follow-ups, difficult-client boundary email templates for freelancers, a portfolio template for freelance designers and a brand photo shot list for coaches. You sell them as digital downloads.

The Pitch

The creator says templates work when they remove a job the buyer already must do, so you're 'selling back an hour, a Saturday, or five stressful decisions' rather than convincing anyone they want another download. She names a niche toolkit for working professionals, like the VA onboarding kit, as one of the three types she would test first.

Who It's Best For

People with hands-on experience in a profession who can turn their own working documents into polished, reusable templates.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling niche templates is a real, low-cost model, and the best-sellers usually come from people who have actually done the job. Template marketplaces are crowded, so the specificity the creator recommends matters, and you still need a way to reach buyers. The creator suggests using ChatGPT or Claude to draft products and then adding your own experience; AI-only templates tend to be generic. The creator is a co-founder of MiloTree, a platform for selling digital products, and the video promotes its free AI product finder and building inside MiloTree, so this is partly a product pitch.
