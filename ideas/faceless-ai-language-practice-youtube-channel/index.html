What It Is

You run a YouTube channel of short videos in which two AI-generated characters hold everyday conversations in the language you want to teach, with the words appearing on screen as they're spoken so learners can follow along. Claude or ChatGPT writes the dialogue and translations, an AI image or video tool creates the characters, and a voice tool such as ElevenLabs or Microsoft Clipchamp, which is free, generates the speech and assembles the video. The channel earns from YouTube ad revenue once it qualifies for the Partner Program, and from selling digital products linked in the description, such as language guides, flashcards or courses.

The Pitch

The creator shows an example channel launched a few months earlier that she says gets tens of thousands of views per video, some over a million. She says it can be started for free and that pairing ad revenue with your own digital products lets you earn before the channel is monetized.

Who It’s Best For

People interested in language learning, ideally bilingual, who are comfortable using AI tools and posting consistently without showing their face.

The Truth (as we see it)

Conversation-practice videos are a real and popular format, and the workflow described is simple. Earnings shown for the example channel are estimates, not confirmed figures. YouTube's rules on repetitive or mass-produced content can block monetization of low-effort AI channels, and if you don't speak the language yourself, translation errors can hurt credibility. The creator offers a free checklist and promotes her paid community, and the video is sponsored by an AI video-creation tool.
