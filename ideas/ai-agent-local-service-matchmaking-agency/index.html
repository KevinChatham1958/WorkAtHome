AI-Agent Matchmaking Agency

Local service matchmaking agency run by an AI agent that mines Facebook groups

  • Services from home
  • Near-zero cost

Video published Oct 19, 2026 by Greg Isenberg

Share on Facebook Link copied

Source video

Greg Isenberg · Meta Muse Will Create 1,000 Millionaires ↗

What It Is

You pick one service and one city (night nurses in Montreal, nannies, cleaners, plumbers) and have a personal AI agent such as Meta's Muse search public Facebook groups, Marketplace and Instagram for providers, rank them by quality signals and build a clean list. When a family or business needs someone, you match them and charge a success fee; the agent can also draft messages and, with a phone add-on, call and text.

The Pitch

The creator says agencies already charge thousands to find providers by trawling Facebook groups by hand, and an agent cuts that cost so much you can charge less and still earn big margins. He argues the cleaned list becomes an asset you own, and that the business can grow into your own group, niche Instagram page and eventually software.

Who It’s Best For

Someone organized and good at judging people who wants to run a referral service from home in a specific local niche.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is the creator's idea and a live demo, not a proven business; he didn't run one. Matching people for childcare or home care carries real responsibility: you'll need proper vetting, background checks and possibly agency licensing where you operate. Scraping groups and messaging members at scale can break platform rules. The episode is sponsored by Brex.
