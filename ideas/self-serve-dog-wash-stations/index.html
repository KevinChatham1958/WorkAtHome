Self-Serve Dog Wash

Operating self-serve dog wash stations that customers pay to use themselves

Local & professional services · Moderate (cost of a self-serve wash unit)

Originally published Jul 23, 2026 — Jack Harrison Explains

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

One of 5 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Install a self-serve dog wash unit (like a Tubby-style machine) in a decent retail location where customers pay $15-25 to wash their own dog rather than dealing with the mess of washing at home; the machine handles the transaction itself, with the operator visiting weekly to restock soap and collect revenue.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a per-wash cost to the owner of about $1.50 in water/electricity/shampoo against a $15-25 customer price, and says a well-placed unit washing 15-30 dogs/day can generate roughly $110,000-220,000/year in revenue.

Best For

Someone able to secure a good retail location and willing to do weekly restocking/collection visits.

The Truth

Self-serve dog wash units are a real, established small-business category with genuinely strong per-transaction margins given the low marginal cost. Revenue figures are general framing rather than a single verified case study.
