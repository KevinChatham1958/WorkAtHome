YouTube Amazon Affiliate Reviews

Amazon Associates affiliate income via YouTube review videos

Marketing & content · Free to join, no ongoing fees

Originally published Aug 2, 2026 — Greg Gottfried

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Greg Gottfried · How To Make Money On Amazon (For Beginners) ↗

One of 4 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Join the Amazon Associates program to get a unique tracked link for any Amazon product, then create YouTube videos (talking about or demonstrating products, without necessarily appearing on camera) with those affiliate links in the description, earning a commission on anything a viewer buys on Amazon within 24 hours of clicking through, not just the specific product featured.

The Pitch

Gottfried's single best piece of advice is to make videos about topics people are actively Googling or searching for on YouTube, since that content earns organic views without needing an existing subscriber base or following, and he notes that a large share of commissions actually come from "secondary sales," other items a visitor adds to their cart beyond the one they originally clicked through for.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable creating video content (camera-on or camera-off, talking about products they already know or own) but who doesn't want to build a large following first, since Gottfried's approach relies on search-driven discovery rather than an audience.

The Truth (as we see it)

Gottfried discloses that videos have worked "head and shoulders above everything else" for him personally compared to photos or social posts, a stated preference based on his own experience rather than a universal claim. The 24-hour attribution window and secondary-sales mechanic are concrete, verifiable facts about how the Amazon Associates program actually works, not marketing claims specific to his own results.
