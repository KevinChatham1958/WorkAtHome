AI-Built Affiliate Review Site

Affiliate review site built with an AI site builder: publish first, add affiliate links once traffic shows up

Adam Enfroy · Affiliate Marketing Tutorial for Beginners 2026 (Step By Step with AI) ↗

What It Is

You set up a WordPress review site in a hobby niche (his demo is a backpacking gear blog) using Hostinger's AI builder. He says it generates the homepage, images and layout in under 10 minutes. You publish 10-20 'best X' comparison articles with plain links to the brands first. Once a post gets a trickle of traffic, around 50 visits a month, you apply to the programs and swap in affiliate links, cloaked with the Thirsty Affiliates plugin. Pinterest or YouTube sends extra traffic to the site.

The Pitch

Enfroy says AI has removed most of the old barriers: research, outlines and the site itself now take hours, not weeks. He argues against relying only on Google because AI Overviews may be taking around 40% of the clicks sites used to get. He sets out a reusable article template (keyword in the URL, H1 and H2s, one H3 per product) and suggests checking each draft against the top three Google results with ChatGPT or Gemini. He suggests starting with Amazon Associates as a catch-all. Over 6-12 months he says to stack display ads (Mediavine), an email list built on a free download like a backpacking gear checklist, and eventually a digital product.

Who It’s Best For

A complete beginner who wants a low-cost, step-by-step start, prefers writing to being on camera, and is happy in a hobby niche where small sites can still rank.

The Truth (as we see it)

The 'publish first, monetize once traffic shows up' sequence is sensible advice for beginners and spares you rejected affiliate applications. He openly says this is not get-rich-quick and that AI drafts need your own experience added. The video is built around a Hostinger walkthrough using his affiliate link (85% off claim), and it also promotes his free AI launch kit. The roughly 40% figure for clicks lost to AI Overviews is his estimate and isn't sourced.
