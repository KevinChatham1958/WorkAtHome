"Best Of" State Trade Directory

AI-built "best of" state directory for one trade, using award badges for backlinks and featured listings

What It Is

You build a directory website listing every real business in one trade across one state, such as a 'Best of Florida Pools 2026' list, using scraped and cleaned Google Maps data. You then email each listed business a 'you made the list' badge to embed on their site, which links back to your directory. The directory earns money from paid featured listings, routing its incoming calls to paying businesses, and as a door-opener for selling other services.

The Pitch

The presenter says businesses agree to post the badge because they now know search and AI-answer visibility (what he calls GEO) leans heavily on 'best of' lists, so the outreach email is essentially just the badge. Each embed is a backlink that raises the directory's domain rating. Revenue options he lists include featured placement at the top of city pages with more photos, routed leads, and using the directory as credibility when pitching. His suggested timeline: register domains and test niches in the first two weeks, scrape and enrich every business in the state by day 30, publish and send badge emails in month two. He stresses keeping it to one vertical and one region because hyperfocused directories work better than national ones.

Who It’s Best For

People who already run or plan local lead-gen sites and want an owned asset that builds links and relationships with local businesses.

The Truth (as we see it)

Badge-for-link outreach is a known SEO tactic and is more likely to get responses than a cold sales pitch, so the mechanic is credible. Directory revenue is usually small on its own; the presenter himself calls it 'small, steady, and very strategic' rather than a primary income. He also says his academy is still building out the directory module, so this part is less proven in his own system than his microsite material. Scraping business data and presenting it as a 'best of' award raises fairness and accuracy questions if no real vetting happens, and Google can discount links obtained this way. He promotes his paid SEO academy throughout.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
