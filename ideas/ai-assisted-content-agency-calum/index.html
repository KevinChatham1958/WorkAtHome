What It Is

You produce short-form video content for business owners and creators: researching topics in their niche, writing ideas and scripts, editing clips and posting them. AI agents do much of the topic research and idea generation, so you spend your time on picking the best ideas and on editing. Everything is done online.

The Pitch

The guest says his agency does $2-3 million a year with four employees by leaning on AI, and that AI now generates ideas better than he does. He also says he made $48,000 in one month from just 13,000 TikTok followers after leaving a job paying under $40,000.

Who It’s Best For

People who already understand what makes short videos work and can manage several clients' deadlines.

The Truth (as we see it)

His agency results come from a team, a custom internal tool and years in the business. A beginner starts with no clients and has to win them. The $48,000 month is a single standout result, not an ordinary one. He gives away an idea-generation skill in the episode, which also works as an advert for his agency.
