Video Editing Retainer via Hook Re-Edits

Landing a $1K-$3K/month video editing retainer by re-editing a prospect's first 30 seconds and sending it unasked on Skool

Standout Content · How I Would Land a $1K–$3K/Month Video Editing Client From Scratch ↗

What It Is

You offer ongoing video editing to coaches and online business owners. To find clients, you pay about $9.99 a month to open a basic Skool community, which gets you into the community-owners group where you can see which owners are active and earning. Each day you pick one owner whose talking-head videos have weak editing, download a recent video, re-edit just the first 30 seconds (cut pauses, fix audio, add motion graphics) and send it with a short "made this for you" message. Interested owners get a sales call, and the goal is a single client paying $1,000 to $3,000 a month, later using results to land bigger clients.

The Pitch

The creator says showing someone their own video improved works far better than a portfolio or a generic pitch, and that Skool messages get read more than Instagram DMs. He says he usually signs a $2,000-$4,000-a-month client within about a week, and now gets roughly $10,000-a-month clients by referral.

Who It’s Best For

A capable video editor who is willing to do daily unpaid sample work for a few weeks and then take sales calls.

The Truth (as we see it)

The "do the work first" approach is a well-known and often effective freelance tactic, and the advice on picking prospects and running a listening-first sales call is practical. The income figures are the creator's own and unverified. The method demands a lot of unpaid labour (he mentions spending up to 12 hours on a single 30-second edit), and many prospects will not reply. Downloading and re-editing someone's video without asking is common practice for samples, but keep it private to that person unless they agree to it being shared.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
