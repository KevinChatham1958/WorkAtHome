What It Is

You audit how you use AI, have an AI tool interview you about the topic and templates you want to share, and package what you know into something people download, such as a PDF guide, a set of prompts or a 'skill' markdown file for an AI assistant. You sell it from a link in your social media bio. Her example is 1,000 buyers at $100 bringing in $100,000 from one product.

The Pitch

The creator says you build it once and sell it for as long as the knowledge stays useful. She argues most people make the mistake of spending 90% of their time perfecting the product when they should spend it marketing and building an audience.

Who It’s Best For

People who already have, or are building, an audience interested in practical AI tips.

The Truth (as we see it)

Her own rating is low because it only works once you have an audience or a partner who has one, which is an honest caveat. The $100,000 figure is a hypothetical, not a result. AI know-how goes out of date quickly and free alternatives are everywhere, so products need regular updates and a clear edge. The video closes by recommending Blotato, a social media posting tool, so part of it works as promotion for that product.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
