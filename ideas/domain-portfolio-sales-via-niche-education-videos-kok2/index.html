Domain Portfolio via AI Videos

Selling a domain-name portfolio through AI niche-education videos

What It Is

You hold a portfolio of domain names (the creator says he has about 2,100) and sell them to people who want to build blogs or websites. To find buyers without selling each one manually, he makes short AI videos that present a niche as a money-making opportunity, using stats such as a domain's GoDaddy estimate, its existing Google rankings, and affiliate offers in that niche. The video ends with a call to action pointing to his sales site rather than leading with 'domain for sale', which he found works better on social media.

The Pitch

He argues that education-style content gets attention where a sales pitch would be skipped, and that one prompt can turn a domain's research notes into a polished video in about half an hour.

Who It’s Best For

People who already understand domain investing and niche websites and have capital to hold domains while they wait for buyers.

The Truth (as we see it)

Domain investing is a real but slow, speculative business; most domains never sell, and renewal fees continue every year. The figures in the videos (GoDaddy value estimates, 'websites in this niche reportedly make') are estimates, not proof a buyer will pay. The creator shows test videos but no domain sales from them, and he estimates that making videos for his whole portfolio would cost around $30,000 in credits, so he would start with five. He promotes his own paid resources.
