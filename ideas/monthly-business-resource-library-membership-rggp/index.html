What It Is

Instead of selling one product once, you run a paid membership that adds new resources every month, such as sales scripts, marketing campaigns, prompts, templates, industry research, content ideas and business tools. Claude helps you produce and organize the new material faster, and members keep paying because the library keeps growing.

The Pitch

The creator calls this one of his favorites because it turns a one-time product into recurring revenue, so you do not start from zero every month.

Who It's Best For

People who can commit to producing useful new material on a steady schedule for one audience.

The Truth (as we see it)

Tanner Chidester sells coaching for business owners through links in the description, and the $26,000-a-day figure is a hypothetical funnel calculation, not results from this product. Memberships live or die on retention: members cancel quickly if new content feels like filler, so the monthly workload is ongoing rather than 'lazy'. The video gives no numbers or examples of a running membership.
