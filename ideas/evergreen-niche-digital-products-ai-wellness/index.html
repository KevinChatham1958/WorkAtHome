Digital Products in Evergreen Niches: Wellness, Fitness Programs, Manifestation, and AI Tools or Courses

What It Is

Build and sell digital products (guides, programs, short courses, templates or simple tools) in one of the niches the presenter rates strongest right now: health and wellness, workout programs, law of attraction and manifestation, teaching others to sell digital products, and anything AI, such as a small library of simple AI tools bundled with PDF guides, or a video course of about six 20-minute lessons on AI agents. A YouTube or TikTok channel on the same topic brings in buyers.

The Pitch

The presenter, who says he has sold digital products for years, argues that health, fitness, manifestation and 'how to sell digital products' are evergreen topics with steady buyers, and that AI is where the growth is: "If you guys are in doubt on any of this, lean into AI." He adds that a fitness program only works if you actually train, while wellness content is easier to enter.

Best For

Someone with real knowledge or credibility in one of these topics who is willing to build an audience around it, since the product sells only as well as the traffic behind it.

The Truth

This is a short list of niches rather than a method: the video doesn't cover pricing, where to sell, or how to get a first sale. His case for manifestation products is openly that buyers who don't get results will blame themselves instead of the product, which is a reason for caution, not a selling point. He also sells a $27 guide on building digital products, one of the niches he recommends.
