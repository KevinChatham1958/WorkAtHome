KDP Books for Readers Over 50

What It Is

Publish low-to-medium-content books specifically targeting readers over 50, across seven niches: chair yoga (seated exercise, avoiding standing-workout injury risk); large print word search puzzles (no writing required, themed around nostalgia to stand out); short story collections (compilations rather than full novels, themed around nostalgia, humor, or inspiration); trivia books for seniors; tai chi for seniors over 60; memory games for seniors; and health-focused cookbooks for seniors (targeting specific conditions like diabetes or arthritis, carrying notably higher per-sale royalties than puzzle or journal books). True large-print formatting is essential (not just claimed), content must be verifiably safe and accurate, and each niche can be split into many sub-niche books forming a sellable series.

The Pitch

The presenter, who says he has made millions selling books on Amazon KDP and runs a mentorship with over 2,200 students, pulls real, live sales-rank data via a free Chrome extension for multiple actual published books in each niche, showing specific current Best Seller Rank numbers translated into revenue estimates (a chair yoga book doing roughly $6,000-10,000/month, a word search book doing $10,000-18,000/month).

Who It’s Best For

Someone willing to research a niche carefully and produce genuinely accurate, appropriately formatted content rather than a generic reskin.

The Truth (as we see it)

The specific sales-rank and revenue figures are pulled from a real, live third-party data tool reflecting actual current book performance, more verifiable than an unsupported claim, though the tool estimates revenue from BSR rather than confirmed payout data. The presenter's own revenue and student count are self-reported; the video promotes his own software tool including an upcoming paid feature.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
