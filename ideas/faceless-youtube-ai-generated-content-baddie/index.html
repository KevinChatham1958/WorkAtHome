What It Is

Build a YouTube channel using stock footage clips paired with an AI-generated voiceover and script (list-style or narration videos), without appearing on camera, monetized through YouTube ad revenue as the channel grows.

The Pitch

Presenter cites the channel 'Top Fives' as reportedly earning about $400,000/year per Social Blade estimates from simple narrated list videos, framing faceless AI content as increasingly accessible now that AI can help generate scripts and video elements.

Best For

Someone comfortable picking a content niche and consistently producing videos, without needing on-camera presence or original filming.

The Truth

Faceless AI-narrated YouTube channels are a well-established content category with real monetization potential, though success depends heavily on consistent output and finding an underserved sub-niche. The $400K figure is a third-party estimate for one specific channel, not a typical outcome, and this video doesn't walk through the actual video-creation tools in detail.
