AI Fitness Reels on Facebook

What It Is

You set up a Facebook page or a profile in professional mode, pick a specific fitness angle using keyword research, and post short AI-generated workout reels. Once Facebook invites the page into its content monetization program, you earn from views. You never appear on camera.

The Pitch

E'Calm shows one new page making over $600 in its first 18 days of monetization, and a page he took over that went from under $100 a month to $16,000 in August and about $27,000 in September after he changed the content.

Who It’s Best For

Patient creators who can post daily and study what kind of reel gets watched.

The Truth (as we see it)

His biggest page wasn't built from scratch; he got it from someone who had already monetized it, and buying or transferring pages can break Facebook's rules. Monetization is by invitation only and payouts change without warning. Fitness content made by AI must still be safe and accurate. The video uses an affiliate link for VidIQ and recommends a paid AI video tool.
