What It Is

You build a consumer mobile app that earns subscription revenue and grow it mainly with organic short-form video on TikTok and Instagram rather than paid ads. The guests behind the communication-skills app Gleam lay out formats to test: talking head, skits, animations, challenges, wall-of-text, founder-led videos, series, memes and, best of all, product demos that show the app working. You post your own videos first until one format gets views, downloads and can be repeated, then hire UGC creators found on Reddit, university clubs, creator platforms or by DM. They suggest paying $10-$25 per video with a minimum view count, plus about $0.50-$0.80 per thousand views as milestone bonuses.

The Pitch

The guests say this framework took their app to top 10 in its App Store category and over $1 million in annual recurring revenue, with more than 500 million views across social media. They argue organic is the cheapest place to start, that one working format is enough to reach $100,000 a month, and that a video that converts organically can later be reused as a paid ad.

Who It’s Best For

People who can build or commission a simple app and are willing to post short videos daily, often on camera, and test formats patiently.

The Truth (as we see it)

The revenue and view numbers are the guests' own and unaudited, and they come from engineers who already knew how to build apps. The video covers distribution only, not how to build the app or choose an idea, and the guests admit most videos fail to convert and that apps which are naturally viral have a big head start. Paying creators and keeping good ones from better-funded competitors takes money and relationship work. The channel offers a free cheat sheet in the description as a sign-up incentive.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
