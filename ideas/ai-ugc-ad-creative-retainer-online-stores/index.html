AI Ad Creatives for Stores

Monthly-retainer service making AI-generated video and image ads for online stores

What It Is

You make ad creative for online stores and charge a monthly fee. The creator's process is to write ad scripts with Claude, turn them into videos using AI actors on an AI ad-generation tool, and make static image ads with an AI image tool. He says one person can produce 15 to 20 ads in an afternoon. Clients are found by cold calling and outreach, and he describes winning an early client by offering two weeks of free work.

The Pitch

The creator says this is the fastest of his three models to get from zero to your first $1,000 and that a business will pay $1,500 to $3,000 a month for this work. He says all you need is a laptop and a few AI tools, and that reaching $1,000 a day takes about 15 clients at $2,000 a month.

Who It’s Best For

People comfortable with cold calling and handling rejection who can judge what makes a good ad.

The Truth (as we see it)

Selling ad creative to e-commerce brands is a real service, and AI tools do speed up production. The creator himself rates it hard to start because of the cold outreach, and says it gets stressful past five or six clients, when you need to hire and losing one client hurts. The $1,500 to $3,000 a month price is his estimate, and many stores are wary of fully AI-generated ads. His own income claims are unaudited, and he promotes free coaching programs paid for by software companies whose tools his students sign up for.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
