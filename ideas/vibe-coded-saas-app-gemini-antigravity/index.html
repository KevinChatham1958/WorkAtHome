Vibe-Coded SaaS With Gemini

Vibe-coding a SaaS app using Google's Gemini/Antigravity coding tools

What It Is

Use Google's AI coding assistant (Antigravity) to build and launch a software app or website without traditional programming skills, then attempt to monetize it as a SaaS product.

The Pitch

The presenter walks through his own repeated attempts (a cross-platform social feed app, an AI meal-planning app, an infrastructure-as-code tool) built entirely with AI coding tools, and is candid that all three ended in zero users and zero dollars because he built before validating demand. He frames the lesson as: before building anything, confirm someone would buy it right now, not after you build it.

Who It's Best For

Someone with a specific person or business already lined up who wants a piece of software, rather than someone building speculatively and hoping to find users afterward.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is the most skeptical entry in the source video, presented by the creator himself as the weakest of the five ideas covered — beginner-friendliness and speed to first income are both rated poorly, largely because building is easy but distribution and finding paying customers is hard, especially against a wave of similar AI-built "SaaS-template" sites the presenter calls "AI slop." Worth reading as a cautionary account of what doesn't work as much as a business idea to copy.
