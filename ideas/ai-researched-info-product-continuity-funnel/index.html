Info Product With Continuity Upsell

AI-researched low-price info product with a recurring continuity upsell

Digital & subscription products · Low startup cost (AI platform subscription)

Originally published Aug 20, 2026 — Bill Mcintosh

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Bill Mcintosh · How Selling Digital Products With AI Changed My Life (My Exact System) ↗

What It Is

Use an AI research-and-build tool to identify a specific, already-researched problem people are actively searching for solutions to (via Reddit and keyword research), bundle a simple, inexpensive solution (checklists, guides, a short course) priced as an impulse buy (often $11 to $30), then convert first-time buyers into an ongoing monthly "continuity" subscription (fresh content drops, a simple tool or calculator, or a private community) rather than relying on one-time sales alone.

The Pitch

The presenter credits an $11 information product with generating over $15 million in revenue over time, attributing the result specifically to the low-friction impulse-buy price point combined with converting buyers into recurring subscribers, since a single customer who sticks around a year can be worth $50 to $100 rather than just the initial $11. The demonstrated workflow uses an AI platform to research a niche's actual pain points from real online discussions, draft the product bundle, build the sales website and members area, and even generate and launch ad campaigns (Meta, Google, and in-chat AI platform ads), positioned as replacing over $1,200 a month in separate marketing tools.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable identifying a real, already-searched-for problem rather than starting from their own idea, and willing to build toward recurring revenue rather than treating a single low-price sale as the end goal.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter is explicit and upfront that this isn't a passive, prompt-and-done system, real ongoing work is still required, which is a fair disclaimer given how the low-price framing could otherwise oversell ease. The specific $15 million lifetime-revenue figure is a personal claim without independent verification. The entire video is a demonstration of, and funnel toward, the presenter's own paid all-in-one AI business platform, a direct financial incentive worth knowing, though the underlying strategy (low-price entry product plus recurring continuity offer) is a well-established, tool-agnostic info-product approach.
