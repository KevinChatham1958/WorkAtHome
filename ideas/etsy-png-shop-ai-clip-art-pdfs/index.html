Etsy Clip Art "PNG Shop"

Etsy "PNG shop" selling AI-generated clip art and wall art as PDF downloads

Digital & subscription products · Very low startup cost

Originally published Jul 9, 2026 — Alex

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Alex · how i make $4800/week selling boring pdfs + claude ai ↗

What It Is

Running an Etsy shop selling cheap AI-generated clip art and printable wall art delivered as a PDF containing a Google Drive download link, built by pulling already-trending design ideas from a trend-research tool and using Claude for the listing copy.

The Pitch

The presenter shows a real large Etsy shop selling exactly this model as proof of concept, then walks through his own workflow: using a paid trend-research tool to find already-trending design niches, cross-checked against Google Trends for legitimacy, generating matching AI art in an image tool, exporting multiple variations, uploading files to a shared Google Drive folder, building a simple PDF delivery card containing that Drive link, and using Claude only to write SEO-optimized Etsy titles, descriptions, and tags. States this is a volume-and-consistency business, not a one-hit-listing business, and that he is not selling a course.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable posting a high volume of low-priced listings consistently rather than expecting one or two designs to carry the business.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter explicitly discloses AI use in his own Etsy listings, as required by Etsy's policy, and states buyers don't seem to mind, a genuinely transparent practice worth noting positively. He states he isn't selling anything in this video, a mild credibility signal, though the specific weekly revenue figures shown are self-reported screenshots, not independently verified. Two paid tools are referenced with affiliate-style links in the description.
