Faceless YouTube channel of simple AI stick-figure history stories

What It Is

Start a faceless YouTube channel on one topic, such as surprising human history, using very simple AI-generated stick-figure images that change every two to three seconds over an AI voiceover. The creator uses ChatGPT to study a successful competitor and pick a video idea, write the script and produce one image prompt per line, generates the images free in Google's image tool, voices the script with ElevenLabs, and lays the images over the audio. A 20-minute video needs roughly 100 images.

The Pitch

He points to a stick-figure channel that reached about 88,000 subscribers in a few months with videos over a million views, and estimates it earns $5,000 to $10,000 a month from ads.

Who It’s Best For

Patient, consistent creators who like research and storytelling and can post often without showing their face.

The Truth (as we see it)

The creator himself ranks this only C-tier: YouTube penalizes low-effort AI content, anyone can copy the format, and ad pay on these channels is modest. The competitor's earnings are his estimate, not reported figures. Channels that last pick one topic and make videos people genuinely want to watch. The video is a six-business tier list; the creator says results he mentions are not typical, and he promotes a free dropshipping course tied to a paid Shopify partnership.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
