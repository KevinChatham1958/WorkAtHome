Authorization-First Amazon Wholesale

Amazon wholesale reselling via brand-direct outreach (2026 authorization-first method)

What It Is

Reselling name-brand products on Amazon by getting direct authorization from small-to-mid-size brands (under $500k/month revenue) to buy at wholesale discount and resell at retail price, rather than unauthorized retail arbitrage.

The Pitch

Since Amazon tightened unauthorized-reseller enforcement at the end of 2024, the presenter argues brand-direct authorization is now mandatory, not optional. The method: research ASINs, UPCs, and profitability data for about a week, then cold-email 30 to 50 small brands a day, deliberately avoiding household names that ignore small buyers. When a brand says no, a value-based follow-up showing them a revenue gap versus their competitors often reopens the conversation. Existing brand relationships become case studies to land bigger brands, and enough demonstrated value can turn a shared reseller slot into an exclusive deal. The presenter recommends recording your own process on Loom so the work can eventually be handed to an outsourced virtual assistant.

Who It’s Best For

Someone with real inventory capital and comfort with high-volume cold outreach and slow relationship-building, not a low-cost or passive idea.

The Truth (as we see it)

The presenter claims over $40 million in personal Amazon sales over 6 years and $15 million in revenue last year, but these are self-reported figures with no account verification shown. This is a meaningfully higher capital bar than most other entries in this database. The 'must go brand-direct' framing is presented as a hard 2024 policy shift; since Amazon policy details can change, it's worth independently verifying current seller-authorization rules before committing capital.
