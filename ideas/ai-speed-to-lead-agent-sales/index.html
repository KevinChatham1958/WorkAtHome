Speed-to-Lead AI Voice Agent

Selling an outbound "speed to lead" AI voice agent to service businesses

What It Is

Sell service businesses an outbound AI voice agent that automatically calls a new website lead within about 20 seconds of them submitting a contact form, connected directly to the business's CRM, replacing the common failure point where a human employee doesn't call back for 20-40 minutes and the lead moves on to a competitor.

The Pitch

Kirk frames the pitch entirely around a concrete pain point rather than the technology itself: a business paying an employee roughly 5% commission (often $4,000-5,000 a month) to dial leads slowly and inconsistently can instead pay $2,000-3,000 a month for an agent that never misses a lead and never needs training, which he pitches as $24,000-36,000 a year in replaced cost at a 95% profit margin for the seller. His broader business model is to focus entirely on sales himself while delegating all backend agent-building to a trusted team of U.S.-based developers (paid around 20% of revenue), arguing this lets him take on far more clients than someone trying to sell and build everything solo.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable being purely the sales and client-relationship side of the business, willing to pay developers to handle technical delivery rather than building agents themselves.

The Truth (as we see it)

Kirk's own $85,000-a-month figure is self-reported and tied to his specific delegation model (developers, ad spend, an outreach team), which comes with real ongoing costs (roughly 25% of revenue by his own accounting) that a true beginner wouldn't have from day one. The specific pain-point-first sales framing, cost of a slow human employee versus a fast agent, is a concrete and independently testable pitch angle regardless of team structure.
