What It Is

You produce a monthly batch of short social media videos for a company using an AI video agent, such as 30 videos a month. The creator works out that at about $12 per video your cost would be around $360 a month, so charging a business $1,000 a month to manage its social media video would leave a margin. The work is writing or prompting the scripts, checking the output, and delivering finished videos.

The Pitch

He says the AI videos are close to ready to post and stick well to the script, which makes a monthly content package practical for one person.

Best For

People with some marketing sense who can find business clients and review content carefully for accuracy.

The Truth

This appears only as a brief pricing example in the video; the creator does not say he runs this service or show any clients. Margins depend on credit prices, which can change, and on how many videos need re-runs. Finding businesses willing to pay $1,000 a month means pitching and selling, and many owners are wary of obviously AI-made content. He promotes his own paid dashboard and classes.
