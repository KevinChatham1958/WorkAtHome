Pet Waste Removal

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (~$500 for basic equipment)

Originally published Apr 9, 2026 — Austin Zaback

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Austin Zaback · 9 Service Businesses You Can Start Under $1,000 (No Experience Needed) ↗

One of 2 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Offer a recurring weekly service that shows up at a customer's home, cleans up dog waste from the yard, sanitizes the turf, and leaves, using simple tools: a rake, scoop, waste bins, disinfectant, and gloves.

The Pitch

Zaback cites U.S. pet-ownership data (66% of households own a pet, 65 million own at least one dog producing over 20 billion pounds of waste a year) and a real example he watched personally: a scooper who built a 300-customer route, did about 30 jobs a day per employee, and grossed roughly $20,000 a month, over a quarter million a year, charging an average of $69 per customer per month.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable with genuinely unglamorous, hands-on outdoor work who's building toward a recurring-revenue route rather than one-off jobs.

The Truth (as we see it)

The $20,000/month example is a single real operator Zaback says he personally observed and spoke with, a specific and checkable-in-principle account rather than an anonymous claim, though it's still one data point rather than an industry average. The pet-waste-removal market size and growth figures cited ($537 million market by 2035) are framed as industry projections, not something Zaback personally verified.
