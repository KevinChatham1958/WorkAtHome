Pay-Per-Appointment Lead Revival

Reviving small businesses' old leads with AI follow-ups, paid per booked appointment

What It Is

Many small businesses have long lists of leads they never followed up. You connect an AI agent to a CRM (HighLevel in the video), send personalized follow-ups to every lead not contacted in the last 90 days, and book appointments with those who respond. Rather than a monthly fee, the creator suggests charging $50-150 per qualified booked meeting.

The Pitch

The creator cites a study that about 43% of leads never get contacted by small service companies, and argues that pay-per-result pricing removes the client's risk, making it easy to sell. Appointment-setting firms already charge $500-2,000 a month for similar work.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with CRM tools and messaging who can sell a results-based deal to service businesses.

The Truth (as we see it)

Database reactivation is a known, legitimate tactic, and pay-per-appointment pricing is easier to sell but means you earn nothing if leads don't book. Messaging old contacts must follow consent and anti-spam rules for email and text. The figures are the creator's suggestions, not results. The creator offers free prompts through his community.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
