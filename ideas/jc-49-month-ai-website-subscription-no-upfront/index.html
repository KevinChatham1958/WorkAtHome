$49/Month Contractor Websites

$49/month AI-built websites for local contractors with no upfront fee

Local & professional services · Very low startup cost (a dollar or two per site in hosting/AI costs, plus an AI subscription)

What It Is

You use AI to build websites for local contractors for a dollar or two each, then sell them on a subscription of about $49 a month with no upfront fee, instead of the $1,000-$5,000 (or more) many businesses pay for a custom site. The work is building the sites with AI, finding clients (he describes calling them with the offer), and maintaining the sites month to month. His math: 50 clients at $49 is about $4,900 a month recurring, at margins he estimates above 95%.

The Pitch

The creator's core argument is that AI has collapsed the cost of building websites while prices have held, and he chooses to undercut the market so the offer is "almost stupid not to say yes" to. Losing one $49 client hurts little, and hundreds of small subscriptions build "real enterprise value." He warns the window may close because AI companies currently subsidize usage.

Who It's Best For

Suits someone comfortable selling to small business owners and handling ongoing site upkeep who prefers many small recurring clients over a few big projects.

The Truth (as we see it)

Low-priced website subscriptions are a real and established model, and the cost side is believable for simple static sites. The harder parts are signing 50 clients by phone, ongoing edit requests and churn, all of which eat into the 95% margin he quotes. His "six figures a month" income claim is his own and unaudited, and the AI-subsidy warning doubles as urgency to join. He promotes his own paid Skool community (the Rank Expander Academy, 7-day free trial, then paid), and the build blueprint zip file he relies on is only available inside it.
