AI Brand Images and UGC Videos

AI-made product images and short UGC-style videos for product brands

Marketing & content · Near-zero cost (ChatGPT and a paid Gemini plan for video)

Originally published Sep 2, 2026 — Alicia Lyttle

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Alicia Lyttle · Turn What You Already Know Into AI Income: Step-by-Step ↗

What It Is

You upload a brand's product image to ChatGPT and use short style codes to generate marketing visuals (airport lightbox ads, press kits, laptop mockups, flavor lineups, people holding the product), then use Gemini to make short TikTok-style UGC videos, including talking ones. You sell this as a content service to product brands and TikTok Shop sellers.

The Pitch

The creator, who ran a digital marketing agency, says images like these used to take a team of graphic designers and a big budget and now take minutes. She suggests posting samples on LinkedIn and at networking events to find brand clients.

Who It's Best For

Someone with a marketing eye who wants a service they can start with tools they already pay for.

The Truth (as we see it)

The tools work as shown, but many people now offer AI product content, so you need taste and reliable output to stand out. AI 'customers' holding a product should not be presented as real testimonials. The creator gives away her style codes in exchange for an email and sells AI training, events and a consultant certification.
