AI Brand Kit Design Service

What It Is

You sell branding packages on Fiverr that include a logo, color palette, typography, icons, usage guidelines, do's and don'ts and a brand book. When an order comes in, the client answers questions about their business; you feed the answers to a Claude prompt that interviews you and writes a custom prompt for each brand element, then generate those elements in ChatGPT and assemble them in Canva as a PDF brand book. The example listing shown charges $125, $285 and $365 for its three packages.

The Pitch

The creator points to a Fiverr seller with 95 orders in their queue and estimates that could be about $21,660 after Fiverr's 20% cut. He says you can reuse the same prompt for every client, and that it lets you "run a design agency without being a professional designer," with potential he rates medium to high once reviews let you raise prices.

Who It’s Best For

People with a decent eye for design who can handle client communication and revisions, and who want a service that pays faster than a product.

The Truth (as we see it)

Brand-kit services do sell on Fiverr, and AI tools genuinely speed up producing the pieces. The revenue math is a hypothetical based on another seller's order queue, not a measured result. AI image tools are still weak at clean, scalable vector logos and consistent text, AI-generated logos may not be eligible for trademark or copyright protection, and buyers increasingly ask about this, so be upfront about your process. New Fiverr sellers also typically start at low prices until they have reviews.
