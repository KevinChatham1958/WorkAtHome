Print-on-demand T-shirts With AI-remixed Designs Based on Proven Sellers

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (AI image tool or design platform subscription, marketplace listing fees)

What It Is

You research niches on Etsy by sorting search results by sales or revenue (for example teacher and nurse shirts outsell accountant shirts) and save best-selling designs as references. You break each reference into four elements (niche, scene/slogan, layout and art style) and use AI image models in a node-based canvas to generate new, original designs that change one element, such as turning a book-lover design into a nursing design. Designs are generated in parallel batches, then upscaled, background-removed, cropped and placed for the target shirt, and turned into listings with mock-ups and AI-written titles and tags. A print-on-demand provider prints and ships each order.

The Pitch

The creator says he has done multiple seven figures in T-shirt sales and that shops with more than 250 listings average far more sales, so volume matters. He argues AI lets you produce 20 to 100 designs in about 20 minutes, while your real skill is learning to recognise why certain designs sell.

Best For

People who enjoy design and research and are willing to build a large catalogue over months.

The Truth

The sales claims are the creator's own and unaudited. The video is built around Listing View, a paid research and design platform he uses throughout, and he points viewers to his free Skool community and his AI print-on-demand course. He warns that copying others' designs gets shops shut down; remixing best sellers too closely can still create copyright or trademark trouble, and marketplaces have their own rules on AI-generated work. Most individual listings sell only a few units a month, so income depends on catalogue size.
