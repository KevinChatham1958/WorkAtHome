Custom Embroidery for Trades and Teams

Custom embroidery business selling to local trades and sports teams

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (a single-needle embroidery machine, $400-780)

What It Is

Embroider logos and designs onto blank hats and apparel using an affordable single-needle embroidery machine (not the expensive commercial multi-needle setups often recommended), selling primarily to local trades (construction crews, landscapers, real estate teams needing branded workwear) and sports clubs reordering patches seasonally, rather than competing on one-off bulk orders.

The Pitch

The presenter steers buyers away from the commonly-recommended $4,800 Ricoma multi-needle machine toward single-needle options under $800 (Brother SE600 at $400, an NC700 at $530, or a PE800 at $780). Blank hats cost $3-7, embroidery adds $2.50-7.50 depending on stitch count, and a 5,000-stitch logo lands around $7.50 all-in; single custom hats sell $15-35, while bulk orders of 50+ drop to $7.50-15 each. He flags two mistakes: giving away digitizing (a real $50 line item per logo) and chasing bulk orders where margins collapse.

Best For

Someone willing to build recurring relationships with local trade businesses and sports clubs for reordering branded gear, rather than treating this as a one-off custom-order shop.

The Truth

The equipment-tier guidance (you don't need the expensive commercial machine) and the two specific pricing mistakes (free digitizing, chasing thin-margin bulk orders) are genuinely useful, practical warnings that could save a beginner real money. The business still depends on landing and keeping those recurring local trade accounts, which takes direct sales effort beyond just running the machine.
