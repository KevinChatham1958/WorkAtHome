Printable Meal Planners

Simple printable meal planners sold on Gumroad with Pinterest carousel marketing

Digital & subscription products · Near-zero cost (free Gumroad listing with a 10% fee; the creator used a $100/month ChatGPT plan)

Originally published Sep 26, 2026 — London Leffler

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

London Leffler · I Found the Simplest Digital Product That Actually Makes Money ↗

What It Is

You make one simple digital product that solves one small problem, here a fillable weekly dinner planner with pantry tracking and a few recipes, priced at $3.99. The creator generated the illustrated planner with ChatGPT in about 21 minutes (buying private-label-rights products is offered as an alternative), listed it on Gumroad for free, and set up a Pinterest business account. For traffic he posts AI-made recipe carousels on Pinterest, using the ads tool to publish them without paying for ads, with each pin linking to the Gumroad page. He plans to post daily and later move to his own website and funnel.

The Pitch

He argues simple products sell because they solve one clear problem without overwhelming the buyer, and that Pinterest, with about 640 million monthly users, can bring free traffic. He points to Pinterest-driven sites estimated at $21,000 to $200,000 a month as proof of the potential.

Best For

Beginners who like design and organizing and are willing to post on Pinterest consistently for months.

The Truth

The revenue figures for other stores are third-party estimates (Ecom Scout), which he says may not be accurate, and the video starts his own 21-day test without results yet. At $3.99 you need many sales to earn meaningful income, and printable planners are a crowded category. He says a free AI plan may not produce the same quality as his paid one. At the end he promotes his own Evolve PLR product launch and affiliate program, so the PLR suggestion is not neutral.
