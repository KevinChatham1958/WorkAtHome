One-on-One AI Tutoring

What It Is

Offer paid, one-on-one or small-group instruction helping individuals or families use AI tools for practical, personal projects, rather than teaching AI as an abstract skill.

The Pitch

Sarah cites a Business Insider-profiled example, a 27-year-old named Derek, who charges $50 an hour and works 4-12 hours a week with three steady clients, projects like helping a woman use AI chatbots to digitize her late brother's diaries. That's roughly $200-600 a week from a small, stable client base. Derek's client-acquisition method is intentionally low-tech: printed posters in local spots like cafes, themselves designed using free AI image tools.

Best For

Someone patient and comfortable teaching non-technical people one-on-one, since the value here is personal guidance and hand-holding, not building a scalable course or content product.

The Truth

The $50-an-hour rate and client count come from a named, published third-party profile (Business Insider), which is a stronger sourcing signal than an unverified personal claim, though it's still one individual's specific results, not a broader track record across many people doing this. The business is inherently limited by the operator's own available hours, since it's built on personal, hands-on sessions rather than something that scales without more of the operator's time.
