Selling Simple 2x4 Furniture

Batch-built 2x4 stools and benches sold on Facebook Marketplace (an honest test)

Physical & print products · Low startup cost (about $8 in materials per stool, $20 per bench, if you already own basic tools)

Originally published Sep 27, 2026 — Lincoln St. Woodworks

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Lincoln St. Woodworks · I Tested Get Rich Quick Woodworking Projects ↗

What It Is

You build simple furniture from big-box lumber, such as a decor stool from one 8-foot 2x4 or an entry bench from one 2x10, batch them with time-saving jigs, and list them on Facebook Marketplace or sell in person. The creator, a professional woodworker, timed and costed popular YouTube projects: three stools took 1 hour 45 minutes, and one bench took about 1 hour 20 minutes including finish.

The Pitch

On paper the stools work out to about $63 an hour at the suggested $45 price, and other creators report selling benches at $65-85. The creator shares practical tips: CA glue as instant clamps, templates for screw holes, pre-cut plugs to save labor, and checking shipping costs before choosing a product.

Who It's Best For

A hobby woodworker who wants to fund tool purchases and lives in a populated area with buyers for handmade decor.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is the rare video that reports failure: after a week, neither the $45 stools nor the $65 bench sold, and copycat listings started a price war. The creator's point is that YouTube popularity doesn't prove local demand, build times are routinely underestimated, bulky items can't be shipped profitably, and Etsy staples like floating shelves face organized competition. The video is sponsored by a business bank (Relay) and promotes the creator's t-shirts.
