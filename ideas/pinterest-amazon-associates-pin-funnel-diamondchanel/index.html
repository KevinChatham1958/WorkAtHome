Pinterest-to-Amazon Affiliate Pins

Marketing & content · Free to start (a Pinterest and Amazon Associates account)

Originally published Aug 10, 2026 — Diamond Chanel

Share on Facebook Link copied

Diamond Chanel · 12 Boring Side Hustles You Can Run From Your Phone (BETTER Than Uber & Doordash) ↗

One of 6 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

Pick a top-selling product from Amazon's Best Sellers list, create a Pinterest pin using the product's own images, title, and description, attach an Amazon Associates affiliate link, and publish — earning a commission whenever someone clicks through the pin and buys.

The Pitch

The presenter cites a case of someone earning over $2,000 without ever showing their face using this method, describing it as a simple, repeatable four-step process (sign up for Amazon Associates, pick a top-5 bestseller, build a Pinterest pin from the product's own assets, attach the affiliate link) that requires no ongoing engagement once pins are published, rating it 4/5 for ease of starting.

Who It's Best For

Someone comfortable creating simple visual pins and willing to publish many of them consistently, since individual pin conversion rates are low and this model depends on volume.

The Truth (as we see it)

The mechanic itself is genuine and low-cost to test, and Pinterest's evergreen, search-driven traffic does suit affiliate content better than more ephemeral social platforms. That said, this is a widely-taught, saturated tactic, and the video gives no detail on how many pins the $2,000 example required or over what timeframe, so expectations should be tempered relative to the simplicity of the pitch.
