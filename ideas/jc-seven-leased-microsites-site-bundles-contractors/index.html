Leasing Microsite Bundles

Leasing ranked microsites and site bundles to contractors (seven sites, $10K/month goal)

Marketing & content · Very low startup cost (about $11 per site per year plus an AI subscription)

Originally published Aug 24, 2026 — Jesse Cunningham

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jesse Cunningham · AI Content vs AI Slop: What Makes a Useful Local Website? ↗

What It Is

You build AI-generated local service sites, keep ownership, and lease either the calls they produce or bundles of sites to local contractors for a flat monthly fee. His target math is seven ranked sites leased at $1,500 a month each, about $10,000 a month, in high-ticket services people need done urgently. Calls are answered 24/7 by an AI agent and form fills go straight to the client. Beginners can start with a simpler one-click site builder before moving to Claude Code, and he notes successful members pair this with Upwork freelancing for faster cash flow.

The Pitch

The creator argues AI-generated content is a method, not "slop," which he defines as thin, useless output, and that his structured sites are better than most local business websites. With every service times every location, he says demand is far larger than the number of people building these sites. A failed site only costs about $11, so you "build more."

Best For

Suits people who like building recurring, owned assets and can handle ongoing relationships with a few contractor clients.

The Truth (as we see it)

Leasing rather than selling sites, and bundling them, are established rank-and-rent practices, and his slop-versus-quality distinction is reasonable. He admits seven sites is usually not enough and more are needed, so the $10,000 figure is an illustration, and member wins shown (such as $5,000 in commissions) are unverified. He promotes his own paid Skool community (the Rank Expander Academy, 7-day free trial, then paid), and the build blueprint zip file he relies on is only available inside it. He also mentions a one-click builder inside it.
