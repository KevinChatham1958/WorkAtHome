AI Team Training Workshops

Paid AI training workshops for business teams, priced per head

Startup cost: Near-zero cost (a slide deck and video-call software) (our estimate; the video doesn’t give figures)

One of 8 gigs covered in this video

What It Is

You teach a company's team how to use AI in a single paid workshop or a short series, charging per person, for example $150 a head. Around 80% of the material stays the same for each client, with about 20% customized. She suggests building a two-hour session with a simple slide deck from free online education, including her own videos, and finding clients by messaging CEOs on LinkedIn and Instagram and posting AI tips so people contact you.

The Pitch

The creator rates this one of the easiest options because businesses have budgets and the content is easy to reuse, so a single workshop can bring in thousands in a day. She also describes it as a natural entry point to paid AI consulting with the same client.

Who It’s Best For

Confident presenters with some business credibility who are comfortable pitching decision-makers and teaching groups live.

The Truth (as we see it)

Companies do pay for practical AI training, and a reusable workshop is an efficient format. Winning business clients by messaging CEOs is harder than the video suggests, especially without a track record or references. Workshops can be run over video, but some clients will want you on site. The video closes by recommending Blotato, a social media posting tool, so part of it works as promotion for that product.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
