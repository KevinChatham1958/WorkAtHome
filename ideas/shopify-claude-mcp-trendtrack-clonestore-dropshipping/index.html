Finding and Validating Winning Dropshipping Products With a Claude-connected Ad-spy Tool, Then Cloning and Improving a Competitor's Store

E-commerce · Low (an ad-spy tool subscription with free trial, a store-cloning tool subscription, a free Claude account)

Originally published Sep 3, 2026 — Jordan Welch

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jordan Welch · Claude + Shopify = $12,000/Month ↗

What It Is

Find winning dropshipping products using an ad-spy research tool, filtering for currently-active ads created in the last 30 days from stores with 10-50 live ads (established but still beatable), looking for products that are trending, not available in physical stores, have $25-30+ profit margin per order, and have visual 'wow factor'; connect the research tool to Claude via MCP so a single prompt scans the tool's data and produces a full product-validation report in about 10 minutes instead of a week of manual research; then connect a second tool to Claude via MCP to clone a validated competitor's store structure, feeding in the validation research so Claude helps craft differentiated angles and copywriting rather than a straight copy.

The Pitch

The presenter reports taking a new store from zero to $424 in sales on day one using an Alaskan volcanic clay mask stick found through this process, and offers a free course plus a free AI-built Shopify store to viewers who sign up through his affiliate partnerships, walking through the entire live process on screen.

Best For

Someone comfortable connecting and using multiple SaaS tools via Claude's MCP integrations and willing to do real product validation before launching.

The Truth

Ad-spy research tools and Claude MCP integrations for automated product/competitor research are real, current, technically accurate capabilities, and the winning-product criteria described reflect standard, sound dropshipping selection logic. The $424-day-one figure is self-reported from one product, and the video is structured as a lead-in to his own free course and multiple paid-tool affiliate partnerships.
