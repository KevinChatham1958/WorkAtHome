One-Result Workshops

Focused workshop, challenge or mini course that delivers one specific result

Digital & subscription products · Near-zero cost

Originally published Oct 14, 2026 — Jillian Leslie

Share on Facebook Link copied
Source

Jillian Leslie · 35 Digital Product Ideas You Can Build in a Weekend (Steal These) ↗

One of 6 ideas covered in this video

What It Is

You teach one specific outcome in a short live workshop, challenge or mini course and charge for access. The creator's examples include a beginner sourdough weekend workshop, a 30-day watercolor challenge, a public speaking confidence mini course built around one upcoming presentation, a job interview answer builder for people who freeze under pressure and a LinkedIn profile makeover kit for career changers. She suggests a live workshop can later lead into a paid membership.

The Pitch

The creator says products work when the buyer already knows they have the problem and you can give a meaningful win quickly. She names a focused live workshop that delivers one result as one of the three product types she would test first, and says confidence-focused products sell because the result is visible.

Who It's Best For

People with a teachable skill and enough comfort presenting to guide a small group to one clear result.

The Truth (as we see it)

Short, outcome-focused workshops are a credible way to test demand before building something bigger, and live delivery lets you learn what buyers actually struggle with. Live workshops usually mean being on camera, and filling seats needs an audience or marketing. The creator is a co-founder of MiloTree, a platform for selling digital products, and the video promotes its free AI product finder and building inside MiloTree, so this is partly a product pitch.
