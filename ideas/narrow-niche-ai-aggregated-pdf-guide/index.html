"One Person, One Problem" PDF Guide

Ultra-narrow-niche AI-aggregated PDF guide ("one person, one problem") digital product

What It Is

Pick one very specific underserved person-and-problem combination (e.g. postpartum weight loss for new mothers rather than general weight loss, or business English for non-native executives rather than general language learning), then use a specialized AI research tool (not a generic chatbot like plain ChatGPT/Claude, which the source argues loses coherence past roughly 10 sources) to assemble findings from 150+ existing studies, reports, and courses into one focused, short guide — positioning yourself as a curator of expert consensus rather than an expert yourself.

The Pitch

The minimum viable version is a roughly 45-page PDF built in about 40 minutes of AI-assisted work, deliberately small rather than a 5-hour course, tested cheaply and relaunched or replaced if it flops — the source's framing is that since each attempt costs nothing (unlike dropshipping's ad spend or FBA's inventory cost), the only real failure is quitting after one try, citing a real example of a first launch making $12 and a second, refined launch making $21,000.

Who It’s Best For

Someone comfortable with a narrow, specific niche rather than a broad topic, and willing to iterate through multiple small product attempts instead of investing heavily in one large course upfront.

The Truth (as we see it)

The narrow-niche-over-broad-niche principle and the emphasis on assembling existing, verifiable expert consensus (rather than inventing new claims) are sound, low-risk approaches to digital products. The presenter of this specific video is explicit that the $1,995 course being summarized isn't necessary to use this method, and that the underlying strategy is free — a genuinely useful signal of good faith worth noting, though the individual case study cited ($12 then $21,000) is the source's own reported data, not independently verified.
