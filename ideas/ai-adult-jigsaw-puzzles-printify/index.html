AI Jigsaw Puzzle Designs

AI-designed adult jigsaw puzzles sold through Printify on Amazon or Etsy

What It Is

You generate detailed illustrations in ChatGPT (the demo is a cozy autumn cottage in a storybook style), upscale them with a free AI upscaler, and upload them as 500 to 1,000 piece puzzles in Printify. Printify connects to Amazon, Etsy or your own store, and prints, boxes and ships each order. The creator recommends sticking to adult puzzles labeled ages 14 and up to avoid Amazon's toy safety paperwork, and starting on Etsy if Amazon's requirements feel like too much.

The Pitch

The creator shows Amazon puzzles selling 1,000 to 3,000+ units a month, estimating one at over $63,000 a month in sales at $21 each, and says you could make similar or better artwork with AI. He rates the effort as low and the potential as low to medium, growing with a catalog and seasonal hits.

Who It’s Best For

People who like making detailed artwork and want a physical product without handling inventory.

The Truth (as we see it)

Print-on-demand puzzles are a real product, and the creator is honest about the Amazon seller fee and toy-category rules. The best-seller figures shown are for established brands, not print-on-demand sellers; per-unit print costs on POD puzzles are high, which squeezes margins compared with the $21 bulk-made examples. Some Amazon categories, including toys and games, can require approval or documentation, and quality of upscaled AI art should be checked in a sample before listing.

Our assessment, based on the video and what’s typical across similar videos. We don’t independently verify income claims or prices.
