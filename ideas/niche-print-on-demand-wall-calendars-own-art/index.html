Niche Print-on-Demand Calendars

Niche print-on-demand wall calendars featuring your own art

What It Is

You design wall calendars using your own artwork and sell them through a print-on-demand provider, which prints and ships each order so you hold no stock. The creator made three calendar designs and one became a steady seller for three years, at an average price of about $35 and roughly $15 profit per calendar, for about $3,000 total profit. She suggests niche angles: a history calendar with important dates for teachers and history fans, or customizable calendars where a family or workplace adds everyone's birthdays. The same images can be reused across prints, mugs and books to save design time.

The Pitch

She calls calendars a clear 'yes' product: no hidden costs, decent margin per unit, and one good design kept selling year after year. She says the trick is to bring your own art and pick a niche, and to follow buyer data from Etsy Insights and Google Trends rather than your own taste, since her best sellers were often the designs she liked least.

Who It’s Best For

Artists and illustrators who already have a body of work and want a low-effort, seasonal product line they can sell online without handling stock.

The Truth (as we see it)

This is a modest, believable account: about $3,000 in profit over three years from three designs is presented plainly, not as a get-rich claim, though the figures are her own and unaudited. Calendars are seasonal, so expect most sales in the last few months of the year and little in between. Print-on-demand margins are thin and marketplaces are crowded, so original art and a clear niche matter. She doesn't pitch a course or paid product in the video.
