Fractional Executive Services

Local & professional servicesLow startup cost
Source: David Heacock — youtube.com/watch?v=EnSJN9zl-yA
What Is It
Offer part-time senior leadership, CFO, COO, or CMO, to businesses that need executive-level guidance but can't afford, or don't need, a full-time hire. You work with several clients at once, each paying a monthly retainer for a set number of hours or specific deliverables.
The Pitch
Package your judgment into a recurring monthly fee, typically $5,000 to $15,000 per client, and take on 4 to 6 clients at once.
Best For
Someone with a genuine, provable track record of running or operating a business, not someone who has only studied the theory of business strategy.
The Truth
Heacock backs this up with his own real hiring experience. Landing the first client still requires real, sometimes slow, relationship-based sales.

AI Ghostwriting

Marketing & contentLow startup cost
Source: Mark Tilbury and Iman Gadzhi — youtube.com/watch?v=ytAW1_g2IfI
What Is It
You use AI tools like Claude, paired with a research tool, to write LinkedIn posts, email newsletters, and ad copy on behalf of founders and creators who know they should be publishing content but don't have time to write it themselves.
The Pitch
The AI does the heavy lifting, drafting research and a first pass at the writing, while you focus on making it sound like the actual client, not a generic AI voice.
Best For
Someone who already writes reasonably well and is comfortable using AI to speed up research and a first draft.
The Truth
Tilbury is fairly upfront that AI only gets you about 75% of the way there. The remaining 25% — taste, editing, knowing what the client would actually say — is a real editorial skill this doesn't remove.

Estate Sale Company

Local & professional servicesVery low startup cost
Source: Chris Koerner on The Koerner Office Podcast (guest: Braden) — youtube.com/watch?v=E9baszgY3J8
What Is It
You contract with homeowners, or their families, to liquidate the entire contents of a house, pricing and selling everything from furniture to collectibles over a weekend sale, then taking a percentage of what sells.
The Pitch
Braden started with almost no money, just tape, a price gun, and folding tables, and says he made about $6,000 profit in his very first week.
Best For
Someone comfortable with sales, organizing, and pricing hundreds of small items, who doesn't mind physical, on-site weekend work.
The Truth
This is one of the more detailed, credible accounts in the database. Worth noting this is a single market's experience; a saturated, larger city could look different.
