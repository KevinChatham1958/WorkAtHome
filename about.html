About Side Hustle Intel

A note for beta testers

Thanks for trying Side Hustle Intel early. Pick one idea in your field, or one you're just curious about, and read the full entry. Then use the form near the bottom of this page to tell us whether it felt honest — and flag anything that felt broken, confusing, or missing.

Side Hustle Intel is a free, searchable database of work-from-home business ideas.

Every entry comes from a real YouTube video — a creator walking through a specific gig, side hustle, or business model. We watch those videos, pull out the idea, and write it up in plain language: what the gig actually is, why the creator thinks it's worth doing, who it's realistically a fit for, and our own honest take — including anything that seems unverified, self-promotional, or worth a second look before you commit time or money.

We don't rank ideas or attach scores. We don't cherry-pick winners. If a creator pitched it and it isn't an outright scam or an employment listing in disguise, it's here — plain-language write-up and all — for you to judge for yourself. Every entry links back to the original video, so you can watch the full source and decide independently.

The database is growing steadily as we work through a backlog of creator videos, batch by batch.
