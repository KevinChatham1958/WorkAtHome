About Side Hustle Intel

A free, independent, searchable database of over 1,000 work-at-home gigs, each one summarized in plain English from a YouTube creator’s video.

Why it exists

YouTube is full of work-at-home ideas. Some are solid. Many are recycled, overhyped, or a lead-in to a paid course. Finding the good ones means sitting through hours of video, and the actual idea is often buried in the middle of a long sales pitch.

Side Hustle Intel does that watching for you. We pull each idea out, explain it in a few short paragraphs, tell you plainly what we think of it, and point you to the original video so you can judge for yourself.

How it works

  1. 1

    We follow the creators

    We track more than 220 work-at-home and side-hustle creators on YouTube and review their videos as they come out.

  2. 2

    We summarize every gig the same way

    Each gig gets the same four parts, so you can compare them at a glance:

What It IsThe gig in plain terms: what you would actually do and who pays you.
The PitchHow the creator presents it, including the claims they make.
Who It’s Best ForThe skills, budget and situation the gig suits.
The Truth (as we see it)Our frank take: what holds up, what’s missing, and whether the creator sells a course or tool.
  1. 3

    You search, filter and decide

    Search by keyword or creator, filter by type of gig and your situation (50+ or retired, stay-at-home parent, low startup cost and more), save the gigs you like, and watch the original video whenever you want the full story.

What we stand for

  • Free to use. No account, no paywall.
  • Independent. We don’t sell courses, and creators don’t pay to be included.
  • Honest, not hyped. The Truth on every gig is our informed opinion, based on what the video says and shows. It is not a guarantee, and no income is promised.
  • Always sourced. Every gig links to the video it came from, so you can hear it from the creator directly.
  • Growing every week. New gigs are added weekly as creators publish new videos.
