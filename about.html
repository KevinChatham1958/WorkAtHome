About Side Hustle Intel

Side Hustle Intel is a free, searchable database of thousands (soon to be!) of work-at-home gigs. Hundreds of gurus are putting out WAH videos every week, so we watch them for you and boil each one down into 4 simple parts — WHAT IT IS, THE PITCH, BEST FOR, and THE TRUTH — so you can scan a lot of ideas without spending hours watching videos.

FAQ

Why a Work-at-Home database?

Hundreds of "gurus" put out WAH gig videos. Thousands of ideas — way too much to watch.

How many gigs are in the database?

Soon to be thousands. Updated weekly.

What does this database do?

Turns WAH videos into searchable, easy-to-scan explanations of the gig, the pitch, and who it's best for.

Can I watch the original video?

Yes. We include the original source URL whenever possible.

Are you rating the gurus or their gigs?

Nope — we're not Consumer Reports. Just our quick take on the idea so you can decide.

What about all those "I made $30,000 my first month" claims?

We don't try to verify income claims. We give you a quick way to search the idea and spot the BS.

Is the database free?

Yes. Free.

Can I search the database?

Yep. There's a robust search feature.
