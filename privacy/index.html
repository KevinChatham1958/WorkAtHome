Privacy

Side Hustle Intel is a free, independent database of work-at-home gigs. We collect as little as we can.

The weekly email

  • If you sign up, we keep your email address, so we can send you one email a week listing the gigs we added.
  • Your address is stored by our email service, Kit, which sends the emails for us. We never sell, rent or share it with anyone else, including the creators whose videos we cover.
  • Every email has an unsubscribe link. One click and you're off the list.
  • Kit tells us whether an email was opened and which links were clicked, in total and per subscriber. We use that only to make the email more useful.

Using the website

  • We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which gigs, searches and filters people use. It uses cookies and doesn't tell us who you are.
  • We use Microsoft Clarity to see how people use the site: anonymous recordings of clicks, taps and scrolling, and heatmaps of where people tap. It helps us find what's confusing or broken. It uses cookies, doesn't tell us who you are, and hides anything you type into forms (such as your email address). Microsoft's privacy statement: privacy.microsoft.com.
  • Gigs you save with the ♡ heart stay in your own browser. We never see them.
  • You don't need an account or an email address to use the site.
